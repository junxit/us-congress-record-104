# CHANGE OF VOTE

**Speaking:** Moynihan, Daniel Patrick (M001054) [D-NY]

```
                             CHANGE OF VOTE

  Mr. MOYNIHAN. Mr. President, on rollcall vote 50, I voted yea. My 
intention was to vote nay. I ask unanimous consent that I be permitted 
to change my vote which in no way would change the outcome of the vote.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
