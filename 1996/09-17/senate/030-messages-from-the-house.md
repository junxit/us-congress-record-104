# MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** S. 677, H.R. 2679

```
                        MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

  At 7:53 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Ms. Goetz, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
passed the following bill, without amendment.

       S. 677. An act to repeal a redundant venue provision, and 
     for other purposes.

  The message also announced that the House agrees to the amendments of 
the Senate to the bill (H.R. 2679) to revise the boundary of the North 
Platte National Wildlife Refuge.
```
