# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 191, S. 533, H.R. 3723, H.R. 3802, H.R. 3803

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 5, rule I, the Chair will 
now put the question on each motion to suspend the rules on which 
further proceedings were postponed earlier today in the order in which 
that motion was entertained.
  Votes will be taken in the following order: H.R. 3802, by the yeas 
and nays; House Joint Resolution 191, de novo; S. 533, de novo; H.R. 
3723, de novo; and H.R. 3803, by the yeas and nays.
  The Chair will reduce to 5 minutes the time for any electronic vote 
after the first vote in this series.

                          ____________________
```
