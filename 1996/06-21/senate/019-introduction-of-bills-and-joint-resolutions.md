# INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** S. 1896, S. 1897

```
              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second time by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Mr. DODD:
       S. 1896. A bill to amend the Family and Medical Leave Act 
     of 1993 to apply the Act to a greater percentage of the 
     United States workforce and to allow employees to take 
     parental involvement leave to participate in or attend their 
     children's educational and extracurricular activities, and 
     for other purposes; to the Committee on Labor and Human 
     Resources.
           By Mrs. KASSEBAUM (for herself, Mr. Kennedy, Mr. 
             Jeffords, Mr. Pell, and Mr. Hatfield):
       S. 1897. A bill to amend the Public Health Service Act to 
     revise and extend certain programs relating to the National 
     Institutes of Health, and for other purposes; to the 
     Committee on Labor and Human Resources.

                          ____________________
```
