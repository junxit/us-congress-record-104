# Daily Digest/Senate Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
(Committees not listed did not meet)
NOMINATIONS
Committee on Foreign Relations: Committee concluded hearings on the 
nominations of John Christian Kornblum, of Michigan, to be Assistant 
Secretary for European and Canadian Affairs, and Barbara Mills Larkin, 
of North Carolina, to be Assistant Secretary for Legislative Affairs, 
both of the Department of State, Madeleine May Kunin, of Vermont, to be 
Ambassador to Switzerland, and A. Vernon Weaver, of Arkansas, to be the 
Representative of the United States to the European Union, with the 
rank and status of Ambassador, after the nominees testified and 
answered questions in their own behalf. Ms. Kunin was introduced by 
Senator Leahy.
```
