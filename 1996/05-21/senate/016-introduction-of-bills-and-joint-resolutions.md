# INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** S. 1785, S. 1786, S. 1787, S. 1788

```
              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second time by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Mr. KENNEDY (for himself and Mr. Kerry):
       S. 1785. A bill to establish in the Department of the 
     Interior the Essex National Heritage Area Commission, and for 
     other purposes; to the Committee on Energy and Natural 
     Resources.
           By Mr. WELLSTONE:
       S. 1786. A bill to require the Secretary of Veterans 
     Affairs and the Secretary of Health and Human Resources to 
     carry out a demonstration project to provide the Department 
     of Veterans Affairs with reimbursement from the medicare 
     program for health care services provided to certain 
     medicare-eligible veterans; to the Committee on Finance.
           By Mr. PRESSLER (for himself, Mr. D'Amato, Mr. Breaux, 
             and Mr. Graham):
       S. 1787. A bill to amend the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of 
     the United States with respect to fireworks; to the Committee 
     on Finance.
           By Mr. FAIRCLOTH:
       S. 1788. A bill to amend the National Labor Relations Act 
     and the Railway Labor Act to repeal those provisions of 
     Federal law that require employees to pay union dues or fees 
     as a condition of employment, and for other purposes; read 
     the first time.

                          ____________________
```
