# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Grassley, Chuck (G000386) [R-IA]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. GRASSLEY. Mr. President, tomorrow morning at 9:15 a.m., the 
Senate will begin a series of rollcall votes on or in relation to the 
amendments to the budget resolution. That series of votes is expected 
to continue throughout the day in an attempt to complete action on the 
budget early Wednesday evening. All Senators are asked to remain in or 
around the Senate Chamber during Wednesday's session in order to 
facilitate the numerous votes. As a reminder, all votes following the 
first will be limited to 10 minutes in length.
```
