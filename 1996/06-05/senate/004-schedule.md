# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 1

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, this morning the Senate will be in a period 
of morning business until the hour of 11 a.m. I believe the Senator 
from Delaware, Senator Roth, is prepared to begin speaking immediately. 
Following morning business, the Senate will begin debate on House Joint 
Resolution 1, the balanced budget constitutional amendment.
  Senators are reminded that a vote on passage on the balanced budget 
amendment will occur at 12 noon tomorrow by unanimous consent. It is 
also possible that the Senate may consider other legislative or 
Executive Calendar items that can be cleared for action. I know that 
the distinguished majority leader, Senator Dole, has been working to 
see if some of these executive items can be cleared. We hope that can 
be worked out during this week.
  I yield the floor.

                          ____________________
```
