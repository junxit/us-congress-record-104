further consideration of S. Con. Res. 63, to express the sense of the 
Congress that the Secretary of Agriculture should dispose of all 
remaining commodities in the disaster of livestock producers whose 
ability to maintain livestock is adversely affected by disaster 
conditions existing in certain areas of the United States, and the 
resolution was then agreed to, after agreeing to the following 
amendment proposed thereto:
  Page S5870

disaster conditions.

correction.
