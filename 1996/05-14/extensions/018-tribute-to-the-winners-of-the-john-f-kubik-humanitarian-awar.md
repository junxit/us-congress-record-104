# TRIBUTE TO THE WINNERS OF THE JOHN F. KUBIK HUMANITARIAN AWARD

**Speaking:** Lipinski, William O. (L000342) [D-IL]

```
     TRIBUTE TO THE WINNERS OF THE JOHN F. KUBIK HUMANITARIAN AWARD

                                 ______


                        HON. WILLIAM O. LIPINSKI

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, May 14, 1996

  Mr. LIPINSKI. Mr. Speaker, I would like to extend my congratulations 
to the winner of this year's John F. Kubik Humanitarian Award, Ms. 
Vlasta Sneberger.
  Ms. Sneberger, a regional branch manager for MidAmerica Federal 
Savings Bank in Berwyn, IL, was presented with the award last month by 
the Sequin Retarded Citizens Association at their annual award dinner.
  Ms. Sneberger was recognized for her tireless service to the 
community. The John F. Kubik Award, named for a local newspaper 
journalist and publisher, was established to embody the community 
spirit and dedication of its namesake.
   Mr. Speaker, I commend Ms. Sneberger on receiving this award and 
wish her many more years of service to her community.

                          ____________________
```
