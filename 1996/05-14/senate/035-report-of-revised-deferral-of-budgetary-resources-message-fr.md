# REPORT OF REVISED DEFERRAL OF BUDGETARY RESOURCES--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT--PM 144

```
  REPORT OF REVISED DEFERRAL OF BUDGETARY RESOURCES--MESSAGE FROM THE 
                           PRESIDENT--PM 144

  The PRESIDING OFFICER laid before the Senate the following message 
from the President of the United States, together with an accompanying 
report; referred jointly, pursuant to the order of January 1, 1975, as 
modified by the order of April 11, 1986, to the Committee on 
Appropriations, to the Committee on the Budget, and to the Committee on 
Foreign Relations.

To the Congress of the United States:
  In accordance with the Congressional Budget and Impoundment Control 
Act of 1974, I herewith report one revised deferral of budgetary 
resources, totaling $1.4 billion. The deferral affects the 
International Security Assistance program.
                                                  William J. Clinton.  
  The White House, May 14, 1996.

                          ____________________
```
