                   EXECUTIVE AND OTHER COMMUNICATIONS

  The following communications were laid before the Senate, together

     cost comparison study relative to Lackland Air Force Base 
     [AFB], Texas; to the Committee on Armed Services.
       EC-2155. A communication from the Chief (Programs and

       EC-2166. A communication from the General Counsel of the
