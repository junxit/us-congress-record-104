# APPOINTMENT BY THE VICE PRESIDENT

```
                   APPOINTMENT BY THE VICE PRESIDENT

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair on behalf of the Vice President, 
pursuant to 22 U.S.C. section 276h through 276k, appoints the Senator 
from Texas [Mrs. Hutchison] as the chairman of the Senate delegation to 
the Mexico-United States Interparliamentary Union during the second 
session of the 104th Congress.
```
