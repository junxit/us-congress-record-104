# APPOINTMENT OF CONFEREE IN LIEU OF CONFEREE H.R. 956, COMMON SENSE PRODUCT LIABILITY AND LEGAL REFORM ACT OF 1995

**Measures:** H.R. 956

```
  APPOINTMENT OF CONFEREE IN LIEU OF CONFEREE H.R. 956, COMMON SENSE 
             PRODUCT LIABILITY AND LEGAL REFORM ACT OF 1995

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, the Chair appoints the 
gentleman from Massachusetts [Mr. Markey] as conferee on the bill (H.R. 
956) to establish legal standards and procedures for product liability 
litigation, and for other purposes, to replace the gentleman from 
Oregon [Mr. Wyden].
  There was no objection.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The Clerk will notify the Senate of the 
change in conferees.

                          ____________________
```
