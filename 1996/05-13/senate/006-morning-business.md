# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Byrd, Robert C. (B001210) [D-WV]

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. BYRD. Is the Senate in a period of morning business?

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. For 5 minutes.
  Mr. BYRD. I thank the Chair.
  Mr. President, I may take just a little longer than 5 minutes. I ask
```
