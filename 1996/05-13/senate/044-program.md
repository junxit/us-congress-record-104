# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, Senators are reminded that a cloture vote on 
the pending Dole amendment No. 3961 to the White House Travel Office 
bill will occur at 2:15 p.m. tomorrow, unless an agreement can be 
reached on the gas tax, minimum wage, and TEAM Act issues. However, 
votes are expected on Tuesday, and it is hoped that a unanimous-consent 
agreement can be reached with respect to the pending White House Travel 
Office legislation.
  Also, it is the intention of the majority leader to begin 
consideration of the budget resolution as early as Wednesday of this 
week. Therefore, late night sessions can be anticipated probably each 
night.

                          ____________________
```
