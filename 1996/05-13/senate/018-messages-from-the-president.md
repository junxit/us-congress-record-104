# MESSAGES FROM THE PRESIDENT

```
                      MESSAGES FROM THE PRESIDENT

  Messages from the President of the United States were communicated to 
the Senate by Mr. Thomas, one of his secretaries.


                      EXECUTIVE MESSAGES REFERRED

  As in executive session in the Presiding Officer laid before the 
Senate messages from the President of the United States submitting two 
withdrawals and sundry nominations which were referred to the 
appropriate committees.
  (The nominations received today are printed at the end of the Senate 
proceedings.)
```
