# TRIBUTE TO DR. DAVID G. CRAIG

**Speaking:** Duncan, John J., Jr. (D000533) [R-TN]

```
                     TRIBUTE TO DR. DAVID G. CRAIG

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JOHN J. DUNCAN, JR.

                              of tennessee

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, August 2, 1996

  Mr. DUNCAN. Mr. Speaker, I want to congratulate Dr. David G. Craig, a 
University of Tennessee human ecology professor, for being named as the 
1996 Higher Education Teacher of the Year. This indeed is a great honor 
and one which Dr. Craig should be very proud to receive.
  The Tennessee Education Association selected Dr. Craig based on 
several criteria. He has demonstrated excellence in the classroom, 
professional merit, and participation in professional, community, and 
political activities at the University of Tennessee.
  I request that a copy of the article ``Professor Distinguished as 
Teacher of Year'' which appeared in the University of Tennessee Daily 
Beacon be placed in the Record at this point. I would like to call it 
to the attention of my colleagues and other readers of the Record.

                          ____________________
```
