# NOMINATIONS TO REMAIN IN STATUS QUO UNTIL SEPTEMBER 2, 1996

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

```
      NOMINATIONS TO REMAIN IN STATUS QUO UNTIL SEPTEMBER 2, 1996

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, as in executive session, I ask unanimous 
consent that all nominations received by the Senate during the 104th 
Congress, 2d session, remain in status quo notwithstanding the August 2 
adjournment until September 2, 1996, and rule XXXI, paragraph 6 of the 
standing rules of the Senate.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
