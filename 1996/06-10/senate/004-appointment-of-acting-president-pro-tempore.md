# APPOINTMENT OF ACTING PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

```
              APPOINTMENT OF ACTING PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will please read a communication to 
the Senate from the President pro tempore [Mr. Thurmond].
  The legislative clerk read as follows:
                                                      U.S. Senate,


                                        President pro tempore,

                                    Washington, DC, June 10, 1996.
     To the Senate:
       Under the provisions of rule I, section 3, of the Standing 
     Rules of the Senate, I hereby appoint the Honorable Jon Kyl, 
     a Senator from the State of Arizona, to perform the duties of 
     the Chair.
                                                   Strom Thurmond,
                                            President pro tempore.

  Mr. KYL thereupon assumed the chair as Acting President pro tempore.

                          ____________________
```
