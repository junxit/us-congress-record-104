# MESSAGES FROM THE PRESIDENT

```
                      MESSAGES FROM THE PRESIDENT

  Messages from the President of the United States were communicated to 
the Senate by Mr. Thomas, one of his secretaries.


                      executive messages referred

  As in executive session the Presiding Officer laid before the Senate 
messages from the President of the United States submitting one 
withdrawal and sundry nominations which were referred to the 
appropriate committees.
  (The nominations and withdrawal received today are printed at the end 
of the Senate proceedings.)
```
