# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** H.R. 3060, H.R. 3364, H.R. 3400

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to the provisions of clause 5 of 
rule I, the chair will not put the question on each motion to suspend 
the rules on which further proceedings were postponed earlier today in 
the order in which that motion was entertained.
  Votes will be taken in the following order: H.R. 3364, by the yeas 
and nays; H.R. 3400, by the yeas and nays; and H.R. 3060, by the yeas 
and nays.
  The Chair will reduce to 5 minutes the time for any electronic vote 
after the first such vote in this series.

                          ____________________
```
