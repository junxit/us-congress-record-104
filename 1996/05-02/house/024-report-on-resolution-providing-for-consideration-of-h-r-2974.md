# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2974, CRIMES AGAINST CHILDREN AND ELDERLY PERSONS INCREASED PUNISHMENT ACT

**Measures:** H.Res. 421, H.R. 2974

```
 REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2974, CRIMES 
     AGAINST CHILDREN AND ELDERLY PERSONS INCREASED PUNISHMENT ACT

  Ms. GREENE of Utah, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a 
privileged report (Rept. No. 104-552) on the resolution (H. Res. 421) 
providing for consideration of the bill (H.R. 2974) to amend the 
Violent Crime Control and Law Enforcement Act of 1994 to provide 
enhanced penalties for crimes against elderly and child victims, which 
was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
