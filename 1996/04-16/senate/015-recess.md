# RECESS

**Speaking:** Dole, Robert J. (D000401) [R-KS]

```
                                 RECESS

  Mr. DOLE. Mr. President, I move we stand in recess until 2:15.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection to standing in recess until 
2:15?
  Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The motion was agreed to, and, at 11:21 a.m., the Senate recessed 
until 2:15 p.m.; whereupon, the Senate reassembled when called to order 
by the Presiding Officer [Mr. Coats].
```
