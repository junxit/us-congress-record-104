# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, for the information of all Senators, this 
morning the Senate will conduct a period for morning business until 
10:45 a.m., with Senator Grassley to speak for up to 15 minutes and 
Senator Hatch for up to 45 minutes.
  Following morning business, the Senate will resume consideration of 
the illegal immigration bill and the pending amendments. The yeas and 
nays are ordered on several of these amendments; however, those votes 
will not occur prior to the scheduled vote at 2:15.
  As a reminder, at 2:15 p.m. today, there will be a cloture vote on 
the motion to proceed to the Whitewater resolution. The Senate will 
recess from the hours of 12:30 p.m., to 2:15 p.m. for the weekly policy 
conferences to meet. The Senate can expect rollcall votes to occur 
throughout the session today in order to make progress on the pending 
illegal immigration bill.
  Mr. President, I yield the floor.

                          ____________________
```
