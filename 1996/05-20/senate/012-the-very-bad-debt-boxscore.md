# THE VERY BAD DEBT BOXSCORE

**Speaking:** Helms, Jesse (H000463) [R-NC]

```
                       THE VERY BAD DEBT BOXSCORE

  Mr. HELMS. Mr. President, at the close of business Friday, May 17, 
1996, the Federal debt stood at $5,114,511,847,123.90.
  On a per capita basis, every man, woman, and child in America owes 
$19,308.54 as his or her share of that debt.
```
