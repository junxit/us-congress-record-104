# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Rev. James David Ford, D.D., offered the following 
prayer:
  In all our days, we pray for faith; in all the hours we yearn for 
hope; in every moment of our time, we pray that Your presence of love 
will be in us and with us and about us. O gracious God, from whom we 
have come and to whom we shall return, bless us, each one, whatever our 
need in the depths of our hearts and souls. Amen.
```
