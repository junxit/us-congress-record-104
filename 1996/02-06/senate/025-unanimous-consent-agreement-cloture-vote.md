# UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT--CLOTURE VOTE

**Speaking:** Dole, Robert J. (D000401) [R-KS], Lugar, Richard G. (L000504) [R-IN]

```
               UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT--CLOTURE VOTE

  Mr. DOLE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the cloture 
vote on the substitute occur at 4:10 today, the time between now and 
then to be equally divided between the distinguished chairman, Senator 
Lugar, and Senator Leahy, or their designees.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection? The Chair hears none, and 
it is so ordered.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Who yields time?
  Mr. LUGAR. I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. LUGAR. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
