# UNANIMOUS-CONSENT REQUEST--H.R. 2980

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS], Daschle, Thomas A. (D000064) [D-SD]

**Measures:** H.R. 2980

```
                  UNANIMOUS-CONSENT REQUEST--H.R. 2980

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent the Senate turn to 
Calendar No. 421, H.R. 2980, a bill relating to stalking, and the bill 
be then read a third time and passed, the motion to reconsider be laid 
upon the table, and any statements relating to the measure appear at 
this point in the Record.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Mr. DASCHLE. Because we have amendments pending, we are not prepared 
at this point to agree to this unanimous consent as well.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard.
```
