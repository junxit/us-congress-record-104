# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, there will now be a 
period for the transaction of morning business not to extend beyond the 
hour of 10 a.m. Under that order, 40 minutes shall be under the control 
of the Democratic leader and 20 minutes under the control of the 
Senator from Georgia.
```
