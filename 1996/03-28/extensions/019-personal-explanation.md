# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Hostettler, John N. (H000807) [R-IN]

**Measures:** H.R. 2937

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______


                        HON. JOHN N. HOSTETTLER

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Wednesday, March 27, 1996

  Mr. HOSTETTLER. Mr. Speaker, due to a snow emergency in my district 
that began early March 19, 1996, I was unable to return to Washington, 
DC, until late evening on March 20, 1996. As a result of this 
unforeseen delay, I missed passage of H.R. 2937. Had I been able to be 
here for these votes I would have voted ``yea'' on roll No. 69.
```
