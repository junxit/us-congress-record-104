# CONGRATULATIONS TO PRESIDENT LEE TENG-HUI

**Speaking:** Hinchey, Maurice D. (H000627) [D-NY]

```
               CONGRATULATIONS TO PRESIDENT LEE TENG-HUI

                                 ______


                        HON. MAURICE D. HINCHEY

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                       Wednesday, March 27, 1996

  Mr. HINCHEY. Mr. Speaker, I wish to convey my congratulations to 
President Lee Teng-hui of the Republic of China. In the first direct 
Presidential election in Chinese history held on March 23, Lee Teng-hui 
won 54 percent of the vote, far outdistancing his three opponents. 
Democracy has finally arrived in the Republic of China and I have 
nothing but admiration and good wishes for President Lee.
  I hope my colleagues will join me in wishing that God may continue to 
grant President Lee, and his Vice President-elect Lien Chan, all the 
wisdom in government their country in the years ahead, and that the 
people of the Republic of China will continue to enjoy prosperity and 
freedom.

                          ____________________
```
