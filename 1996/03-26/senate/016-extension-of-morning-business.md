# EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS], Bradley, Bill (B001225) [D-NJ]

```
                     EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. LOTT. I now ask there be a period for morning business until the 
hour of 12:30, with the time between now and 12:30 equally divided 
between the two leaders or their designees.
  Mr. BRADLEY. Mr. President, reserving the right to object.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair recognizes the Senator from New 
Jersey.
  Mr. BRADLEY. I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The bill clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. LOTT. I renew my unanimous consent request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The Chair recognizes the Senator from Massachusetts.

                          ____________________
```
