# EXECUTIVE MESSAGES REFERRED

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 168

```
                      EXECUTIVE MESSAGES REFERRED

  As in executive session the Presiding Officer laid before the Senate 
messages from the President of the United States submitting sundry 
nominations which were referred to the Committee on Armed Services.
  (The nominations received today are printed at the end of the Senate 
proceedings.)

[[Page S2858]]



                        MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

  At 4:39 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mr. Hays, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
passed the following joint resolution, in which it requests the 
concurrence of the House:

       H.J. Res. 168. Joint resolution waiving certain enrollment 
     requirements with respect to two bills of the One Hundred 
     Fourth Congress.
```
