# THE BAD DEBT BOXSCORE

**Speaking:** Helms, Jesse (H000463) [R-NC]

```
                         THE BAD DEBT BOXSCORE

  Mr. HELMS. Mr. President, as of the close of business yesterday, 
March 25, 1996, the Federal debt stood--down-to-the-penny--at 
$5,063,054,197,564.33. On a per capita basis, every man, woman and 
child in America owes $19,141.70 as his or her share of that debt.
```
