# ANTARCTIC SCIENCE TOURISM AND CONSERVATION ACT OF 1996

**Speaking:** Bond, Christopher S. (B000611) [R-MO]

**Measures:** S. 1645

```
         ANTARCTIC SCIENCE TOURISM AND CONSERVATION ACT OF 1996

  Mr. BOND. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the immediate consideration of Calendar 513.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report.
  The bill clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 1645) to regulate U.S. scientific and tourist 
     activities in Antarctica, to conserve Antarctic resources, 
     and for other purposes.

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection to the immediate 
consideration of the bill?
  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the bill.
```
