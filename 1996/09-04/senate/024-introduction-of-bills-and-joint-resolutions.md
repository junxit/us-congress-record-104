# INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** S. 2052, S. 2053, S. 2054

```
              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second time by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Mrs. BOXER:
       S. 2052. A bill to provide for disposal of certain public 
     lands in support of the Manzanar National Historic Site in 
     the State of California, and for other purposes; to the 
     Committee on Energy and Natural Resources.
           By Mr. GRASSLEY (for himself, Mr. D'Amato, and Mr. 
             Shelby):
       S. 2053. A bill to strengthen narcotics reporting 
     requirements and to require the imposition of certain 
     sanctions on countries that fail to take effective action 
     against the production of and trafficking in illicit 
     narcotics and psychotropic drugs and other controlled 
     substances, and for other purposes; read the first time.
           By Mr. COCHRAN:
       S. 2054. A bill to amend the Higher Education Act of 1965 
     to exempt certain small lenders from the audit requirements 
     of the guaranteed student loan program; to the Committee on 
     Labor and Human Resources.

                          ____________________
```
