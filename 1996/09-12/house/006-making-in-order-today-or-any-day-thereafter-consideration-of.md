**Speaking:** Myers, John T. (M001130) [R-IN]

**Measures:** H.R. 3816

```
MAKING IN ORDER TODAY OR ANY DAY THEREAFTER CONSIDERATION OF CONFERENCE 
 REPORT ON H.R. 3816, ENERGY AND WATER DEVELOPMENT APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 
                                  1997
```
