# NOTICE OF HEARING

**Speaking:** Craig, Larry E. (C000858) [R-ID]

**Measures:** S. 987

```
                           NOTICE OF HEARING


               committee on energy and natural resources

  Mr. CRAIG. Mr. President, I would like to announce for the public 
that a hearing has been scheduled before the Subcommittee on Forest and 
Public Land Management.

[[Page S10507]]

  The hearing will take place Wednesday, September 25, 1996, at 2:30 
p.m. in room SD-366 of the Dirksen Senate Office Building in 
Washington, DC.
  The purpose of this hearing is to receive testimony on S. 987, a bill 
to provide for the full settlement of all claims of Swain County, NC, 
against the United States under the agreement dated July 30, 1943, and 
for other purposes.
  Those who wish to submit written statements should write to the 
Committee on Energy and Natural Resources, U.S. Senate, Washington, DC 
20510. For further information, please call Judy Brown or Mark Rey at 
(202) 224-6170.

                          ____________________
```
