# BIRTHDAY GREETINGS FOR MRS. ESTHER WERNER

**Speaking:** Ashcroft, John (A000356) [R-MO]

```
               BIRTHDAY GREETINGS FOR MRS. ESTHER WERNER

  Mr. ASHCROFT. Madam President, I rise today to recognize Mrs. Esther 
Werner of Creve Coeur, MI. Mrs. Werner celebrated her 90th birthday on
```
