# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Chafee, John H. (C000269) [R-RI]

**Measures:** S. 1664

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. CHAFEE. Mr. President, on behalf of the majority leader, let me 
say there will be a period of morning business until the hour of 10 
o'clock. At 10 o'clock, the Senate will resume consideration of S. 
1664, the immigration bill, with Senator Simpson to be recognized to 
offer an amendment.
  Rollcall votes can be expected throughout the day on the immigration 
bill. It is the hope of the majority leader that we may complete action 
on that bill, the immigration bill, during today's session. It is also 
possible for the Senate to consider the omnibus appropriations 
conference report if that measure becomes available.

                          ____________________
```
