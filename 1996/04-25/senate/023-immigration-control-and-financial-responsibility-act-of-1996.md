# IMMIGRATION CONTROL AND FINANCIAL RESPONSIBILITY ACT OF 1996

**Speaking:** Kempthorne, Dirk (K000088) [R-ID]

```
      IMMIGRATION CONTROL AND FINANCIAL RESPONSIBILITY ACT OF 1996

  The Senate continued with the consideration of the bill.


         Amendment No. 3746 to Amendment No. 3745, As Modified

  Mr. KEMPTHORNE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that amendment 
No. 3746 be modified, and I send the modification to the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The amendment (No. 3746), as modified, is as follows:

       At the end of the amendment add the following: 
     ``Notwithstanding any other provisions of the bill, 
     provisions of the bill regarding the use of volunteers shall 
     become effective 30 days after enactment''.
```
