# SEQUENTIAL REFERRAL OF S. 1660

**Speaking:** Kempthorne, Dirk (K000088) [R-ID]

**Measures:** S. 1660

```
                     SEQUENTIAL REFERRAL OF S. 1660

  Mr. KEMPTHORNE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that if and 
when the Environment and Public Works Committee reports the bill S. 
1660, the National Invasive Species Act of 1996, the bill be 
sequentially referred to the Committee on Commerce, Science, and 
Transportation for a period not to exceed 20 calendar days; further, 
that if the measure has not been reported following that period, it be 
automatically discharged and placed on the calendar.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
