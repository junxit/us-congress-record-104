# Daily Digest/COMMITTEE MEETINGS FOR 1996-01-25

```
                    COMMITTEE MEETINGS FOR THURSDAY,

                            JANUARY 25, 1996

        (Committee meetings are open unless otherwise indicated)


                                 Senate

  Committee on Energy and Natural Resources, Subcommittee on Forests

Rayburn.
  Committee on National Security, Subcommittee on Military Research

  Joint Hearing: Senate Committee on Governmental Affairs, 
Subcommittee on Post Office and Civil Service, to hold joint 
hearings with the House Committee on Government Reform and 
Oversight's Subcommittee on Postal Service to examine proposals to 
reform the United States Postal Service, focusing on international 
postal operations, 9:30 a.m., SD-342.
```
