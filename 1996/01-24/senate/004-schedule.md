# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. LOTT. This afternoon, the Senate will be in session for a period 
of morning business. There will be no rollcall votes during today's 
session. The Senate may consider any legislative items that can be 
cleared for action by unanimous consent. All Senators should be 
reminded the continuing resolution expires Friday of this week. It is 
therefore expected the Senate will consider a new continuing resolution 
when one becomes available from the House. The Senate may also consider 
the Department of Defense authorization conference report as well as 
the START II Treaty.
  I yield the floor, Mr. President.

                          ____________________
```
