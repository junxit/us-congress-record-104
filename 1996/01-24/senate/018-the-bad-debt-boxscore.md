# THE BAD DEBT BOXSCORE

**Speaking:** Helms, Jesse (H000463) [R-NC]

```
                         THE BAD DEBT BOXSCORE

  Mr. HELMS. Mr. President, the Federal Government is, as the saying 
goes, living on borrowed time, not to mention borrowed money--nearly $5 
trillion of it. As of the close of business yesterday, Tuesday, January 
23, the Federal debt stood at $4,987,963,203,048.04. On a per capita 
basis, every man, woman, and child in America owes $18,932.74 as his or 
her share of the Federal debt.
```
