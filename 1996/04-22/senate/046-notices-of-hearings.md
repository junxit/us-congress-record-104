# NOTICES OF HEARINGS

**Speaking:** McCain, John (M000303) [R-AZ]

**Measures:** S. 1264

```
                          NOTICES OF HEARINGS


                      committee on Indian affairs

  Mr. McCAIN. Mr. President, I would like to announce that the Senate 
Committee on Indian Affairs has rescheduled the business meeting that 
was originally scheduled for 9 a.m. on Tuesday, April 23, 1996, to 9:30 
a.m. on Tuesday, April 23, 1996.
  Those wishing additional information should contact the Committee on 
Indian Affairs at 224-2251.


                      committee on indian affairs

  Mr. McCAIN. Mr. President, I would like to announce that the Senate 
Committee on Indian Affairs will conduct a joint hearing with the 
Subcommittee on Native American and Insular Affairs of the House 
Committee on Natural Resources during the session of the Senate on 
Thursday, April 25, 1996, on S. 1264, a bill to provide certain 
benefits of the Missouri River Basin Pick-Sloan Project to the Crow 
Creek Sioux Tribe, and for other purposes. The hearing will be held at 
9:00 a.m. in room 485 of the Russell Senate Office Building.
  Those wishing additional information should contact the Committee on 
Indian Affairs at 224-2251.

                          ____________________
```
