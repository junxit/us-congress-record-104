# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Dole, Robert J. (D000401) [R-KS]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. DOLE. Mr. President, for the information of all Senators, the 
Senate will resume the term limits legislation at 10 a.m. At 12 noon on 
Tuesday, under a previous order, there will be 30 minutes of debate 
regarding the health insurance reform bill. It is hoped that an 
agreement can be reached Tuesday morning enabling the cloture vote on 
the term limits legislation to occur at 3:45 or 4 o'clock tomorrow 
afternoon. This would allow for the final passage vote on the health 
insurance reform bill to occur at 2:15 on Tuesday, immediately 
following the recess for the party conferences. The Senate may also 
turn to other legislative items that can be cleared.
  So if we can work out this vote on term limits, that would probably 
be another 1\1/2\ hours for debate equally divided.

                          ____________________
```
