# MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING ADJOURNMENT

**Measures:** H.R. 3034

```
          MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING ADJOURNMENT


                          ENROLLED BILL SIGNED

  Under the authority of the order of the Senate of January 4, 1995, 
the Secretary of the Senate, on April 19, 1996, during the adjournment 
of the Senate, received a message from the House of Representatives 
announcing that the Speaker has signed the following enrolled bill:

       H.R. 3034. An act to amend the Indian Self-Determination 
     and Education Assistance Act to extend for two months the 
     authority for promulgating regulations under the Act.

  Under the authority of the order of the Senate of January 4, 1995, 
the enrolled bill was signed subsequently on April 19, 1996, during the 
adjournment of the Senate, by the President pro tempore [Mr. Thurmond].

                          ____________________
```
