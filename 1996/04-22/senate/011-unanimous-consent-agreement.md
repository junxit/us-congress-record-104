# UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT

**Speaking:** Murray, Patty (M001111) [D-WA], Feinstein, Dianne (F000062) [D-CA]

```
                      UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT

  Mrs. MURRAY. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that 15 minutes 
of the Democratic leader's time be reserved for Senator Kennedy.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mrs. MURRAY. Mr. President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mrs. FEINSTEIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mrs. FEINSTEIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent to be 
recognized for the purposes of morning business for such time as I may 
consume.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered. The 
Senator may proceed.
  Mrs. FEINSTEIN. Thank you very much, Mr. President.

                          ____________________
```
