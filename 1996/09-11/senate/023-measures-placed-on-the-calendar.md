# MEASURES PLACED ON THE CALENDAR

**Measures:** S. 391, H.R. 2512

```
                    MEASURES PLACED ON THE CALENDAR

  The following measure was placed on the calendar under the order of 
February 10, 1995:

       S. 391. A bill to authorize and direct the Secretaries of 
     the Interior and Agriculture to undertake activities to halt 
     and reverse the decline in forest health on Federal lands, 
     and for other purposes.

  The following measure was read the first and second times by 
unanimous consent and placed on the calendar:

       H.R. 2512. An act to provide for certain benefits of the 
     Pick-Sloan Missouri River basin program to the Crow Creek 
     Sioux Tribe, and for other purposes.
```
