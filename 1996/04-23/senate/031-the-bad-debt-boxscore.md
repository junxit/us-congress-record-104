# THE BAD DEBT BOXSCORE

**Speaking:** Helms, Jesse (H000463) [R-NC]

```
                         THE BAD DEBT BOXSCORE

  Mr. HELMS. Mr. President, at the close of business Monday, April 22, 
1996, the Federal debt stood at $5,101,586,172,580.18.
  On a per capita basis, every man, woman, and child in America owes 
$19,273.81 as his or her share of that debt.
```
