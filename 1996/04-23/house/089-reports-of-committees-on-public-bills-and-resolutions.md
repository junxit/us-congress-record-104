         REPORTS OF COMMITTEES ON PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

the Clerk for printing and reference to the proper calendar, as

     proper disposal of used nickel cadmium batteries, small 
     sealed lead-acid batteries, and certain other batteries, and 
     for other purposes; with an amendment (Rept. 104-530). 
     Referred to the Committee of the Whole House on the State of 
     the Union.

     bill to amend the

     (Rept. 104-531). Referred to the Committee of the Whole House
