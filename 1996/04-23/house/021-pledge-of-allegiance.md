# PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE

**Speaking:** Gilman, Benjamin A. (G000212) [R-NY]

**Measures:** H.R. 1561

```
                          PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Will the gentleman from Ohio [Mr. Traficant] 
come forward and lead the House in the Pledge of Allegiance.
  Mr. TRAFICANT led the Pledge of Allegiance as follows:

       I pledge allegiance to the Flag of the United States of 
     America, and to the Republic for which it stands, one nation 
     under God, indivisible, with liberty and justice for all.

[[Page H3666]]



POSTPONING FURTHER CONSIDERATION OF VETO MESSAGE ON H.R. 1561, AMERICAN 
                         OVERSEAS INTERESTS ACT

  Mr. GILMAN. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that notwithstanding 
the order of the House of Monday, April 15, 1996, further consideration 
of the veto message on H.R. 1561 be postponed until Tuesday, April 30, 
1996.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from New York?
  There was no objection.
```
