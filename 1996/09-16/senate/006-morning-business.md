# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there will now 
be a period for the transaction of morning business for not to exceed 2 
hours, with the time until 1 p.m. to be under the control of the 
Democratic leader, Mr. Daschle, and the time between 1 and 2 p.m. to be 
under the control of the Senator from Georgia, Mr. Coverdell.

                          ____________________
```
