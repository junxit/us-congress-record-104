# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Gorton, Slade (G000333) [R-WA]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. GORTON. For the information of all Senators, under the previous 
order, tomorrow morning the Senate will be debating the energy and 
water appropriations conference report. Following that debate, at 11 
a.m., the Senate will resume the Interior appropriations bill debate 
and the pending Bumpers amendment on grazing.
  As a reminder to all Members, at approximately 2:35 tomorrow, there 
will be two consecutive rollcall votes on those issues. Senators should 
expect additional votes throughout the day on Tuesday and may also turn 
to any other items cleared for action.
```
