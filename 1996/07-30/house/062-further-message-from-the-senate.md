# FURTHER MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** H.R. 3754

```
                    FURTHER MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A further message from the Senate by Mr. Lundregan, one of its 
clerks, announced that the Senate had passed with amendments in which 
the concurrence of the House is requested, a bill of the House of the 
following title:

       H.R. 3754. An act making appropriations for the Legislative 
     Branch for the fiscal year ending September 30, 1997, and for 
     other purposes.

  The message also announced that the Senate insists upon its 
amendments to the bill (H.R. 3754) ``An Act making appropriations for 
the Legislative Branch for the fiscal year ending September 30, 1997, 
and for other purposes,'' requests a conference with the House on the 
disagreeing votes of the two Houses thereon, and appoints Mr. Mack, Mr. 
Bennett, Mr. Campbell, Mr. Hatfield, Mrs. Murray, Ms. Mikulski, and Mr. 
Byrd to be the conferees on the part of the Senate.

                          ____________________
```
