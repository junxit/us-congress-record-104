# Text of House Amendment 5

**Speaking:** Green, Gene (G000410) [D-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 123

```
H.R. 123

                     Offered By: Mr. Green of Texas

           (Page and line number references are to H.R. 3898)

       Amendment No. 5: Page 4, line 12, after the period, insert 
     the following:
       In order for the Federal Government to meet its obligation 
     of encouraging individuals to learn the English language, 
     this chapter shall take effect only after the date of the 
     enactment of an appropriation act which provides funding for 
     State programs under the Adult Education Act at a level that 
     exceeds $500,000,000.
```
