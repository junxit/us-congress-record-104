**Speaking:** Borski, Robert A. (B000644) [D-PA]

**Measures:** H.R. 3592

```
  AUTHORIZING THE CLERK TO CORRECT SECTION 585 IN ENGROSSMENT OF H.R. 
                 3592, WATER RESOURCES DEVELOPMENT ACT

  Mr. BORSKI. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that in the 
engrossment of the bill, H.R. 3592, the Clerk be directed to make a
```
