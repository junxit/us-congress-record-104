# Text of House Amendment 19

**Speaking:** Velazquez, Nydia M. (V000081) [D-NY]

**Measures:** H.R. 123

```
H.R. 123

                       Offered By: Ms. Velazquez

           (Page and line number references are to H.R. 3898)

       Amendment No. 19: Page 4, strike lines 24 through 25, and 
     insert the following:
       ``(1) to communicate in any form with representatives of 
     the Federal Government in the language in which the person 
     feels most comfortable;''

       ``(d) Elected Officials.--Every elected offical is entitled 
     to communicate in any form with constituents in the language 
     in which the elected official feels most comfortable;''.
```
