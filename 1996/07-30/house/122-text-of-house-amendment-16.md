# Text of House Amendment 16

**Speaking:** Serrano, Jose E. (S000248) [D-NY]

**Measures:** H.R. 123

```
H.R. 123

                        Offered By: Mr. Serrano

           (Page and line number references are to H.R. 3898)

       Amendment No. 16: Page 8, before line 4, insert the 
     following new section:

     SEC. 103. PROHIBITION AGAINST COMBAT DUTY FOR MEMBERS OF THE 
                   ARMED FORCES NOT FLUENT IN ENGLISH.

       A member of the Armed Forces who is not fluent in the 
     English language in accordance with section 165(a) may not be 
     assigned to combat duty.
```
