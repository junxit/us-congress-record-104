# Text of House Amendment 24

**Speaking:** Velazquez, Nydia M. (V000081) [D-NY]

**Measures:** H.R. 123

```
H.R. 123

                       Offered By: Ms. Velazquez

           (Page and line number references are to H.R. 3898)

       Amendment No. 24: Insert at end of Title II, the following 
     new section:

     SEC.   . LIMITATION OF BURDEN TO TAXPAYERS.

       No provisions of this Act shall take effect on the date of 
     enactment if the Director of the Office of Management and 
     Budget determines that implementation of these provisions 
     will increase the tax burden of taxpayers.
```
