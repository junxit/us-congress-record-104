# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Rev. James David Ford, D.D., offered the following 
prayer:
  As the Sun rises to greet each day and gives light and warmth to 
every person, so may Your word of grace, O God, greet us each morning 
and guide us on our daily walk. We recognize that when we look only to 
ourselves and our own vision, we often falter and faint and we can lose 
our bearing and drift with the winds of time and the tides of the 
moment. Speak to us, O God, in the depths of our hearts, nurture our 
souls, enlighten our minds, and encourage us to use our hands in the 
works of justice and of peace. Amen.
```
