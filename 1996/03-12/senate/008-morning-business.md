# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the Senate will now 
proceed to a period for the transaction of morning business until 10 
a.m., with Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each, with 
one exception: Senator Feinstein will be recognized to speak for up to 
15 minutes.
```
