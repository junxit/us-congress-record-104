# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, MARCH 25, 1996

**Speaking:** Coats, Daniel (C000542) [R-IN]

**Measures:** H.R. 1296

```
                   ORDERS FOR MONDAY, MARCH 25, 1996

  Mr. COATS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand in adjournment until the 
hour of 10 a.m. on Monday, March 25; further, that immediately 
following the prayer, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, 
no resolutions come over under the rule, the call of the Calendar be 
dispensed with, the morning hour be deemed to have expired, and the 
time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, 
and the Senate then proceed to the consideration of calendar No. 300, 
H.R. 1296, the Presidio legislation; and further, that Senator 
Murkowski be recognized at that time to offer a substitute amendment.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
