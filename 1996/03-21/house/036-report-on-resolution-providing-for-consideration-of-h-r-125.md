# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 125, GUN CRIME ENFORCEMENT AND SECOND AMENDMENT RESTORATION ACT OF 1996

**Measures:** H.R. 125, H.Res. 388

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 125, GUN CRIME 
        ENFORCEMENT AND SECOND AMENDMENT RESTORATION ACT OF 1996

  Mr. SOLOMON, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 104-490) on the resolution (H. Res. 388) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 125) to repeal the ban on 
semiautomatic assault weapons and the ban on large capacity ammunition 
feeding devices, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered 
to be printed.
```
