# AUTHORIZING THE CLERK TO MAKE CORRECTIONS IN ENGROSSMENT OF H.R. 2202, IMMIGRATION IN THE NATIONAL INTEREST ACT OF 1995

**Speaking:** Inglis, Bob (I000023) [R-SC]

**Measures:** H.R. 2202

```
AUTHORIZING THE CLERK TO MAKE CORRECTIONS IN ENGROSSMENT OF H.R. 2202, 
            IMMIGRATION IN THE NATIONAL INTEREST ACT OF 1995

  Mr. INGLIS of South Carolina. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent 
that, in the engrossment of the bill, H.R. 2202, the Clerk be 
authorized to correct section numbers, cross-references, the table of 
contents, and punctuation, and to make such stylistic, clerical, 
technical, conforming, and other changes as may be necessary to reflect 
the actions of the House in amending the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from South Carolina?
  There was no objection.
```
