# Daily Digest/NEW PUBLIC LAWS

**Measures:** H.R. 2778

```
                            NEW PUBLIC LAWS

      (For last listing of Public Laws, see Daily Digest p. D220)
  H.R. 2778, to provide that members of the Armed Forces performing 
services for the peacekeeping effort in the Republic of Bosnia and 
Herzegovina shall be entitled to certain tax benefits in the same 
manner as if such services were performed in a combat zone. Signed 
March 20, 1996. (P.L. 104-117)
```
