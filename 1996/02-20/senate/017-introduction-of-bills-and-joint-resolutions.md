# INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** S. 1569, S. 1570

```
              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second time by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Mr. BREAUX:
       S. 1569. A bill to provide for 1 additional Federal judge 
     for the middle district of Louisiana; to the Committee on the 
     Judiciary.
           By Mr. McCAIN:
       S. 1570. A bill to amend the Employee Retirement Income 
     Security Act of 1974 and the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to 
     provide that the restriction on the assignment or alienation 
     of pension plan benefits shall not apply to court-ordered 
     criminal fines or victim restitution; to the Committee on 
     Finance.
```
