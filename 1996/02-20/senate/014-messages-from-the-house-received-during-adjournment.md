# MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING ADJOURNMENT

**Measures:** H.R. 2657

```
          MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING ADJOURNMENT

  Under the authority of the order of the Senate of January 4, 1995, 
the Secretary of the Senate, on February 16, 1996, during the 
adjournment of the Senate received a message for the House of 
Representatives announcing that the House agrees to the amendment of 
the Senate to the bill (H.R. 2657) to award a congressional gold medal 
to Ruth and Billy Graham.
```
