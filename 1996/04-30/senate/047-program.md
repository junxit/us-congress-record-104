# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Grassley, Chuck (G000386) [R-IA]

**Measures:** S. 1664

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. GRASSLEY. Mr. President, the Senate will tomorrow resume 
consideration of S. 1664. That is the immigration bill. That will be 
tomorrow morning. Senators should be reminded that there will be a 
cloture vote on the bill immediately following the vote on the Simpson 
amendment.
  It is the hope of the majority leader that we will complete action on 
the immigration bill during Wednesday's session. All Senators can
```
