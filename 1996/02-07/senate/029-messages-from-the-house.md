# MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.Res. 363

```
                        MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

  At 10:25 a.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Ms. Goetz, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
agreed to the following resolution (H. Res. 363) that the Honorable 
Constance A. Morella, a Representative from the State of Maryland, be, 
and she is hereby, elected Speaker pro tempore during any absence of 
the Speaker, such authority to continue not later than Tuesday, 
February 27, 1996.
```
