# RETIRING SENATORS

**Speaking:** Boxer, Barbara (B000711) [D-CA]

```
                           RETIRING SENATORS

  Mrs. BOXER. Mr. President, 13 dedicated people are leaving the U.S. 
Senate this year. Each of them will leave a mark on this institution. 
Each has contributed to its accomplishments. Each has been an able and 
honorable representative of his or her State.
  I count them all as friends, and I hope that the friendship I have 
shared with them will continue after they leave public life. I wish 
them all godspeed, good health, and long happy lives after the Senate.

                          ____________________
```
