# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Roth, Toby (R000459) [R-WI]

**Measures:** H.R. 2579

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. TOBY ROTH

                              of wisconsin

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, September 27, 1996

  Mr. ROTH. Mr. Speaker, yesterday, the House approved H.R. 2579, the 
U.S. National Tourism Organization Act. Due to the speed with which the 
bill moved through the House, Congressman Bob Franks of new Jersey and 
Congressman Frank Mascara of Pennsylvania were unable to be added to 
the list of cosponsors.
  I would like to recognize their support and thank them for their 
desire to cosponsor H.R. 2579.
```
