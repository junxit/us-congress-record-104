# CLOTURE MOTION

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

**Measures:** H.R. 2546

```
                             CLOTURE MOTION

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I send a cloture motion to the desk and ask 
for its immediate consideration.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The cloture motion having been presented under 
rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.
  The bill clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the conference 
     report to accompany H.R. 2546, the D.C. appropriations bill:
         Robert Dole, James M. Jeffords, Richard Lugar, Conrad 
           Burns, Strom Thurmond, Slade Gorton, Charles Grassley, 
           Robert F. Bennett, Christopher Bond, Nancy Kassebaum, 
           Mark Hatfield, Arlen Specter, Mitch McConnell, Ted 
           Stevens, Connie Mack, and Pete V. Domenici.

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, for the information of all Senators, this 
cloture vote will occur on Tuesday, February 27, at 2:15 p.m., and will 
be the first vote of the week.

                          ____________________
```
