# THE BAD DEBT BOXSCORE

**Speaking:** Helms, Jesse (H000463) [R-NC]

```
                         THE BAD DEBT BOXSCORE

  Mr. HELMS. Mr. President, at the close of business Thursday, February 
22, the Federal debt stood at $4,987,959,914,205.44, about $13 billion 
shy of the $5 trillion mark, which the Federal debt will exceed in a 
few months.
  On a per capita basis, every man, woman, and child in America owes 
$18,932.73 as his or her share of that debt.
```
