# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, under the previous order, when the Senate 
completes its business today, it will stand in recess until the hour of 
3 p.m., Monday, February 26, and that following the prayer, Senator 
Akaka will be recognized to read President George Washington's Farewell 
Address. I ask unanimous consent that following the reading of the 
address, there be deemed to have been a period for morning business so 
that Senators may submit statements for the Record until 4 p.m., or 
until the reading of Washington's Farewell Address is completed, 
whichever is later.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. LOTT. No further business will be transacted on Monday and there 
will be no rollcall votes. Senators are reminded that the next rollcall 
vote will be at 2:15 p.m., Tuesday, February 27, to invoke cloture on 
the District of Columbia appropriations conference report.

                          ____________________
```
