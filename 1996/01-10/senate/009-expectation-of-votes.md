**Speaking:** Daschle, Thomas A. (D000064) [D-SD]

```

majority leader's remarks about the expectations for votes for the next 
couple of weeks. In case Senators are not clear, as I understand it, 
unless some significant unforeseen development arises, we

the week following are weeks within which votes will not be likely.
```
