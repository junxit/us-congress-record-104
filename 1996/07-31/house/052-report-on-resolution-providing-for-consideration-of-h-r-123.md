# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 123, ENGLISH LANGUAGE EMPOWERMENT ACT OF 1996

**Measures:** H.R. 123, H.Res. 499

```
 REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 123, ENGLISH 
                    LANGUAGE EMPOWERMENT ACT OF 1996

  Mr. GOSS, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged report 
(Rept. 104-734) on the resolution (H. Res. 499) providing for 
consideration of the bill (H.R. 123) to amend title 4, United States 
Code, to declare English as the official language of the Government of 
the United States, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered 
to be printed.
```
