# REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING POINTS OF ORDER AGAINST CONFERENCE REPORT ON H.R. 3230, NATIONAL DEFENSE AUTHORIZATION ACT FOR FISCAL YEAR 1997

**Measures:** H.Res. 498, H.R. 3230

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING POINTS OF ORDER AGAINST CONFERENCE REPORT 
 ON H.R. 3230, NATIONAL DEFENSE AUTHORIZATION ACT FOR FISCAL YEAR 1997

  Mr. GOSS, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged report 
(Rept. No. 104-732) on the resolution (H. Res. 498) waiving points of 
order against the conference report to accompany the bill (H.R. 3230) 
to authorize appropriations for fiscal year 1997 for military 
activities of the Department of Defense, to prescribe military 
personnel strengths for fiscal year 1997, and for other purposes, which 
was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
