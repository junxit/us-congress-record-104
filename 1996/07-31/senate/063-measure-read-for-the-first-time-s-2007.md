# MEASURE READ FOR THE FIRST TIME--S. 2007

**Speaking:** Ford, Wendell H. (F000268) [D-KY]

**Measures:** S. 2007

```
                MEASURE READ FOR THE FIRST TIME--S. 2007

  Mr. FORD. Mr. President, I understand that S. 2007, introduced today 
by Senator Biden, is at the desk and I ask for its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report.
  The assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 2007) to clarify the intent of Congress with 
     respect to the Federal carjacking prohibition.

  Mr. FORD. Now, Mr. President, I ask for its second reading, and I 
will object to my own request on behalf of Senators on the Republican 
side of the aisle.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The bill will be read for the second time on the next legislative 
day.

                          ____________________
```
