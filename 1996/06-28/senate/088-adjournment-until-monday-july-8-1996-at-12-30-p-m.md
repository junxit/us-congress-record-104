# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL MONDAY, JULY 8, 1996, AT 12:30 P.M.

**Speaking:** Nickles, Don (N000102) [R-OK]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 192

```
         ADJOURNMENT UNTIL MONDAY, JULY 8, 1996, AT 12:30 P.M.

  Mr. NICKLES. Mr. President, if there is no further business to come 
before the Senate, I now ask the Senate stand in adjournment under the 
provisions of House Concurrent Resolution 192, the adjournment

1996, at 12:30 p.m.
```
