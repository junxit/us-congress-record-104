  Mr. NICKLES. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
immediately proceed to executive session to consider the following 
nominations on today's Executive Calendar en bloc: Executive Calendar 
Nos. 608, 665 through 674, and all nominations on the Secretary's desk 
in the Air Force, the Army, and Marine Corps.
  I further ask unanimous consent that the nominations be confirmed, 
the motions to reconsider be laid upon the table, any statements 
relating to the nominations appear at the appropriate place in the 
Record, the President be immediately notified of the Senate's action, 
and that the Senate then return to legislative session.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
