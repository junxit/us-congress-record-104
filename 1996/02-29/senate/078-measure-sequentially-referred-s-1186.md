# MEASURE SEQUENTIALLY REFERRED--S. 1186

**Speaking:** Warner, John (W000154) [R-VA]

**Measures:** S. 1186

```
                 MEASURE SEQUENTIALLY REFERRED--S. 1186

  Mr. WARNER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Committee on Energy and Natural Resources reports S. 1186 regarding the 
Flathead Irrigation and Power Project, the bill be sequentially 
referred to the Committee on Indian Affairs for a period of 20 days, 
excluding days when the Senate is not in session; further, that if the 
Indian Affairs Committee has not reported the measure at the end of 20 
session days, the bill be discharged from the committee and placed back 
on the calendar.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
