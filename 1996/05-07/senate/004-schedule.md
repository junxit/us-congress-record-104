# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

**Measures:** H.R. 2937

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. LOTT. Thank you, Mr. President. Today, there will be a period for 
morning business until the hour of 12:30 p.m. Following morning 
business, the Senate will resume consideration of H.R. 2937, a bill 
regarding the White House Travel Office. The Senate will recess between 
the hours of 12:30 p.m. and 2:15 p.m. today in order to accommodate the 
respective party luncheons.
  Under a previous order, the first vote today will occur at 2:15 p.m. 
and will be on the cloture motion to the White House Travel Office 
bill. As a reminder, in conjunction with the cloture vote today, 
Senators have until 12:30 p.m. to file second-degree amendments to the 
bill. Other votes are likely throughout the day on H.R. 2937 or any 
other items cleared for action.

                          ____________________
```
