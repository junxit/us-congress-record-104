# APPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS TO ADVISORY BOARD ON WELFARE INDICATORS

```
     APPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS TO ADVISORY BOARD ON WELFARE INDICATORS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, and pursuant to the 
provisions of section 232(c)(2) of Public Law 102-432, the Chair 
announces the Speaker's appointment to the Advisory Board on Welfare 
Indicators the following Members on the part of the House: Ms. Eloise 
Anderson of California, Mr. Wade F. Horn of Maryland, Mr. Marvin H. 
Kosters of Virginia, and Mr. Robert Greenstein of the District of 
Columbia.
  There was no objection.
```
