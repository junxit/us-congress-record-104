# APPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS TO BRITISH-AMERICAN INTERPARLIAMENTARY GROUP

```
  APPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS TO BRITISH-AMERICAN INTERPARLIAMENTARY GROUP

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, and pursuant to the 
provisions of section 168(b) of Public Law 102-138, the Chair announces 
the Speaker's appointment of the following Members of the House to the 
British-American Interparliamentary Group: Mr. Hamilton of Indiana, Mr. 
Lantos of California, Mr. Hastings of Florida, and Mrs. Kennelly of 
Connecticut.
  There was no objection.
```
