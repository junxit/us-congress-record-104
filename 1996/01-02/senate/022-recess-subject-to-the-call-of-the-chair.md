# RECESS SUBJECT TO THE CALL OF THE CHAIR

```
                RECESS SUBJECT TO THE CALL OF THE CHAIR

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the Senate will now 
stand in recess subject to the call of the Chair.
  Thereupon, at 1:40 p.m., the Senate took a recess, subject to the 
call of the Chair.
  The Senate reassembled at 4:43 p.m., when called to order by the 
Acting President pro tempore [Mr. Warner].
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The majority leader is recognized.

                          ____________________
```
