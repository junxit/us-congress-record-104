# REPORTS ON MOTOR VEHICLE SAFETY AND HIGHWAY SAFETY FOR CALENDAR YEAR 1995--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT--PM-176

```
 REPORTS ON MOTOR VEHICLE SAFETY AND HIGHWAY SAFETY FOR CALENDAR YEAR 
                1995--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT--PM-176

  The PRESIDING OFFICER laid before the Senate the following message 
from the President of the United States, together with an accompanying 
report; which was referred to the Committee on Commerce, Science, and 
Transportation.

To the Congress of the United States:
  I transmit herewith the 1995 calendar year reports as prepared by the 
Department of Transportation on activities under the Highway Safety 
Act, the National Traffic and Motor Vehicle Safety Act of 1966, and the 
Motor Vehicle Information and Cost Savings Act of 1972, as amended.
                                                  William J. Clinton.  
  The White House, October 3, 1996.

                          ____________________
```
