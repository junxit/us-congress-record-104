# SENATE RESOLUTION 315--RELATIVE TO THE PROPOSED ADJOURNMENT OF THE SESSION

**Measures:** S.Res. 315

```
[[Page S12313]]



  SENATE RESOLUTION 315--RELATIVE TO THE PROPOSED ADJOURNMENT OF THE 
                                SESSION

  Mr. LOTT submitted the following resolution; which was considered and 
agreed to:

                              S. Res. 315

       Resolved, That a committee of two Senators be appointed by 
     the Presiding Officer to join a similar committee of the 
     House of Representatives to notify the President of the 
     United States that two Houses have completed their business 
     of the session and are ready to adjourn unless he has some 
     further communication to make to them.
```
