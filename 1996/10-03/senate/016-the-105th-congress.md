# THE 105TH CONGRESS

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

```
                           THE 105TH CONGRESS

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, Senators should be aware that the 105th 
Congress will convene at noon on Tuesday, January 7.
  There had earlier been an indication that we would not need to do 
that on the 3d. But we have made a change and have agreed that it will 
be January 7 immediately following the swearing in of the newly elected 
Members of the 105th Congress.
  A live quorum will occur. All Senators are requested to be present 
for this live quorum on January 7.
  Also, Senators should be aware that Congress will count the electoral 
votes in the House Chamber at 1 p.m. on Thursday, January 9.

                          ____________________
```
