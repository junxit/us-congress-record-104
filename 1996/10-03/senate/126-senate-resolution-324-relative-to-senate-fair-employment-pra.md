# SENATE RESOLUTION 324--RELATIVE TO SENATE FAIR EMPLOYMENT PRACTICES

**Measures:** S.Res. 324

```
  SENATE RESOLUTION 324--RELATIVE TO SENATE FAIR EMPLOYMENT PRACTICES

  Mr. LOTT submitted the following resolution; which was considered and 
agreed to.

                              S. Res. 324

       Resolved, That the Secretary of the Senate shall transfer 
     an amount not to exceed $100,000, from the resolution and 
     reorganization reserve of the miscellaneous items 
     appropriations account, within the contingent fund of the 
     Senate, for use by the Director of the Office of Senate Fair 
     Employment Practices for salaries and expenses of such Office 
     through January 30, 1997, related to carrying out the 
     responsibilities of such Office in accordance with section 
     506 of the Congressional Accountability Act of 1995 (2 U.S.C. 
     1435). Effective date is October 1, 1996.

                          ____________________
```
