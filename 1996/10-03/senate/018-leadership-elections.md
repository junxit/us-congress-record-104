# LEADERSHIP ELECTIONS

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

```
                          LEADERSHIP ELECTIONS

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, also, one final note: Leadership elections 
for the 105th Congress will take place on Tuesday, December 3, and 
organizational and orientation meetings will occur throughout the day 
on Wednesday, December 4.
  So we will have the organizational meetings December 3 and 4, and we 
will reconvene on the 7th of January for the necessary swearing in and 
for the counting of the electoral college votes then on the 9th.

                          ____________________
```
