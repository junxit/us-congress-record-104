# THE BAD DEBT BOXSCORE

**Speaking:** Helms, Jesse (H000463) [R-NC]

```
                         THE BAD DEBT BOXSCORE

  Mr. HELMS. Mr. President, at the close of business yesterday, March 
14, 1996, the Federal debt stood at $5,035,165,720,616.33.
  On a per capita basis, every man, woman, and child in America owes 
$19,111.91 as his or her share of that debt.
```
