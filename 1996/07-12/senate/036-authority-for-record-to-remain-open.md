# AUTHORITY FOR RECORD TO REMAIN OPEN

**Speaking:** Gorton, Slade (G000333) [R-WA]

```
                  AUTHORITY FOR RECORD TO REMAIN OPEN

  Mr. GORTON. I ask unanimous consent that notwithstanding the 
adjournment of the Senate today, Senators have until the hour of 1 p.m. 
in order to file first-degree amendments to the defense appropriations 
bill. I further ask that the Record remain open until 1 p.m. to allow 
Senators to submit statements.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
