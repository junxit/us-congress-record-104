# INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** S. 1950, S. 1951, S. 1952

```
              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second time by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Mr. LAUTENBERG:
       S. 1950. A bill to amend the Federal Water Pollution 
     Control Act to improve the quality of coastal recreation 
     waters, and for other purposes; to the Committee on 
     Environment and Public Works.
           By Mr. FORD (for himself, Mr. Hollings, Mr. Helms, Mr. 
             Warner, Mr. Byrd, Mr. Heflin, Mr. Thurmond, Mr. 
             Shelby, and Mr. Cohen):
       S. 1951. A bill to ensure the competitiveness of the United 
     States textile and apparel industry; to the Committee on 
     Finance.
           By Mr. THOMPSON (for himself and Mr. Biden):
       S. 1952. A bill to amend the Juvenile Justice and 
     Delinquency Prevention Act of 1974, and for other purposes; 
     to the Committee on the Judiciary.
```
