# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Abraham, Spencer (A000355) [R-MI]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. ABRAHAM. Mr. President, for the information of all Senators, the 
Senate will convene at 10 a.m. Shortly after convening, the Senate will 
consider a sense-of-the-Senate resolution regarding the anniversary of 
the Oklahoma City bombing. The Senators are asked to be on the floor 
promptly at 10 a.m., as there will be a brief period of silence to 
remember the tragedy.
  Following morning business, the Senate will then begin consideration 
of the term limits legislation. No rollcall votes will occur during 
Friday's session.
  When the Senate completes debate Friday, it will resume consideration 
of the term limits legislation on Monday. No rollcall votes will occur 
during Monday's session. However, Senators are encouraged to debate the 
legislation and offer any amendments during Friday's and Monday's 
sessions of the Senate. The Senate may also be asked to turn to any 
other legislative items that can be cleared for action.

                          ____________________
```
