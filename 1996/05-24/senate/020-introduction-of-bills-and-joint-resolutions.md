# INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** S. 1822, S. 1823

```
              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second time by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Mr. HARKIN:
       S. 1822. A bill to amend the Food Security Act of 1985 to 
     permit the Secretary of Agriculture to waive the prohibition 
     on the termination of conservation reserve contracts for 
     certain lands, and for other purposes; to the Committee on 
     Agriculture, Nutrition, and Forestry.
           By Mr. DOLE:
       S. 1823. A bill to restore the American family, enhance 
     support and work opportunities for families with children, 
     reduce out-of-wedlock pregnancies, reduce welfare dependence 
     by requiring work, control welfare spending, and increase 
     State flexibility; read twice and placed on the calendar.

                          ____________________
```
