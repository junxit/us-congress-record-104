# PERMISSION FOR COMMITTEE ON HOUSE OVERSIGHT TO FILE REPORT ON HOUSE RESOLUTION 417, PROVIDING AMOUNTS FOR EXPENSES OF SELECT SUBCOMMITTEE ON UNITED STATES ROLE IN IRANIAN ARMS TRANSFERS TO CROATIA...

**Speaking:** Hyde, Henry J. (H001022) [R-IL]

**Measures:** H.Res. 417

```
  PERMISSION FOR COMMITTEE ON HOUSE OVERSIGHT TO FILE REPORT ON HOUSE 
 RESOLUTION 417, PROVIDING AMOUNTS FOR EXPENSES OF SELECT SUBCOMMITTEE 
 ON UNITED STATES ROLE IN IRANIAN ARMS TRANSFERS TO CROATIA AND BOSNIA

  Mr. HYDE. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the Committee on 
House Oversight may have until midnight tonight, May 6, 1996, to file a 
report on House Resolution 417, providing amounts for the expenses of 
the Select Subcommittee on the United States role in Iranian arms 
transfers to Croatia and Bosnia of the Committee on International 
Relations in the Second Session of the 104th Congress.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request from the 
gentleman from Illinois?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
