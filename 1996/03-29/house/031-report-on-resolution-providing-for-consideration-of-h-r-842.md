# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 842, TRUTH IN BUDGETING ACT

**Measures:** H.Res. 396, H.R. 842

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 842, TRUTH IN 
                             BUDGETING ACT

  Mr. GOSS, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged report 
(Rept. No. 104-514) on the resolution (H. Res. 396) providing for 
consideration of the bill (H.R. 842) to provide off-budget treatment 
for the Highway Trust Fund, the Airport and Airway Trust Fund, the 
Inland Waterways Trust Fund, and the Harbor Maintenance Trust Fund, 
which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
