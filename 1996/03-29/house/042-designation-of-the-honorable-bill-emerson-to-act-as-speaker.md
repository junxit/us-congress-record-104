# DESIGNATION OF THE HONORABLE BILL EMERSON TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS THROUGH APRIL 15, 1996

```
DESIGNATION OF THE HONORABLE BILL EMERSON TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE 
  TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS THROUGH APRIL 15, 1996

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Speaker:

                                               Washington, DC,

                                                   March 29, 1996.
       I hereby designate the Honorable Bill Emerson to act as 
     Speaker pro tempore to sign enrolled bills and joint 
     resolutions through Monday, April 15, 1996.
                                                    Newt Gingrich,
                          Speaker of the House of Representatives.

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objections, the designation is 
agreed to.
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
