# APPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS OF THE BRITISH-AMERICAN INTERPARLIAMENTARY GROUP

```
APPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS OF THE BRITISH-AMERICAN INTERPARLIAMENTARY GROUP

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Gutknecht). Without objection, and 
pursuant to the provisions of section 168(b) of Public Law 102-138, the 
Chair announces the Speaker's appointment of the following Members of 
the House to the British-American Interparliamentary Group: Mr. 
Clinger, Pennsylvania, vice chair; Mr. Brownback, Kansas; Mr. Emerson, 
Missouri; Mr. Linder, Georgia; Ms. Molinari, New York; Mr. Petri, 
Wisconsin; and Ms. Pryce, Ohio.
  There was no objection.
```
