# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF HOUSE JOINT RESOLUTION 159, TAX LIMITATION CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 159, H.Res. 395

```
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF HOUSE JOINT 
        RESOLUTION 159, TAX LIMITATION CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT

  Mr. GOSS, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged report 
(Rept. No. 104-513) on the resolution (H. Res. 395) providing for 
consideration of the joint resolution (H.J. Res. 159) proposing an 
amendment to the Constitution of the United States to require two-
thirds majorities for bills increasing taxes, which was referred to the 
House Calendar and ordered to be printed.

                          ____________________
```
