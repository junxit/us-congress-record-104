# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Thomas, Craig (T000162) [R-WY]

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. DeWine). Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period for the transaction of morning business not to 
extend beyond the hour of 12:30 p.m., with Senators permitted to speak 
therein for not to exceed 5 minutes each.

to 30 minutes.

that 30 minutes, I be permitted to speak for about 10 minutes, the

for 10 minutes.
```
