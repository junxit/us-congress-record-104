# AUTHORITY FOR SUBMISSION OF STATEMENTS REGARDING THE DEATH OF EDMUND S. MUSKIE

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

```
AUTHORITY FOR SUBMISSION OF STATEMENTS REGARDING THE DEATH OF EDMUND S. 
                                 MUSKIE

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that Senators have 
until April 20, 1996, to submit statements with regard to the death of 
the late Senator Edmund S. Muskie, and that the statements then be 
printed as a Senate document.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
