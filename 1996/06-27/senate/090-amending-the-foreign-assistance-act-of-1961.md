**Speaking:** McCain, John (M000303) [R-AZ]

**Measures:** H.R. 3121

```
              AMENDING THE FOREIGN ASSISTANCE ACT OF 1961

  Mr. McCAIN. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the immediate consideration of Calendar No. 467, H.R. 3121.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report.
  The assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 3121) to amend the Foreign Assistance Act of 
     1961, and for other purposes.
```
