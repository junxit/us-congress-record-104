# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO REPORT

**Speaking:** McCain, John (M000303) [R-AZ]

```
                   AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO REPORT

  Mr. McCAIN. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that during the 
adjournment of the Senate, Senate committees may file committee-
reported Legislative and Executive Calendar business on Tuesday July 2, 
from 11 a.m. to 2 p.m..

  Mr. McCAIN. Madam President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. McCAIN. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
