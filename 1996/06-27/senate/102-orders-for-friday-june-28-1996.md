# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, JUNE 28, 1996

**Speaking:** McCain, John (M000303) [R-AZ]

```
                    ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, JUNE 28, 1996

  Mr. McCAIN. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand in adjournment until the 
hour of 8:30 a.m. on Friday, June 28; further, that immediately 
following the prayer, the Journal of the proceedings be deemed approved 
to date, no resolutions come over under the rule, the call of the 
calendar be dispensed with, the morning hour be deemed to have expired, 
and the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and the Senate then immediately resume consideration of the 
defense authorization bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
