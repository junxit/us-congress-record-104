# GRANTING MEMBERS OF THE HOUSE PRIVILEGE TO EXTEND AND REVISE REMARKS IN CONGRESSIONAL RECORD

**Speaking:** Watts, J. C., Jr. (W000210) [R-OK]

```
GRANTING MEMBERS OF THE HOUSE PRIVILEGE TO EXTEND AND REVISE REMARKS IN 
                          CONGRESSIONAL RECORD

  Mr. WATTS of Oklahoma. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that for 
today all Members be permitted to extend their remarks and to include 
extraneous material in that section of the Record entitled ``Extension 
of Remarks.''
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Oklahoma?
  There was no objection.
```
