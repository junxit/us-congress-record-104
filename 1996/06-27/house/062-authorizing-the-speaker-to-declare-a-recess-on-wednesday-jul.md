# AUTHORIZING THE SPEAKER TO DECLARE A RECESS ON WEDNESDAY, JULY 10, 1996, FOR THE PURPOSE OF RECEIVING IN JOINT MEETING HIS EXCELLENCY, BINYAMIN NETANYAHU, PRIME MINISTER OF ISRAEL

**Speaking:** Watts, J. C., Jr. (W000210) [R-OK]

```
  AUTHORIZING THE SPEAKER TO DECLARE A RECESS ON WEDNESDAY, JULY 10, 
  1996, FOR THE PURPOSE OF RECEIVING IN JOINT MEETING HIS EXCELLENCY, 
              BINYAMIN NETANYAHU, PRIME MINISTER OF ISRAEL

  Mr. WATTS of Oklahoma. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that it 
may be in order at any time on Wednesday, July 10, 1996, for the 
Speaker to declare a recess, subject to the call of the Chair, for the 
purpose of receiving in joint meeting his Excellency, Binyamin 
Netanyahu, Prime Minister of Israel.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Oklahoma?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
