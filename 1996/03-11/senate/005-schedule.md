# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

**Measures:** H.R. 3019

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, today there will be a period for morning 
business until the hour of 1 p.m., with Senators permitted to speak for 
up to 10 minutes each, with the exception of Senator Murkowski of 
Alaska who has 15 minutes under his control.
  At 1 p.m. today, the Senate will begin consideration of H.R. 3019, 
the continuing resolution appropriations bill. No rollcall votes will 
occur during today's session. However, amendments are expected to be 
offered today during the consideration of the bill. Senators should be 
reminded that there will be at least two rollcall votes beginning at 
2:15 tomorrow afternoon. The first vote will be on invoking cloture on 
the D.C. appropriations conference report, to be followed by a vote on 
invoking cloture on the motion to proceed to the Whitewater resolution.
  Mr. President, I yield the floor.

                          ____________________
```
