# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
PERSIAN GULF WAR SYNDROME
Committee on Government Reform and Oversight: Subcommittee on Human 
Resources and Intergovernmental Relations held a hearing on the status 
of efforts to identify Persian Gulf War Syndrome, Part I. Testimony was 
heard from Robyn Nishimi, President's Advisory Committee on Persian 
Gulf Veterans' Illnesses; the following officials of the Institute of 
Medicine, National Academy of Sciences: David Rall, M.D., Foreign 
Secretary; and John Bailar, M.D., Chairman, Committee to Review the 
Health Consequences of Service During the Persian Gulf War; and public 
witnesses.
  Hearings continue March 28.
```
