# Text of House Amendment 5

**Speaking:** Gutknecht, Gil (G000536) [R-MN]

**Measures:** H.R. 3816

```
H.R. 3816

                       Offered By: Mr. Gutknecht

       Amendment No. 5: Page 36, after line 10, insert the
```
