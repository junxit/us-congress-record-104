# Text of House Amendment 28

**Speaking:** Gutknecht, Gil (G000536) [R-MN]

**Measures:** H.R. 3814

```
H.R. 3814

                       Offered By: Mr. Gutknecht

       Amendment No. 28: Page 116, after line 2, insert the
```
