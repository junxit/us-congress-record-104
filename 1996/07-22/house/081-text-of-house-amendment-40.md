# Text of House Amendment 40

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

**Measures:** H.R. 3814

```
H.R. 3814

                        Offered By: Mr. Schumer

       Amendment No. 40: In the item relating to ``DEPARTMENT OF 
     JUSTICE--Office of Justice Programs--violent crime reduction 
     programs, state and local law enforcement assistance''--
       (1) after each of the first and second dollar amounts, 
     insert the following: ``(reduced by $5,000,000)''; and
       (2) at the end of such item, insert the following:
       In addition, for local firefighter and emergency services 
     training grants authorized by section 819 of the 
     Antiterrorism and Effective Death Penalty Act of 1996 (Pub. 
     L. 104-132; 110 Stat. 1316), $5,000,000.
```
