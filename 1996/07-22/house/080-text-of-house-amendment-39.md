# Text of House Amendment 39

**Speaking:** Schroeder, Patricia (S000142) [D-CO]

**Measures:** H.R. 3814

```
H.R. 3814

                       Offered By: Mrs. Schroeder

       Amendment No. 39: Page 116, after line 2, insert the 
     following new section:
       Sec. 615. The amounts otherwise provided by this Act are 
     revised by increasing the amount made available for ``Equal 
     Employment Opportunity Commission--Salaries and Expenses'', 
     and reducing the amount made available for ``DEPARTMENT OF 
     JUSTICE--Federal Prison System--salaries and expenses'', by 
     $13,000,000 and $14,000,000, respectively.
```
