# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Domenici, Pete V. (D000407) [R-NM]

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. DOMENICI. This morning the Senate will immediately resume 
consideration of the reconciliation bill until the hour of 2 p.m. Any 
Senator still intending to offer an amendment to that bill must do so 
prior to that time. Under the consent agreement reached on Friday, all 
previously ordered votes on amendments as well as votes ordered today 
will begin at 9:30 a.m. tomorrow morning. No rollcall votes will occur 
today. However, all Senators should be notified that there will be a 
lengthy series of rollcall votes on Tuesday morning. Also, at 2 o'clock 
today the Senate will begin the Agriculture appropriations bill, and 
once again any votes ordered in relation to that bill will occur 
following the stacked votes at 9:30 a.m. tomorrow morning.

                          ____________________
```
