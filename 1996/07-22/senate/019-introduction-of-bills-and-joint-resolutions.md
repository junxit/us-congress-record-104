# INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** S. 1978, S. 1979, S. 1980, S. 1981

```
              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second time by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Mr. DORGAN (for himself and Mr. Byrd):
       S. 1978. A bill to establish an Emergency Commission To End 
     the Trade Deficit; to the Committee on Finance.
           By Mr. JEFFORDS:
       S. 1979. A bill to amend the Social Security Act to help 
     disabled individuals become economically self-sufficient and 
     eligible for health care coverage through work incentives and 
     a medicare buy-in program, and for other purposes; to the 
     Committee on Finance.
           By Mr. LAUTENBERG:
       S. 1980. A bill to prohibit the public carrying of a 
     handgun, with appropriate exceptions for law enforcement 
     officials and others; to the Committee on the Judiciary.
           By Mr. CRAIG:
       S. 1981. A bill to establish a Joint United States-Canada 
     Commission on Cattle and Beef to identify, and recommend 
     means of resolving, national, regional, and provincial trade-
     distorting differences between the countries with respect to 
     the production, processing, and sale of cattle and beef, and 
     for other purposes; to the Committee on Finance.

                          ____________________
```
