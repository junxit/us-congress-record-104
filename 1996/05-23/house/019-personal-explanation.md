# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Hoke, Martin R. (H000707) [R-OH]

**Measures:** H.R. 1227

```
                              {time}  1545
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Mr. HOKE. Mr. Speaker, I was just called to my office and informed 
that I was not recorded on the last vote on H.R. 1227. I was present on 
the floor at the time, from the time of the first Goodling amendment, 
and apparently inadvertently left the floor without having cast my 
vote, although I was under the impression that I had.
  My vote on final passage of 1227 would have been ``yes.''
```
