# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Bunn, Jim (B001063) [R-OR]

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______


                             HON. JIM BUNN

                               of oregon

                    in the house of representatives

                         Thursday, May 23, 1996

  Mr. BUNN of Oregon. Mr. Speaker, due to a thunderstorm, my plane was 
approximately 2 hours late arriving at National Airport on May 21, 
1996. Because of this delay, I was unable to cast my vote on vote 
numbers 180, 181, and 182.
  Had I been present, I would have voted yea on vote 180, nay on vote 
181, and yea on vote 182. I ask unanimous consent to have these votes 
entered into the Record at the appropriate place.
```
