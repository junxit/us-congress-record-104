# RECESS

**Speaking:** Domenici, Pete V. (D000407) [R-NM], Exon, J. James (E000284) [D-NE], Grassley, Chuck (G000386) [R-IA]

```
                                 RECESS

  Mr. DOMENICI. I ask unanimous consent that the Senate stand in recess 
until 2 p.m. today.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 1:13 p.m. recessed until 
2:01 p.m.; whereupon, the Senate reassembled when called to order by 
the Presiding Officer [Mr. Thomas].
  Mr. EXON. Mr. President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. GRASSLEY. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
