# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** H.R. 3161, H.R. 3166

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 5 of rule I, the Chair 
will now put the question on each motion to suspend the rules on which 
further proceedings were postponed on Tuesday, July 16, 1996, in the 
order in which that motion was entertained.
  Votes will be taken in the following order:
  H.R. 3166, as amended, de novo; and
  H.R. 3161, by the yeas and nays.
  The Chair will also put the question on the Speaker's approval of the 
Journal.
  The Chair will reduce to 5 minutes the time for any electronic vote 
after the first such vote in this series.

                          ____________________
```
