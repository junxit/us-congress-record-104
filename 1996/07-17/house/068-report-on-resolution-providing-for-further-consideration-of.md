# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR FURTHER CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3734, PERSONAL RESPONSIBILITY ACT OF 1996

**Measures:** H.Res. 482, H.R. 3734

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR FURTHER CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3734, 
                  PERSONAL RESPONSIBILITY ACT OF 1996

  Mr. GOSS (during consideration of the Special Order of the gentleman 
from Georgia, Mr. Kingston) from the Committee on Rules, submitted a 
privileged report (Rept. No. 104-686) on the resolution (H. Res. 482) 
providing for further consideration of the bill (H.R. 3734) to provide 
for reconciliation pursuant to section 201(a)(1) of the concurrent 
resolution on the budget for fiscal year 1997, which was referred to 
the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
