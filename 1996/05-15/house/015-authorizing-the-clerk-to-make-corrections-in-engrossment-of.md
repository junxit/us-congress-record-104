# AUTHORIZING THE CLERK TO MAKE CORRECTIONS IN ENGROSSMENT OF H.R. 3230, NATIONAL DEFENSE AUTHORIZATION ACT FOR FISCAL YEAR 1997

**Speaking:** Spence, Floyd (S000718) [R-SC]

**Measures:** H.R. 3230

```
AUTHORIZING THE CLERK TO MAKE CORRECTIONS IN ENGROSSMENT OF H.R. 3230, 
        NATIONAL DEFENSE AUTHORIZATION ACT FOR FISCAL YEAR 1997

  Mr. SPENCE. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that in the 
engrossment of the bill, H.R. 3230, the Clerk be authorized to correct 
section numbers, punctuation, cross references, and to make such other 
technical, clerical, and conforming changes as may be necessary to 
reflect the actions of the House in amending the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from South Carolina?
  There was no objection.
```
