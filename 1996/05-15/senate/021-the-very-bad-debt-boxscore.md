# THE VERY BAD DEBT BOXSCORE

**Speaking:** Helms, Jesse (H000463) [R-NC]

```
                       THE VERY BAD DEBT BOXSCORE

  Mr. HELMS. Mr. President, the Federal Government is running on 
borrowed money--more than $5 trillion of it. As of the close of 
business yesterday, May 14, 1996, the Federal debt stood at 
$5,096,217,391,261.73. On a per capita basis, every man, woman, and 
child in America owes $19,242.02 as his or her share of the Federal 
debt.
```
