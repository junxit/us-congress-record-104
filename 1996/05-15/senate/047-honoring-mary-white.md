# HONORING MARY WHITE

**Speaking:** Lieberman, Joseph I. (L000304) [D-CT]

```
                          HONORING MARY WHITE

● Mr. LIEBERMAN. Mr. President, I rise today to pay tribute to a 
very worthy constituent, Mrs. Mary White. Mrs. White is retiring this 
month after years of service to both her State and her country, by 
working for the Immigration and Naturalization Service.
  Mrs. White spent 21 years in public service. Her work for INS over 
the past 6 years has been exemplary, and has set a very high standard 
for her peers. My office, as well as many others, has enjoyed an 
excellent working relationship with the Immigration and Naturalization 
service over the years, due in large part to the cooperation we 
received from Mrs. White. On numerous occasions she has performed 
flawlessly, always keeping an attitude that focused on helping others.
  My best wishes go out to Mrs. White and her family. May she enjoy 
this new stage in her life, and be ever mindful of the respect and 
esteem we hold her in.

                          ____________________
```
