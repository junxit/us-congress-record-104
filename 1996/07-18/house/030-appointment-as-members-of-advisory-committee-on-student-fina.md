# APPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS OF ADVISORY COMMITTEE ON STUDENT FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE

```
   APPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS OF ADVISORY COMMITTEE ON STUDENT FINANCIAL 
                               ASSISTANCE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, and pursuant to the 
provisions of section 491 of the Higher Education Act, as amended by 
section 407 of Public Law 99-498, the Chair announces the Speaker's 
appointment of the following Members to the Advisory Committee on 
Student Financial Assistance on the part of the House: Mr. Thomas E. 
Dillon of California and Mr. William A. Irwin of Pennsylvania.
  There was no objection.
```
