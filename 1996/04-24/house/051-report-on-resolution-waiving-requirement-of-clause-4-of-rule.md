# REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING REQUIREMENT OF CLAUSE 4 OF RULE XI WITH RESPECT TO CONSIDERATION OF CERTAIN RESOLUTIONS

```
 REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING REQUIREMENT OF CLAUSE 4 OF RULE XI WITH 
            RESPECT TO CONSIDERATION OF CERTAIN RESOLUTIONS

report (Rept. No. 104-535) on the resolution (H. Res. 412) waiving a
```
