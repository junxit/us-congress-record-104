# MEASURE INDEFINITELY POSTPONED--S. 1298

**Speaking:** Simpson, Alan K. (S000429) [R-WY]

**Measures:** S. 1298

```
                MEASURE INDEFINITELY POSTPONED--S. 1298

  Mr. SIMPSON. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Commerce 
Committee be discharged from further consideration of S. 1298, a bill

then be indefinitely postponed.
```
