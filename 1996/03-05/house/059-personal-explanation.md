# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Abercrombie, Neil (A000014) [D-HI]

**Measures:** H.R. 2778

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Mr. ABERCROMBIE. Mr. Speaker, earlier today the House considered on 
the suspension calendar, H.R. 2778, a bill to give special tax 
treatment to United States troops in Bosnia. As a strong supporter of 
the members of our Armed Forces I strongly support this measure. Had I 
been present on roll No. 44 I would have noted ``aye.''

                          ____________________
```
