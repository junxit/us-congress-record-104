# REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING POINTS OF ORDER AGAINST CONFERENCE REPORT ON H.R. 927, CUBAN LIBERTY AND DEMOCRATIC SOLIDARITY (LIBERTAD) ACT OF 1996

**Measures:** H.Res. 370, H.R. 927

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING POINTS OF ORDER AGAINST CONFERENCE REPORT 
ON H.R. 927, CUBAN LIBERTY AND DEMOCRATIC SOLIDARITY (LIBERTAD) ACT OF 
                                  1996

  Mr. DIAZ-BALART, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 104-470) on the resolution (H. Res. 370) waiving 
points of order against the conference report to accompany the bill 
(H.R. 927) to seek international sanctions against the Castro 
government in Cuba, to plan for support of a transition government 
leading to a democratically elected government in Cuba, and for other 
purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be 
printed.
```
