# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to the provisions of clause 5 of 
rule I, the Chair announces that he will postpone further proceedings 
today on each motion to suspend the rules on which a recorded vote or 
the yeas and nays are ordered or on which a vote is objected to under 
clause 4 of rule XV. Such rollcall votes, if postponed, will be taken 
after the debate is concluded on all motions to suspend the rules.
```
