# ORDERS FOR TOMORROW

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

```
                          ORDERS FOR TOMORROW

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that at 9:30 a.m. on 
Wednesday, March 6, immediately following the prayer, the Journal of 
proceedings be deemed approved to date, the time for the two leaders 
reserved, and there be a period for morning business until the hour of 
11 a.m. with Senators permitted to speak for up to 5 minutes each, with 
the following exceptions: Senator Feinstein for 15 minutes, Senator 
Dorgan for 15 minutes, Senator Bingaman or his designee for 30 minutes, 
and Senator Thomas for 30 minutes.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
