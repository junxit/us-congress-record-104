# MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.R. 2428

```
                        MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

  At 12:32 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mr. Hays, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House agrees 
to the amendments of the Senate to the bill (H.R. 2428) to encourage 
the donation of food and grocery products to nonprofit organizations 
for distribution to needy individuals by giving the Model Good 
Samaritan Food Donation Act the full force and effect of law.
```
