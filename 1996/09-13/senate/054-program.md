# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Coverdell, Paul (C000813) [R-GA]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. COVERDELL. Mr. President, for the information of all Senators, 
under the previous order, there will be 2 hours for morning business on 
Monday. At 2 p.m., the Senate will resume the Interior appropriations 
bill. No rollcall votes will occur on Monday, and any rollcall votes 
ordered during the session of the Senate on Monday will be stacked to 
occur on Tuesday at 9:30 a.m. on a case-by-case basis. The Senate could 
be asked to turn to any other legislation that may be cleared for 
action.
```
