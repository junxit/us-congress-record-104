# INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** S. 2072, S. 2073, S. 2074

```
              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second time by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Mr. GRAMS:
       S. 2072. A bill to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 
     to eliminate the requirement that States pay unemployment 
     compensation on the basis of services performed by election 
     workers; to the Committee on Finance.
           By Mr. NICKLES:
       S. 2073. A bill to require the District of Columbia to 
     comply with the 5-year time limit for welfare recipients, to 
     prohibit any future waiver of such limit, and for other 
     purposes; read the first time.
           By Mr. THURMOND:
       S. 2074. A bill to repeal the Federal estate and gift taxes 
     and the tax on generation-skipping transfers; to the 
     Committee on Finance.

                          ____________________
```
