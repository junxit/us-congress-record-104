# NOTICE OF HEARING

**Speaking:** McCain, John (M000303) [R-AZ]

```
                           NOTICE OF HEARING


                      committee on indian affairs

  Mr. McCAIN. Mr. President, I would like to announce that the Senate 
Committee on Indian Affairs will meet during the session of the Senate 
on Wednesday, October 2, 1996, beginning at 9:30 a.m. to conduct an 
oversight hearing on the regulatory activities of the National Indian 
Gaming Commission [NIGC]. The hearing will be held in room 216 of the 
Hart Senate Office Building.
  Those wishing additional information should contact the Committee on 
Indian Affairs at 224-2251.
```
