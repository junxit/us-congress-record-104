# WAIVING CERTAIN ENROLLING REQUIREMENTS IN H.R. 4278--HOUSE JOINT RESOLUTION 197

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS], Stevens, Ted (S000888) [R-AK]

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 197, H.R. 4278

```
   WAIVING CERTAIN ENROLLING REQUIREMENTS IN H.R. 4278--HOUSE JOINT 
                             RESOLUTION 197

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the immediate consideration of House Joint Resolution 197, 
which was received from the House, and further, the joint resolution be 
considered read three times and passed, the motion to reconsider be 
laid upon the table.
  Mr. STEVENS. Reserving the right to object, what is that?
  Mr. LOTT. That is regarding hand enrollment of the omnibus 
appropriations bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Thomas). Without objection, it is so 
ordered.
  The joint resolution (H.J. Res. 197) was considered, ordered to a 
third reading, read for a third time, and passed.
  Mr. LOTT. I yield the floor.

                          ____________________
```
