# DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE AUTHORIZATION ACT--MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** S. 1124

```
    DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE AUTHORIZATION ACT--MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. If the Senator from New Mexico will forbear 
for a moment, the Chair wishes to announce that under the order of 
September 6, 1995, the Senate, having received a message from the House 
on S. 1124, therefore disagrees with the House amendment, agrees to a 
conference with the House, and the Chair appoints the following 
conferees which the clerk will state.
  The Presiding Officer (Mr. Warner) appointed Mr. Thurmond, Mr. 
Warner, Mr. Cohen, Mr. Lott, Mr. Nunn, Mr. Exon, and Mr. Levin 
conferees on the part of the Senate.
  Mr. DOMENICI addressed the Chair.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator from New Mexico is recognized.

                          ____________________
```
