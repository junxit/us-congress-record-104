# MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING RECESS

**Measures:** H.R. 1643

```
             MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING RECESS

  Under the authority of the order of the Senate of January 4, 1995, 
the Secretary of the Senate, on January 5, 1996, during the recess of 
the Senate, received a message from the House of Representatives 
announcing that the House agrees to the amendment of the Senate to the 
bill (H.R. 1643) to authorize the extension of nondiscriminatory 
treatment (most-favored-nation treatment) to the products of Bulgaria, 
with amendments, in which it requests the concurrence of the Senate.
```
