# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** S. 1124

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Mr. Lundregan, one of its clerks, 
announced that the Senate disagrees to 

[[Page H323]]
the amendments of the House of Representatives to the bill (S. 1124) 
``An Act to authorize appropriations for fiscal year 1996 for military 
activities of the Department of Defense, to prescribe personnel 
strengths for such fiscal year for the Armed Forces, and for other 
purposes.'', agrees to a conference asked by the House on the 
disagreeing votes of the two Houses thereon, and appoints Mr. Thurmond, 
Mr. Warner, Mr. Cohen, Mr. Lott, Mr. Nunn, Mr. Exon, and Mr. Levin, be 
the conferees on the part of the Senate.

                          ____________________
```
