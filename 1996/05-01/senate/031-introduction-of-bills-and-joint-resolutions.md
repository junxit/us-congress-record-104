# INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** S. 1719, S. 1720

```
              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second time by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Mrs. HUTCHISON:
       S. 1719. A bill to require the Secretary of the Interior to 
     offer to sell to certain public agencies the indebtedness 
     representing the remaining repayment balance of certain 
     Bureau of Reclamation projects in Texas, and for other 
     purposes; to the Committee on Energy and Natural Resources.
           By Mr. DOLE (for himself, Mr. Kennedy, and Mr. Kerry):
       S. 1720. A bill to establish the Nicodemus National 
     Historic Site and the New Bedford National Historic Landmark; 
     ordered held at the desk.
```
