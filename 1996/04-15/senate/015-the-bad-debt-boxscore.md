# THE BAD DEBT BOXSCORE

**Speaking:** Helms, Jesse (H000463) [R-NC]

```
                         THE BAD DEBT BOXSCORE

  Mr. HELMS. Mr. President, at the close of business Friday, April 12, 
1996, the Federal debt stood at $5,145,722,307,691.76.
  On a per capita basis, every man, woman, and child in America owes 
$19,444.53 as his or her share of that debt.
```
