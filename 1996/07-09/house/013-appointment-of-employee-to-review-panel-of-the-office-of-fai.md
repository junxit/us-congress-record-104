# APPOINTMENT OF EMPLOYEE TO REVIEW PANEL OF THE OFFICE OF FAIR EMPLOYMENT PRACTICES

```
     APPOINTMENT OF EMPLOYEE TO REVIEW PANEL OF THE OFFICE OF FAIR 
                          EMPLOYMENT PRACTICES

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, and pursuant to the 
provisions of rule LI, the Chair announces the Speaker's appointment to 
the review panel of the Office of Fair Employment Practices the 
following employee of the House of Representatives to fill the existing 
vacancy thereon:
  Mr. Alan F. Coffey, Jr., General Counsel and Staff Director of the 
Committee on the Judiciary.
  There was no objection.
```
