# UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENTS

**Speaking:** Kassebaum, Nancy Landon (K000017) [R-KS]

```
                      UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENTS

  Mrs. KASSEBAUM. Mr. President, if I may just do some housecleaning 
for the majority leader.
  I ask unanimous consent that immediately following the stacked votes 
beginning at 12 noon on Wednesday, there be a period for the 
transaction of morning business not to exceed 1 hour, with 40 minutes 
of the time under the control of the Democratic leader or his designee, 
and 20 minutes under the control of Senator Thomas.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mrs. KASSEBAUM. I further ask that at 9 a.m. on Thursday there be a 
period for the transaction of morning business not to exceed 1 hour, 40 
minutes under the control of Senator

[[Page S7461]]

Daschle or his designee, and 20 minutes under the control of Senator 
Coverdell.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mrs. KASSEBAUM. I yield the floor, Mr. President.

                          ____________________
```
