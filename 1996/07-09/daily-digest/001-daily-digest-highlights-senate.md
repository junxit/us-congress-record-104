pension simplification issues, to provide a further extension of 
certain expiring tax provisions, to allow certain teaching hospitals to 
provide tax-free academic housing to faculty and medical and nursing 
students, to establish revenue offsets, and to make certain technical 
corrections.

Portal Act of 1947 relating to the payment of wages to employees who 
use employer-owned vehicles.
