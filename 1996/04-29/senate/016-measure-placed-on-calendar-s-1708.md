# MEASURE PLACED ON CALENDAR--S. 1708

**Speaking:** Kyl, Jon (K000352) [R-AZ], Kennedy, Edward M. (K000105) [D-MA]

**Measures:** S. 1708

```
                  MEASURE PLACED ON CALENDAR--S. 1708

  Mr. KYL. Mr. President, I understand there is a bill due for its 
second reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will read the bill for the second 
time.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 1708) to amend title 28, United States Code, to 
     clarify the remedial jurisdiction of inferior Federal courts.

  Mr. KYL. Mr. President, I object to further proceedings on this 
matter at this time.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The bill will be placed on the calendar under 
rule XIV.
  Mr. KENNEDY addressed the Chair.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator from Massachusetts.
  Mr. KENNEDY. Mr. President, as I understand the floor situation, we 
will now return for a continued discussion on the immigration bill, and 
then at 5 o'clock, the time has been designated for a vote on cloture 
relating to a matter on that immigration bill. Am I correct?

                          ____________________
```
