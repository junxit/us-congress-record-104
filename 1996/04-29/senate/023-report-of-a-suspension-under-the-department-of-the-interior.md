# REPORT OF A SUSPENSION UNDER THE DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR AND RELATED AGENCIES APPROPRIATIONS ACT FOR FISCAL YEAR 1996--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT RECEIVED DURING THE ADJOURNMENT OF THE SENATE...

```
REPORT OF A SUSPENSION UNDER THE DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR AND RELATED 
  AGENCIES APPROPRIATIONS ACT FOR FISCAL YEAR 1996--MESSAGE FROM THE 
    PRESIDENT RECEIVED DURING THE ADJOURNMENT OF THE SENATE--PM 141

  Under the authority of the order of the Senate of January 4, 1995, 
the Secretary of the Senate on April 26, 1996, received a message from 
the President of the United States, together with an accompanying 
report; which was referred to the Committee on Appropriations:

To the Congress of the United States:
  I hereby report that I have exercised the authority provided to me 
under subsection 325(c) of the Department of the Interior and Related 
Agencies Appropriations Act, 1996, to suspend subsection 325(a) and 
325(b) of such Act. A copy of the suspension is attached.
                                                  William J. Clinton.  
  The White House, April 26, 1996.

                          ____________________
```
