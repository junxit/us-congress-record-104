# Daily Digest/NEW PUBLIC LAWS

**Measures:** H.R. 1880, H.R. 2437, H.R. 2704, H.R. 3364

```
                            NEW PUBLIC LAWS

      (For last listing of Public Laws, see Daily Digest p. D704)
  H.R. 1880, to designate the United States Post Office building 
located at 102 South McLean, Lincoln, Illinois, as the ``Edward Madigan 
Post Office Building''. Signed July 9, 1996. (P.L. 104-157)
  H.R. 2437, to provide for the exchange of certain lands in Gilpin 
County, Colorado. Signed July 9, 1996. (P.L. 104-158)
  H.R. 2704, to provide that the United States Post Office building 
that is to be located on the 2600 block of East 75th Street in Chicago, 
Illinois, shall be known and designated as the ``Charles A. Hayes Post 
Office Building''. Signed July 9, 1996. (P.L. 104-159)
  H.R. 3364, to designate a United States courthouse in Scranton, 
Pennsylvania, as the ``William J. Nealon United States Courthouse''. 
Signed July 9, 1996. (P.L. 104-160)
```
