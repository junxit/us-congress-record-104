# ORDER OF PROCEDURE

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS], Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                           ORDER OF PROCEDURE

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, it is our intention at this point to ask 
unanimous consent with regard to the Executive Calendar and then have a 
closing script, which would involve us closing up for tonight. We would 
come in in the morning at 9 and have morning business which, I believe, 
was requested by the Democratic leader, equally divided between 9 and 
10. And then at 10 we would go to the Department of Defense 
appropriations bill.
  I know how seriously the two Senators from Nevada feel about this 
issue. I appreciate them letting me intervene at this point. I look 
forward to working with them later as we go along.
  Mr. REID. Reserving the right to object, it is my understanding that 
this is wrap-up, and there is going to be no more after we finish here.
  Mr. LOTT. That is right.
  Mr. REID. I thank the majority leader.

                          ____________________
```
