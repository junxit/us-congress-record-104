# UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT--S. 1745

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

**Measures:** S. 1745

```
                  UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT--S. 1745

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
resume consideration of the Department of Defense authorization bill at 
11:30 a.m. on Wednesday, with the remaining 30 minutes of debate in 
order as previously divided. Further, that the vote occur on passage of 
the defense bill at 12 noon and that the previously scheduled votes 
begin immediately following final passage.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
