# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

**Measures:** S. 295

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, for the information of all Senators, the 
Senate will resume consideration of the small business tax package 
legislation tomorrow morning. Under the order, the Senate will vote on 
the pending amendments to that bill beginning at 2:15 p.m. on Tuesday. 
Following the 2:15 p.m. votes, the Senate will begin consideration of 
S. 295, the TEAM Act, under the provisions of the previous consent 
agreement. The Senate will recess on Tuesday from 12:30 p.m. to 2:15 
p.m. for the policy luncheons.
  Also as a reminder, at 10 a.m. on Wednesday, there will be a joint 
meeting of Congress to hear an address by the Prime Minister of Israel.

                          ____________________
```
