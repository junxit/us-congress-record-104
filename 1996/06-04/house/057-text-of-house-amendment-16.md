# Text of House Amendment 16

**Speaking:** Kennedy, Joseph P., II (K000110) [D-MA]

**Measures:** H.R. 3540

```
H.R. 3540

                Offered by: Mr. Kennedy of Massachusetts

       Amendment No. 16: Page 97, line 5, insert the following new 
     section:


            prohibition of funds for school of the americas

       Sec. 573. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used for the School of the Americas.
```
