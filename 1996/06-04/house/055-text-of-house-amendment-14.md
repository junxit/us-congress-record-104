# Text of House Amendment 14

**Speaking:** Hastings, Alcee L. (H000324) [D-FL]

**Measures:** H.R. 3540

```
H.R. 3540

                  Offered by: Mr. Hastings of Florida

       Amendment No. 14: Page 22, line 15, insert the following:
       (n) The Congress--
       (1) finds that the rising number of reports of religious 
     persecutions in Russia is of concern;
       (2) urges the Secretary of State to be attentive to this 
     growing problem; and
       (3) urges the Government of Russia to eliminate 
     restrictions on religious institutions, such as the 
     restrictions placed on the Jewish Agency for Israel, and to 
     reissue operating licenses allowing such Agency to reopen 
     their offices.
```
