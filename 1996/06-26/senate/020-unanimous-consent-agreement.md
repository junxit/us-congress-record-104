# UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT

**Speaking:** Thurmond, Strom (T000254) [R-SC], Exon, J. James (E000284) [D-NE]

```
                      UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT

  Mr. THURMOND. At this time, I ask unanimous-consent that yesterday's 
agreement on minimum wage be further modified to allow for the two 
leaders to void this agreement up until the hour of 5:30 p.m. today.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. EXON. I am sorry; I could not hear the Senator.
  What was the unanimous consent request, I ask my friend from South 
Carolina, to do what at 5:15?
  Mr. THURMOND. To allow for the two leaders to void this agreement up 
until the hour of 5:30 p.m. today.
  Mr. EXON. I have no objection. I thank my friend from South Carolina.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
