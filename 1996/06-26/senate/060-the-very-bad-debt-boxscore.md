# THE VERY BAD DEBT BOXSCORE

**Speaking:** Helms, Jesse (H000463) [R-NC]

```
                       THE VERY BAD DEBT BOXSCORE

  Mr. HELMS. Mr. President, at the close of business yesterday, 
Tuesday, June 25, 1996, the Federal debt stood at 
$5,114,148,773,023.82.
  On a per capita basis, every man, woman, and child in America owes 
$19,287.21 as his or her share of that debt.
```
