the Sanders amendment to increase funding for the Court of Veterans 
Appeals, I would have voted ``yes.'' On rollcall No. 273, the Shays-
Lowey amendment on increasing funds for the Housing Opportunities for 
Persons with AIDS amendment I would have voted ``yes.'' The funding for 
this program has remained flat for the last 3 years while the number of 
AIDS cases has increased by an additional one-third in that time and 
the number of States and cities qualifying for grants has increased by 
23 percent. We must do better--this Congress should not be abandoning 
people with AIDS and having them live on the street.
