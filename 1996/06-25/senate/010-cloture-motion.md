# CLOTURE MOTION

**Measures:** S. 1219

```
                             CLOTURE MOTION

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the cloture motion 
having been presented under rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to 
read the cloture motion.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:


                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on S. 1219, the 
     campaign finance reform bill:
       Trent Lott, John McCain, Judd Gregg, Bob Smith, Rick 
     Santorum, Sheila Frahm, Claiborne Pell, Jeff Bingaman, David 
     Pryor, John F. Kerry, Paul Wellstone, Patty Murray, Fred 
     Thompson, Bob Graham, Herb Kohl, Russell D. Feingold.

                          ____________________
```
