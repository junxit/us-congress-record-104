# RECESS

```
                                 RECESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the hour of 1 
o'clock having arrived, the Senate will now stand in recess until the 
hour of 2:15 p.m.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 1:02 p.m., recessed until 2:15 p.m.; 
whereupon, the Senate reassembled when called to order by the Presiding 
Officer (Mr. Coats).
```
