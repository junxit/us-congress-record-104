# Text of House Amendment 4

**Speaking:** Gutknecht, Gil (G000536) [R-MN]

**Measures:** H.R. 3675

```
H.R. 3675

                       Offered By: Mr. Gutknecht

       Amendment No. 4: Page 55, after line 15, insert the
```
