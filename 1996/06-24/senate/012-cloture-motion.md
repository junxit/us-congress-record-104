# CLOTURE MOTION

**Speaking:** McCain, John (M000303) [R-AZ]

**Measures:** S. 1745

```
                             CLOTURE MOTION

  Mr. McCAIN. Mr. President, I send a cloture motion to the desk and 
ask for its immediate consideration.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The cloture motion having been presented under 
rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:


                             Cloture Motion

       We the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on Calendar No. 
     433, S. 1745, the Department of Defense authorization bill.
         Trent Lott, Don Nickles, Dirk Kempthorne, Rod Grams, Jim 
           Jeffords, Craig Thomas, Kay Bailey Hutchison, Judd 
           Gregg, Bill Frist, Fred Thompson, Mike DeWine, Rick 
           Santorum, John Ashcroft, Sheila Frahm, Ben Nighthorse 
           Campbell, Hank Brown.

                          ____________________
```
