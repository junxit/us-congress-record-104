# ORDER FOR ADJOURNMENT

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS], Graham, Bob (G000352) [D-FL]

```
                         ORDER FOR ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. LOTT. If there is no further business to come before the Senate, 
I ask unanimous consent that the Senate stand in adjournment under the 
previous order following the remarks of Senator Graham of Florida and 
Senator Grassley of Iowa.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. LOTT. I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Clerk will call the roll.
  The bill clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. GRAHAM. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
