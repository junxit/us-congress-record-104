# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Manzullo, Donald A. (M001138) [R-IL]

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______


                        HON. DONALD A. MANZULLO

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, May 22, 1996

  Mr. MANZULLO. Mr. Speaker, on Thursday last, due to a family 
emergency, I missed rollcall votes 178 and 179 pertaining to the fiscal 
year 1997 budget resolution. Had I been present, I would have voted on 
rollcall No. 178, ``no'' and on rollcall No. 179, ``yes.'' I request 
unanimous consent that my statement be included following the vote in 
the permanent record.
```
