# REPORT OF THE NATIONAL SCIENCE FOUNDATION FOR FISCAL YEARS 1994 AND 1995--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT--PM 149

```
  REPORT OF THE NATIONAL SCIENCE FOUNDATION FOR FISCAL YEARS 1994 AND 
                1995--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT--PM 149

  The PRESIDING OFFICER laid before the Senate the following message 
from the President of the United States, together with an accompanying 
report; which was referred to the Committee on Labor and Human 
Resources:

To the Congress of the United States:
  As required by the provisions of section 3(f) of the National Science 
Foundation Act of 1950, as amended (42 U.S.C. 1862(f)), I transmit 
herewith the combined annual reports of the National Science Foundation 
for fiscal years 1994 and 1995.
                                                  William J. Clinton.  
  The White House, May 22, 1996.

                          ____________________
```
