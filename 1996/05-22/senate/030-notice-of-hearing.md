# NOTICE OF HEARING

**Speaking:** McCain, John (M000303) [R-AZ]

```
                           NOTICE OF HEARING


                      committee on indian affairs

  Mr. McCAIN. Mr. President, I would like to announce that the Senate 
Committee on Indian Affairs will conduct an oversight hearing during 
the session of the Senate on Tuesday, June 11, 1996, at 9:30 a.m. on 
Indian trust funds management by the Department of the Interior and 
implementation of the Indian Trust Fund Management Act of 1994. The 
hearing will be held in room 485 of the Russell Senate Office Building.
  Those wishing additional information should contact the Committee on 
Indian Affairs at 224-2251.

                          ____________________
```
