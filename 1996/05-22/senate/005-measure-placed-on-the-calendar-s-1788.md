# MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 1788

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

**Measures:** S. 1788

```
                MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 1788

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I understand there is a bill due for its 
second reading. I ask for that.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Inhofe). The Senator is correct. The clerk 
will read the bill for the second time.
  The assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 1788) to amend the National Labor Relations Act 
     and the Railway Labor Act to repeal those provisions of 
     Federal law that require employees to pay union dues or fees 
     as a condition of employment, and for other purposes.

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I object to further proceedings of this 
matter at this time.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The objection is heard. The bill will be 
placed on the calendar.

                          ____________________
```
