# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEE TO MEET

**Speaking:** Domenici, Pete V. (D000407) [R-NM]

```
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEE TO MEET


                      committee on small business

  Mr. DOMENICI. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Committee on Small Business be authorized to meet during the session of 
the Senate on Wednesday, May 22, 1996, at 4:30 p.m., to mark up 
legislation pending in the committee and to vote on the nomination of 
Ms. Ginger Ehn Lew to be Deputy Administrator of the U.S. Small 
Business Administration.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
