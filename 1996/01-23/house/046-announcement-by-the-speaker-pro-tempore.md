# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to the provisions of clause 5 of 
rule I, the Chair announces he will postpone further proceedings today 
on each motion to suspend the rules on which a recorded vote or the 
yeas and nays are ordered or on which the vote is objected to under 
clause 4 of rule XV. Such rollcall votes, if postponed, will be taken 
after debate has been concluded on all motions to suspend the rules.
```
