# JOINT SESSION OF THE TWO HOUSES--MESSAGE OF THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES

```
JOINT SESSION OF THE TWO HOUSES--MESSAGE OF THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED 
                                 STATES

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the Senate will now 
proceed to the Hall of the House of Representatives.
  Thereupon, at 8:38 p.m., the Senate, preceded by the Secretary of the 
Senate, Kelly D. Johnston, and the Sergeant at Arms, Howard O. Greene, 
Jr., proceeded to the Hall of the House of Representatives to hear the 
address by the President of the United States.
  (The address by the President of the United States, this day 
delivered by him to the joint session of the two Houses of Congress, 
appears in the proceedings of the House of Representatives in today's 
Record.)

                          ____________________
```
