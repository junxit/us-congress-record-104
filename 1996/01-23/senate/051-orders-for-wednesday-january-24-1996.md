# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, JANUARY 24, 1996

**Speaking:** Craig, Larry E. (C000858) [R-ID]

```
                 ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, JANUARY 24, 1996

  Mr. CRAIG. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that immediately 
following the joint session of Congress this evening, the Senate stand 
in adjournment until the hour of 5 p.m. on Wednesday, January 24; that 
immediately following the prayer, the Journal of proceedings be deemed 
approved to date, no resolution come over under the rule, the call of 
the calendar be dispensed with, the morning hour be deemed to have 
expired, and the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use 
later in the day, and there then be a period for morning business until 
5:30, with Senators permitted to speak for up to 5 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
