# APPOINTMENT AS MEMBER TO LIBRARY OF CONGRESS TRUST FUND BOARD

```
     APPOINTMENT AS MEMBER TO LIBRARY OF CONGRESS TRUST FUND BOARD

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, and pursuant to the 
provisions of section 1 of 2 U.S.C. 154, as amended, by section 1 of 
Public Law 102-246, the Chair announces the Speaker's appointment to 
the Library of Congress Trust Fund Board the following member on the 
part of the House:
  Mrs. Marguerite S. Roll, Paradise Valley, AZ, to a 3-year term. There 
was no objection.
```
