# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3136, CONTRACT WITH AMERICA ADVANCEMENT ACT OF 1996

**Measures:** H.Res. 391, H.R. 3136

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3136, CONTRACT 
                  WITH AMERICA ADVANCEMENT ACT OF 1996

  Mr. GOSS, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged report 
(Rept. No. 104-500) on the resolution (H. Res. 391) providing for 
consideration of the bill (H.R. 3136) to provide for enactment of the 
Senior Citizens' Right to Work Act of 1996, the Line-Item Veto Act, and 
the Small Business Growth and Fairness Act of 1996, and to provide for 
a permanent increase in the public debt limit, which was referred to 
the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
