Partial Abortion Ban Act: By a yea-and-nay vote of 286 yeas to 129 
nays, with 1 voting ``present'', Roll No. 94, the House agreed to the 
Canady motion to concur in the Senate amendments to H.R. 1833, to amend 
title 18, United States Code, to ban partial birth abortions--clearing 
the measure for the President.
  Pages H2905-29
  H. Res. 389, the rule which provided for the motion to concur in the 
Senate amendments to the bill, was agreed to earlier by a yea-and-nay 
vote of 269 yeas to 148 nays, Roll No. 93.

measures:
  Anniversary of Iraqi massacre of Kurds: H. Res. 379, expressing the 
sense of the House of Representatives concerning the eighth anniversary 
of the massacre of over 5,000 Kurds as a result of a gas bomb attack by 
the Iraqi Government (agreed to by a yea-and-nay vote of 409 yeas, Roll 
No. 95). This measure was debated on Tuesday; and
  Pages H2929-30

by a yea-and-nay vote of 408 years, Roll No. 96). This measure was 
debated on Tuesday.
