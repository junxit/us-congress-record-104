# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 9 A.M. TOMORROW

**Measures:** S.Res. 234

```
                   ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 9 A.M. TOMORROW

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, and pursuant to the 
provisions of Senate Resolution 234, in memory of a great Senator and 
devoted friend of so many of us, the late Senator Edmund S. Muskie of 
Maine, the Senate stands adjourned.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 9:11 p.m., adjourned until Thursday, March 
28, 1996, at 9 a.m.
```
