# THE BAD DEBT BOXSCORE

**Speaking:** Helms, Jesse (H000463) [R-NC]

```
                         THE BAD DEBT BOXSCORE

  Mr. HELMS. Mr. President, at the close of business yesterday, March 
26, 1996, the Federal debt stood at $5,066,587,916,694.66.

$19,154.54 as his or her share of that debt.
```
