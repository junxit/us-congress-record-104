# REPORT ON THE TRADE AGREEMENTS PROGRAM FOR CALENDAR YEAR 1995 AND THE TRADE POLICY AGENDA FOR CALENDAR YEAR 1996--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT--PM 136

```
 REPORT ON THE TRADE AGREEMENTS PROGRAM FOR CALENDAR YEAR 1995 AND THE 
     TRADE POLICY AGENDA FOR CALENDAR YEAR 1996--MESSAGE FROM THE 
                           PRESIDENT--PM 136

  The PRESIDING OFFICER laid before the Senate the following message 
from the President of the United States, together with an accompanying 
report; which was referred to the Committee on Finance.


To the Congress of the United States:
  As required by section 163 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended (19 
U.S.C. 2213), I transmit herewith the 1996 Trade Policy Agenda and 1995 
Annual Report on the Trade Agreements Program.
                                                  William J. Clinton.  
  The White House, March 27, 1996.

                          ____________________
```
