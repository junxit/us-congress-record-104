# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Funderburk, David (F000426) [R-NC]

**Measures:** H.R. 3259

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______


                         HON. DAVID FUNDERBURK

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, May 29, 1996

  Mr. FUNDERBURK. Mr. Speaker, on May 22 I was unavoidably absent and 
missed several amendment votes on H.R. 3259, the FY 1997 Intelligence 
Authorization Act. Had I been present, I would have voted: ``aye'' on 
rollcall 184, the Richardson amendment; ``no'' on rollcall 185, the 
Sanders amendment; and ``no'' on rollcall 186, the Conyers amendment.
```
