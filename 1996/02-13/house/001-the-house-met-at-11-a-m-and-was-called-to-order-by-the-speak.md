# The House met at 11 a.m. and was called to order by the Speaker pro
tempore [Mrs. Morella].

```
[[Page H1237]]

  The House met at 11 a.m. and was called to order by the Speaker pro 
tempore [Mrs. Morella].
  The Chaplain, Rev. James David Ford, D.D., offered the following 
prayer:
  Let us pray using the words of Psalm 66:
  Praise God with shouts of joy, all people.
  Sing to the glory of his name; offer him glorious praise.
  Say to God, ``How wonderful are the things You do.
  Your power is so great that Your enemies bow down in fear before You.
  Everyone on Earth worships You; they sing praises to You, they sing 
praises to Your name.'' Amen.
```
