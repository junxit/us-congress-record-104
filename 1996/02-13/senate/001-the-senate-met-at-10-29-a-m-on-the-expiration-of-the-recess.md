# The Senate met at 10:29 a.m., on the expiration of the recess, and was called to order by the President pro tempore [Mr. Thurmond].

```
[[Page S1265]]

  The Senate met at 10:29 a.m., on the expiration of the recess, and 
was called to order by the President pro tempore [Mr. Thurmond].

           RECESS UNTIL 10:30 A.M., FRIDAY, FEBRUARY 16, 1996

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the Senate 
stands in recess until 10:30 a.m., Friday, February 16, 1996.
  Thereupon, at 10:30 a.m., the Senate recessed until Friday, February 
16, 1996, at 10:30 a.m.
```
