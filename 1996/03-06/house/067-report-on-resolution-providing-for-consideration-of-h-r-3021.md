# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3021, GUARANTEEING CONTINUING FULL INVESTMENT OF SOCIAL SECURITY AND OTHER FEDERAL FUNDS IN OBLIGATIONS OF THE UNITED STATES

**Measures:** H.Res. 371, H.R. 3021

```
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3021, 
 GUARANTEEING CONTINUING FULL INVESTMENT OF SOCIAL SECURITY AND OTHER 
           FEDERAL FUNDS IN OBLIGATIONS OF THE UNITED STATES

  Mr. LINDER, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 104-473) on the resolution (H. Res. 371) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 3021) to guarantee the continuing 
full investment of Social Security and other Federal funds in 
obligations of the United States, which was referred to the House 
Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
