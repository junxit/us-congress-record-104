          REPORT OF COMMITTEE ON PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

  Under clause 2 of rule XIII, reports of committees were delivered to

     States (Rept. 104-473). Referred to the House Calendar.

     Resolution providing for consideration of the bill (H.R. 
     3019) making appropriations for fiscal year 1996 to make a 
     further downpayment toward a balanced budget, and for other 
     purposes (Rept. 104-474). Referred to the House Calendar.
