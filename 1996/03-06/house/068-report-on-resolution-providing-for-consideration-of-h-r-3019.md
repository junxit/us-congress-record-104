# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3019, THE BALANCED BUDGET DOWNPAYMENT ACT, II

**Measures:** H.Res. 372, H.R. 3019

```
  REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3019, THE 
                  BALANCED BUDGET DOWNPAYMENT ACT, II

  Mr. LINDER, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 104-474) on the resolution (H. Res. 372) providing 
for the consideration of the bill (H.R. 3019) making appropriations for 
fiscal year 1996 to make a further downpayment toward a balanced 
budget, and for other purposes, which was referred to the House 
Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
