# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF SENATE AMENDMENTS TO H.R. 1833, PARTIAL BIRTH ABORTION BAN ACT

**Measures:** H.Res. 389, H.R. 1833

```
 REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF SENATE AMENDMENTS 
              TO H.R. 1833, PARTIAL BIRTH ABORTION BAN ACT

  Mrs. WALDHOLTZ, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 104-492) on the resolution (H. Res. 389) providing 
for consideration of the Senate amendments to the bill (H.R. 1833) to 
amend title 18, United States Code, to ban partial-birth abortions
```
