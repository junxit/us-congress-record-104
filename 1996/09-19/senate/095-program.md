# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Stevens, Ted (S000888) [R-AK]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. STEVENS. Mr. President, tomorrow morning at 10 a.m., the Senate 
will vote on or in relation to the Grassley amendment to the maritime 
bill. Other rollcall votes are possible on the remaining amendments to 
the maritime bill. It is hoped that a unanimous-consent agreement 
regarding the maritime bill can be reached tomorrow morning which would 
allow Members to know the voting schedule for the remainder of Friday's 
session. The Senate may also be asked to turn to consideration of any 
other items cleared for action.
```
