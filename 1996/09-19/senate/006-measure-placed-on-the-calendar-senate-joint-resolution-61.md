# MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--SENATE JOINT RESOLUTION 61

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 61

```
       MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--SENATE JOINT RESOLUTION 61

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I understand there is a resolution at the 
desk that is due for its second reading.

       A joint resolution (S.J. Res. 61) granting the consent of
```
