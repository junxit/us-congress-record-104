# CRAWFORD NATIONAL FISH HATCHERY CONVEYANCE ACT

**Speaking:** Stevens, Ted (S000888) [R-AK]

**Measures:** H.R. 3287

```
             CRAWFORD NATIONAL FISH HATCHERY CONVEYANCE ACT

  Mr. STEVENS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent the Environment 
and Public Works Committee be discharged of H.R. 3287, and further that 
the Senate proceed now to its immediate consideration.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The clerk will report.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 3287) to direct the Secretary of the Interior 
     to convey the Crawford National Fish Hatchery to the city of 
     Crawford, Nebraska.

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection to the immediate 
consideration of the bill?
  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the bill.
  Mr. STEVENS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent the bill be 
deemed read a third time and passed, the motion to reconsider be laid 
upon the table, and any statements relating to the bill be printed at 
this point in the Record.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The bill (H.R. 3287) was deemed read for a third time and passed.

                          ____________________
```
