# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 172

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Mr. Lundregan, one of its clerks, 
announced that the Senate had passed with an amendment in which the 
concurrence of the House is requested, a resolution of the House of the 
following title:

       H. Con. Res. 172. Concurrent resolution authorizing the 
     1996 Summer Olympic Torch Relay to be run through the Capitol 
     Grounds, and for other purposes.

  The message further announced that pursuant to Public Law 104-127, 
the Chair, on behalf of the majority leader, appoints Sheri L. Chapman, 
of Idaho, and Richard K. Golb, of California, to the Water Rights Task 
Force.
  The message also announced that pursuant to Public Law 104-127, the 
Chair, on behalf of the Democratic leader, appoints Elizabeth Ann 
Ricke, of Colorado, to the Water Rights Task Force.

                          ____________________
```
