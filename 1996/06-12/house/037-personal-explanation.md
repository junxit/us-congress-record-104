# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Harman, Jane (H000213) [D-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 3103

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Ms. HARMAN. Mr. Speaker, because my flight from California was 
delayed yesterday, I was unable to be present to vote on several 
rollcall votes.
  Had I been present, I would have voted ``present'' on rollcall 225, 
approval of the journal.
  I would have voted ``yes'' on rollcall vote 226, the motion to 
instruct House conferees to H.R. 3103, the bill to improve the 
portability and continuity of health insurance coverage.
  I would have voted ``yes'' on rollcall vote 227, Mr. Frank's 
amendment to prohibit IMET funds for Indonesia.
  And I would have voted ``yes'' on rollcall vote 228, final passage of 
the foreign operations appropriations bill for fiscal 1997.

                          ____________________
```
