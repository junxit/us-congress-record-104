# LAYING ON TABLE HOUSE RESOLUTION 352, AUTHORIZING SPEAKER TO DECLARE RECESSES SUBJECT TO THE CALL OF THE CHAIR

**Speaking:** Solomon, Gerald B. H. (S000675) [R-NY]

**Measures:** H.Res. 352

```
 LAYING ON TABLE HOUSE RESOLUTION 352, AUTHORIZING SPEAKER TO DECLARE 
               RECESSES SUBJECT TO THE CALL OF THE CHAIR

  Mr. SOLOMON. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that House 
Resolution 352, authorizing the Speaker to declare recesses subject to 
the call of the Chair from February 2, 1996, through February 26, 1996, 
be laid on the table, because it is no longer relevant.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from New York?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
