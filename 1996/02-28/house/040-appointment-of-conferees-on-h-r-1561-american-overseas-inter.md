# APPOINTMENT OF CONFEREES ON H.R. 1561, AMERICAN OVERSEAS INTERESTS ACT OF 1995

**Speaking:** Gilman, Benjamin A. (G000212) [R-NY]

**Measures:** H.R. 1561

```
APPOINTMENT OF CONFEREES ON H.R. 1561, AMERICAN OVERSEAS INTERESTS ACT 
                                OF 1995

  Mr. GILMAN. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent to take from the 
Speaker's table the bill (H.R. 1561) to consolidate the foreign affairs 
agencies of the United States; to authorize appropriations for the 
Department of State and related agencies for fiscal years 1996 and 
1997; to responsibly reduce the authorizations of appropriations for 
United States foreign assistance programs for fiscal years 1996 and 

[[Page H1491]]
1997, and for other purposes, with a Senate amendment thereto, disagree 
to the Senate amendment, and agree to the conference requested by the 
Senate.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from New York? The Chair hears none and, without objection, 
appoints the following conferees: Messrs. Gilman, Goodling, Hyde, Roth, 
Bereuter, Smith of New Jersey, Burton of Indiana, Ms. Ros-Lehtinen, and 
Messrs. Hamilton, Gejdenson, Lantos, Torricelli, Berman, and Ackerman.
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
