# ELECTION OF MEMBER TO COMMITTEE ON WAYS AND MEANS

**Speaking:** Armey, Richard K. (A000217) [R-TX]

**Measures:** H.Res. 343

```
           ELECTION OF MEMBER TO COMMITTEE ON WAYS AND MEANS

  Mr. ARMEY. Mr. Speaker, by direction of the Republican Conference, I 
call up a privileged resolution (H. Res. 343) and ask for its immediate 
consideration.
  The Clerk read the resolution, as follows:

                              H. Res. 343

       Resolved, That the following named Member be, and he is 
     hereby, elected to the following standing committees of the 
     House of Representatives:
       Committee on Ways and Means: Mr. Hayes of Louisiana.

  The resolution was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```
