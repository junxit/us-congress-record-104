# ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS

**Measures:** S. 1028, S. 1473, S. 1520

```
                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                S. 1028

  At the request of Mrs. Kassebaum, the name of the Senator from 
Massachusetts [Mr. Kerry] was added as a cosponsor of S. 1028, a bill 
to provide increased access to health care benefits, to provide 
increased portability of health care benefits, to provide increased 
security of health care benefits, to increase the purchasing power of 
individuals and small employers, and for other purposes.


                                S. 1473

  At the request of Ms. Snowe, the name of the Senator from Michigan 
[Mr. Abraham] was added as a cosponsor of S. 1473, a bill to authorize 
the Administrator of General Services to permit the posting in space 
under the control of the Administrator of notices concerning missing 
children, and for other purposes.


                                S. 1520

  At the request of Mr. Helms, the names of the Senator from Alaska 
[Mr. Stevens] and the Senator from Oklahoma [Mr. Inhofe] were added as 
cosponsors of S. 1520, a bill to award a congressional gold medal to 
Ruth and Billy Graham.

                          ____________________
```
