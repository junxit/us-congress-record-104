# NOMINATION JOINTLY REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON FINANCE AND COMMITTEE ON BANKING, HOUSING, AND URBAN AFFAIRS

**Speaking:** Dole, Robert J. (D000401) [R-KS]

```
 NOMINATION JOINTLY REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON FINANCE AND COMMITTEE ON 
                  BANKING, HOUSING, AND URBAN AFFAIRS

  Mr. DOLE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the nomination 
of Stuart E. Eizenstat, of Maryland, to be Under Secretary of Commerce 
for International Trade, received on January 4, 1996, be jointly 
referred to the Committee on Finance and the Committee on Banking, 
Housing, and Urban Affairs.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
