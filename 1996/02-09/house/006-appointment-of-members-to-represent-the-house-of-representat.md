# APPOINTMENT OF MEMBERS TO REPRESENT THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES AT CEREMONIES FOR THE OBSERVANCE OF GEORGE WASHINGTON'S BIRTHDAY

**Speaking:** Montgomery, G. V. (Sonny) (M000865) [D-MS]

```
  APPOINTMENT OF MEMBERS TO REPRESENT THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES AT 
     CEREMONIES FOR THE OBSERVANCE OF GEORGE WASHINGTON'S BIRTHDAY

  Mr. MONTGOMERY. Madam Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that it shall 
be in order for the Speaker to appoint two Members of the House, one 
upon the recommendation of the minority leader, to represent the House 
of Representatives at appropriate ceremonies for the observance of 
George Washington's birthday to be held on Thursday, February 22, 1996.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Mississippi?
  There was no objection.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to the order of the House of today, 
the Chair appoints the following Members to represent the House of 
Representatives at appropriate ceremonies for the observance of George 
Washington's birthday to be held on Thursday, February 22, 1996: Mr. 
Davis of Virginia and Mr. Moran of Virginia.

                          ____________________
```
