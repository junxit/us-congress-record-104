# MAKING IN ORDER AT ANY TIME CONSIDERATION OF CONFERENCE REPORT ON H.R.

**Speaking:** Vucanovich, Barbara F. (V000124) [R-NV]

**Measures:** H.R. 3517

```
MAKING IN ORDER AT ANY TIME CONSIDERATION OF CONFERENCE REPORT ON H.R. 
 3517, MILITARY CONSTRUCTION APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 1997, AND CONFERENCE 
   REPORT ON H.R. 3845, DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 1997

  Mrs. VUCANOVICH. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that it be in 
order at any time to consider conference reports to accompany the bills 
H.R. 3517 and 3845, that all points of order against both conference 
reports and against their consideration be waived, and that both 
conference reports be considered as read when called up.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Ney). Is there objection to the request 
of the gentlewoman from Navada?
  There was no objection.
```
