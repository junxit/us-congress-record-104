# LEGISLATIVE PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Armey, Richard K. (A000217) [R-TX]

```
                          LEGISLATIVE PROGRAM

  (Mr. ARMEY asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute.)
  Mr. ARMEY. Mr. Speaker, let me first advise the Members, and I do 
understand how hard we are all working,

[[Page H9811]]

we are all anxious to complete our work to make our departures for our 
August recess work period.
  At this time I can only advise Members, to the best of my knowledge, 
we should expect additional votes this evening within the hour. At any 
point during the evening, when I find information by which I can advise 
otherwise, I will ask for time to do so. But my best advice at this 
point is we must be prepared to stay for additional votes tonight, and 
I will keep Members informed.
  I want to also express my appreciation to the Members on both sides 
of the aisle for your patience with these times being as they are.
```
