# NOTICE OF HEARING

**Speaking:** Murkowski, Frank H. (M001085) [R-AK]

```
                           NOTICE OF HEARING


               Committee on Energy and Natural Resources

  Mr. MURKOWSKI. Mr. President, I would like to announce for the 
information of the Senate and the public that a field hearing has been 
scheduled before the Committee on Energy and Natural Resources to 
receive testimony on the issue of competitive change in the electric 
power industry. The hearing will focus on regional issues associated 
with competitive change.
  The hearing will take place on Monday, September 9, beginning at 9 
a.m. at the Champlain College Alumni Auditorium, on Maple Street in 
Burlington, Vermont 05401.
  Those wishing to testify or submit written statements should write to 
the Committee on Energy and Natural Resources, United States Senate, 
Washington, D.C. 20510. For further information, please contact Shawn 
Taylor or Howard Useem.

                          ____________________
```
