# UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT--S. 1959

**Speaking:** Murkowski, Frank H. (M001085) [R-AK]

**Measures:** S. 1959

```
                  UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT--S. 1959

  Mr. MURKOWSKI. Mr. President, I now ask unanimous consent that 
following passage of the foreign operations appropriations bill, the 
Senate then begin consideration of Calendar No. 496, S. 1959, the 
energy and water appropriations bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. MURKOWSKI. Mr. President, amendments are expected to be offered 
to the energy and water appropriations bill; therefore, Members can 
expect additional rollcall votes on Friday following the stacked 
sequence beginning at 9:30 a.m.
```
