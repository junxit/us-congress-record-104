# EXTENDING CONVERSION DEADLINE FOR THE VESSEL "M/V TWIN DRILL"

**Measures:** S. 808

```
    EXTENDING CONVERSION DEADLINE FOR THE VESSEL ``M/V TWIN DRILL''

  The bill (S. 808) to extend the deadline for the conversion of the 
vessel M/V Twin Drill, and for other purposes, was considered, ordered 
to be engrossed for a third reading, read the third time, and passed; 
as follows:

                                 S. 808

       Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of 
     the United States of America in Congress assembled,

     SECTION 1. EXTENSION OF DEADLINE FOR CONVERSION.

       Section 601(d) of the Coast Guard Authorization Act of 1993 
     (Public Law 103-206, 107 Stat. 2445) is amended--
       (1) in paragraph (3), by striking ``June 30, 1995'' and 
     inserting ``June 30, 1996''; and
       (2) in paragraph (4), by striking ``12 months'' and 
     inserting ``24 months''.

                          ____________________
```
