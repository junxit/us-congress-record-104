# THE VERY BAD DEBT BOXSCORE

**Speaking:** Helms, Jesse (H000463) [R-NC]

```
                       THE VERY BAD DEBT BOXSCORE

  Mr. HELMS. Mr. President, at the close of business yesterday, 
Tuesday, September 24, the Federal debt stood at $5,195,854,879,174.22.
  Five years ago, September 24, 1991, the Federal debt stood at 
$3,629,138,000,000.
  Ten years ago, September 24, 1986, the Federal debt stood at 
$2,107,495,000,000.
  Fifteen years ago, September 24, 1981, the Federal debt stood at 
$979,131,000,000.
  Twenty-five years ago, September 24, 1971, the Federal debt stood at 
$415,688,000,000. This reflects an increase of more than $4 trillion 
($4,780,166,879,174.22) during the 25 years from 1971 to 1996.

                          ____________________
```
