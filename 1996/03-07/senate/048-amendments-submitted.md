# AMENDMENTS SUBMITTED

**Measures:** H.R. 3021

```
                          AMENDMENTS SUBMITTED

                                 ______


             THE FEDERAL FUNDS FULL INVESTMENT ACT OF 1996

                                 ______


                      MOYNIHAN AMENDMENT NO. 3465

  Mr. MOYNIHAN proposed an amendment to the bill (H.R. 3021) to 
guarantee the continuing full investment of Social Security and other 
Federal funds in obligations of the United States; as follows:

       Strike all matter after the enactment clause and insert the 
     following:

                       TITLE  --PUBLIC DEBT LIMIT

     SEC. 01. INCREASE IN PUBLIC DEBT LIMIT.

       Subsection (b) of section 3101 of title 31, United States 
     Code, is amended by striking the dollar amount contained in 
     the first sentence and inserting ``$5,400,000,000,000''.
```
