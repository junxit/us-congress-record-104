# ACTION TAKEN ON H.R. 497 VITIATED

**Speaking:** Baucus, Max (B000243) [D-MT], Dorgan, Byron L. (D000432) [D-ND]

**Measures:** H.R. 497, S. 1597

```
                   ACTION TAKEN ON H.R. 497 VITIATED

  Mr. BAUCUS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the action 
just taken on the second reading of H.R. 497 be vitiated.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. BAUCUS. Mr. President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. DORGAN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The Senator from North Dakota is recognized.
  Mr. DORGAN. I thank the Chair.
  (The remarks of Mr. Dorgan pertaining to the introduction of S. 1597 
are located in today's Record under ``Statements on Introduced Bills 
and Joint Resolutions.'')

                          ____________________
```
