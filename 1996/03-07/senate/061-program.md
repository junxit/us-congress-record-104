# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, for the information of all Senators, the 
Senate will begin consideration of the continuing resolution at 1 p.m., 
on Monday. Several amendments are expected to be offered. However, any 
votes with respect to those amendments will be postponed to occur on 
Tuesday, at a time to be determined by the two leaders. Therefore, 
there will be no rollcall votes during Monday's session of the Senate.
  In addition, Mr. President, a cloture motion was filed on both the 
D.C. appropriations conference report and the legislation with respect 
to Whitewater. Under a previous order, those two cloture votes will 
occur beginning at 2:15 p.m., Tuesday, and they will be back-to-back 
votes. Additional amendments and votes will occur on Tuesday with 
respect to the continuing resolution. It is the hope of the leadership 
that the continuing resolution can be completed by the close of 
business Tuesday.

                          ____________________
```
