# MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.R. 3021

```
                        MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

  At 11:19 a.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Ms. Goetz, one of its reading clerks, announced that the Speaker 
appoints the following Members on the part of the House to the Advisory 
Commission on Intergovernmental Relations: Mr. Shays of Connecticut and 
Mr. Portman of Ohio.
                                                                    ____

  At 12:22 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mr. Hays, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
passed the following bill, in which it requests the concurrence of the 
Senate:

       H.R. 3021. An act to guarantee the continuing full 
     investment of Social Security and other Federal funds in 
     obligations of the United States.

                          ____________________
```
