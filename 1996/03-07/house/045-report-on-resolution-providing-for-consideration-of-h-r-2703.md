# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2703, THE EFFECTIVE DEATH PENALTY AND PUBLIC SAFETY ACT OF 1996

**Measures:** H.Res. 376, H.R. 2703, H.R. 3021

```
  REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2703, THE 
         EFFECTIVE DEATH PENALTY AND PUBLIC SAFETY ACT OF 1996

  Mr. GOSS, for the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged report 
(Rept. No. 104-191) on the resolution (H. Res. 376) providing for the 
consideration of the bill (H.R. 2703) to combat terrorism, which was 
referred to the House Calender and ordered to be printed.

[[Page H1959]]



                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Mr. Lundregan, one of its clerks, 
announced that the Senate had passed without amendment a bill of the 
House of the following title:

       H.R. 3021. An act to guarantee the continuing full 
     investment of Social Security and other Federal funds in 
     obligations of the United States.
```
