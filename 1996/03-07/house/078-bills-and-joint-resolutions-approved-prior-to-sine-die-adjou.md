 BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS APPROVED PRIOR TO SINE DIE ADJOURNMENT OF 
                THE FIRST SESSION OF THE 104TH CONGRESS

  The President notified the Clerk of the House that, prior to the sine 
die adjournment of the first session of the 104th Congress, he approved 
and signed on the following dates bills and joint resolutions of the 
House of the following titles:

           April 10, 1995:
