# APPOINTMENT BY THE PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** S.Res. 4, S.Res. 400

```
                APPOINTMENT BY THE PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair, on behalf of the President pro 
tempore, pursuant to Senate Resolution 400, 94th Congress, and Senate 
Resolution 4, 95th Congress, appoints the following Senators to the 
Select Committee on Intelligence: the Senator from Pennsylvania [Mr. 
Specter], the Senator from Indiana [Mr. Lugar], the Senator from 
Alabama [Mr. Shelby], the Senator from Ohio [Mr. DeWine], the Senator 
from Arizona [Mr. Kyl], the Senator from Oklahoma [Mr. Inhofe], the 
Senator from Texas [Mrs. Hutchison], the Senator from Maine [Mr. 
Cohen], and the Senator from Colorado [Mr. Brown].

                          ____________________
```
