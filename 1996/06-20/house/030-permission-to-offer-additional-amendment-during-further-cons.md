**Measures:** H.R. 3662

 PERMISSION TO OFFER ADDITIONAL AMENDMENT DURING FURTHER CONSIDERATION 
     OF H.R. 3662, DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR AND RELATED AGENCIES
