# SALES OF NUCLEAR TECHNOLOGY TO CHINESE COMPANY

**Speaking:** Traficant, James A., Jr. (T000350) [D-OH]

```
             SALES OF NUCLEAR TECHNOLOGY TO CHINESE COMPANY

  (Mr. TRAFICANT asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. TRAFICANT. Mr. Speaker, the White House approved a $140 million 
sale of nuclear technology to a Chinese company that has already sold 
nuclear technology to Pakistan and Iran. The White House said do not 
worry, this time the Chinese Energy Corp. has promised not to do this 
again. Unbelievable. Who is on first? What is on second?
  America gives money to Russia, Russia sells billions of dollars' 
worth of technology to China, China sells the technology to our 
enemies. The White House threatens China, then the White House sells 
nuclear technology to China, and China says do not worry.
  Beam me up, here. It is completely evident the left hand at the White 
House does not know what the far left hand is doing. I yield back the 
balance of any nuclear reactors that we will be facing in the future.

                          ____________________
```
