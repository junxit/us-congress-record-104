  Mr. HAMILTON. Mr. Speaker, I would like to bring to my colleagues' 
attention information submitted pursuant to the Arms Export Control Act 
with respect to U.S. foreign military sales during fiscal year 1995.
  The first table details worldwide government-to-government foreign 
military sales during fiscal year 1995 for defense articles and 
services and for construction sales. Total FMS sales for fiscal year 
1995 were $9.029 billion, a decline from $12.865 billion in fiscal year 
1994.
  The second table details licenses/approval for the export of 
commercially sold defense articles and services for fiscal year 1995.
