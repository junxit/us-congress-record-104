# JOINT MEETING OF THE TWO HOUSES--ADDRESS BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE FRENCH REPUBLIC

**Speaking:** Faircloth, Lauch (F000437) [R-NC]

```
JOINT MEETING OF THE TWO HOUSES--ADDRESS BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE FRENCH 
                                REPUBLIC

  Mr. FAIRCLOTH. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
President of the Senate be authorized to appoint a committee on the 
part of the Senate to join with a like committee on the part of the 
House of Representatives to escort His Excellency Jacques Chirac, 
President of the French Republic, into the House Chamber for the joint 
meeting on Thursday, February 1, 1996.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
