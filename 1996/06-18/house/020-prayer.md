# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Rev. James David Ford, D.D., offered the following 
prayer:
  May Your Spirit, O God, be with us all the day long and remain with 
us in our hopes and in our sorrows, in our dreams and in our defeats. 
Cause us never to forget Your heavenly vision and let us never walk 
away from the gifts of Your good grace. We know, gracious God, that 
Your Spirit is over all the world and given to every person and is 
present in our lives this day and every day, we pray. Amen.
```
