# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Kempthorne, Dirk (K000088) [R-ID]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. KEMPTHORNE. Mr. President, for the information of all Senators, 
at 9:30 a.m., Thursday, there will be 3 hours of debate time remaining 
on the Greenspan nomination. Under the previous order, a vote will 
occur on the Greenspan nomination at 2 p.m., to be followed by any 
votes required on the remaining nominees to the Federal Reserve System.
  The Senate will also resume the DOD authorization bill during 
tomorrow's session. Senators can therefore expect rollcall votes 
throughout the day.
```
