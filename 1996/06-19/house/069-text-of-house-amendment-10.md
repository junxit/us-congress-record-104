# Text of House Amendment 10

**Speaking:** Brown, George E., Jr. (B000918) [D-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 3666

```
H.R. 3666

                  Offered By: Mr. Brown of California

       Amendment No. 10: At the end of the bill, insert after the 
     last section (preceding the short title) the following new 
     section:
       Sec.  . None of the funds made available in this Act for 
     the National Aeronautics and Space Administration may be used 
     for the National Center for Science Literacy, Education and 
     Technology at the American Museum of Natural History.
```
