# Text of House Amendment 14

**Speaking:** Gutknecht, Gil (G000536) [R-MN]

**Measures:** H.R. 3666

```
H.R. 3666

                       Offered By: Mr. Gutknecht

       Amendment No. 14: Page 95, after line 21, insert the 
     following new section:
       Sec. 422. Each amount appropriated or otherwise made

     or otherwise made available by a provision of law is hereby 
     reduced by 1.9 percent.
```
