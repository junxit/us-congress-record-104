# Text of House Amendment 23

**Speaking:** Hoke, Martin R. (H000707) [R-OH]

**Measures:** H.R. 3666

```
H.R. 3666

                          Offered By: Mr. Hoke

       Amendment No. 23: At the end of the item relating to 
     ``DEPARTMENT OF HOUSING AND URBAN DEVELOPMENT--Community 
     Planning and Development--community development block grants 
     fund'', insert the following:
       Of the amount made available under this heading, $5,000,000 
     shall be available for the John Heinz Neighborhood 
     Development Program under section 123 of the Housing and 
     Urban-Rural Recovery Act of 1983.
```
