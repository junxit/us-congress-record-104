# Text of House Amendment 9

**Speaking:** Bentsen, Ken (B000400) [D-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 3666

```
H.R. 3666

                        Offered By: Mr. Bentsen

       Amendment No. 9: At the end of the bill, insert after the 
     last section (preceding the short title) the following new 
     section:
       Sec. 422. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used by the Environmental Protection Agency to issue, 
     implement, administer, or enforce any rule or order when it 
     is made known to the Federal official having authority to 
     obligate or expend such funds that the rule or order 
     authorizes any person to import into the United States 
     (pursuant to an exemption under section 6(e)(3)(B) of the 
     Toxic Substances Control Act or otherwise) any waste 
     containing concentrations or more than 50 parts per million 
     (ppm) or polychlorinated biphenyls for the purposes of 
     disposal or treatment.
```
