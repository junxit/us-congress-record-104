# Text of House Amendment 22

**Speaking:** Hoekstra, Peter (H000676) [R-MI]

**Measures:** H.R. 3666

```
H.R. 3666

                        Offered By: Mr. Hoekstra

       Amendment No. 22: At the end of the bill, insert after the 
     last section (preceding the short title) the following new 
     section:
       Sec.  . None of the funds made available in this Act for 
     the Corporation for National and Community Service may be 
     used to award any grant to any national service program when 
     it is made known to the Federal official having authority to 
     obligate or expend such funds that the amount of such grant 
     exceeds $21,000 per program participant, using the accounting 
     methodology utilized by the Government Accounting Office in 
     its cost study of the Corporation.
```
