# Text of House Amendment 20

**Speaking:** Hoekstra, Peter (H000676) [R-MI]

**Measures:** H.R. 3666

```
H.R. 3666

                        Offered By: Mr. Hoekstra

       Amendment No. 20: At the end of the bill, insert after the 
     last section (preceding the short title) the following new 
     section:
       Sec.  . None of the funds made available in this Act for 
     the Corporation for National and Community Service may be 
     used for training and technical assistance contracts.
```
