# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** H.R. 3005, H.R. 3107

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Collins of Georgia). Pursuant to clause 
5 of rule I, the Chair will now put the question on each motion to 
suspend the rules on which further proceedings were postponed on 
Tuesday, June 18, 1996, in the order in which that motion was 
entertained.
  Votes will be taken in the following order: H.R. 3005, by the yeas 
and nays, and H.R. 3107 by the yeas and nays.
  The Chair will reduce to 5 minutes the time for any electronic vote 
after the first such vote in this series.
```
