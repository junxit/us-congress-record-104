# NATIONAL TECHNOLOGY TRANSFER AND ADVANCEMENT ACT OF 1995

**Measures:** H.R. 2196

```
        NATIONAL TECHNOLOGY TRANSFER AND ADVANCEMENT ACT OF 1995

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The pending business is on the question de 
novo of suspending the rules and concurring in the Senate amendments to 
the bill, H.R. 2196.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentlewoman from Maryland [Mrs. Morella] that the House suspend the 
rules and concur in the Senate amendments to the bill, H.R. 2196.
  The question was taken; and (two-thirds having voted in favor 
thereof) the rules were suspended and the Senate amendments were 
concurred in.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.
```
