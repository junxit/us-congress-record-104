# AMERICA CONTINUES TO BE GREAT

**Speaking:** Burns, Conrad R. (B001126) [R-MT]

```
                     AMERICA CONTINUES TO BE GREAT

  Mr. BURNS. Mr. President, I associate my remarks with those 
recognizing Oklahoma City. That tragedy and what we learn from it is 
another sign that America continues to be great. The wounds will heal. 
There will always be scars, but we pick up and we continue to thrive 
and thrive in this great and free country.
  So we salute Oklahoma, Oklahoma City, and all the Americans whose 
lives were touched by that tragedy.
  Mr. President, I yield the floor.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator from Tennessee.

                          ____________________
```
