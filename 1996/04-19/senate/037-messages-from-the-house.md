**Measures:** H.Res. 406

                        MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

  At 10:45 a.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered

agreed to the following resolution:

       H. Res. 406. Resolution in tribute to Secretary of Commerce

     April 3, 1996, while in service to their country on a mission
