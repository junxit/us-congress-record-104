# A PROCLAMATION REMEMBERING SHELLY McPECK KELLY

**Speaking:** Ney, Robert W. (N000081) [R-OH]

```
             A PROCLAMATION REMEMBERING SHELLY McPECK KELLY

                                 ______


                           HON. ROBERT W. NEY

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, April 18, 1996

  Mr. NEY. Mr. Speaker, I commend the following article to my 
colleagues:

       Whereas, Shelly McPeck Kelly, a United States Air Force 
     Technical Sergeant that died in the plane crash along with 
     Commerce Secretary Ron Brown, and
       Whereas, Shelly McPeck Kelly, was a loyal and devoted wife, 
     and loving mother of two; and,
       Whereas, Shelly McPeck Kelly, served faithfully as an 
     airplane stewardess in the United States Air Force achieving 
     the rank of Technical Sergeant, and
       Whereas, Shelly McPeck Kelly, should be commended for her 
     service to the United States of America during the Bosnian 
     Peacekeeping Operation; and,
       Whereas, the residents of Eastern Ohio join me in honoring 
     Shell McPeck Kelly for her brave and loyal citizenship to the 
     United States.

                          ____________________
```
