# CONTINUITY OF CARE WEEK

**Speaking:** Rush, Bobby L. (R000515) [D-IL]

```
                        CONTINUITY OF CARE WEEK

                                 ______


                           HON. BOBBY L. RUSH

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, March 14, 1996

  Mr. RUSH. Mr. Speaker, the concept of continuity of care is an 
essential component of today's health care delivery system.
  The professional responsible for continuity of care comprise a 
variety of disciplines, educational backgrounds, and practice in 
diverse setting. These professionals function as facilitators, 
caregivers, and advocates to ensure that patients receive quality, 
cost-effective health services.
  Mr. Speaker, in recognition of these individuals' dedication and 
commitment to health care, the third week of September 1996, and each 
September thereafter, shall be known as ``Continuity of Care Week.''
```
