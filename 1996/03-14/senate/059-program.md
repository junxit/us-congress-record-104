# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Abraham, Spencer (A000355) [R-MI]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. ABRAHAM. For the information of all Senators, the Senate will 
debate the small business regulatory relief bill tomorrow. Any votes 
ordered in relation to that bill will occur on Tuesday.
  On Friday, following the small business bill debate, the Senate will 
resume consideration of the continuing resolution. Senators should be 
prepared to offer their amendments during Friday's session.
  Under the previous order, there will be no votes until Tuesday, March 
19.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
