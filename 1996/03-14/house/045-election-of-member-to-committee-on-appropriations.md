# ELECTION OF MEMBER TO COMMITTEE ON APPROPRIATIONS

**Speaking:** Fazio, Vic (F000053) [D-CA]

**Measures:** H.Res. 383

```
           ELECTION OF MEMBER TO COMMITTEE ON APPROPRIATIONS

  Mr. FAZIO of California. Mr. Speaker, I offer a privileged resolution 
(H. Res. 383) and ask for its immediate consideration.
  The Clerk read the resolution, as follows:

                              H. Res. 383

       Resolved, That the following named Member be, and is 
     hereby, elected to the following standing committees of the 
     House of Representatives: To the Committee on Appropriations, 
     the following Member: Jose Serrano of New York.

  The resolution was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```
