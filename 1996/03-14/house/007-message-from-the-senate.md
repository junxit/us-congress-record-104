# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** H.R. 2854

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Mr. Lundregan, one of its clerks, 
announced that the Senate had passed with amendments a bill of the 
House of the following title:

       H.R. 2854. An act to modify the operation of certain 
     agricultural programs.

  The message also announced that the Senate insists upon its amendment 
to the bill (H.R. 2854) ``An act to modify the operation of certain 
agricultural programs,'' requests a conference with the House on the 
disagreeing votes of the two Houses thereon, and appoints Mr. Lugar, 
Mr. Dole,  Mr. Helms, Mr. Cochran, Mr. McConnell, Mr. Craig, Mr. Leahy, 
Mr. Pryor, Mr. Heflin, Mr. Harkin, and Mr. Conrad, to be the conferees 
on the part of the Senate.
```
