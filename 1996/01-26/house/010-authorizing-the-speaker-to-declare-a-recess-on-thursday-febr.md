# AUTHORIZING THE SPEAKER TO DECLARE A RECESS ON THURSDAY, FEBRUARY 1, 1996, FOR THE PURPOSE OF RECEIVING IN JOINT MEETING HIS EXCELLENCY JACQUES CHIRAC, PRESIDENT OF FRANCE

**Speaking:** Coble, Howard (C000556) [R-NC]

```
 AUTHORIZING THE SPEAKER TO DECLARE A RECESS ON THURSDAY, FEBRUARY 1, 
  1996, FOR THE PURPOSE OF RECEIVING IN JOINT MEETING HIS EXCELLENCY 
                  JACQUES CHIRAC, PRESIDENT OF FRANCE

  Mr. COBLE. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that it may be in 
order at any time on Thursday, February 1, 1996, for the Speaker to 
declare a recess, subject to the call of the Chair, for the purpose of 
receiving in joint meeting His Excellency Jacques Chirac, President of 
France.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from North Carolina?
  There was no objection.
```
