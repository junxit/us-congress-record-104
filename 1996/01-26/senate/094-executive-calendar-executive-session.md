# EXECUTIVE CALENDAR (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Dole, Robert J. (D000401) [R-KS]

                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mr. DOLE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
immediately proceed to executive session to consider the following 
nominations: Executive Calendar nominations Nos. 346, 347, 397, and all 
nominations reported out of the Armed Services Committee today with the 
exception of Admiral Prueher; I further ask unanimous consent that the 
nominations be considered en bloc, the motions to reconsider be laid 
upon the table en bloc, that any statements relating to the nominations 
appear at the appropriate place in the Record, that the President be 
immediately notified of the Senate's action, and that the Senate then 
return to legislative session.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  So the nominations were considered and confirmed, en bloc, as 
follows:
