# NOTICE OF HEARING

**Speaking:** Murkowski, Frank H. (M001085) [R-AK]

```
                           NOTICE OF HEARING


               committee on energy and natural resources

  Mr. MURKOWSKI. Mr. President, I would like to announce for the 
information of the Senate and the public that a hearing has been 
scheduled before the Committee on Energy and Natural Resources to 
receive testimony on the issue of U.S. climate change policy.
  The hearing will take place on Tuesday, September 17, beginning at 
9:30 a.m. in room 366 of the Dirksen Senate Office Building.
  Those who wish to testify or submit written statements should write 
to the Committee on Energy and Natural Resources, U.S. Senate, 
Washington, DC 20510. For further information, please contact David 
Garman at (202) 224-8115.

                          ____________________
```
