# THE VERY BAD DEBT BOXSCORE

**Speaking:** Helms, Jesse (H000463) [R-NC]

```
                       THE VERY BAD DEBT BOXSCORE

  Mr. HELMS. Mr. President, at the close of business Friday, August 30, 
the Federal debt stood at $5,208,303,439,417.93.
  On a per capita basis, every man, woman, and child in America owes 
$19,607.09 as his or her share of that debt.
```
