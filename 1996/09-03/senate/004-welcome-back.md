# WELCOME BACK

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

```
                              WELCOME BACK

  Mr. LOTT. Welcome back, Mr. President. I hope you had a restful 
August period and time for renewal, as well as perhaps a little 
campaigning. I want to thank the Chaplain for opening the session in 
the way he has this morning, and extend a welcome back to my colleague, 
the Democratic leader, this morning, and hope he had time for rest and 
renewal, too, in addition to the conventions we both had to attend. I 
look forward to working with him during the next month or so.
```
