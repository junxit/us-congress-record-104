**Speaking:** Lightfoot, Jim (L000305) [R-IA]

**Measures:** H.R. 1530, H.R. 2076

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______


                           HON. JIM LIGHTFOOT

                                of iowa

                    in the house of representatives

                       Wednesday, January 3, 1996

  Mr. LIGHTFOOT. Speaker, due to my son's hospitalization I was unable 
to be present and voting on January 3, 1996.

of H.R. 1530, the National Defense Authorization Act and in favor of
```
