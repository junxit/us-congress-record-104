# EXTENSION OF MOST-FAVORED-NATION TREATMENT TO PRODUCTS OF BULGARIA

**Speaking:** Obey, David R. (O000007) [D-WI]

**Measures:** H.R. 1643

```
   EXTENSION OF MOST-FAVORED-NATION TREATMENT TO PRODUCTS OF BULGARIA

  Mr. OBEY. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent to take from the 
Speaker's table the bill (H.R. 1643) to authorize the extension of 
nondiscriminatory treatment (most-favored-nation treatment) to the 
products of Bulgaria in order to permit the immediate consideration in 
the House of the Senate amendment to the House amendment that is the 
Dole appropriation.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Under the guidelines consistently issued by 
successive Speakers as recorded on page 534 of the House Rules Manual, 
the Chair is constrained not to entertain the gentleman's request until 
it has been cleared by the bipartisan floor and committee leaderships.

                          ____________________
```
