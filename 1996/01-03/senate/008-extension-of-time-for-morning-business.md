# EXTENSION OF TIME FOR MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Inhofe, James M. (I000024) [R-OK]

```
                 EXTENSION OF TIME FOR MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Coats). The Chair now wishes to advise the 
Senate under the previous order there was now to be a period for the 
transaction of morning business not to exceed beyond the hour of 12:30 
p.m., with Senators permitted to speak therein for not to exceed 5 
minutes each.
  Mr. INHOFE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent the period for 
morning business be extended and the time allowed to each Member be 
extended to 10 minutes.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
