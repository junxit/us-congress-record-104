# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Rev. James David Ford, D.D., offered the following 
prayer:
  Be with us, O God, all the day long, and may Your mercies ever 
surround us. May we never leave the brightness of Your presence, and 
may the glory of Your blessings touch us in all we do. Be with us this 
day, and may the bounty of Your glory bless every person, now and 
evermore. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
