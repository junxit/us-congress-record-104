# THE CALENDAR

**Speaking:** Cohen, William S. (C000598) [R-ME]

**Measures:** S. 811, H.R. 1743, H.R. 2243

```
                              THE CALENDAR

  Mr. COHEN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the immediate consideration of Calendar No. 366, H.R. 1743, 
Calendar No. 367, H.R. 2243, and Calendar No. 375, S. 811, en bloc; 
further, I ask unanimous consent that reported amendments to the text, 
as may appear, be agreed to, the bills be deemed read a third time, 
passed, the motions to reconsider be laid upon the table, en bloc, and 
that any statements relating to these measures be placed at the 
appropriate place in the Record.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
