# NATIONAL CORRECTIONAL OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES WEEK

**Speaking:** Cohen, William S. (C000598) [R-ME]

**Measures:** S.Res. 243

```
           NATIONAL CORRECTIONAL OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES WEEK

  Mr. COHEN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Judiciary 
Committee be discharged from further consideration of S. Res. 243, 
designating ``National Correctional Officers and Employees Week,'' and 
that the Senate then proceed to its immediate consideration.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered. The clerk 
will report.
  The assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A resolution (S. Res. 243) designating the week of May 5, 
     1996 as ``National Correctional Officers and Employees 
     Week.''

  The Senate proceeded to consider the resolution.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, the resolution is agreed to 
and the preamble is agreed to.
  The resolution (S. Res. 243) was agreed to.
  The preamble was agreed to.
  [The text of the resolution will appear in a future issue of the 
Record.]
  Mr. COHEN. Mr. President, I move to reconsider the vote by which the 
resolution was agreed to and I move to lay that motion on the table.
  The motion to lay on the table was agreed to.

                          ____________________
```
