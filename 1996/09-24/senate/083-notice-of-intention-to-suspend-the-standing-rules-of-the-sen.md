# NOTICE OF INTENTION TO SUSPEND THE STANDING RULES OF THE SENATE

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

**Measures:** H.R. 3610

```
    NOTICE OF INTENTION TO SUSPEND THE STANDING RULES OF THE SENATE

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, pursuant to rule 5, paragraph 1 of the 
Standing Rules of the Senate, I hereby give written notice to suspend 
rule 28 of the Standing Rules of the Senate, titles 3 and 6 of the 
Budget Act and all provisions of the budget resolutions for 
consideration of the conference report to accompany H.R. 3610, the DOD 
appropriations bill.

                          ____________________
```
