# ADJOURNMENT

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

```
                              ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I now ask unanimous consent the Senate stand 
in adjournment for 1 minute and, upon reconvening, the Journal of 
proceedings be deemed approved to date, the morning hour be deemed to 
have expired, and the time for the two leaders be reserved for their 
use later in the day.
  There being no objection, at 6:36 p.m., the Senate adjourned until 
6:37 p.m. the same day.
  The Senate met at 6:37 p.m., and was called to order by the Honorable 
Mike DeWine, a Senator from the State of Ohio.
```
