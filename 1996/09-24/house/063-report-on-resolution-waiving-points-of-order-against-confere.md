# REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING POINTS OF ORDER AGAINST CONFERENCE REPORT ON H.R. 3259, INTELLIGENCE AUTHORIZATION ACT FOR FISCAL YEAR 1997

**Measures:** H.Res. 529, H.R. 3259

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING POINTS OF ORDER AGAINST CONFERENCE REPORT 
   ON H.R. 3259, INTELLIGENCE AUTHORIZATION ACT FOR FISCAL YEAR 1997

  Mr. QUILLEN, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 104-830) on the resolution (H. Res. 529) waiving 
points of order against the conference report to accompany the bill 
(H.R. 3259) to authorize appropriations for fiscal year 1997 for 
intelligence and intelligence-related activities of the U.S. 
Government, the Community Management Account, and the Central 
Intelligence Agency Retirement and Disability System, and for other 
purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be 
printed.
```
