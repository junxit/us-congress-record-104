# THE VERY BAD DEBT BOXSCORE

**Speaking:** Helms, Jesse (H000463) [R-NC]

```
                       THE VERY BAD DEBT BOXSCORE

  Mr. HELMS. Mr. President, at the close of business yesterday, 
Wednesday, September 25, the Federal debt stood at 
$5,198,780,826,934.47.
  One year ago, September 25, 1995, the Federal debt stood at 
$4,949,969,000,000.
  Five years ago, September 25, 1991, the Federal debt stood at 
$3,630,755,000,000.
  Ten years ago, September 25, 1986, the Federal debt stood at 
$2,109,249,000,000.
  Fifteen years ago, September 25, 1981, the Federal debt stood at 
$979,210,000,000. This reflects an increase of more than $4 trillion 
($4,319,570,826,934.47) during the 15 years from 1981 to 1996.

                          ____________________
```
