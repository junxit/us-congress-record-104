# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 145, H.Con.Res. 189, H.Res. 535, H.R. 2092, H.R. 3456, H.R. 3852, H.R. 4137

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 5, rule I, the Chair will 
now put the question on each motion to suspend the rules on which 
further proceedings were postponed yesterday and today in the order in 
which that motion was entertained.
  Votes will be taken in the following order: H.R. 3852, by the yeas 
and nays: H.R. 4137, by the yeas and nays; H.R. 3456, by the yeas and 
nays; H.R. 2092, by the yeas and nays; House Resolution 535, by the 
yeas and nays; House Concurrent Resolution 145, by the yeas and nays; 
and House Concurrent Resolution 189, by the yeas and nays.
  The Chair will reduce to 5 minutes the time for any electronic vote 
after the first such vote in this series.

                          ____________________
```
