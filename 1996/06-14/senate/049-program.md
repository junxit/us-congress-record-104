# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Mack, Connie, III (M000019) [R-FL]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. MACK. For the information of all Senators, the Senate will begin 
consideration of the DOD authorization bill on Tuesday. Senators may 
give opening statements on the bill beginning at 10 a.m.; however, no 
amendments will be in order prior to 2:15 on Tuesday. Also, the Senate 
will recess from the hour of 12:30 until 2:15 p.m. for the weekly 
policy conferences to meet. As a reminder, the Senate will resume 
debate on the Greenspan nomination on Thursday, June 20, with a vote to 
occur on the nomination at 2 p.m. on that day.

                          ____________________
```
