# RECOGNIZING TRENT EDWARDS

**Speaking:** Baker, Bill (B000078) [R-CA]

```
                       RECOGNIZING TRENT EDWARDS

                                 ______


                            HON. BILL BAKER

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                          Friday, May 10, 1996

  Mr. BAKER of California. Mr. Speaker, on June 23, 1995, Victoria A. 
Olivarez was in a terrible accident that left her extended through the 
windshield of her van. As the van became engulfed in flames, a man 
named Trent Edwards stopped to help. Trent is from Pleasanton, CA, a 
lovely community in my district. He is a retired technical school 
employee.
  With utter disregard for his personal safety, Trent attempted to drag 
Ms. Olivarez through her windshield. Joined by another man, Trent was 
ultimately successful in bringing Ms. Olivarez to safety. Although 
taken to the hospital, she tragically died several hours later while 
being treated for her injuries.
  Trent Edwards was hospitalized, as well. He suffered first- and 
second-degree burns to his face, head, and arms from the scorching heat 
of the burning van. One thing I failed to mention earlier: Trent 
Edwards is 80 years old.
  For his remarkable act, Trent has been awarded a Carnegie Medal for 
Extraordinary Heroism by the Carnegie Hero Fund Commission of 
Pittsburgh, PA. Accompanying this award was a $2,500 grant and the 
recognition due an act of undaunted courage.
  In my term as a public servant, I have seldom come across any 
American more richly deserving of the thanks of our country than Trent 
Edwards. His bravery and decency are an example for all of us. I am 
honored to bring him to the attention of my colleagues in the 
Congressional Record, and am proud to count him as a constituent and 
fellow citizen of our great country.

                          ____________________
```
