# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Horn, Stephen (H000789) [R-CA]

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. STEPHEN HORN

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, July 24, 1996

  Mr. HORN. Mr. Speaker, on rollcall No. 347, the Allard amendment, 
which would eliminate $5 million in appropriations for the Technology 
Administration which develops and promotes politics and programs that 
facilitate private sector innovations, I was unavoidably detained on 
official business with staff members of the Subcommittee on Government 
Management, Information, and Technology.
```
