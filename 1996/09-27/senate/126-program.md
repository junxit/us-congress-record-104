# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Nickles, Don (N000102) [R-OK]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. NICKLES. Following morning business on Saturday, the Senate will 
be awaiting House action on an omnibus appropriations bill, if produced 
from negotiations. The Senate may also be asked to turn to 
consideration of any other items cleared for action. Rollcall votes are 
therefore possible throughout the day on Saturday. The leadership will 
attempt to give adequate notice to Members in the event that rollcall 
votes prove to be necessary.
```
