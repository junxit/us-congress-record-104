# ELECTING GARY LEE SISCO AS SECRETARY OF THE SENATE

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

**Measures:** S.Res. 307

```
           ELECTING GARY LEE SISCO AS SECRETARY OF THE SENATE

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I send a resolution to the desk electing 
Gary Lee Sisco as Secretary of the Senate.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. The clerk will report.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A resolution (S. Res. 307) electing Gary Lee Sisco of 
     Tennessee as Secretary of the Senate.

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the resolution 
be considered, agreed to, and the motion to reconsider be laid upon the 
table.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The resolution (S. Res. 307) was considered and agreed to, as 
follows:

                              S. Res. 307

       Resolved, That Gary Lee Sisco of Tennessee be and he is 
     hereby elected Secretary of the Senate.

                          ____________________
```
