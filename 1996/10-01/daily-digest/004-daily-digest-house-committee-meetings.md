# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
ADMINISTRATION'S DRUG POLICY
Committee on Government Reform and Oversight: Subcommittee on National 
Security, International Affairs and Criminal Justice held a hearing on 
Review of Internal Administration Study Critical of the 
Administration's Drug Policy, and White House Suppression of Study. 
Testimony was heard from Gen. Barry McCaffrey, USA, (Ret.), Director, 
Office of National Drug Control Policy; Adm. Robert E. Kramek, USCG, 
Commandant, U.S. Coast Guard, Department of Transportation and U.S. 
Interdiction Coordinator; and public witnesses.
```
