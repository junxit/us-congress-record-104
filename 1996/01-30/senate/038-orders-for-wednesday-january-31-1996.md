# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, JANUARY 31, 1996

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

```
                 ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, JANUARY 31, 1996

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it stand in adjournment until the hour of 
11 a.m., Wednesday, January 31; further, that immediately following the 
prayer, the Journal of the proceedings be deemed approved to date, no 
resolutions come over under the rule, the call of the calendar be 
dispensed with, the morning hour be deemed to have expired, the time 
for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and 
that there then be a period for morning business until the hour of 1 
p.m., with the time equally divided between the two parties, and the 
time consumed in quorum calls during the morning business period be 
equally divided.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
