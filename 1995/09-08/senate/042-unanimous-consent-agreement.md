# UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT

**Speaking:** Santorum, Rick (S000059) [R-PA]

```
                      UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT

  Mr. SANTORUM. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent following the 
third rollcall vote on Monday, the Senate resume consideration of the 
Feinstein amendment, No. 2469, there be 30 minutes to be equally 
divided between Senators Hutchison and Feinstein, and that the vote 
occur on or in relation to that amendment following the conclusion or 
yielding back of time. 

[[Page S 13139]]

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
