# THE BAD DEBT BOXSCORE

**Speaking:** Helms, Jesse (H000463) [R-NC]

```
                         THE BAD DEBT BOXSCORE

  Mr. HELMS. Mr. President, as of the close of business yesterday, 
September 7, the Federal debt stood at $4,968,651,845,437.79. On a per 
capita basis, every man, woman, and child in America owes $18,861.09 as 
his or her share of that debt.
```
