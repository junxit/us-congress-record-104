# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Bond, Christopher S. (B000611) [R-MO]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. BOND. Mr. President, for the information of all Senators, the 
Senate will resume consideration of VA-HUD operations appropriations 
bill tomorrow morning at 9:30. Under the previous order, there will be 
a rollcall vote at approximately 2:20 on or in relation to the Bumpers 
space station amendment. Additional rollcall votes can be expected 
throughout Tuesday's session, day and night, in order to finish action 
on the VA-HUD appropriations bill.
  As a reminder, the majority leader has announced once all the 
necessary appropriations items are completed the Senate would then 
stand in recess until Tuesday, October 10.

                          ____________________
```
