```
 REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 4, PERSONAL 
                       RESPONSIBILITY ACT OF 1995

report (Rept. No. 104-83) on the resolution (H. Res. 117) providing for
```
