# ADJOURNMENT TO TUESDAY, MARCH 21, 1995

**Speaking:** Schroeder, Patricia (S000142) [D-CO]

```
                 ADJOURNMENT TO TUESDAY, MARCH 21, 1995
  Mrs. SCHROEDER. Mr. Speaker, I move that the House do now adjourn.
  The motion was agreed to.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to the provisions of House 
Concurrent Resolution 41 of the 104th Congress, the House stands 
adjourned until 12:30 p.m., Tuesday, March 21, 1995 for morning hour 
debates.
  Thereupon (at 4 o'clock and 29 minutes p.m), pursuant to the 
provisions of House Concurrent Resolution 41, the House adjourned until 
Tuesday, March 21, 1995, at 12:30 p.m.
```
