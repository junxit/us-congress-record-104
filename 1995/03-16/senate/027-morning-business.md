# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Dole, Robert J. (D000401) [R-KS]

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. DOLE. Mr. President, I ask that there be a period for morning 
business with Members permitted to speak therein for an indefinite 
time, unless there is some agreement on equal time. I think Senator 
McCain wants to speak for a couple of hours.
  Mr. President, was leader time reserved?
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. It was.
  Mr. DOLE. I ask unanimous consent that I may use part of my leader's 
time.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
