# RESERVATION OF LEADER TIME

**Speaking:** Craig, Larry E. (C000858) [R-ID]

**Measures:** H.R. 889

```
                       RESERVATION OF LEADER TIME

  Mr. CRAIG. Mr. President, this morning, the leader time has been 
reserved.


                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. CRAIG. Mr. President, the majority leader has indicated that the 
Senate will resume consideration of H.R. 889, the supplemental 
appropriations bill, if an agreement can be reached with respect to a 
limited number of amendments. Senators should, therefore, be aware that 
rollcall votes are expected throughout today's session.
```
