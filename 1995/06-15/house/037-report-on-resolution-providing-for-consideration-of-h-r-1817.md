# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 1817, MILITARY CONSTRUCTION APPROPRIATIONS ACT FOR FISCAL YEAR 1996

**Measures:** H.Res. 167, H.R. 1817

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 1817, MILITARY 
          CONSTRUCTION APPROPRIATIONS ACT FOR FISCAL YEAR 1996

  Mr. QUILLEN, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 104-140) on the resolution (H. Res. 167) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 1817) making appropriations for

ending September 30, 1996, and for other purposes, which was referred
```
