# PERMISSION TO FILE PRIVILEGED REPORT ON BILL MAKING APPROPRIATIONS FOR FOREIGN OPERATIONS, EXPORT FINANCING, AND RELATED PROGRAMS FOR FISCAL YEAR 1996

**Speaking:** Callahan, Sonny (C000052) [R-AL]

```
PERMISSION TO FILE PRIVILEGED REPORT ON BILL MAKING APPROPRIATIONS FOR 
 FOREIGN OPERATIONS, EXPORT FINANCING, AND RELATED PROGRAMS FOR FISCAL 
                               YEAR 1996

  Mr. CALLAHAN. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the Committee

report on a bill making appropriations for foreign operations, export 
financing, and related programs for the fiscal year ending September 
30, 1996, and for other purposes.

gentleman from Alabama?
```
