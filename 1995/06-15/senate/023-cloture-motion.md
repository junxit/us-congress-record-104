# CLOTURE MOTION

**Speaking:** Chafee, John H. (C000269) [R-RI]

**Measures:** S. 440

```
                             CLOTURE MOTION

  Mr. CHAFEE. Mr. President, I send a cloture motion to the desk that 
is signed by 16 Senators.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:


                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     hereby move to bring to a close debate on the motion to 
     proceed to Calendar Number 114, S. 440, the National Highway 
     System bill, signed by 16 Senators.
         Bob Dole, Lauch Faircloth, Larry Pressler, Rod Grams, Don 
           Nickles, Robert F. Bennett, Craig Thomas, James M. 
           Inhofe, Pete V. Domenici, John W. Warner, Hank Brown, 
           John Chafee, Christopher Bond, Kay Bailey Hutchison, 
           Bob Smith, and Dirk Kempthorne.

                          ____________________
```
