# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Pressler, Larry (P000513) [R-SD]

**Measures:** S. 652

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. PRESSLER. This morning, the leader time has been reserved, and 
the Senate will immediately resume consideration of S. 652, the 
telecommunications bill. Under the consent agreement from last night, 
there are approximately nine amendments that are still pending to the 
telecommunications bill. Members should be on notice that at 12:15 the 
Senate will begin a series of rollcall votes on or in relation to those 
pending amendments with the last vote in the order being on final 
passage.
  The Senate is open for business. We welcome Senators to come to the 
floor to make their speeches and deal with their amendments.
```
