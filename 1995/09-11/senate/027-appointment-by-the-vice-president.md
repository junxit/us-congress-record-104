# APPOINTMENT BY THE VICE PRESIDENT

```
                   APPOINTMENT BY THE VICE PRESIDENT

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair, on behalf of the Vice President, 
pursuant to title 46, section 1295(b), of the United States Code, as 
amended by Public Law 101-595, appoints the following Senators to the 
Board of Visitors of the U.S. Merchant Marine Academy: the Senator from 
Louisiana [Mr. Breaux], from the Committee on Commerce, Science, and 
Transportation; and the Senator from Hawaii [Mr. Inouye], at large.
```
