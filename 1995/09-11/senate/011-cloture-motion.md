# CLOTURE MOTION

**Speaking:** Dole, Robert J. (D000401) [R-KS]

**Measures:** H.R. 4

```
                             CLOTURE MOTION

  Mr. DOLE. Mr. President, I send a cloture motion to the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The cloture motion having been presented under 
rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

  We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the provisions of 
rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, do hereby move to bring 
to a close debate on the Dole substitute amendment to H.R. 4, the 
welfare reform bill.

         Bob Packwood, Hank Brown, Bob Dole, Paul D. Coverdell, 
           Conrad Burns, Don Nickles, Trent Lott, Bill Roth, Rick 
           Santorum, Ted Stevens, Pete V. Domenici, Robert F. 
           Bennett, Mike
```
