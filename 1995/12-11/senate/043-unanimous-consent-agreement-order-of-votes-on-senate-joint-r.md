# UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT--ORDER OF VOTES ON SENATE JOINT RESOLUTION 31

**Speaking:** Hatch, Orrin G. (H000338) [R-UT]

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 31

```
UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT--ORDER OF VOTES ON SENATE JOINT RESOLUTION 
                                   31

  Mr. HATCH. Mr. President, when the Senate reconvenes at 2:15 p.m., 
there will be 2 minutes of debate, followed by up to five consecutive 
rollcall votes relating to Senate Joint Resolution 31.
  I ask unanimous consent that those votes occur in the order in which 
they were offered.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
