# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there will now 
be a period for the transaction of morning business not to extend 
beyond the hour of 1 p.m., with Senators permitted to speak therein for 
up to 10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Kyl). The distinguished Senator from 
Nevada is recognized.
```
