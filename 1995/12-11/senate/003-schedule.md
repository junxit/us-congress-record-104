# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Kyl, Jon (K000352) [R-AZ]

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 31

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. KYL. Mr. President, on behalf of the leader I would like to make 
the following announcement: Today there will be a period for morning 
business until the hour of 1 p.m. At 1 p.m., the Senate will resume 
consideration of Senate Joint Resolution 31, the constitutional 
amendment regarding the desecration of the U.S. flag.
  Under the provisions of the consent agreement reached on Friday, 
amendments will be offered and debated today, however no rollcall votes 
will occur during today's session. Any votes ordered on the amendments 
will be stacked to begin at 2:15, Tuesday afternoon.

                          ____________________
```
