# The House met at 10 a.m.

```
   [[Page H2395]] The House met at 10 a.m.


                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Rev. James David Ford, D.D., offered the following 
prayer:
  Teach us, O gracious God, to be responsive to the prayers and 
blessings and support that other people share with us. When we truly 
examine our lives, we see how those about us have favored us with both 
material and spiritual gifts and we, too, often only accept the gift 
and never offer our appreciation to the giver. Remind us always, O God, 
to be grateful for the support and advocacy of other people in our 
daily lives so we will respond with a true spirit of thanksgiving. 
Amen.

                          ____________________
```
