# REMOVAL OF NAME OF MEMBER AS COSPONSOR OF HOUSE JOINT RESOLUTION 2

**Speaking:** McIntosh, David M. (M000481) [R-IN]

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 2

```
   REMOVAL OF NAME OF MEMBER AS COSPONSOR OF HOUSE JOINT RESOLUTION 2

  Mr. McINTOSH. Mr. Speaker, as the language of joint resolution, House 
Joint Resolution 2 has been substantially altered in markup, I ask 
unanimous consent to have my name removed as a cosponsor of the 
legislation.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Indiana?
  There was no objection.
```
