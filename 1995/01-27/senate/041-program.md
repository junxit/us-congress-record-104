# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Grassley, Chuck (G000386) [R-IA], Nunn, Sam (N000171) [D-GA]

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 1

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. GRASSLEY. Mr. President, for the information, then, of all 
Senators, at 2 p.m. on Monday, the Senate will begin consideration of 
House Joint Resolution 1, and that is the balanced budget amendment. 
That is an amendment to the Constitution. For that day it will be 
debate only.
  For the information of all of my colleagues, there will be no 
rollcall votes during Monday's session of the Senate.
  I now ask, Mr. President, unanimous consent that at the completion of 
the remarks of the distinguished minority leader, the Senate stand in 
adjournment under the previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. NUNN. Mr. President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. NUNN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
