# RECESS UNTIL TOMORROW AT 10 A.M.

**Speaking:** Hatch, Orrin G. (H000338) [R-UT]

```
                    RECESS UNTIL TOMORROW AT 10 A.M.

  Mr. HATCH. Mr. President, if there is no further business to come 
before the Senate, and no other Senator is seeking recognition, I ask 
unanimous consent that the Senate stand in recess under the previous
```
