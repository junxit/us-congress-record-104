# APPRECIATION FOR RABBI JOSHUA HABERMAN

**Speaking:** Hatfield, Mark O. (H000343) [R-OR]

```
                 APPRECIATION FOR RABBI JOSHUA HABERMAN

  Mr. HATFIELD. I thank Rabbi Joshua Haberman for his willingness to 
come this week and open our Senate with a prayer. He is the rabbi of 
one of the largest congregations here in the Washington, DC, district. 
I am very grateful, and I know on behalf of the leadership of both 
sides we wish to express our deep gratitude to the rabbi.
```
