# ORDER OF PROCEDURE

**Speaking:** McCain, John (M000303) [R-AZ]

```
                           ORDER OF PROCEDURE

  Mr. McCAIN. For the information of my colleagues, although the 
cloture vote on the majority leader's substitute amendment will occur 
at 6 p.m. tomorrow, other amendments will be offered throughout the 
day. Therefore, rollcall votes can be expected. The Senate has reached 
an agreement with respect to the Bradley amendment for a total of 45 
minutes beginning at 10:30 a.m.; therefore, a vote can be expected 
prior to 12 noon.
```
