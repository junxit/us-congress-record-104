# CLOTURE MOTION

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

**Measures:** S. 395

```
                             CLOTURE MOTION

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I send a cloture motion to the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The cloture motion having been presented under 
rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.
  The assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We the undersigned Senators in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on Calendar No. 
     101, S. 395, Alaska Power Administration bill.
         Frank H. Murkowski, Hank Brown, Jon Kyl, Conrad Burns, 
           Thad Cochran, Larry Pressler, Pete V. Domenici, Strom 
           Thurmond, Ted Stevens, Trent Lott, Rod Grams, Dirk 
           Kempthorne, Craig Thomas, Bill Frist, Dan Coats, Orrin 
           Hatch.

                          ____________________
```
