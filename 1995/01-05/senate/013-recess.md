# RECESS

**Speaking:** Grassley, Chuck (G000386) [R-IA]

```
                                 RECESS

  Mr. GRASSLEY. Mr. President, this request is from the floor leader. I 
ask unanimous consent that the Senate stand in recess from 3:15 p.m. 
until 4 p.m. today.
  There being no objection, at 3:15 p.m., the Senate recessed until 4 
p.m.; whereupon, the Senate reassembled when called to order by the 
Presiding Officer (Mrs. Hutchison).
```
