# THE DEATH OF DR. ARCHIE H. CARMICHAEL

**Speaking:** Heflin, Howell (H000445) [D-AL]

```
                 THE DEATH OF DR. ARCHIE H. CARMICHAEL
  Mr. HEFLIN. Mr. President, I rise for a point of personal privilege 
to lament the death yesterday of Dr. Archie H. Carmichael III, of 
Tuscumbia, Sheffield, and Muscle Shoals, AL. He was a very 
distinguished physician. He was an internist. Dr. Carmichael graduated 
from Vanderbilt Medical School and practiced for many years in the 
Shoals area of Alabama. His grandfather, Archie H. Carmichael, served 
as a Member of Congress. He comes from a very distinguished family in 
Alabama. It is sad that he has passed away.
  At some later date, I will have more to say about Dr. Carmichael.
  Mr. COCHRAN addressed the Chair.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. The distinguished Senator from 
Mississippi.

                          ____________________
```
