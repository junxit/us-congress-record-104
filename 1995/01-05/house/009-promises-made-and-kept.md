# PROMISES MADE AND KEPT

**Speaking:** Solomon, Gerald B. H. (S000675) [R-NY]

```
                         PROMISES MADE AND KEPT

  (Mr. SOLOMON asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. SOLOMON. Mr. Speaker, yesterday was a great success. It was an 
exciting day for this House and for the American people.
  Our opening day was about promises made and about promises kept, and 
we began to keep those promises yesterday. We fulfilled the first part 
of our Contract With America with a bipartisan coalition, and we thank 
you on that side of the aisle, because it means we are going to work 
together. We look forward to continuing to work in a bipartisan fashion 
with our Democratic colleagues on the other side of the aisle to pass 
the rest of this contract for 
[[Page H128]] America, which is going to bring back the Reagan 
revolution once again.

                          ____________________
```
