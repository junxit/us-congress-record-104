# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Rev. James David Ford, D.D., offered the following 
prayer:
  We are grateful, O God, that Your spirit is with us wherever we are 
and is sufficient for all our needs. If we are on the mountaintop full 
of joy and anticipation, You are there in our jubilation, and if we 
walk through the valley of the shadow of death, You support us with 
Your strong and abiding grace. May our spirits be open to Your spirit, 
and our wills to Your will, that we will walk the path of life with 
confidence and with peace.
  In Your name we pray. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
