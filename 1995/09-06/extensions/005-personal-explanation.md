# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Martinez, Matthew G. (M000206) [D-CA]

**Measures:** S. 21

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______


                        HON. MATTHEW G. MARTINEZ

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, September 6, 1995
  Mr. MARTINEZ. Mr. Speaker, although I intended to vote for S. 21, the 
Bosnia and Herzegovina Self-Defense Act, on August 1, 1995, my vote was 
recorded in the negative. As my voting record reflects, I have 
consistently supported all efforts to lift the arms embargo on Bosnia 
and Herzegovina.
```
