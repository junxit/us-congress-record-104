# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Grassley, Chuck (G000386) [R-IA]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. GRASSLEY. Madam President, for the information of all Senators, 
the Senate will resume consideration of the welfare reform bill at 
10:30 a.m. tomorrow. Under a previous order, there will be a rollcall 
vote on the Daschle amendment No. 2282, as modified, at 4 p.m. 
tomorrow. The vote on the Daschle amendment will be the first vote of 
Thursday's session. However, rollcall votes are expected thereafter on 
other amendments on the welfare reform bill, and a late night session 
is expected in order to make substantial progress on that bill.

                          ____________________
```
