# RECESS

**Speaking:** Moynihan, Daniel Patrick (M001054) [D-NY], Santorum, Rick (S000059) [R-PA]

```
                                 RECESS

  Mr. MOYNIHAN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
stand in recess for a period of 20 minutes.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 6:15 p.m., recessed until 
6:33 p.m.; whereupon, the Senate reassembled when called to order by 
the Presiding Officer [Mr. DeWine].
  Mr. SANTORUM addressed the Chair.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator from Pennsylvania.
  Mr. SANTORUM. Mr. President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
```
