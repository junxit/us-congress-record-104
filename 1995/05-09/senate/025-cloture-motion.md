# CLOTURE MOTION

**Measures:** H.R. 956

```
                             CLOTURE MOTION

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the cloture motion 
having been presented under rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to 
read the motion.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We the undersigned Senators in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the pending 
     substitute amendment to H.R. 956, the Product Liability bill.
         Slade Gorton, Dan Coats, Richard G. Lugar, John Ashcroft, 
           Rod Grams, Kay Bailey Hutchison, Judd Gregg, Strom 
           Thurmond, Trent Lott, Rick Santorum, Larry E. Craig, 
           Bob Smith, Don Nickles, R.F. Bennett, John McCain, 
           Connie Mack.

                          ____________________
```
