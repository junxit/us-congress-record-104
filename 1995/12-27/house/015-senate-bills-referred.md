# SENATE BILLS REFERRED

**Measures:** S. 1507, S. 1509

```
                         SENATE BILLS REFERRED

  Bills of the Senate of the following titles were taken from the 
Speaker's table and, under the rule, referred as follows:

       S. 1507. An act to provide for the extension of the Parole 
     Commission to oversee cases of prisoners sentenced under 
     prior law, to reduce the size of the Parole Commission, and 
     for other purposes; to the Committee on the Judiciary.
       S. 1509. An act to amend the Impact Aid program to provide 
     for a hold-harmless with respect to amounts for payments 
     relating to 

[[Page H15639]]
     the Federal acquisition of real property, to permit certain local 
     educational agencies to apply for increased payments for 
     fiscal year 1994 under the Impact Aid program, and to amend 
     the Impact Aid program to make a technical correction with 
     respect to maximum payments for certain heavily impacted 
     local educational agencies; to the Committee on Economic and 
     Educational Opportunities.

                          ____________________
```
