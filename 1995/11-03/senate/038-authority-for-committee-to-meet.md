# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEE TO MEET

**Speaking:** Dole, Robert J. (D000401) [R-KS]

```
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEE TO MEET


           Committee on commerce, science, and transportation

  Mr. DOLE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Committee 
on Commerce, Science, and Transportation be allowed to meet during the 
Friday, November 3, 1995, session of the Senate for the purpose of 
conducting a hearing on the nominations of S. Jane Bobbitt, to be 
Assistant Secretary for Legislative and Intergovernmental Affairs at 
the Department of Commerce; Charles A. Hunnicutt, to be Assistant 
Secretary for International Aviation at the Department of 
Transportation; and Nancy E. McFadden, to be general counsel of the 
Department of Transportation.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
