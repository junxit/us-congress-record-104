# Daily Digest/Senate Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
(Committees not listed did not meet)
NOMINATIONS
Committee on Commerce, Science, and Transportation: Committee concluded 
hearings on the nomination of Nancy E. McFadden, of California, to be 
General Counsel, and Charles A. Hunnicutt, of Georgia, to be an 
Assistant Secretary, both of the Department of Transportation, and Jane 
Bobbitt, of West Virginia, to be an Assistant Secretary of Commerce, 
after the nominees testified and answered questions in their own 
behalf. Ms. McFadden was introduced by Senator Mikulski, Mr. Hunnicutt 
was introduced by Senator Coverdell, and Ms. Bobbitt was introduced by 
Senators Byrd and Rockefeller, and Rep. Mollohan.
```
