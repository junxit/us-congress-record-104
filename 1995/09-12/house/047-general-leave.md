# GENERAL LEAVE

**Speaking:** Meyers, Jan (M000684) [R-KS]

**Measures:** H.R. 1594

```
                             GENERAL LEAVE

  Mrs. MEYERS of Kansas. Mr. Speaker, on behalf of the gentleman from 
Illinois [Mr. Fawell], I ask unanimous consent that all Members may 
have 5 legislative days within which to revise and extend their remarks 
on H.R. 1594, to place restrictions on the promotion by the Department 
of Labor and other Federal agencies and instrumentalities of 
economically targeted investments in connection with employee benefit 
plans.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Dickey). Is there objection to the 
request of the gentlewoman from Kansas?
  There was no objection.
```
