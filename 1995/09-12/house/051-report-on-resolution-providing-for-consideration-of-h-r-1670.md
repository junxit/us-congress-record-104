# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 1670, FEDERAL ACQUISITION REFORM ACT OF 1995

**Measures:** H.Res. 219, H.R. 1670

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 1670, FEDERAL 
                     ACQUISITION REFORM ACT OF 1995

  Mr. GOSS, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged report 
(Rept. No. 104-244) on the resolution (H. Res. 219) providing for the 
consideration of the bill (H.R. 1670) to revise and streamline the 
acquisition laws of the Federal Government, to reorganize the 
mechanisms for resolving Federal procurement disputes, and for other 
purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be 
printed.
```
