# COMMUNICATION FROM THE CHAIRMAN OF THE DEMOCRATIC CAUCUS

```
        COMMUNICATION FROM THE CHAIRMAN OF THE DEMOCRATIC CAUCUS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Shays) laid before the House the 
following communication from the Honorable Vic Fazio, chairman of the 
Democratic Caucus:
                                                Democratic Caucus,


                                     House of Representatives,

                                                September 5, 1995.
     Hon. Newt Gingrich,
     Speaker, House of Representatives, Washington, DC.
       Dear Mr. Speaker: This is to inform you that Representative 
     W.J. (Billy) Tauzin is no longer a member of the Democratic 
     Caucus.
           Sincerely,
                                                        Vic Fazio,
                                                         Chairman.
     

                          ____________________
```
