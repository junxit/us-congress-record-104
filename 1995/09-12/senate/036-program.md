# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Hutchison, Kay Bailey (H001016) [R-TX]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mrs. HUTCHISON. Mr. President, for the information of all Senators, 
the Senate will resume consideration of the welfare reform bill 
tomorrow morning. Under a previous consent agreement, there will be a 
rollcall vote at 9:10 a.m. on or in relation to the Moseley-Braun 
amendment No. 2471. Following that vote, there will be a lengthy series 
of rollcall votes on amendments with a minimal amount of debate time 
between each vote. All Members, therefore, can expect a large number of 
rollcall votes during Wednesday's session of the Senate beginning at 
9:10 a.m.

                          ____________________
```
