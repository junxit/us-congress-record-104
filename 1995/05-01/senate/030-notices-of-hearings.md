```
                          NOTICES OF HEARINGS


                      committee on Indian affairs

  Mr. McCAIN. Mr. President, I would like to announce that the Senate 
Committee on Indian Affairs will be holding

Administration's 7(a) Business Loan Program.
  For further information, please contact Paul Cooksey at 224-5175.
```
