# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

**Measures:** H.R. 655

```
Committee Meetings
HYDROGEN FUTURE ACT
Committee on Rules: Granted, by a voice vote, an open rule providing 
for 1 hour of debate on H.R. 655, Hydrogen Future Act of 1995. The rule 
makes in order the amendment in the nature of a substitute recommended 
by the Committee on Science as an original bill for the purpose of 
amendment and each section of the amendment shall be considered as 
read. Finally, the rule provides one motion to recommit with or without 
instructions. Testimony was heard from Chairman Walker and 
Representative Brown of California.

Joint Meetings
CHECHNYA
  Commission on Security and Cooperation in Europe (Helsinki 
Commission): Commission resumed hearings on the current crisis in 
Chechnya, receiving testimony from Ariel Cohen, Heritage Foundation, 
Washington, D.C.; and Sergei Adamovich Kovalev and Yevgeniya Albats, 
both of Russia.
  Hearings were recessed subject to call.
```
