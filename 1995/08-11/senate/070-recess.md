# RECESS

**Speaking:** Roth Jr., William V. (R000460) [R-DE]

```
                                 RECESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, the Senate stands in recess 
until 3 p.m.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 2:11 p.m., recessed until 
3:01 p.m.; whereupon, the Senate reassembled when called to order by 
the Presiding Officer (Mr. Frist).
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair in his capacity as a Senator from 
Tennessee suggests the absence of a quorum.
  The clerk will call the roll.
  The assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. ROTH. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  

                          ____________________
```
