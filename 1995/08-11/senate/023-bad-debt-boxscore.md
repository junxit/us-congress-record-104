# BAD DEBT BOXSCORE

**Speaking:** Helms, Jesse (H000463) [R-NC]

```
                           BAD DEBT BOXSCORE

  Mr. HELMS. Mr. President, as of the close of business yesterday, 
Thursday 10, the Federal debt stood at $4,943,017,430,508.20. On a per 
capita basis, every man, woman, and child in America owes $18,763.78 as 
his or her share of that debt.
```
