# THE SMALL BUSINESS LENDING ENHANCEMENT ACT OF 1995

**Measures:** S. 895

```
           THE SMALL BUSINESS LENDING ENHANCEMENT ACT OF 1995

                                 ______


                        NUNN AMENDMENT NO. 2426

  Mr. DOLE (for Mr. Nunn) proposed an amendment to the bill (S. 895) to 
amend the Small Business Act to reduce the level of participation by 
the Small Business Administration in certain loans guaranteed by the 
administration, and for other purposes; as follows:

       To amend the Committee substitute; on page 14, add the 
     following new section:

     ``SEC.     . PILOT PREFERRED SURETY BOND GUARANTEE PROGRAM 
                   EXTENSION.

       ``Section 207 of the Small Business Administration 
     Reauthorization and Amendment Act of 1988 (15 U.S.C. 694b 
     note) is amended by striking ``September 30, 1995'' and 
     inserting ``September 30, 1997.''
```
