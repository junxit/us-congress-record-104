# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there will now 
be a period for the transaction of morning business not to extend 
beyond the hour of 10:30 a.m., with the time until 10:30 a.m. under the 
control of the Senator from Idaho [Mr. Craig], or his designee.
  The Senator from Idaho is now recognized.
```
