# ORDER OF PROCEDURE

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS], Daschle, Thomas A. (D000064) [D-SD]

```

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that following the 
conclusion of the minority leader's statement, the Senate stand in 
recess under the previous order.

  The bill clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. DASCHLE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
```
