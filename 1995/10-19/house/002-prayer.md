# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Rev. James David Ford, D.D., offered the following 
prayer:
  We pray, O God, that peace will reign in our world and we specially 
pray that peace will reign in our hearts. We are grateful that even in 
lives that know the tension between the ideals of the mind and the 
reality of an imperfect world there can be a sense of calm, and even 
with great responsibilities that seem to overwhelm there can be 
serenity. Grant to all Your people, O God, the gift of peace and calm 
and serenity, this day and every day, we pray. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
