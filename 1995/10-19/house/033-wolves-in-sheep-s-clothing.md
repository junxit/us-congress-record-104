# WOLVES IN SHEEP'S CLOTHING

**Speaking:** Schroeder, Patricia (S000142) [D-CO]

```
                       WOLVES IN SHEEP'S CLOTHING

  (Mrs. SCHROEDER asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Mrs. SCHROEDER. Mr. Speaker, we have heard of wolves in sheep's 
clothing, and today that is what we see. Today we see all sorts of 
people from the other side of the aisle parade down here and say trust 
the party that fought tooth and nail not to have Medicare 30 years ago, 
but trust them now. Trust the party who had seniors arrested last week 
in this body when they tried to ask questions. Trust the party who has 
this 961 harmless page bill that none of them could pass a test on and 
they have had no hearings on, but trust them.
  There is nothing harmful in here. Trust the party whose leader, 
Speaker Gingrich says the main thing coming out of the session will be 
the tax cut for the rich. That is the crown jewel of this whole session 
and the seniors are going to get the gruel that is what we are doing 
today. The rich get the jewel, they get the gruel, but they keep saying 
trust their party and listen to the trustees. That is wrong.

                          ____________________
```
