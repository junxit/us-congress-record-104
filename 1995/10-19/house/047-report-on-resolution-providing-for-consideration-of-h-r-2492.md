# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2492, LEGISLATIVE BRANCH APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 1996

**Measures:** H.Res. 239, H.R. 2492

```
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2492, 
              LEGISLATIVE BRANCH APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 1996

  Mrs. WALDHOLTZ, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 104-283) on the resolution (H. Res. 239) providing 
for the consideration of the bill (H.R. 2492), making appropriations

1996, and for other purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar 
and ordered to be printed.
```
