# APPOINTMENT OF CONFEREES--H.R. 1617

**Measures:** H.R. 1617

```
                  APPOINTMENT OF CONFEREES--H.R. 1617

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Pursuant to the order of October 11, 1995, the 
Chair appoints the following Senators to serve as conferees on the part 
of the Senate on H.R. 1617, a bill to consolidate and reform workforce 
development and literary programs.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Bennett) appointed Mrs. Kassebaum, Mr. 
Jeffords, Mr. Coats, Mr. Gregg, Mr. Frist, Mr. DeWine, Mr. Ashcroft, 
Mr. Abraham, Mr. Gorton, Mr. Kennedy, Mr. Pell, Mr. Dodd, Mr. Simon, 
Mr. Harkin, Ms. Mikulski, and Mr. Wellstone conferees on the part of 
the Senate.

                          ____________________
```
