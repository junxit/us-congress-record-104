# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Wellstone, Paul D. (W000288) [D-MN], Kassebaum, Nancy Landon (K000017) [R-KS]

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Coats). Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period for the transaction of morning business not to 
extend beyond the hour of 10:30 a.m., with Senators permitted to speak 
therein for up to 5 minutes each.
  Under that previous order, the Senator from Kansas [Mrs. Kassebaum] 
is recognized to speak for up to 10 minutes.
  Mr. WELLSTONE. Will the Senator yield for a moment?
  Mrs. KASSEBAUM. Yes.
  Mr. WELLSTONE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that I might be 
granted 10 minutes to speak as in morning business.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The Senator from Kansas is recognized.

                          ____________________
```
