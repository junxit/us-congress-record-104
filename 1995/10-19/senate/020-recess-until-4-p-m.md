# RECESS UNTIL 4 P.M.

```
                          RECESS UNTIL 4 P.M.

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The hour of 12 o'clock having arrived, the 
Senate stands in recess until 4 p.m.
  Thereupon, at 12 noon, the Senate recessed until 4 p.m.; whereupon, 
the Senate reassembled when called to order by the Presiding Officer 
(Mr. Thompson).

```
