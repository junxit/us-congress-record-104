# TRIBUTE TO SUSAN HOFFMANN

**Speaking:** Dole, Robert J. (D000401) [R-KS]

```
                       TRIBUTE TO SUSAN HOFFMANN

  Mr. DOLE. Mr. President, I would like to take a moment to recognize a 
staffer who has recently left my Topeka, KS office, Susan Hoffmann. 
Susie was a dedicated member of my staff for almost 8 years and has 
recently moved on to pursue her career with the Community Bankers 
Association in Topeka.
  Susie is a graduate of my alma mater, Washburn University, and has 
worked for several years helping the Young Republicans in the State. 
She was committed to assisting constituents with their concerns about 
government and they knew Susie was always there to lend a helping hand 
to a Kansan in need. She made a difference in hundreds of people's 
lives, because she cared.
  Mr. President, I know my staff joins me in wishing Susan Hoffmann the 
best of luck in her future endeavors.

                          ____________________
```
