# REFERRAL OF AMTRAK APPROPRIATIONS AUTHORIZATION

**Speaking:** Kassebaum, Nancy Landon (K000017) [R-KS]

**Measures:** S. 1318

```
            REFERRAL OF AMTRAK APPROPRIATIONS AUTHORIZATION

  Mrs. KASSEBAUM. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that Calendar 
206, S. 1318, the Amtrak and Local Rail 

[[Page S 15313]]
Revitalization Act of 1995, be referred to the Finance Committee solely 
for the consideration of title 10 of the bill, for not to exceed 15 
calendar days; and further, that if the bill has not been reported from 
the committee after the 15 days, it automatically be discharged and 
placed on the calendar.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
