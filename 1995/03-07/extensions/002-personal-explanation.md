# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Gutierrez, Luis V. (G000535) [D-IL]

**Measures:** H.R. 1022

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______


                         HON. LUIS V. GUTIERREZ

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, March 7, 1995
  Mr. GUTIERREZ. Mr. Speaker, at the end of the afternoon of Tuesday, 
February 28, 1995, I was unavoidably absent from this Chamber and 
therefore missed rollcall votes Nos. 178, 179, 180, 181, 182, 183--all 
votes regarding amendments and final passage of H.R. 1022--the Risk 
Assessment and Cost Benefit Act of 1995. I want the Record to show that 
if I had had the opportunity to be in this Chamber when these votes 
were cast, I would have votes the following way: rollcall vote No. 178, 
``yea''; roll call vote No. 179, ``nay''; rollcall vote No. 180, 
``yea''; rollcall vote No.181, ``yea''; rollcall vote No. 182--``yea''; 
rollcall vote No. 183, ``nay.''


                          ____________________
```
