# MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.R. 1868, H.R. 2491, H.R. 2492

```
                        MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

  At 12 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered by 
Mr. Hays, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
passed the following bill, in which it requests the concurrence of the 
Senate:

       H.R. 2492. An act making appropriations for the legislative 
     branch for the fiscal year ending September 30, 1996, and for 
     other purposes.

  The message also announced that the Speaker appoints the following 
Member as an additional conferee in the conference on the disagreeing 
votes of the two Houses on the amendment of the Senate to the bill 
(H.R. 2491) to provide for reconciliation pursuant to section 105 of 
the concurrent resolution on the budget for fiscal year 1996:
  From the Committee on Agriculture, for consideration of title I of 
the House bill, and subtitles A-C of title of the Senate amendment, and 
modifications committed to conference: Mr. Brown of California.
  The message further announced that the House agrees to the report of 
the committee of conference on the disagreeing votes of the two Houses 
on the amendments of the Senate to the bill (H.R. 1868) making 
appropriations for foreign operations, export financing, and related 
programs for fiscal year ending September 30, 1996, and for other 
purposes; that the House recedes from its disagreement to an amendment 
of the Senate and concurs therein with an amendment in which it 
requests the concurrence of the Senate.
```
