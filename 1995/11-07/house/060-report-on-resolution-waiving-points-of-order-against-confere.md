REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING POINTS OF ORDER AGAINST CONFERENCE REPORT 
 ON S. 395, ALASKA POWER ADMINISTRATION ASSET SALE AND TERMINATION ACT

report (Rept. No. 104-314) on the resolution (H. Res. 256) waiving
