# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Rev. James David Ford, D.D., offered the following 
prayer:
  Let us pray using the words of Psalm 138:
  I give Thee thanks, O Lord, with my whole heart; before the gods I 
sing Thy praise; I bow down toward Thy holy temple and give thanks to 
Thy name for Thy steadfast love and Thy faithfulness; for Thou hast 
exalted above everything Thy name and Thy word. On the day I called, 
Thou didst answer me, my strength of soul Thou didst increase. Amen.
```
