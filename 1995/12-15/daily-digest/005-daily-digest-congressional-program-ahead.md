# Daily Digest/CONGRESSIONAL PROGRAM AHEAD

**Measures:** H.R. 194, S. 594, H.R. 1129, H.R. 1527, H.R. 1710, H.R. 2107

```
                      CONGRESSIONAL PROGRAM AHEAD

                  Week of December 18 through 23, 1995

                             Senate Chamber

  During the week, Senate may consider conference reports, when 
available, continuing appropriations, if necessary, and any cleared 
legislative and executive business.

  (Senate will recess on Tuesday, December 19, 1995, from 12:30 p.m. 
until 2:15 p.m. for respective party conferences.)


                           Senate Committees

        (Committee meetings are open unless otherwise indicated)
  Committee on Appropriations: December 19, Subcommittee on Foreign 
Operations, to hold hearings to review the current situation in 
Bosnia, 10 a.m., SD-192.
  Committee on Energy and Natural Resources: December 20, to hold 
hearings on S. 594, to provide for the administration of certain 
Presidio properties at minimal cost to the Federal taxpayer, 9:30 
a.m., SD-366.
  December 21, Full Committee, business meeting, to consider pending 
calendar business, 9:30 a.m., SD-366.
  Committee on Environment and Public Works: December 19, business 
meeting, to consider pending calendar business, 2:30 p.m., SD-406.
  Committee on the Judiciary: December 19, to hold hearings to 
examine trends in youthful drug use, 10:30 a.m., SD-226.
  December 19, Full Committee, to hold hearings on pending 
nominations, 2 p.m., SD-226.
  December 20, Full Committee, business meeting, to consider pending 
calendar business, 10 a.m., SD-226.
  December 21, Full Committee, business meeting, to consider pending 
committee business, 10 a.m., SD-226.
  Special Committee To Investigate Whitewater Development 
Corporation and Related Matters: December 18, to resume hearings to 
examine certain matters relating to the Whitewater Development 
Corporation, 1 p.m., SH-216.


                            House Committees

  Committee on International Relations, December 18, Subcommittee on 
International Operations and Human Rights, hearing on Trial, 
Conviction, and Imprisonment of Wei Jingsheng: How Should It Affect 
United States Policy? 2 p.m., 2172 Rayburn.
  December 20, Subcommittee on Western Hemisphere Affairs, hearing 
on Violations of the U.S. Embargo on Cuba, 2p.m., 2172 Rayburn.
  Committee on Resources, December 19, Subcommittee on National 
Parks, Forests and Lands, to mark up the following bills: H.R. 1129, 
to amend the National Trails Systems Act to designate the route from 
Selma to Montgomery as a National Historic Trail; H.R. 2107, Visitor 
Services Improvement and Outdoor Legacy Act of 1995; H.R. 194, to 
direct the Secretary of the Interior to make matching contributions 
toward the purchase of the Sterling Forest in the State of New York; 
and H.R. 1527, to amend the National Forest Ski Area Permit Act of 
1986 to clarify the authorities and duties of the Secretary of 
Agriculture in issuing ski area permits on National Forest System 
lands and to withdraw lands within ski area permit boundaries from 
the operation of the mining and mineral leasing laws, 10 a.m., 1334 
Longworth.
  December 19, Salvage Timber and Forest Health Task Force, hearing 
on Salvage Timber and Forest Health focusing on Southern Salvage and 
Forest Health Issues, 10 a.m., 1324 Longworth.
  Committee on Rules. December 18, to consider H.R. 1710, 
Comprehensive Antiterrorism Act of 1995, 5 p.m., H-3133 Capitol.
  Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence, December 19, to 
continue hearings on IC21: The Intelligence Community in the 21st 
Century, 10 a.m., 2118 Rayburn.
```
