# INTRODUCTION OF PIPELINE SAFETY LEGISLATION

**Speaking:** Petri, Thomas E. (P000265) [R-WI]

```
              INTRODUCTION OF PIPELINE SAFETY LEGISLATION

                                 ______


                          HON. THOMAS E. PETRI

                              of wisconsin

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, March 9, 1995
  Mr. PETRI. Mr. Speaker, today, I am introducing legislation, at the 
request of the pipeline industry, to reauthorize our pipeline safety 
programs. This legislation represents the consensus view of both the 
natural gas and hazardous liquid pipeline industries on the future 
direction of pipeline safety programs and will be considered at a 
Surface Transportation Subcommittee hearing to be held next week.


                          ____________________
```
