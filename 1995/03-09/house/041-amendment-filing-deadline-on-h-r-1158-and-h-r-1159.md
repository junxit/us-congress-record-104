# AMENDMENT FILING DEADLINE ON H.R. 1158 AND H.R. 1159

**Speaking:** Solomon, Gerald B. H. (S000675) [R-NY]

**Measures:** H.R. 1158, H.R. 1159

```
          AMENDMENT FILING DEADLINE ON H.R. 1158 AND H.R. 1159

  Mr. SOLOMON. Mr.Speaker, earlier today I announced a preprinting 
requirement for amendments to the two supplemental appropriations and 
rescissions bills, H.R. 1158 and H.R. 1159 and noted that amendments 
should be submitted for printing no later than Monday, March 13, 1995.
  I now ask unanimous consent that Members have until 5 p.m. on Monday, 
March 13, which is a pro forma day to file their amendments for 
preprinting in the Congressional Record.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from New York?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
