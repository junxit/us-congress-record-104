# NOTICE OF HEARING

**Speaking:** Nickles, Don (N000102) [R-OK]

**Measures:** S. 92

```
                           NOTICE OF HEARING


               committee on energy and natural resources

  Mr. NICKLES. Mr. President, I would like to announce for the 
information of the Senate and the public that a hearing has been 
scheduled before the Subcommittee on Energy Production and Regulation.
  The hearing will take place Tuesday, March 21, 1995, at 10 a.m. in 
room SD-366 of the Dirksen Senate Office Building in Washington, DC.
  The purpose of this hearing is to receive testimony on S. 92, a bill 
to provide for the reconstitution of outstanding repayment obligations 
of the administrator of the Bonneville Power Administration for the 
appropriated capital investments in the Federal Columbia River Power 
System.
  Those who wish to submit written statements should write to the 
Committee on Energy and Natural Resources, U.S. Senate, Washington, DC 
20510. For further information, please call Howard Useem or Judy Brown 
at (202) 224-6567.

                          ____________________
```
