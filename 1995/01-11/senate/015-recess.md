# RECESS

**Speaking:** Grassley, Chuck (G000386) [R-IA], Dole, Robert J. (D000401) [R-KS]

```
                                 RECESS

  Mr. GRASSLEY. Mr. President, since there are no further amendments, 
other than the managers' package--and that is to this bill that is 
before us--and no other Senators are seeking the floor at this time, I 
ask unanimous consent that the Senate now stand in recess until 4:30 
p.m. this afternoon.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 3:09 p.m., recessed until 
4:30 p.m.; whereupon, the Senate reassembled when called to order by 
the Presiding Officer [Mrs. Hutchison].
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair, acting in her capacity as Senator 
from Texas, suggests the absence of a quorum.
  The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. DOLE. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
