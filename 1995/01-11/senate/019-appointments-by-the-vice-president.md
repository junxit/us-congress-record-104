# APPOINTMENTS BY THE VICE PRESIDENT

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

**Measures:** S.Res. 4, S.Res. 448, S.Res. 127, S.Res. 100

```
                   APPOINTMENTS BY THE VICE PRESIDENT

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair, on behalf of the Vice President, 
and upon the recommendation of the majority leader, pursuant to Senate 
Resolution 4 (95th Congress), Senate Resolution 448 (96th Congress), 
Senate Resolution 127 (98th Congress), and Senate Resolution 100 (101st 
Congress), appoints the following Senators as the majority membership 
of the Select Committee on Indian Affairs: The Senator from Arizona 
[Mr. McCain], the Senator from Alaska [Mr. Murkowski], the Senator from 
Washington [Mr. Gorton], the Senator from New Mexico [Mr. Domenici], 
the Senator from Kansas [Mrs. Kassebaum], the Senator from Oklahoma 
[Mr. Nickles], the Senator from Wyoming [Mr. Thomas], the Senator from 
Utah [Mr. Hatch], and the Senator from Georgia [Mr. Coverdell].
  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
