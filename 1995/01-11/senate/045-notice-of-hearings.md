# NOTICE OF HEARINGS

**Speaking:** Stevens, Ted (S000888) [R-AK]

```
                           NOTICE OF HEARINGS


                 COMMITTEE ON RULES AND ADMINISTRATION

  Mr. STEVENS. Mr. President, I wish to announce that the Committee on

Building, on Wednesday, January 18, and Thursday, January 19, 1995, at
```
