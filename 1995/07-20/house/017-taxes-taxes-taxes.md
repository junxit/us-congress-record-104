# TAXES, TAXES, TAXES

**Speaking:** Traficant, James A., Jr. (T000350) [D-OH]

```
                          TAXES, TAXES, TAXES

  (Mr. TRAFICANT asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. TRAFICANT. Mr. Speaker, retirement tax, income tax, property tax, 
excise tax, sales tax, beer tax, tobacco tax, cable tax, telephone tax, 
gasoline tax, hotel tax, surtaxes, taxes on taxes, and, don't forget 
when you die, inheritance tax. But also how about tolls, user fees, 
service charges, licenses, transfers. And some experts around the 
country are saying we don't need tax reform.
  Beam me up, Mr. Speaker. Maybe, just maybe, these so-called experts 
are so dumb, we could throw them at the ground and they would probably 
miss.


                          ____________________
```
