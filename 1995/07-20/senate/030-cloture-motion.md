# CLOTURE MOTION

**Measures:** S. 343

```
                             CLOTURE MOTION

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, pursuant to rule 
XXII, the Chair lays before the Senate the pending cloture motion, 
which the clerk will state.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:


                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the Dole-
     Johnston substitute amendment to S. 343, the regulatory 
     reform bill:
       Bob Dole, Christopher S. Bond, Bill Roth, Frank H. 
     Murkowski, Rod Grams, John Ashcroft, Spencer Abraham, Craig 
     Thomas, Pete V. Domenici, Bill Frist, Fred Thompson, Mike 
     DeWine, Thad Cochran, Larry E. Craig, Bob Smith, Chuck 
     Grassley.

                          ____________________
```
