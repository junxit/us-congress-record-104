# GRANTING MEMBERS OF THE HOUSE PRIVILEGE TO EXTEND AND REVISE REMARKS IN CONGRESSIONAL RECORD FOR REMAINDER OF 104TH CONGRESS

**Speaking:** Bereuter, Doug (B000403) [R-NE]

```
GRANTING MEMBERS OF THE HOUSE PRIVILEGE TO EXTEND AND REVISE REMARKS IN 
          CONGRESSIONAL RECORD FOR REMAINDER OF 104TH CONGRESS

  Mr. BEREUTER. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that for the 
remainder of the first session of the 104th Congress, all Members be 
permitted to extend their remarks and to include extraneous material in 
the section of the Record entitled ``Extensions of Remarks.''
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Nebraska?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
