# BRUCE R. THOMPSON U.S. COURTHOUSE AND FEDERAL BUILDING DESIGNATION

**Speaking:** Mack, Connie, III (M000019) [R-FL]

**Measures:** H.R. 395

```
   BRUCE R. THOMPSON U.S. COURTHOUSE AND FEDERAL BUILDING DESIGNATION

  Mr. MACK. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the immediate consideration of calendar No. 256, H.R. 395, a 
bill to designate a U.S. Courthouse and Federal building in Reno, NV; 
that the bill be deemed read the third time, passed, and the motion to 
reconsider be laid upon the table; and further, that any statements 
relating thereto be placed in the Record at the appropriate place as if 
read.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  So the bill (H.R. 395) was deemed read the third time, and passed.

                          ____________________
```
