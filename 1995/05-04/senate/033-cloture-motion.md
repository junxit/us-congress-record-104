# CLOTURE MOTION

**Measures:** H.R. 956

```
                             CLOTURE MOTION

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the hour of 2:02 
having arrived, the cloture motion having been presented under rule 
XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.
  The assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We the undersigned Senators in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the Gorton

           Pete V. Domenici, Conrad Burns, John Ashcroft, Dan 
           Coats, Bill Frist, Olympia J. Snowe, Spencer Abraham,
```
