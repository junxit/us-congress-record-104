# ORDER FOR RECESS UNTIL TOMORROW

**Speaking:** Dole, Robert J. (D000401) [R-KS], Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

```
                    ORDER FOR RECESS UNTIL TOMORROW

  Mr. DOLE. If there is no further business to come before the Senate, 
I now ask unanimous consent that the Senate stand in recess under the 
previous order following the remarks of Senator Specter, who I 
understand is on the way to the floor.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. DOLE. I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will please call the roll.
  The assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
