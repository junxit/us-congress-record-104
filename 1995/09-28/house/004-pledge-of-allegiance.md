# PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE

```
                          PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Will the gentlewoman from North Carolina 
[Mrs. Myrick] come forward and lead the House in the Pledge of 
Allegiance.
  Mrs. MYRICK led the Pledge of Allegiance as follows:

       I  pledge  allegiance  to  the  Flag  of  the United  
     States  of  America,  and  to the Republic  for  which it 
     stands,  one  nation under  God,  indivisible,  with  liberty 
      and justice for all.

                          ____________________
```
