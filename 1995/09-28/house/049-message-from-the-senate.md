# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** S. 895

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Mr. Lundregan, one of its clerks, 
announced, ``that the Senate disagrees to the amendments of the House 
to the bill (S. 895) `An Act to amend the Small Business Act to reduce 
the level of participation by the Small Business Administration in 
certain loans guaranteed by the Administration, and for other 
purposes', agrees to a conference asked by the House on the disagreeing 
votes of the two Houses thereon, and appoints Mr. Bond, Mr. Burns, Mr. 
Coverdell, Mr. Bumpers, and Mr. Nunn, to be the conferees on the part 
of the Senate''.
```
