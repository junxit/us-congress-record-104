# PERMISSION TO FILE CONFERENCE REPORT ON S. 395, ALASKA POWER ADMINISTRATION ASSET SALE AND TERMINATION ACT

**Speaking:** Jones, Walter B., Jr. (J000255) [R-NC]

**Measures:** S. 395

```
     PERMISSION TO FILE CONFERENCE REPORT ON S. 395, ALASKA POWER 
             ADMINISTRATION ASSET SALE AND TERMINATION ACT

  Mr. JONES of North Carolina. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent 
that the managers be given until midnight tonight, November 6, 1995, to 
file a conference report on the Senate bill (S. 395) to authorize and 
direct the Secretary of Energy to sell the Alaska Power Administration, 
and for other purposes.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from North Carolina?
  There was no objection.
```
