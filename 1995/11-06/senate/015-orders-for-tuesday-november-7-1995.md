# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, NOVEMBER 7, 1995

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

```
                  ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, NOVEMBER 7, 1995

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today it stand in adjournment until the hour of 
10 a.m., Tuesday, November 7, that following the prayer, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, no resolutions come over under the 
rule, the call of the calendar be dispensed with, the morning hour be 
deemed to have expired, and the time for the two leaders be reserved 
for their use later in the day, and there then be the period for the 
transaction of morning business until 11 a.m., with Senators permitted 
to speak for up to 10 minutes each, with the following exceptions, 
Senator Murkowski for 20 minutes and Senator Graham for 20 minutes.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
