# RECESS UNTIL MONDAY, AUGUST 7, 1995, AT 9 A.M.

```
             RECESS UNTIL MONDAY, AUGUST 7, 1995, AT 9 A.M.

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. If no other Senator is seeking recognition, 
under the previous order the Senate stands in recess until Monday, 
August 7, 1995 at 9 a.m.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 5:46 p.m., recessed until 
Monday, August 7, 1995, at 9 a.m. 

```
