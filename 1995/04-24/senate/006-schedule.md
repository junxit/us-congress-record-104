# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Dole, Robert J. (D000401) [R-KS]

**Measures:** H.R. 956

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. DOLE. Mr. President, following the leaders' time, there will be 
morning business until 1 o'clock, with Senators permitted to speak for 
up to 5 minutes each.
  Shortly, Senator Nickles will submit a Senate resolution regarding 
the bombing in Oklahoma City.
  It is also my intention to begin consideration of H.R. 956, the 
Product Liability Act.
  I am prepared to say there will be no rollcall votes today, but that 
will be up to the managers on the product liability bill. There will be 
a vote on the Nickles resolution, if agreeable, at noon tomorrow.

                          ____________________
```
