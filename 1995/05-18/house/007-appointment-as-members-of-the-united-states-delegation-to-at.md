# APPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS OF THE UNITED STATES DELEGATION TO ATTEND MEETING OF THE CANADA-UNITED STATES INTERPARLIAMENTARY GROUP

```
   APPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS OF THE UNITED STATES DELEGATION TO ATTEND 
      MEETING OF THE CANADA-UNITED STATES INTERPARLIAMENTARY GROUP

  The SPEAKER. Pursuant to the provisions of 22 U.S.C. 276d, the Chair 
appoints as members of the United States delegation to attend the 
meeting of the Canada-United States Interparliamentary Group 
the following Members of the House: Mr. Manzullo of Illinois, Chairman; 
Mr. Latham of Iowa; Mr. Crapo of Idaho; Ms. Dunn of Washington; Mr. 
Zimmer of New Jersey; Mrs. Johnson of Connecticut; Mr. Goodling of 
Pennsylvania; Mr. Johnston of Florida; Mr. de la Garza of Texas; Mr. 
Gibbons of Florida; Ms. Slaughter of New York; and Mr. McNulty of New 
York.

                          ____________________
```
