# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Hutchison, Kay Bailey (H001016) [R-TX]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mrs. HUTCHISON. For the information of all Senators, tomorrow morning 
the Senate will debate the Domenici substitute, which is the text of 
President Clinton's budget, with that vote to occur at 10:45 a.m. That 
will be the last vote for the day. However, Senators Lautenberg and 
Rockefeller will offer an amendment on which there will be debate. A 
vote will occur on that amendment at 3:15 p.m., Monday, under a 
previous order. That 3:15 vote on Monday is the first vote of the day. 
Additional rollcall votes can be expected throughout the day and into 
the evening on Monday.


                          ____________________
```
