# NOTICES OF HEARINGS

**Speaking:** McCain, John (M000303) [R-AZ]

**Measures:** S. 479

```
                          NOTICES OF HEARINGS

  Mr. McCAIN. Mr. President, I would like to announce that the Senate 
Committee on Indian Affairs will be holding a hearing on Tuesday, May 
23, 1995, beginning at 9:30 a.m., in room 485 of the 
[[Page S6941]] Russell Senate Office Building on S. 479, a bill to 
provide for administrative procedures to extend Federal recognition to 
certain Indian groups.
  Those wishing additional information should contact the Committee on 
Indian Affairs at 224-2251.
```
