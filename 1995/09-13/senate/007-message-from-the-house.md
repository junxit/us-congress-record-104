# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** S. 895

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 12:39 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Ms. Goetz, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House had 
passed the bill (S. 895) to amend the Small Business Act to reduce the 
level of participation by the Small Business Administration in certain 
loans guaranteed by the Administration, and for other purposes, with 
amendments; that it insists upon its amendments and asks a conference 
with the Senate on the disagreeing votes of the two Houses thereon; and 
appoints Mrs. Meyers of Kansas, Mr. Torkildsen, Mr. Longley, Mr. 
LaFalce, and Mr. Poshard as the managers of the conference on the part 
of the House.
```
