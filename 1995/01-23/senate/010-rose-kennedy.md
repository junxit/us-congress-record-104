# ROSE KENNEDY

**Speaking:** Kempthorne, Dirk (K000088) [R-ID]

```
                              ROSE KENNEDY

  Mr. KEMPTHORNE. Mr. President, I want to join my voice with so many 
other voices in expressing sorrow at the loss of Rose Kennedy, a woman 
who epitomized, I think, strength and class and grace; and say to 
Senator Kennedy and all the members of the Kennedy family that our 
thoughts and our prayers are with them as we celebrate the life of that 
remarkable woman.


                          ____________________
```
