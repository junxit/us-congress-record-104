# APPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS OF THE JOINT ECONOMIC COMMITTEE

```
         APPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS OF THE JOINT ECONOMIC COMMITTEE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to the provisions of 15 U.S.C. 
1024(a), the Chair, without objection, appoints as members of the Joint 
Economic Committee the following members on the part of the House:
  Mr. Saxton of New Jersey.
  Mr. Ewing of Illinois;
  Mr. Quinn of New York;
  Mr. Manzullo of Illinois;
  Mr. Sanford of South Carolina;
  Mr. Thornberry of Texas;
  Mr. Stark of California;
  Mr. Obey of Wisconsin;
  Mr. Hamilton of Indiana; and
  Mr. Mfume of Maryland.
  There was no objection.
```
