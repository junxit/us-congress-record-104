# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Deutsch, Peter (D000275) [D-FL]

**Measures:** S. 2, H.R. 4822

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______


                           HON. PETER DEUTSCH

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, January 19, 1995

  Mr. DEUTSCH. Mr. Speaker, I rise to voice my support for S. 2, the 
Congressional compliance bill, and the conference report which passed 
the House on January 17, 1995. As the record shows, I voted in support 
of this measure twice: Once on August 10, 1994 at the close of the 103d 
Congress (H.R. 4822); and again on January 4, 1995, when the House of 
Representatives passed this measure in the 104th Congress. Accordingly, 
had I not been unavoidably detained in travel, I would have voted 
``yea'' on the vote for S. 2.

                          ____________________
```
