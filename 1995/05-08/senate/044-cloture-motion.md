# CLOTURE MOTION

**Speaking:** Hutchison, Kay Bailey (H001016) [R-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 956

```
                             CLOTURE MOTION

  Mrs. HUTCHISON. Mr. President, I send a cloture motion to the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The cloture motion having been presented under 
rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the Coverdell 
     substitute amendment to H.R. 956, the product liability bill.
         Bob Dole, Slade Gorton, Pete Domenici, Frank Murkowski, 
           Spencer Abraham, Trent Lott, Kay Hutchison, Chuck 
           Grassley, Rick Santorum, Jay Rockefeller, Larry 
           Pressler, Larry Craig, Don Nickles, Conrad Burns, 
           Christopher Bond, Bill Frist.

                          ____________________
```
