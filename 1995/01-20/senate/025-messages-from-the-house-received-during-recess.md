# MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING RECESS

```
             MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING RECESS

  Under the authority of the order of the Senate of January 4, 1995, 
the Secretary of the Senate, on January 20, 1995, during the recess of 
the Senate, received a message from the House of Representatives 
announcing that pursuant to the provisions of title 15, United States 
Code, section 1024(a), the Speaker appoints the following Members on 
the part of the House to serve as members of the Joint Economic 
Committee: Mr. Saxton, Mr. Ewing, Mr. Quinn, Mr. Manzullo, Mr. Sanford, 
Mr. Thornberry, Mr. Stark, Mr. Obey, Mr. Hamilton, and Mr. Mfume.
```
