# ELECTION OF REPUBLICAN MEMBERS TO COMMITTEE ON STANDARDS OF OFFICIAL CONDUCT

**Speaking:** Hastert, J. Dennis (H000323) [R-IL]

**Measures:** H.Res. 41

```
 ELECTION OF REPUBLICAN MEMBERS TO COMMITTEE ON STANDARDS OF OFFICIAL 
                                CONDUCT

  Mr. HASTERT. Mr. Speaker, by direction of the Republican Conference, 
I offer a privileged resolution (H. Res. 41) and ask for its immediate 
consideration.
  The Clerk read the resolution, as follows:
                        [[Page H455]] H. Res. 41

       Resolved, That the following named Members, be, and they 
     are hereby, elected to the Committee on Standards of Official 
     Conduct of the House of Representatives:
       Mrs. Johnson of Connecticut, Chairman; Mr. Bunning; Mr. 
     Goss; Mr. Hobson; and Mr. Schiff.

  The resolution was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```
