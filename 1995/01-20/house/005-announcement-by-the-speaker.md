# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER

```
                      ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER

  The SPEAKER. The Chair announces that consistent with clause 9 of 
rule XIV, statements and rulings of the Chair appearing in the Record 
will be a substantially verbatim account of those words as spoken 
during the proceedings of the House, subject only to technical, 
grammatical, and typographical corrections.
  Without objection, the permanent Record of January 18 at pages 301 
and 303 will reflect this policy.
  There was no objection.
```
