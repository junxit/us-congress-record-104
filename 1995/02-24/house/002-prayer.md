# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  Rev. Harold Bradley, assistant to the president, Georgetown 
University, Washington, DC, offered the following prayer:
  O loving and gracious God, we offer our thanks to You for Your gifts 
that brighten our days and give meaning to our lives. We pray for sound 
minds so that we can contemplate and appreciate the marvels of Your 
creation, and we pray for good hearts that allow us to do those good 
works that honor You and serve people whatever their need. May Your 
spirit, O God, that is with us whatever our circumstance, protect, 
sustain, and bless us so we will live as You would have us live and be 
faithful in deeds of justice and mercy. May Your grace be with us this 
day and every day, we pray. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
