# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Rev. James David Ford, D.D., offered the following 
prayer:
  Remind us always, O gracious God, that Your blessings and redemptive 
power are showered on all the people from every background and every 
nation. While we attempt to serve with enthusiasm and zeal in our place 
and time, may we never act with undue pride about our responsibility, 
but rather go about our work with humility and diligence. Encourage us 
to focus on the goals of justice with an attitude of mercy for in so 
doing we will truly be Your people. In Your name, we pray. Amen.
```
