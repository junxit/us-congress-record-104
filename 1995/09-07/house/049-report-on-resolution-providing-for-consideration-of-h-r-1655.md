# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 1655, INTELLIGENCE AUTHORIZATION ACT, 1996

**Measures:** H.Res. 216, H.R. 1655

```
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 1655, 
                  INTELLIGENCE AUTHORIZATION ACT, 1996

  Mr. GOSS, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged report 
(Rept. No. 104-241) on the resolution (H. Res. 216) providing for the 
consideration of the bill (H.R. 1655) to authorize appropriations for 
fiscal year 1996 for intelligence and intelligence-related activities 
of the U.S. Government, the Community Management Account, and the 
Central Intelligence Agency Retirement and Disability System, and for 
other purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to 
be printed.
```
