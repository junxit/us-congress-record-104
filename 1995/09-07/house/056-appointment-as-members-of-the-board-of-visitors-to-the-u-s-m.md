# APPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS OF THE BOARD OF VISITORS TO THE U.S. MERCHANT MARINE ACADEMY

```
 APPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS OF THE BOARD OF VISITORS TO THE U.S. MERCHANT 
                             MARINE ACADEMY

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, and pursuant to the 
provisions of section 1295b(h) of title 46, United States Code, the 
Chair announces the Speaker's appointment as members of the Board of 
Visitors to the U.S. Merchant Marine Academy the following Members of 
the House:
  Mr. King of New York, and Mr. Manton of New York.
  There was no objection.
```
