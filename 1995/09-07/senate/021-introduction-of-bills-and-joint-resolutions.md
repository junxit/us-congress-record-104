# INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** S. 1219, S. 1220, S. 1221, S. 1222

```
              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second time by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Mr. McCAIN (for himself, Mr. Feingold, Mr. Thompson, 
             Mr. Pell, and Mr. Wellstone):
       S. 1219. A bill to reform the financing of Federal 
     elections, and for other purposes; to the Committee on Rules 
     and Administration.
           By Mrs. BOXER:
       S. 1220. A bill to provide that Members of Congress shall 
     not be paid during Federal Government shutdowns; to the 
     Committee on Governmental Affairs.
           By Mrs. KASSEBAUM (for herself and Mr. Jeffords):
       S. 1221. A bill to authorize appropriations for the Legal 
     Services Corporation Act, and for other purposes; to the 
     Committee on Labor and Human Resources.
           By Mr. FAIRCLOTH:
       S. 1222. A bill to prevent the creation of an international 
     bailout fund within the International Monetary Fund, and for 
     other purposes; to the Committee on Foreign Relations.
```
