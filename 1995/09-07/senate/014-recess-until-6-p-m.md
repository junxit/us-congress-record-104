**Speaking:** Dole, Robert J. (D000401) [R-KS]

```
                          RECESS UNTIL 6 P.M.

  Mr. DOLE. Mr. President, I move the Senate stand in recess until 6 
p.m.

6 p.m.; whereupon the Senate reassembled when called to order by the
```
