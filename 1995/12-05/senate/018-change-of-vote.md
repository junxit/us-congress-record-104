# CHANGE OF VOTE

**Speaking:** McCain, John (M000303) [R-AZ]

**Measures:** H.R. 1058

```
                             CHANGE OF VOTE

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator from Arizona.
  Mr. McCAIN. Mr. President, on the rollcall vote on the conference 
report accompanying H.R. 1058, I was recorded as voting in the 
affirmative. I ask unanimous consent to change my vote, which was 
recorded as ``yes'', to ``no.'' It will not change the outcome of the 
vote.
  I ask unanimous consent I be recorded as a ``no'' vote.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  [The foregoing tally has been changed to reflect the above order.]

                          ____________________
```
