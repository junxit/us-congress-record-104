# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Daschle, Thomas A. (D000064) [D-SD]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. DASCHLE. Mr. President, for the information of Senators, it is 
the hope of the majority leader to begin consideration of the 
continuing resolution tomorrow at 12, following morning business. It is 
also the hope of the leader to consider the debt limit extension during 
Thursday's session. Rollcall votes are therefore expected to occur 
during Thursday's session of the Senate and a late night session could 
be anticipated in order to complete the action of these measures.
```
