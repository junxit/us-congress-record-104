# THE NEW MAJORITY

**Speaking:** Hayworth, J. D. (H000413) [R-AZ]

```
                              {time}  1220
                            THE NEW MAJORITY

  (Mr. HAYWORTH asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. HAYWORTH. Mr. Speaker, I will lay aside my prepared remarks this 
morning to answer my good friend the gentleman from California.
  It never ceases to amaze me how the guardians of the old order who 
were here as part of an incredible corrupt regime during the former 
majority are so willing to hop on the Speaker and his positive agenda 
for change.
  With all due respect to my friends on the other side of the aisle, 
why do you not join with us to put your shoulder to the wheel and 
govern this Nation? We have the majority. Get over it.
  Mr. Speaker, I yield back the balance of my time.

                          ____________________
```
