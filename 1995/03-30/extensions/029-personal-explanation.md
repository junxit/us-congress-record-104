# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** DeFazio, Peter A. (D000191) [D-OR]

**Measures:** H.R. 956

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______


                         HON. PETER A. DeFAZIO

                               of oregon

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, March 30, 1995
  Mr. DeFAZIO. Mr. Speaker, on March 9, I was having dinner at a nearby 
restaurant with the Oregon State labor commissioner and apparently my 
electronic beeper malfunctioned and I missed a recorded vote on the Cox 
amendment to H.R. 956 which would cap non-economic pain and suffering 
damages in health care liability cases at $250,000. If I had been 
present I would have voted ``no.''
```
