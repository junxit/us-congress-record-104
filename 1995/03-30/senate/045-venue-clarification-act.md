# VENUE CLARIFICATION ACT

**Measures:** S. 532

```
                        VENUE CLARIFICATION ACT

  The bill (S. 532) to clarify the rules governing venue, and for other 
purposes, was considered, ordered to be engrossed for a third reading, 
read the third time, and passed; as follows:

       Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of 
     the United States of America in Congress assembled,

     SECTION 1. VENUE.

       Paragraph (3) of section 1391(a) of title 28, United States 
     Code, is amended by striking ``the defendants are'' and 
     inserting ``any defendant is''.
```
