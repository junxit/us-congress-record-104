# TERM LIMITS

**Speaking:** Myrick, Sue Wilkins (M001134) [R-NC]

```
                              TERM LIMITS

  (Mrs. MYRICK asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute.)
  Mrs. MYRICK. Mr. Speaker, I would like to remind my colleagues that 
hearings begin today in subcommittee on the most fundamental 
congressional reform issue we will face this session--term limits.
  As these hearings begin, I ask my colleagues to join team 290--a 
bipartisan group of Members of Congress committed to passing a term 
limits amendment in the 104th Congress.
  We are gathering commitments to support final passage of a term 
limits amendment this session. Please join us by signing the team 290 
board in the Speaker's lobby today. If you can't join today, the board 
will be up from 9 to 5:30 daily.
  Please join team 290 and show your commitment to true congressional 
reform through term limits.

                          ____________________
```
