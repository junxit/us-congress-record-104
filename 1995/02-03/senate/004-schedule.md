# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 1

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, this morning the time for the two leaders is 
reserved, and there will now be a period for the transaction of morning 
business until the hour of 10 a.m., with Senators permitted to speak 
for up to 10 minutes each, with the following Senators permitted to 
speak for the designated times: Senator Bond 10 minutes and Senator 
Hutchison 10 minutes. At 10 a.m., the Senate will resume consideration 
of House Joint Resolution 1, the constitutional balanced budget 
amendment.
  I should advise Members we do expect that an amendment will be laid 
down this morning for debate only.
  Mr. President, I yield the floor.

                          ____________________
```
