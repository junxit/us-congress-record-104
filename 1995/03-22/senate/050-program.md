# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Coats, Daniel (C000542) [R-IN]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. COATS. Mr. President, for the information of my colleagues, 
Members who still have amendments on the list must offer those 
amendments by 10 a.m. Thursday morning. Votes can therefore be expected

the pending line-item veto.

amendments that are on the list, that have been cleared to be on that 
list under unanimous consent, must offer those amendments by 10 a.m. 
Thursday morning. Votes will be expected throughout the day, including 
final passage of the pending line-item veto bill.
```
