# CLOTURE MOTION

**Speaking:** Gorton, Slade (G000333) [R-WA]

**Measures:** H.R. 956

```
                             CLOTURE MOTION

  Mr. GORTON. Mr. President, I send two separate motions to invoke 
cloture on the Gorton amendment No. 596 to the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will read the motion.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We the undersigned Senators in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the Gorton 
     Amendment No. 596 to H.R. 956, the Product Liability bill.
         Bob Dole, Slade Gorton, Rick Santorum, Jim Inhofe, Conrad 
           Burns, Pete V. Domenici, Hank Brown, Spencer Abraham, 
           Paul D. Coverdell, Larry E. Craig, Dirk Kempthorne, Bob 
           Smith, Trent Lott, Chuck Grassley, Judd Gregg, Mitch 
           McConnell.
```
