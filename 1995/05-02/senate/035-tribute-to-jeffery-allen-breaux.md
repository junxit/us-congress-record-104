# TRIBUTE TO JEFFERY ALLEN BREAUX

**Speaking:** Breaux, John B. (B000780) [D-LA]

```
                    TRIBUTE TO JEFFERY ALLEN BREAUX

  Mr. BREAUX. Mr. President, today I would like to honor Jeffery Allen 
Breaux. Jeff was a native of my hometown of Crowley, LA, and he passed 
away on April 15, 1995. It is with extreme sorrow that I pay tribute to 
him on behalf of his parents, Mr. and Mrs. Larry J. Broussard, Sr.
```
