# REPORT ON RESCISSION PROPOSALS--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT--PM 43

```
   REPORT ON RESCISSION PROPOSALS--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT--PM 43

  The PRESIDING OFFICER laid before the Senate the following message 
from the President of the United States, together with an accompanying 
report; which was referred jointly, pursuant to the order of January 
30, 1975, as modified by the order of April 11, 1986, to the Committee 
on Appropriations, to the Committee on the Budget, to the Committee on 
Commerce, Science, and Transportation, and to the Committee on the 
Judiciary.

To the Congress of the United States:
  In accordance with the Congressional Budget and Impoundment Control 
Act of 1974, I herewith report three rescission proposals, totaling 
$132.0 million.
  The proposed rescissions affect the Departments of Justice and 
Transportation, and the National Aeronautics and Space Administration.
                                                  William J. Clinton.  
  The White House, May 2, 1995.
  

                          ____________________
```
