# 75TH ANNIVERSARY OF EMMANUEL COLLEGE OF BOSTON

**Speaking:** Kennedy, Joseph P., II (K000110) [D-MA]

```
             75TH ANNIVERSARY OF EMMANUEL COLLEGE OF BOSTON

                                 ______


                       HON. JOSEPH P. KENNEDY II

                            of massachusetts

                    in the house of representatives

                          Tuesday, May 2, 1995
  Mr. KENNEDY of Massachusetts. Mr. Speaker, I would like to take this 
moment to recognize Emmanuel College of Boston on its 75th anniversary. 
Emmanuel College was founded in 1919 by Sister Helen Madeleine Ingraham 
and the Sisters of Notre Dame.
  As the oldest women's Catholic college in New England, Emmanuel 
College's mission has been one of providing women with an outstanding 
liberal arts education rooted in Catholic heritage.
  Mr. Speaker, I wish the students, administrators, faculty, and 
alumnae of Emmanuel College a happy 75th anniversary and continued 
success in the future.
```
