# EXECUTIVE CALENDAR (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Dole, Robert J. (D000401) [R-KS]

```
                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mr. DOLE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
immediately proceed to executive session to consider the following 
nomination on today's Excutive Calendar: Calendar No. 439.
  I further ask unanimous consent that the nomination be confirmed, the 
motion to reconsider be laid upon the table, that any statements 
relating to the nomination appear at the appropriate place in the 
Record, that the President be immediately notified of the Senate's 
action, and that the Senate then return to legislative session.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  So the nomination was considered and confirmed, as follows:

       Jed S. Rakoff, of New York, to be U.S. District Judge for 
     the Southern District of New York.

                          ____________________
```
