# ORDER FOR RECESS SUBJECT TO THE CALL OF THE CHAIR

**Speaking:** Dole, Robert J. (D000401) [R-KS]

```
           ORDER FOR RECESS SUBJECT TO THE CALL OF THE CHAIR

  Mr. DOLE. Mr. President, I will make one more unanimous-consent 
request.
  I ask unanimous consent that following the remarks of Senators Leahy, 
Dorgan, and Nickles--unless there are others wishing to speak--the 
Senate would stand in recess subject to the call of the chair.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Is there objection?
  Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
