  Tied Aid Credit Program Authorization: Senate passed H.R. 2203, to

the United States, and to allow the Export-Import Bank to conduct a 
demonstration project, clearing the measure for the President.

  Page S19311
  Federal Trademark Dilution Act: Senate passed H.R. 1295, to amend the 
Trademark Act of 1946 to make certain revisions relating to the 
protection of famous marks, clearing the measure for the President.

  Page S19312
  Minority Committee Appointments: Senate agreed to S. Res. 206, making 
minority party appointments for the Committee on Veterans' Affairs.
