# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
EXECUTIVE BRANCH TRAVEL PRACTICES
Committee on Government Reform and Oversight: Subcommittee on 
Government Management, Information, and Technology held a hearing on 
the Use of Government Transportation by Executive Branch Officials. 
Testimony was heard from Representatives Bartlett of Maryland; David B. 
Buckley, Special Assistant to the Inspector General, Department of 
Defense; Mark E. Gebicke, Director, Military Operations and 
Capabilities Issues, National Security and International Affairs 
Division, GAO; Peter B. Zuidema, Director, Aircraft Management 
Division, Federal Supply Service, GSA; and a public witness.
```
