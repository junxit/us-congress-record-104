# REGULATORY REFORM

**Speaking:** Dole, Robert J. (D000401) [R-KS], Pressler, Larry (P000513) [R-SD]

```
                           REGULATORY REFORM

  Mr. DOLE. Mr. President, we have had our colleagues, a number on each 
side--five, six, seven on each side--meeting in Senator Daschle's 
office on reg reform. They have made some progress. I am not certain 
what will be the final result.
  We hope this afternoon, at least at 4 o'clock, to either go to reg 
reform or to try to proceed to reg reform--I think it depends on what 
happens during talks in the afternoon--to demonstrate, first of all, we 
are gaining a lot of support for the bill and, second, that it would be 
on the table, on the floor when we come back after the recess. We are 
not quite there yet, but I think they are working in good faith on each 
side.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator from South Dakota.
  
[[Page S9227]]

  Mr. PRESSLER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that I may speak 
as in morning business.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator has that right.
  

                          ____________________
```
