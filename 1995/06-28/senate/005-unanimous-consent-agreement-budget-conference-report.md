# UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT--BUDGET CONFERENCE REPORT

**Speaking:** Dole, Robert J. (D000401) [R-KS]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 67

```
         UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT--BUDGET CONFERENCE REPORT

  Mr. DOLE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that at 12 noon--
this has been cleared by the Democratic leader--the Senate begin 4 
hours debate to be equally divided in the usual form on the budget 
conference report, and that when the Senate receives the conference 
report to cover the budget, House Concurrent Resolution 67, there be 6 
hours remaining for consideration.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. DOLE. I hope we may be able to use some more time later in the 
day.

                          ____________________
```
