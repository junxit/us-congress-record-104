# TRIBUTE TO CLAIRE CRIM

**Speaking:** Dole, Robert J. (D000401) [R-KS]

```
                         TRIBUTE TO CLAIRE CRIM

  Mr. DOLE. Mr. President, for 37 years, Senators, staffers, and 
members of the public who have dealt with the Senate Services 
Department have come into contact with Claire Crim.
  It is Claire who has welcomed staff and visitors, routed phone calls, 
filed work orders, and entered computer data. She has fulfilled all 
these duties and more with a great degree of skill and professionalism.
  Claire is retiring from her position as customer service/records 
specialist at the end of the month, and I join with all my colleagues 
in thanking her for her nearly four decades of services, and in wishing 
her a happy and healthy retirement.


                          ____________________
```
