# CHANGE OF VOTES

**Speaking:** Akaka, Daniel K. (A000069) [D-HI]

**Measures:** S. 440, H.R. 1058

```
                            CHANGE OF VOTES

  Mr. AKAKA. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that I be allowed 
to 

[[Page S9318]]
change my vote on final passage of H.R. 1058, vote No. 295, the 
Securities Reform Act of 1995. I voted in favor of the passage of the 
bill. It was my intention to vote ``no.'' This change in vote will not 
alter the outcome of the vote.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection? Without objection, it is 
so ordered.
  Mr. AKAKA. Mr. President, I also ask unanimous consent that I be 
allowed to change my June 20, 1995, vote on the motion to table the 
Lautenberg amendment, vote No. 270, relating to highway speed limits 
during the debate on S. 440, the National Highway System designation 
bill. I had inadvertently voted in support of the motion to table the 
amendment. I wish to be recorded as having voted against the motion to 
table the Lautenberg amendment. This change in vote will not alter the 
outcome of the original vote.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection? Without objection, it is 
so ordered.


                          ____________________
```
