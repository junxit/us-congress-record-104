# Text of House Amendment 33

**Speaking:** Volkmer, Harold L. (V000112) [D-MO]

**Measures:** H.R. 1905

```
H.R. 1905

                        Offered By: Mr. Volkmer

       Amendment No. 33: At the end of the bill, insert after the 
     last section (preceding the short title) the following new 
     section:
       Sec. 505. (a) Limitation on Use of Funds.--None of the 
     funds made available in this Act may be used for a spallation 
     neutron source.
       (b) Corresponding Reduction in Funds.--The amount otherwise 
     provided in this Act for ``Energy Supply, Research and 
     Development Activities'' is hereby reduced by $8,000,000.
```
