# Text of House Amendment 83

**Speaking:** Jackson Lee, Sheila (J000032) [D-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 1868

```
H.R. 1868

                      Offered By: Ms. Jackson-Lee

       Amendment No. 83: Page 78, after line 6, insert the 
     following new section:


                    limitation on funds for ethiopia

       Sec. 564. None of the funds appropriated in this Act may be 
     made available to the government of Ethiopia unless the State 
     Department monitors, during fiscal year 1996, the Ethiopian 
     government's human rights progress.
```
