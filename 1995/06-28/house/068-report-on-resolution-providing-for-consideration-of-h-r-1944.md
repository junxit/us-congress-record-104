# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 1944, RESCISSIONS AND DISASTER SUPPLEMENTAL ACT OF 1995

**Measures:** H.Res. 176, H.R. 1944

```
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 1944, 
           RESCISSIONS AND DISASTER SUPPLEMENTAL ACT OF 1995

  Mr. DREIER, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 104-166) on the resolution (H. Res. 176) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 1944) making emergency supplemental 
appropriations for additional disaster assistance, for antiterrorism 
initiatives, for assistance in the recovery from the tragedy that 
occurred at Oklahoma City, and making rescissions for the fiscal year 
ending September 30, 1995, and for other purposes which was referred to 
the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
