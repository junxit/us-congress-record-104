# ADJOURNMENT TO TUESDAY, MAY 9, 1995

**Speaking:** Owens, Major R. (O000159) [D-NY]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 58

```
                  ADJOURNMENT TO TUESDAY, MAY 9, 1995

  Mr. OWENS. Mr. Speaker, I move that the House do now adjourn.
  The motion was agreed to.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to the provisions of House 
Concurrent Resolution 58 of the 104th Congress, the House stands

debates.
  Thereupon (at 2 o'clock and 20 minutes p.m.), pursuant to House 
Concurrent Resolution 58, the House adjourned until Tuesday, May 9,
```
