# DISPENSING WITH CALENDAR WEDNESDAY BUSINESS ON WEDNESDAY NEXT

**Speaking:** Armey, Richard K. (A000217) [R-TX]

```
     DISPENSING WITH CALENDAR WEDNESDAY BUSINESS ON WEDNESDAY NEXT

  Mr. ARMEY. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that business in 
order under the Calendar Wednesday rule be dispensed with on Wednesday, 
May 10, 1995.

minutes for the courtesy they have extended Mr. Bonior, the Democratic
```
