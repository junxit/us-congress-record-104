# MEASURE READ THE SECOND TIME--S. 939

**Speaking:** Warner, John (W000154) [R-VA]

**Measures:** S. 939

```
                  MEASURE READ THE SECOND TIME--S. 939

  Mr. WARNER. Mr. President, I understand there is a bill at the desk 
that is due for its second reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will read the bill for the second 
time.
  The assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 939) to amend Title 18 United States Code to ban 
     partial-birth abortions.

  Mr. WARNER. Mr. President, at this time, under the instructions of 
the majority leader, I interpose an objection to further proceedings on 
this matter.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The bill will be placed on the calendar.

                          ____________________
```
