# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

**Speaking:** Chafee, John H. (C000269) [R-RI]

```
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET


             subcommittee on post office and civil service

  Mr. CHAFEE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Subcommittee on Post Office and Civil Service, Committee on 
Governmental Affairs, be authorized to meet during the session of the 
Senate on Monday, June 19, 1995, to review Federal pensions.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.


               subcommittee on taxation and irs oversight

  Mr. CHAFEE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Subcommittee on Taxation and IRS Oversight of the Committee on Finance 
be permitted to meet on Monday, June 19, 1995, beginning at 2 p.m. in 
room SD-215, to conduct a hearing on S corporation reform and the home 
office deduction.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  

                          ____________________
```
