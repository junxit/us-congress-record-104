Committee on Foreign Relations: Committee ordered favorably reported

Secretary of State for Diplomatic Security, Anthony Cecil Eden

Joseph Lane Kirkland, of the District of Columbia, and Jeanne 
Moutoussamy-Ashe, of New York, each to be a U.S. Alternate 
Representative, all to the 50th Session of the General Assembly of the 
United Nations.
