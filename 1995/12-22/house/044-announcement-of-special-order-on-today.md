# ANNOUNCEMENT OF SPECIAL ORDER ON TODAY

**Speaking:** Dornan, Robert K. (D000435) [R-CA]

```
                 ANNOUNCEMENT OF SPECIAL ORDER ON TODAY

  (Mr. DORNAN asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. DORNAN. Mr. Speaker, I am going to do a 60-minute special order 
in a few moments here after a few 5-minutes. I raced in this morning 
and missed the 1-minute.
  I just wanted to say that since there is a 50-50 chance we will not 
have any votes next week, and I hope to head off to Europe to visit 
with our troops in the field, I wanted to do a tribute for a half hour 
to our men and women in uniform today as we close out 1945, the last 
year of World War II.
  I also want to do a half hour on execution-style, a few inches from 
infanticide, partial-birth coupe de grace abortion. We may not think 
that is proper at this time of year, but on December 28, which we may 
miss, it is the Feast of the Holy Innocents to remind us of the Herod 
slaughter of innocent children, trying to kill the Messiah, whose birth 
many of us will celebrate next Monday.

                          ____________________
```
