# NOMINATIONS TO REMAIN IN STATUS QUO, WITH EXCEPTIONS

**Speaking:** Warner, John (W000154) [R-VA]

```
          NOMINATIONS TO REMAIN IN STATUS QUO, WITH EXCEPTIONS

  Mr. WARNER. Mr. President, as in executive session, I ask unanimous 
consent that all nominations received by the Senate remain in status 
quo, notwithstanding the provisions of Rule 31, paragraph 6, except the 
following:
  Henry Foster; PN234-2, Thomas J. Flanagan; PN343-2, five Navy 
promotions to Captain and below (list begins with Christopher J. 
Remshak); PN632-2, Navy Promotion of Margaret V. Abrashoff; PN628-2, 
Navy appointment to Lieutenant--Richard Drake.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
