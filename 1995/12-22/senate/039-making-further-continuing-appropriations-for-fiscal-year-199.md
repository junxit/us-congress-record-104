# MAKING FURTHER CONTINUING APPROPRIATIONS FOR FISCAL YEAR 1996

**Speaking:** Dole, Robert J. (D000401) [R-KS]

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 136

```
     MAKING FURTHER CONTINUING APPROPRIATIONS FOR FISCAL YEAR 1996

  Mr. DOLE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate turn 
to House Joint Resolution 136, a continuing resolution just received 
from the House; that the joint resolution be read a third time and 
passed; and that the motion to reconsider be laid upon the table.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  So the joint resolution (H. J. Res. 136) was read the third time and 
passed.
```
