# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Abraham, Spencer (A000355) [R-MI]

**Measures:** S. 908

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. ABRAHAM. Mr. President, for the information of all Senators, the 
Senate will begin consideration of the energy and water appropriations 
bill at 1:30 p.m. until 2 p.m. for opening statements.
  At 2 p.m., the Senate will resume S. 908, the State Department 
reorganization bill. A cloture motion was filed today. Therefore, 
Senators must file first-degree amendments to the State Department bill 
by 1 p.m. on Monday in order to qualify postcloture.
  Also, the majority leader has announced that no votes will occur on 
Monday prior to 6 p.m. However, amendments are expected to be offered 
to the State Department reorganization bill. Therefore, votes can be 
expected to occur into the evening.
  Also, the leader has announced the strong possibility that the Senate 
could be asked to be in session on Saturday, August 5, in order to 
complete the necessary business prior to the August recess.
  Also, the cloture vote on the State Department reorganization bill 
will occur on Tuesday, August 1, at a time to be determined by the two 
leaders.

                          ____________________
```
