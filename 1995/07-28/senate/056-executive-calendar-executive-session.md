**Speaking:** Abraham, Spencer (A000355) [R-MI]

                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mr. ABRAHAM. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to executive session to consider the following nominations: 
Executive Calendar nominations Nos. 259 through 267, and all 
nominations placed on the Secretary's desk in the Air Force, Army, and 
Navy.
  I further ask unanimous consent that the nominations be confirmed en 
bloc; that the motions to reconsider be laid upon the table en bloc; 
that any statements relating to the nominations appear at the 
appropriate place in the Record; that the President be immediately 
notified of the Senate's action; and that the Senate then return to 
legislative session.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
