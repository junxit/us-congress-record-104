# Text of House Amendment 75

**Speaking:** Fazio, Vic (F000053) [D-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 2099

```
H.R. 2099

                         Offered By: Mr. Fazio

       Amendment No. 75: Page 87, after line 25, insert the 
     following new title:

                    TITLE VI--ADDITIONAL PROVISIONS

                     DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS

                      Departmental Administration
```
