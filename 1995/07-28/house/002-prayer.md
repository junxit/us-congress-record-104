# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Rev. James David Ford, D.D., offered the following 
prayer:
  May our voices, O God, rise in praise of Your gifts to us and to 
every person; may our faces look to Your Word to learn the way of 
wisdom; may our hearts experience Your presence and the joy of Your 
forgiving grace; may our hands reach in friendship to all people and 
may our very souls be touched by the depths of Your healing and by the 
power of Your love. O gracious God, from whom all blessings flow, from 
whom we have begun and to whom we shall return, be with us this day we 
pray. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
