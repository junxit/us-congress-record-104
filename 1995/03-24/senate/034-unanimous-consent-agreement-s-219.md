# UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT--S. 219

**Speaking:** Dole, Robert J. (D000401) [R-KS]

**Measures:** S. 219

```
                  UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT--S. 219

  Mr. DOLE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that at 11:30 a.m., 
Monday, March 27, the Senate begin 6 hours of general debate equally 
divided in the usual form on the subject of S. 219, the regulatory 
moratorium bill; further that at the hour of 10 a.m., Tuesday, March 
28, the Senate proceed to the consideration of S. 219.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
