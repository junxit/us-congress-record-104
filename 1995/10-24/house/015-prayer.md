# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Rev. James David Ford, D.D., offered the following 
prayer:
  We know that Your words of grace and truth reign in all eternity, O 
God, and today we pray that those same words will live in our hearts 
and minds and souls. O gracious Creator, from whom we have come and to 
whom we shall return, we pray that Your message of good will and 
understanding, of life and peace, of faith and hope and love, will 
prevail not only in the wonders of the heavens, but lead us in our 
tasks, guide us in our thoughts, forgive us in our errors, and bring us 
in the way everlasting. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
