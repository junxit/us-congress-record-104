# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** H.R. 117, S. 1322

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 5, rule I, the Chair will 
now put the question on each question on which further proceedings were 
postponed earlier today in the order in which that question was 
entertained.
  Votes will be taken in the following order:

yeas and nays; and, No. 3, S. 1322 by the yeas and nays.
  The Chair will reduce to 5 minutes the time for any electronic vote 
after the first such vote in this series.
```
