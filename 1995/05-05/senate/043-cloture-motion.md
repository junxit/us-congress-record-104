# CLOTURE MOTION

**Speaking:** Coverdell, Paul (C000813) [R-GA]

**Measures:** H.R. 956

```
                             CLOTURE MOTION

  Mr. COVERDELL. Mr. President, I send a cloture motion to the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The cloture motion having been presented under 
rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We the undersigned Senators in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the pending 
     substitute amendment to H.R. 956, the product liability bill:
         Slade Gorton, Dan Coats, Richard G. Lugar, John Ashcroft, 
           Rod Grams, Kay Bailey Hutchison, Judd Gregg, Strom 
           Thurmond, Jay Rockefeller, Trent Lott, Rick Santorum, 
           Larry E. Craig, [[Page S6226]] Bob Smith, Don Nickles, 
           R.F. Bennett, John McCain, Connie Mack.

                          ____________________
```
