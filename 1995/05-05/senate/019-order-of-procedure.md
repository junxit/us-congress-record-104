# ORDER OF PROCEDURE

**Speaking:** Dole, Robert J. (D000401) [R-KS]

```
                           ORDER OF PROCEDURE

  Mr. DOLE. Mr. President, I say for the benefit of my colleagues and 
others who would probably like to get out of here, we are waiting for 
an amendment to be drafted. It is almost complete. Upon completion, it 
will be sent to the desk. Then there will be a cloture petition filed, 
and we will be able to leave for the day.
  It should not be long. I am told 15 or 20 minutes.

                          ____________________
```
