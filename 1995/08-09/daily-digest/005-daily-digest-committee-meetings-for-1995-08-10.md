# Daily Digest/COMMITTEE MEETINGS FOR 1995-08-10

**Measures:** S. 177, S. 699, S. 868, H.R. 2108

```
                    COMMITTEE MEETINGS FOR THURSDAY,

                            AUGUST 10, 1995

        (Committee meetings are open unless otherwise indicated)


                                 Senate

  Committee on Appropriations, Subcommittee on Labor, Health and 
Human Services, and Education, to hold hearings to examine proposed 
legislation relating to Title X (family planning) and other women's

1995 Emergency Appropriations Supplemental and Rescissions bill 
(P.L. 104-19), 9:30 a.m., SD-366.

  Committee on Foreign Relations, business meeting, to consider 
pending calendar business, 10 a.m., SD-419.
  Committee on Governmental Affairs, business meeting, to mark up S. 
177, to repeal the Ramspeck Act, S. 868, Federal Employees Emergency 
Leave Transfer Act, S. 699, Office of Government Ethics 
Authorization Act, H.R. 2108, District of Columbia Convention Center

authorizing funds for the Congressional Awards Board, and to

  Committee on the Judiciary, to hold hearings to examine United

SD-226.
  Special Committee To Investigate Whitewater Development

216.

a.m., 2128 Rayburn.
```
