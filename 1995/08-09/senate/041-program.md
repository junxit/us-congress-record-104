# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Hatfield, Mark O. (H000343) [R-OR]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. HATFIELD. Mr. President, for the information of all Senators, the 
Senate will resume consideration of the transportation appropriations 
bill at 9:10 a.m. tomorrow, with a rollcall vote occurring at 
approximately 9:15 or 9:20 a.m. Additional rollcall votes have been 
stacked, with the remaining stacked votes limited to 10 minutes in 
length. Also, the Senate will consider the DOD authorization bill and 
the DOD appropriations bill. All Members should expect a late night 
session on Thursday in order to make progress and possibly complete 
action on all of these bills.
```
