# Daily Digest/Senate Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
(Committees not listed did not meet)
SOCIAL SECURITY AND DISABILITY TRUST FUNDS
Committee on Finance: Subcommittee on Social Security and Family Policy 
held hearings to examine the 1995 Board of Trustees Annual Report of 
the Social Security and Disability Trust Funds, receiving testimony 
from Senator Kerrey; Shirley S. Chater, Commissioner, and Harry 
Ballantyne, Chief Actuary, both of the Social Security Administration, 
Department of Health and Human Services; and Stanford Ross, Washington, 
D.C., and David Walker, Atlanta, Georgia, both Public Trustees of the 
Social Security and Medicare Trust Funds.
  Hearings were recessed subject to call.
```
