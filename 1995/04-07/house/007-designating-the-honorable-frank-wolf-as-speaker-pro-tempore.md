# DESIGNATING THE HONORABLE FRANK WOLF AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS THROUGH MAY 1, 1995

```
  DESIGNATING THE HONORABLE FRANK WOLF AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO SIGN 
        ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS THROUGH MAY 1, 1995

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Speaker of the House of Representatives:

                                               Washington, DC,

                                                    April 7, 1995.
       I hereby designate the Honorable Frank R. Wolf to act as 
     Speaker pro tempore to sign enrolled bills and joint 
     resolutions through May 1, 1995.
                                                    Newt Gingrich,
                          Speaker of the House of Representatives.
     

                          ____________________
```
