# THE BAD DEBT BOXSCORE

**Speaking:** Helms, Jesse (H000463) [R-NC]

```
                         THE BAD DEBT BOXSCORE

  Mr. HELMS. Mr. President, the skyrocketing Federal debt is now 
slightly in excess of $13 billion shy of $5 trillion.
  As of the close of business Friday, November 24, the Federal debt--
down to the penny--stood at exactly $4,989,260,237,257.80 or $18,939.32 
on a per capita basis for every man, woman, and child.
```
