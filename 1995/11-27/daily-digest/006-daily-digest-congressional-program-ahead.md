# Daily Digest/CONGRESSIONAL PROGRAM AHEAD

**Measures:** H.R. 1058, S. 1224, S. 1394, S. 1396, S. 1423, H.R. 1868, H.R. 1977, H.R. 2076, H.R. 2099, H.R. 2131, H.R. 2519, H.R. 2525, H.R. 2546, H.R. 2564

                      CONGRESSIONAL PROGRAM AHEAD

              Week of November 28 through December 2, 1995

                             Senate Chamber

  On Tuesday, Senate may begin consideration of S. 1396, ICC Sunset 
Act.
  During the week, Senate may also consider conference reports (when 
available), and any cleared legislative business.

  (Senate will recess on Tuesday, November 28, 1995, from 12:30 p.m. 
until 2:15 p.m. for respective party conferences.)


                           Senate Committees

        (Committee meetings are open unless otherwise indicated)
  Committee on Armed Services: November 28, to hold hearings on the 
use of United States military forces to enforce the Bosnian peace 
agreement and the role of NATO and other foreign nations in the 
implementation force, 10:15 a.m., SD-G50.
  Committee on Energy and Natural Resources: November 29, 
Subcommittee on Forests and Public Land Management, to hold joint 
oversight hearings with the House Resources Committee on the 
Administration's implementation of section 2001 of the Funding 
Rescissions Act of 1995, 9:30 a.m., SD-366.
  November 30, Full Committee, business meeting, to consider pending 
calendar business, 9:30 a.m., SD-366.
  Committee on Governmental Affairs: November 29, Subcommittee on 
Oversight of Government Management and The District of Columbia, to 
hold hearings on S. 1224, to amend subchapter IV of chapter 5 of 
title 5, United States Code, relating to alternative means of 
dispute resolution in the administrative process, 9:30 a.m., SD-342.
  Committee on the Judiciary: November 29, Subcommittee on 
Immigration, business meeting, to mark up S. 1394, to reform the 
legal immigration of immigrants and nonimmigrants to the United 
States, 9:30 a.m., SR-385.
  November 29, Subcommittee on Antitrust, Business Rights, and 
Competition, to hold hearings on issues relating to franchise 
relocation in professional sports, 10 a.m., SD-226.
  November 30, Full Committee, business meeting, to consider pending 
calendar business, 10 a.m., SD-226.
  November 30, Full Committee, to hold hearings on pending 
nominations, 2 p.m., SD-226.
  November 30, Subcommittee on Immigration, business meeting, to 
continue to mark up S. 1394, to reform the legal immigration of 
immigrants and nonimmigrants to the United States, 2 p.m., SR-385.
  Committee on Labor and Human Resources: November 29, to hold 
hearings on S. 1423, to amend the Occupational Safety and Health Act 
of 1970 to make modifications to certain provisions, 9:30 a.m., SD-
430.
  Select Committee on Intelligence: November 28, 29 and 30, to hold 
closed hearings on intelligence matters, Tuesday at 2 p.m., 
Wednesday at 4:30 p.m. and Thursday at 9:30 a.m., SH-219.
  Special Committee To Investigate Whitewater Development 
Corporation and Related Matters: November 28, 29 and 30 and December 
1, to resume hearings to examine certain issues relative to the 
Whitewater Development Corporation, 10 a.m., SH-216.
