# NOTICE OF HEARING

**Speaking:** Roth Jr., William V. (R000460) [R-DE]

```
                           NOTICE OF HEARING


                   Committee on governmental affairs

  Mr. ROTH. Mr. President, today I am pleased to announce that the 
Senate Committee on Governmental Affairs will hold a joint hearing with 
the House Committee on Government Reform and Oversight on Thursday, 
January 12, 1994, at 10 a.m. in the Rayburn House Office Building, room 
2154. This joint House-Senate hearing will concern the legislative 
line-item veto issue. Expert witnesses will testify on the necessity 
for such legislation.
```
