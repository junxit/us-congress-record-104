# SENATE RESOLUTION 19--RELATIVE TO COMMITTEE FUNDING

**Measures:** S.Res. 19

```
          SENATE RESOLUTION 19--RELATIVE TO COMMITTEE FUNDING

  Mr. DOLE (for himself, Mr. Daschle, Mr. Domenici, Mr. Mack, and Mr. 
Nickles) submitted the following resolution; which was indefinitely 
postponed:

                               S. Res. 19

       Resolved, It is the sense of the Senate that the Committee 
     on Rules and Administration when it reports the committee 
     funding resolution for 1995-96 it should reduce funding for 
     committees by 15 percent from the level provided for 1993-94.
```
