# SENATE RESOLUTION 6--ELECTING SHEILA BURKE AS THE SECRETARY OF THE SENATE

```
  SENATE RESOLUTION 6--ELECTING SHEILA BURKE AS THE SECRETARY OF THE 
                                 SENATE

agreed to:
```
