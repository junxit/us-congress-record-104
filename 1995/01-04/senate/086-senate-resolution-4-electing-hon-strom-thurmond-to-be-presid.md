# SENATE RESOLUTION 4--ELECTING HON. STROM THURMOND TO BE PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE OF THE SENATE

**Measures:** S.Res. 4

```
 SENATE RESOLUTION 4--ELECTING HON. STROM THURMOND TO BE PRESIDENT PRO 
                         TEMPORE OF THE SENATE

  Mr. DOLE (for himself and Mr. Byrd) submitted the following 
resolution, which was considered and agreed to:

                               S. Res. 4

       Resolved, That the Honorable Strom Thurmond, a Senator from 
     the state of South Carolina, be and he is hereby, elected 
     President of the Senate pro tempore, to hold office during 
     the pleasure of the Senate, in accordance with rule I, 
     paragraph 1, of the Standing Rules of the Senate.
     

                          ____________________
```
