# SENATE RESOLUTION 3--FIXING THE HOUR OF THE DAILY MEETING OF THE SENATE

**Measures:** S.Res. 3

```
SENATE RESOLUTION 3--FIXING THE HOUR OF THE DAILY MEETING OF THE SENATE

  Mr. COCHRAN submitted the following resolution, which was considered 
and agreed to:

                               S. Res. 3

       Resolved, That the hour of daily meeting of the Senate be 
     12 o'clock meridian unless otherwise ordered.

                          ____________________
```
