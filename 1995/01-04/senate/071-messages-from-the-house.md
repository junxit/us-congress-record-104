# MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.Res. 2, H.Res. 3

```
                        MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

  At 3:03 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mr. Hays, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
agreed to the following resolutions:

       H. Res. 2. Resolution informing the Senate that a quorum of 
     the House of Representatives has assembled.
       H. Res. 3. Resolution notifying the President of the United 
     States that a quorum of each House has assembled and Congress 
     is ready to receive any communication that he may be pleased 
     to make.

                          ____________________
```
