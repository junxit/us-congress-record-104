# ADMINISTRATION OF OATH TO THE SECRETARY OF THE SENATE

```
         ADMINISTRATION OF OATH TO THE SECRETARY OF THE SENATE

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. The Secretary of the Senate will be 
escorted to the desk for the oath of office.
  The Honorable Sheila Burke, escorted by the Honorable Martha Pope, 
advanced to the desk of the President pro tempore; the oath prescribed 
by law was administered to her by the President pro tempore.
  [Applause, Senators rising.]
```
