# SENATE RESOLUTION 5--NOTIFYING THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES OF THE ELECTION OF A PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** S.Res. 5

```
 SENATE RESOLUTION 5--NOTIFYING THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES OF 
                THE ELECTION OF A PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

   Mr. DOLE (for himself and Mr. Byrd) submitted the following 
resolution, which was considered and agreed to:

                               S. Res. 5

       Resolved, That the President of the United States be 
     notified of the election of the Honorable Strom Thurmond, a 
     Senator from the State of South Carolina, as President pro 
     tempore.

                          ____________________
```
