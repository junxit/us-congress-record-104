# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there will now 
be a period for the transaction of morning business not to extend 
beyond the hour of 2 p.m. with Senators permitted to speak therein for 
not to exceed 10 minutes each. Under the previous order, the Senator 
from North Dakota [Mr. Conrad] is to be recognized to speak for up to 
15 minutes.
  Mr. GORTON addressed the Chair.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Burns). The Senator from Washington is 
recognized.
```
