# APPOINTMENT BY THE PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

```
                APPOINTMENT BY THE PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair, on behalf of the President pro 
tempore, and upon the recommendation of the Republican leader, pursuant 
to 22 U.S.C. 276l, appoints the Senator from Alaska [Mr. Stevens] as 
chairman of the Senate delegation to the British-American 
Interparliamentary Group during the 104th Congress.
```
