# APPOINTMENT BY THE PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

```
                APPOINTMENT BY THE PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair announces on behalf of the President 
pro tempore of the Senate and the Speaker of the House of 
Representatives, pursuant to section 201 (a)(2) of Public Law 93-344, 
the appointment of Ms. June Ellenoff O'Neill as Director of the 
Congressional Budget Office for the term of office beginning on January 
3, 1995, effective March 1, 1995.
  Mr. HATCH addressed the Chair.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator from Utah is recognized.

                          ____________________
```
