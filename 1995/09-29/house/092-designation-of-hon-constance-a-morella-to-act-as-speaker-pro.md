# DESIGNATION OF HON. CONSTANCE A. MORELLA TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS THROUGH OCTOBER 10, 1995

```
DESIGNATION OF HON. CONSTANCE A. MORELLA TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE 
 TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS THROUGH OCTOBER 10, 1995

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Speaker of the House:


                                               Washington, DC,

                                               September 29, 1995.
       I hereby designate the Honorable Constance A. Morella to 
     act as Speaker pro tempore to sign enrolled bills and joint 
     resolutions through October 10, 1995.
                                                    Newt Gingrich,
     Speaker of the House of Representatives.

                          ____________________
```
