# AUTHORIZING THE SPEAKER TO DECLARE RECESSES AT ANY TIME ON WEDNESDAY, OCTOBER 11, 1995

**Speaking:** Shays, Christopher (S001144) [R-CT]

```
 AUTHORIZING THE SPEAKER TO DECLARE RECESSES AT ANY TIME ON WEDNESDAY, 
                            OCTOBER 11, 1995

  Mr. SHAYS. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that it may be in 
order for the Speaker to declare recesses at any time on Wednesday, 
October 11, 1995, for the purpose of a joint meeting to commemorate the 
50th anniversary of World War II.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Connecticut?
  There was no objection.
```
