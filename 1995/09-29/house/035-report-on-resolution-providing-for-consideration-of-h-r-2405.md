**Measures:** H.Res. 234, H.R. 2405

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2405, OMNIBUS 
               CIVILIAN SCIENCE AUTHORIZATION ACT OF 1995

  Mr. LINDER, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 104-270) on the resolution (H. Res. 234) providing 
for the consideration of the bill (H.R. 2405) to authorize
```
