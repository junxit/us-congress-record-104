# TRIBUTE TO MARCIA SMITH

**Speaking:** Bereuter, Doug (B000403) [R-NE]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO MARCIA SMITH

  (Mr. BEREUTER asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. BEREUTER. Madam Speaker, I rise today to pay tribute and 
commendation to an exceptional public servant and a member of the 
congressional staff. After nearly exactly 10 years on my staff, my 
scheduler and personal secretary, Marcia Smith, will be leaving today. 
She has been prototypically an exceptional congressional employee, and 
I wanted to bring that fact to the attention of the House. Her 
performance here, her skill, the way she has met people from across the 
world who have come through my office, has been absolutely an 
outstanding reflection upon this institution, on this Member, and on my 
constituents in Nebraska.
  I thank her for her tremendous service in what is undoubtedly one of 
the most hectic jobs for all of us, the scheduler-secretary. Her 
performance here has been exceptional, and I will miss her greatly in 
my work and life. We wish her well and great success and joy in her new 
career and life in Chicago. Good luck to you, Marcia.

                          ____________________
```
