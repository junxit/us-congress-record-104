# THE BAD DEBT BOXSCORE

**Speaking:** Helms, Jesse (H000463) [R-NC]

```
                         THE BAD DEBT BOXSCORE

  Mr. HELMS. Mr. President, the skyrocketing Federal debt is now 
slightly in excess of $11 billion shy of $5 trillion.
  As of the close of business Thursday, December 7, the Federal debt--
down to the penny--stood at exactly $4,989,071,101,377.59 or $18,938.60 
on a per capita basis for every man, woman, and child.
```
