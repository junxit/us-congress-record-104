# REQUEST FOR PERMISSION TO ADDRESS THE HOUSE FOR 1 MINUTE

**Speaking:** Deutsch, Peter (D000275) [D-FL], Tiahrt, Todd (T000260) [R-KS]

```
        REQUEST FOR PERMISSION TO ADDRESS THE HOUSE FOR 1 MINUTE

  Mr. DEUTSCH. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that I be able to 
address the House for 1 minute on this particular issue.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Florida?
  Mr. TIAHRT. Reserving the right to object, Mr. Speaker, do we not 
have 5 minutes coming up now where everybody is going to get a turn to 
speak?
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The Chair will entertain 1-minute speech 
requests.
  Mr. TIAHRT. I object, Mr. Speaker. Let us go to the 5 minutes and 
continue the business of the House.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Objection is heard.

                          ____________________
```
