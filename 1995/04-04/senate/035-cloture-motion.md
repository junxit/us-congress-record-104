# CLOTURE MOTION

**Speaking:** Dole, Robert J. (D000401) [R-KS]

**Measures:** H.R. 1158

```
                             CLOTURE MOTION

  Mr. DOLE. Mr. President, I send a cloture motion to the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The cloture motion having been presented under 
rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.
  The assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     hereby move to bring to a close debate on the Hatfield 
     amendment No. 420 to H.R. 1158, the supplemental 
     appropriations bill, signed by 17 Senators as follows:
         Senators Mark Hatfield, Pete Domenici, Rick Santorum, 
           Larry Pressler, Mitch McConnell, Slade Gorton, Rod 
           Grams, Ben Nighthorse Campbell, Conrad Burns, Mike 
           DeWine, Nancy Kassebaum, Ted Stevens, Jesse Helms, 
           Robert F. Bennett, Spencer Abraham, Dirk Kempthorne, 
           and Fred Thompson.
           

                          ____________________
```
