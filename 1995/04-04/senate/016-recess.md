# RECESS

**Speaking:** Dole, Robert J. (D000401) [R-KS]

```
                                 RECESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the Senate will 
stand in recess until the hour of 2:15 p.m.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 12:15 p.m., recessed until 2:23 p.m.; 
whereupon, the Senate reassembled when called to order by the Vice 
President.
  The VICE PRESIDENT. Senators will please take their seats, clear the 
aisles, and cease audible conversation.
  Mr. DOLE addressed the Chair.
  The VICE PRESIDENT. The majority leader.
  Mr. DOLE. Mr. President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The VICE PRESIDENT. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. DOLE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Thomas). Without objection, it is so 
ordered.

                          ____________________
```
