# REMOVAL OF NAMES OF MEMBERS AS COSPONSORS OF H.R. 42

**Speaking:** Waxman, Henry A. (W000215) [D-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 42

```
          REMOVAL OF NAMES OF MEMBERS AS COSPONSORS OF H.R. 42

  Mr. WAXMAN. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent to delete the names 
of the gentleman from Minnesota [Mr. Oberstar], the gentleman from 
California [Mr. Bono], and the gentlewoman from Florida [Ms. Ros-
Lehtinen] as cosponsors of the bill, H.R. 42, the Ryan White 
Reauthorization Act of 1995.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from California?
  There was no objection.
```
