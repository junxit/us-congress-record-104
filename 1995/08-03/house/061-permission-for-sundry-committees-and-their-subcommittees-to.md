# PERMISSION FOR SUNDRY COMMITTEES AND THEIR SUBCOMMITTEES TO SIT FOR BALANCE OF WEEK DURING THE 5-MINUTE RULE

**Speaking:** Armey, Richard K. (A000217) [R-TX], Watt, Melvin L. (W000207) [D-NC]

```
  PERMISSION FOR SUNDRY COMMITTEES AND THEIR SUBCOMMITTEES TO SIT FOR 
                BALANCE OF WEEK DURING THE 5-MINUTE RULE

  Mr. ARMEY. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that all committees 
and their subcommittees be allowed to sit for the balance of the week 
while the House is under the 5-minute rule with the exception of the 
Committee on Resources.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. LaHood). Is there objection to the 
request of the gentleman from Texas?
  Mr. WATT. Reserving the right to object. Mr. Speaker, and I will not 
object, I am advised by the Democratic leadership that they have 
consented to the request.
  Mr. Speaker, I withdraw my reservation of objection.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Texas?
  There was no objection.
  

                          ____________________
```
