# RECESS UNTIL 9 A.M. TOMORROW

**Speaking:** Warner, John (W000154) [R-VA]

```
                      RECESS UNTIL 9 A.M. TOMORROW

  Mr. WARNER. Mr. President, seeing no Senators desiring to be 
recognized for the purpose of morning business, and since there is no 
further business to come before the Senate, I now ask unanimous consent 
that the Senate stand in recess under the previous order.

Friday, August 4, 1995, at 9 a.m.
```
