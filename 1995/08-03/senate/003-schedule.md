# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

**Measures:** S. 1026

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, this morning the leader time has been 
reserved and the Senate will resume consideration of S. 1026, the 
Department of Defense authorization bill. Under the order, Senator 
Dorgan is to be recognized to offer an amendment regarding the national 
missiles defense. That amendment is limited to a 90-minute time 
limitation. Therefore, Senators may anticipate a rollcall vote at 
approximately at 10:30 a.m. if all debate time is used. Additional 
rollcall votes are expected throughout the day today and the Senate is 
expected to remain in session into the evening.
  I yield the floor, Mr. President, so that Senator Dorgan and others 
might be recognized.

                          ____________________
```
