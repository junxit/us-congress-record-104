# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Rev. James David Ford, D.D., offered the following 
prayer:
  Remind us, O God, of the privilege of public service, a calling to 
represent people with wisdom and diligence and perseverance. May not 
the difficulties of any time cause us to lose the vision of those goals 
that unite us as one people, of those ideals that express our 
expectations and our yearning for justice. Give each person, gracious 
God, the strength of character and the integrity of the heart, that 
will enable us to be the people You would have us be. Bless us this day 
and every day, we pray. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
