# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Rev. James David Ford, D.D., offered the following 
prayer:
  May the glory of Your word, O God, be heard in every heart and every 
land; may that word bring gladness to people who seek hope and 
confidence and health in their daily lives; may that word remind of 
truth and integrity and honesty; may that word direct to the ways of 
peace and knowledge, and may that word of faith lift every person who 
yearns for justice and freedom. Bless us this day and every day, we 
pray. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
