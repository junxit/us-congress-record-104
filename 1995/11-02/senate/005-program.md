# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Murkowski, Frank H. (M001085) [R-AK]

**Measures:** S. 1372

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. MURKOWSKI. Mr. President, the leader has asked me to communicate 
this news to the Senate this morning. I am told that there will be a 
period for the transaction of morning business until 12 noon.
  Following morning business, the majority leader has stated that it 
will be his intention to begin consideration of S. 1372 regarding the 
Social Security earnings limit.
  The Senate may also be asked to begin consideration of the 
legislative branch appropriations bill during today's session.
  As usual, all Senators should anticipate rollcall votes throughout 
the day and possibly well into the night.

                          ____________________
```
