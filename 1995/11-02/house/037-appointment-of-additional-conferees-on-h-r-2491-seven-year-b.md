# APPOINTMENT OF ADDITIONAL CONFEREES ON H.R. 2491, SEVEN-YEAR BALANCED BUDGET RECONCILIATION ACT OF 1995

```
 APPOINTMENT OF ADDITIONAL CONFEREES ON H.R. 2491, SEVEN-YEAR BALANCED 
                   BUDGET RECONCILIATION ACT OF 1995

authority granted in clause 6 of rule X, the Speaker appoints as
```
