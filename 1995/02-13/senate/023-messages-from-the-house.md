# MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.R. 668, H.R. 729

```
                        MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

  At 4 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered by 
Mr. Hays, one of its reading clerks, announced that the Speaker has 
signed the following bills, in which it requests the concurrence of the 
Senate:

       H.R. 668. An act to control crime by further streamlining 
     deportation of criminal aliens; and
       H.R. 729. An act to control crime by a more effective death 
     penalty.
```
