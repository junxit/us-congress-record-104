# BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

**Measures:** H.R. 1944

```
                    BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

approval, bills of the House of the following title:

           On July 21, 1995:

     appropriations for additional disaster assistance, for anti-
     terrorism initiatives, for assistance in the recovery from
```
