# Text of House Amendment 18

**Speaking:** Klug, Scott L. (K000274) [R-WI]

**Measures:** H.R. 2076

```
H.R. 2076

                          Offered By: Mr. Klug

       Amendment No. 18: Page 45, lines 18 through 22, strike 
     ``for the repair, acquisition, leasing, or conversion of 
     vessels, including related equipment to maintain and

     modernize the existing fleet and to continue planning the modernization 
     of the fleet, for the National Oceanic and Atmospheric 
     Administration,'' and insert ``for the National Oceanic and 
     Atmospheric Administration for entering into contracts with 
     private-sector parties or universities for (1) data 
     collection and (2) the leasing or chartering of vessels,''.
```
