# Text of House Amendment 3

**Speaking:** Klug, Scott L. (K000274) [R-WI]

**Measures:** H.R. 2099

```
H.R. 2099

                          Offered By: Mr. Klug

       Amendment No. 3: Page 48, after line 25, insert the 
     following new section:

     SEC. 211. DEMONSTRATION PROJECT FOR ELIMINATION OF TAKE-ONE-
                   TAKE-ALL REQUIREMENT.

       In order to demonstrate the effects of eliminating the 
     requirement under section 

[[Page H7552]]
     8(t) of the United States Housing Act of 1937, notwithstanding any 
     assistance provided under any program under section 8 of such 
     Act for the multifamily housing project consisting of the 
     dwelling units located at 2401-2479 Sommerset Circle, in 
     Madison, Wisconsin, or on behalf of residents in such 
     project, section 8(t) of such Act shall not apply with 
     respect to such project.
```
