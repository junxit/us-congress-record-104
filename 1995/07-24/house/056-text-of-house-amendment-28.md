# Text of House Amendment 28

**Speaking:** Andrews, Robert E. (A000210) [D-NJ]

**Measures:** H.R. 2002

```
H.R. 2002

                        Offered By: Mr. Andrews

       Amendment No. 28: At the end of the bill, add the following 
     new title:

                 TITLE V--ADDITIONAL GENERAL PROVISIONS

       Sec. 501. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used for planning or execution of the military airport 
     program.
```
