**Measures:** S. 395, S. 440

```
   APPOINTMENT OF CONFEREES IN LIEU OF CONFEREE ON S. 440, NATIONAL 
    HIGHWAY SYSTEM DESIGNATION ACT OF 1995 AND S. 395, ALASKA POWER 
             ADMINISTRATION ASSET SALE AND TERMINATION ACT

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Sharp). Without objection, the Chair

change in conferees.
```
