# ELECTION OF MEMBER TO COMMITTEE ON ECONOMIC AND EDUCATIONAL OPPORTUNITIES

**Speaking:** Hoyer, Steny H. (H000874) [D-MD]

**Measures:** H.Res. 236

```
      ELECTION OF MEMBER TO COMMITTEE ON ECONOMIC AND EDUCATIONAL 
                             OPPORTUNITIES

  Mr. HOYER. Mr. Speaker, I offer a privileged resolution (H. Res. 236) 
and ask for its immediate consideration.
  The Clerk read the resolution, as follows:

                          House Resolution 236

       Resolved, That the following named Member be, and is 
     hereby, elected to the following standing committee of the 
     House of Representatives:
       To the Committee on Economic and Educational Opportunities: 
     the following Member: Chaka Fattah of Pennsylvania.
  The resolution was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```
