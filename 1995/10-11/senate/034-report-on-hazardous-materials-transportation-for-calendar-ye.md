# REPORT ON HAZARDOUS MATERIALS TRANSPORTATION FOR CALENDAR YEARS 1992- 93--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT--PM 87

```
 REPORT ON HAZARDOUS MATERIALS TRANSPORTATION FOR CALENDAR YEARS 1992-
                 93--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT--PM 87

  The PRESIDING OFFICER laid before the Senate the following message 
from the President of the United States, together with an accompanying 
report; which was referred to the Committee on Commerce, Science, and 
Transportation.

To the Congress of the United States:
  In accordance with Public Law 103-272, as amended (49 U.S.C. 
5121(e)), I transmit herewith the Biennial Report on Hazardous 
Materials Transportation for Calendar Years 1992-1993 of the Department 
of Transportation.
                                                  William J. Clinton.  
  The White House, October 11, 1995.

                          ____________________
```
