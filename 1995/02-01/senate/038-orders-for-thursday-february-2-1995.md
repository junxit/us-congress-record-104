# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, FEBRUARY 2, 1995

**Speaking:** Hatch, Orrin G. (H000338) [R-UT]

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 1

```
                 ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, FEBRUARY 2, 1995

  Mr. HATCH. I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate completes its 
business today, it stand in recess until the hour of 9:30 a.m. on 
Thursday, February 2, 1995; that following the prayer, the Journal of 
proceedings be deemed approved to date, and the time for the two 
leaders be reserved for their use later in the day; that there then be 
a period for the transaction of morning business not to extend beyond 
the hour of 10:30 a.m., with Senators permitted to speak for not more 
than 5 minutes each, with the following Senators to speak for up to the 
designated times: Senator Murkowski, 20 minutes; Senator Conrad, 15 
minutes; Senator Dorgan, 10 minutes; Senator Campbell, 10 minutes.
  I further ask unanimous consent that at 10:30 a.m. the Senate resume 
consideration of House Joint Resolution 1, the constitutional balanced 
budget amendment.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection? Without objection, it is 
so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
