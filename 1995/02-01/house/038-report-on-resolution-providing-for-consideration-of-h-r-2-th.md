# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2, THE LINE- ITEM VETO ACT

**Measures:** H.R. 2, H.Res. 55

```
 REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2, THE LINE-
                             ITEM VETO ACT

  Mr. GOSS, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged report 
(Rept. No. 104-15) on the resolution (H. Res. 55) providing for the 
consideration of the bill (H.R. 2) to give the President item veto 
authority over appropriations acts and targeted tax benefits in revenue 
acts, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be 
printed.
```
