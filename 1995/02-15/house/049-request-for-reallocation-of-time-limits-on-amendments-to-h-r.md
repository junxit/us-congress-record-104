# REQUEST FOR REALLOCATION OF TIME LIMITS ON AMENDMENTS TO H.R. 7 TO BE OFFERED BY MR. TRAFICANT, MR. ENGEL, AND MR. SKELTON

**Speaking:** Traficant, James A., Jr. (T000350) [D-OH], Berman, Howard L. (B000410) [D-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 7

```
 REQUEST FOR REALLOCATION OF TIME LIMITS ON AMENDMENTS TO H.R. 7 TO BE 
          OFFERED BY MR. TRAFICANT, MR. ENGEL, AND MR. SKELTON

  Mr. TRAFICANT. Mr. Chairman, I ask unanimous consent that the 
amendment to be offered by myself, the amendment to be offered by the 
gentleman from New York [Mr. Engel], and the amendment to be offered by 
the gentleman from Missouri [Mr. Skelton], be considered in a time 
frame tomorrow not in excess of a total of 10 minutes or 9 minutes, 3 
minutes for each amendment.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Ohio?
  Mr. BERMAN. Mr. Speaker, I object.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Objection is heard.
```
