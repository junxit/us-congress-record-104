# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Dole, Robert J. (D000401) [R-KS]

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. DOLE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that there now be a 
period for the transaction of morning business, not to extend beyond 
the hour of 9:30 p.m., with Senators permitted to speak for up to 5 
minutes each.

  Mr. DOLE. I suggest the absence of a quorum
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. DOLE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  [[Page S2755]] The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so
```
