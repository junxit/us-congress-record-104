# APPOINTMENT OF MS. JUNE ELLENOFF O'NEILL AS DIRECTOR OF THE CONGRESSIONAL BUDGET OFFICE

```
      APPOINTMENT OF MS. JUNE ELLENOFF O'NEILL AS DIRECTOR OF THE 
                      CONGRESSIONAL BUDGET OFFICE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to the provisions of section 
201(a)(2) of the Congressional Budget and Impoundment Control Act of 
1974, Public Law 93-344, the Chair announces that the Speaker and the 
President pro tempore of the Senate on Wednesday, February 22, 1995 did 
jointly appoint Ms. June Ellenoff O'Neill as director of the 
Congressional Budget Office, effective March 1, 1995, for the term of 
office beginning January 3, 1995.
```
