# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Harman, Jane (H000213) [D-CA]

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION
  Ms. HARMAN. Mr. Speaker, I was unavoidably absent earlier this 
afternoon for several votes. Had I been present, I would have voted 
``no'' on rollcall 566, the Wolf amendment.
  I would have voted ``yea'' on rollcall 567, the Coleman amendment.
  I would have voted ``no'' on rollcall 568, the Andrews amendment.
  I would have voted ``no'' on rollcall 569, sustaining the ruling of 
the Chair.
  And, I would have voted ``no'' on rollcall 570, final passage of the 
Transportation appropriations bill.
  I ask unanimous consent that my statement appear immediately after 
the votes.


                          ____________________
```
