# Text of House Amendment 50

**Speaking:** Kim, Jay (K000181) [R-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 2076

```
H.R. 2076

                          Offered by: Mr. Kim

       Amendment No. 50: At the end of the bill, insert after the 
     last section (preceding the short title) the following new 
     section:
       Sec. 609. None of the funds made available in this Act for 
     the Department of State may be used to permit or facilitate 
     making local currencies available to Members and employees of 
     the Congress to travel to North Korea except when it is made 
     known to the Federal official having authority to obligate or 
     expend such funds that North Korea does not have a policy of 
     discriminating, on the basis of national origin or political 
     philosophy, against Members and employees of the Congress in 
     permitting travel to North Korea.
```
