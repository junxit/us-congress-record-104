# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

```

filed on the antiterrorism bill earlier today by the leader. Therefore, 
Members should be aware that if they have an amendment on the list they

under rule XXII. Senators should be aware that rollcall votes are
```
