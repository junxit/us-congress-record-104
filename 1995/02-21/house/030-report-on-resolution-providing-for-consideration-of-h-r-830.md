# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 830, PAPERWORK REDUCTION ACT OF 1995

**Measures:** H.Res. 91, H.R. 830

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 830, PAPERWORK 
                         REDUCTION ACT OF 1995

  Mr. LINDER, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 104-43) on the resolution (H. Res. 91) providing for 
the consideration of the bill (H.R. 830) to amend chapter 35 of title 
44, United States Code, to 
[[Page H1954]] further the goals of the Paperwork Reduction Act to have 
Federal agencies become more responsible and publicly accountable for 
reducing the burden of Federal paperwork on the public, and for other 
purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be 
printed.
```
