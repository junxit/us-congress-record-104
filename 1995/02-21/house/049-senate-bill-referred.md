# SENATE BILL REFERRED

**Speaking:** Christensen, Jon (C000377) [R-NE]

**Measures:** S. 257

```
                          SENATE BILL REFERRED

  A bill of the Senate of the following title was taken from the 
Speaker's table and, under the rule, referred as follows:

       S. 257. An act to amend the charter of the Veterans of 
     Foreign Wars to make eligible for membership those veterans 
     that have served within the territorial limits of South 
     Korea; to the Committee on the Judiciary.
                       [[Page H1967]] ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. CHRISTENSEN. Madam Speaker, I move that the House do now adjourn.
  The motion was agreed to; accordingly (at 11 o'clock and 54 minutes 
p.m.), the House adjourned until tomorrow, Wednesday, February 22, 
1995, at 11 a.m.
```
