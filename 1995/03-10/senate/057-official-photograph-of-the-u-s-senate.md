# OFFICIAL PHOTOGRAPH OF THE U.S. SENATE

**Speaking:** Gregg, Judd (G000445) [R-NH]

```
                 OFFICIAL PHOTOGRAPH OF THE U.S. SENATE

  Mr. GREGG. Mr. President, the official photograph of the U.S. Senate 
in session will be taken by the National Geographic Society on Tuesday, 
April 4, 1995, at 2:15 p.m. All Senators are now on notice to be on the 
floor at 2:15 on April 4 for the picture.
```
