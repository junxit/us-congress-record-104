# MEASURE READ FOR THE FIRST TIME--H.R. 988

**Speaking:** Gregg, Judd (G000445) [R-NH]

**Measures:** H.R. 988

```
               MEASURE READ FOR THE FIRST TIME--H.R. 988

  Mr. GREGG. Mr. President, I inquire of the Chair if H.R. 988 has 
arrived from the House of Representatives.
  [[Page S3819]] The PRESIDING OFFICER. It has arrived.
  Mr. GREGG. Therefore, I ask for its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 988) to reform the Federal civil justice 
     system.

  Mr. GREGG. I now ask for the second reading, and I object.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard.
  Mr. GREGG. The bill will remain at the desk to be read a second time 
following the next adjournment of the Senate.

                          ____________________
```
