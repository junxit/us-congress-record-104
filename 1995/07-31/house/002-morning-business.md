# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to the order of the House of May 
12, 1995, the Chair will now recognize Members from lists submitted by 
the majority and minority leaders for morning hour debates.
  The Chair will alternate recognition between the parties with each 
party limited to not to exceed 30 minutes and each Member other than 
the majority and minority leader limited to 5 minutes.
  The Chair recognizes the gentleman from Oregon [Mr. Wyden] for 5 
minutes.
```
