# ORDER OF PROCEDURE

**Speaking:** Dole, Robert J. (D000401) [R-KS], Boxer, Barbara (B000711) [D-CA]

```
                           ORDER OF PROCEDURE

  Mr. DOLE. Mr. President, as I understand it, the Senator from 
California wishes to speak for 10 minutes and the Senator from Rhode 
Island for 10 minutes.
  So I ask unanimous consent that if there is no further business to 
come before the Senate, the Senate stand in recess under the previous 
order after the completion of the remarks by the Senator from 
California, Senator Boxer, and the remarks of the Senator from Rhode 
Island, Senator Pell.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. DeWine). Without objection, it is so 
ordered.
  Under the order, the Senator from California is recognized.
  Mrs. BOXER. Thank you very much, Mr. President.
```
