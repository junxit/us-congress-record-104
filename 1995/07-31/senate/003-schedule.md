# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Dole, Robert J. (D000401) [R-KS]

**Measures:** S. 908, H.R. 1905

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. DOLE. I thank the President pro tempore. Let me explain to my 
colleagues, leaders' time has been reserved, and there will be a period 
for morning business until 1:30 p.m. At 1:30 p.m, we will begin 
consideration of H.R. 1905, the energy and water appropriations bill, 
for opening statements until 2 p.m. today.
  It may be possible, unless there is an objection, to proceed on that 
for a little bit beyond 2 p.m, depending on whether or not we are 
prepared or ready to resume consideration of S. 908, the State 
Department reorganization bill.
  Cloture was filed on that bill on Friday. A cloture vote will occur 
tomorrow. I think perhaps it will be tomorrow morning sometime prior to 
the policy luncheon of both sides of the aisle.
  First-degree amendments must be filed by 1 p.m. in order to qualify 
under the postcloture. There will be no votes today before 6 p.m. There 
could be votes depending on what happens with S. 908. There will be no 
votes on anything with reference to H.R. 1905.

                          ____________________
```
