# RECESS

**Speaking:** Helms, Jesse (H000463) [R-NC], Rockefeller, John D., IV (R000361) [D-WV]

```
                                 RECESS

  Mr. HELMS. Mr. President, I believe there is no Senator seeking 
recognition. On behalf of the majority leader, I ask unanimous consent 
the Senate stand in recess until the hour of 3 p.m. today.
  There being no objection, at 2:09 p.m., the Senate recessed until 3 
p.m.; whereupon, the Senate reassembled when called to order by the 
Presiding Officer (Mr. Gorton).
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Presiding Officer in his capacity as a 
Senator from the State of Washington notes the absence of a quorum.
  The clerk will call the roll.
  The assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. ROCKEFELLER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
order for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Thompson). Without objection, it is so 
ordered.
  Mr. ROCKEFELLER. Mr. President, I ask that I be allowed to speak as 
in morning business.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
