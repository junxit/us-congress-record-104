# TRIBUTE TO WILLIAM GREBE SCHUETTE

**Speaking:** Camp, Dave (C000071) [R-MI]

```
                   TRIBUTE TO WILLIAM GREBE SCHUETTE

                                 ______


                             HON. DAVE CAMP

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, July 26, 1995
  Mr. CAMP. Mr. Speaker, it is with great pleasure that I rise today to 
honor the birth of William Grebe Schuette. At 7:43 a.m. on July 21, 
1995, the Honorable Bill Schuette, former Member of Congress, and his 
wife Cynthia welcomed their first son, William Grebe, into the world.
  The birth of William Grebe Schuette marks an exciting time in the 
lives of the Schuette family, which also includes daughter Heidi. I 
hope that my colleagues will join me in wishing Bill, Cynthia, and 
Heidi a heart-felt congratulations on the new addition to their family.


                          ____________________
```
