# EXECUTIVE SESSION

**Speaking:** Pressler, Larry (P000513) [R-SD]

```
                           EXECUTIVE SESSION

                                 ______


                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mr. PRESSLER. Mr. President, in executive session, I ask unanimous 
consent that the Senate immediately proceed to the consideration of the 
following Executive Calendar nominations:
  Calendar No. 175, Robert F. Rider; Calendar No. 176, John D. Hawke, 
and Calendar No. 177, Linda Lee Robertson.
  I further ask unanimous consent that the nominations be considered en 
bloc, the motions to reconsider be laid upon the table en bloc, that 
any statements relating to the nominations appear at the appropriate 
place in the Record, that the President be immediately notified of the 
Senate's action, and that the Senate then return to legislative 
session.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The nominations were considered and confirmed en bloc, as follows:
                          U.S. Postal Service

       Robert F. Rider, of Delaware, to be a Governor of the 
     United States Postal Service for the term expiring December 
     8, 2004. (Reappointment)

                       Department of the Treasury

       John D. Hawke, Jr., of New York, to be Under Secretary of 
     the Treasury.
       Linda Lee Robertson, of Oklahoma, to be a Deputy Under 
     Secretary of the Treasury.
     

                          ____________________
```
