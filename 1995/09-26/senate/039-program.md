# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Bond, Christopher S. (B000611) [R-MO]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. BOND. Mr. President, on behalf of the leader, it is my pleasure 
to announce for the information of all Senators that the Senate will 
resume consideration of the VA-HUD appropriations bill tomorrow morning 
at 9:15. Under the previous order, there will be four rollcall votes at 
approximately 9:20 a.m., with a brief period of time between each vote. 
The leader has indicated the Senate will complete action on this 
appropriations bill hopefully by early afternoon. Therefore, additional 
rollcall votes can be expected throughout Wednesday's session in order 
to finish action on the VA-HUD appropriations bill and to make progress 
on the Labor-HHS appropriations bill. 

[[Page S 14334]]

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
