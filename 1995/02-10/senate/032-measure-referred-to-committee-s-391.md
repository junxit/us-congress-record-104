# MEASURE REFERRED TO COMMITTEE--S. 391

**Speaking:** Hatch, Orrin G. (H000338) [R-UT]

**Measures:** S. 391

```
                 MEASURE REFERRED TO COMMITTEE--S. 391

  Mr. HATCH. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that a bill 
introduced by Senator Craig, S. 391, the Federal Lands Forest Health 
Protection and Restoration Act of 1995 be referred to the Committee on 
Energy and Natural Resources and that when and if the bill is reported 
by that committee, it be referred jointly to the Committee on 
Agriculture and the Committee on Environment and Public Works for not 
to exceed 20 days of session, and if on the 20th day either committee 
has not reported the bill, the committee's be discharged from further 
consideration of the bill and the bill be placed on the Senate 
calendar.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
