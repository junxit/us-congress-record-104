# RECESS UNTIL MONDAY, FEBRUARY 13, 1995

**Speaking:** Hatch, Orrin G. (H000338) [R-UT]

```
                 RECESS UNTIL MONDAY, FEBRUARY 13, 1995

  Mr. HATCH. Mr. President, if there is no further business to come 
before the Senate, and no other Senator is seeking recognition, I now 
ask unanimous consent the Senate stand in recess under the previous 
order.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 3:57 p.m., recessed until 
Monday, February 13, 1995, at 12 noon.
```
