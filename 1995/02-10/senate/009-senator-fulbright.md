# SENATOR FULBRIGHT

**Speaking:** Helms, Jesse (H000463) [R-NC]

```
                           SENATOR FULBRIGHT

  Mr. HELMS. Mr. President, all of us who knew and/or served with 
Senator J. William Fulbright were saddened at the news of his passing. 
I had the privilege of serving my first 2 years in the Senate with this 
distinguished gentleman. He was an able U.S. Senator.
  Senator Fulbright presided over the Senate Foreign Relations 
Committee with dignity and distinction. I join the American people in 
extending my deepest sympathies to his family.

                          ____________________
```
