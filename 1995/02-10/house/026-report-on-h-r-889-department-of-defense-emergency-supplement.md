# REPORT ON H.R. 889, DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE EMERGENCY SUPPLEMENTAL APPROPRIATIONS, 1995

**Measures:** H.R. 889

```
   REPORT ON H.R. 889, DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE EMERGENCY SUPPLEMENTAL 
                          APPROPRIATIONS, 1995

  Mr. LIVINGSTON, from the Committee on Appropriations, submitted a 
privileged report (Rept. No. 104-29) on the bill (H.R. 889) making 
emergency supplemental appropriations and rescissions to preserve and 
enhance the military readiness of the Department of Defense for the 
fiscal year ending September 30, 1995, and for other purposes, which 
was referred to the Union Calendar and ordered to be printed.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. All points of order are reserved on the 
bill.
```
