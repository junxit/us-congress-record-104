# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Rev. James David Ford, D.D., offered the following 
prayer:
  Enable us, O Almighty God, to take the heavenly vision of harmony and 
peace and mercy and truth and translate that vision into the work that 
we do with our hearts and minds and hands. Encourage to take our ideas 
and ideals, our hopes and dreams, our faith and our convictions into 
the realm of daily action and personal responsibility. May we so heed 
Your word of truth and Your message of justice that Your will may be 
done on Earth as it is in Heaven. In Your name, we pray. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
