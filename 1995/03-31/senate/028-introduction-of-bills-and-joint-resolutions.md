# INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** S. 657, S. 658, S. 659

```
              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second time by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Mr. DOMENICI:
       S. 657. A bill to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 
     to repeal the 1993 Federal income tax rate increases on 
     trusts established for the benefit of individuals with 
     disabilities or for college education costs of a beneficiary; 
     to the Committee on Finance.
           By Mr. BINGAMAN:
       S. 658. A bill to expand the boundary of the Santa Fe 
     National Forest, and for other purposes; to the Committee on 
     Energy and Natural Resources..
           By Mr. FEINGOLD:
       S. 659. A bill to amend the Food, Agriculture, 
     Conservation, and Trade Act of 1990 to replace the 
     prohibition on higher State make allowances for the 
     processing of milk with a requirement that the support 
     purchase price for milk be reduced if a person collects a 
     State make allowance that is higher than the Federal make 
     allowance and the milk is purchased by the Commodity Credit 
     Corporation, and for other purposes; to the Committee on 
     Agriculture, Nutrition, and Forestry.
```
