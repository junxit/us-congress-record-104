# SENATE RESOLUTION 98---RELATING TO TAX AVOIDANCE BY CERTAIN AMERICAN CITIZENS

**Measures:** S.Res. 98

```
 SENATE RESOLUTION 98---RELATING TO TAX AVOIDANCE BY CERTAIN AMERICAN 
                                CITIZENS

  Mr. KENNEDY submitted the following resolution; ordered to lie over, 
under the rule:

                               S. Res. 98

       Resolved, it is the sense of the Senate that--
       (1) the Congress of the United States should act as quickly 
     as possible to amend the Internal Revenue Code to end the tax 
     avoidance by United States citizens who relinquish their 
     United States citizenship; and
       (2) The effective date of such amendment to the Internal 
     Revenue Code should be February 6, 1995.
```
