# U.N. CONTROL OF U.S. FORCES UNCONSCIONABLE

**Speaking:** Johnson, Sam (J000174) [R-TX]

```
               U.N. CONTROL OF U.S. FORCES UNCONSCIONABLE

  (Mr. SAM JOHNSON of Texas asked and was given permission to address 
the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. SAM JOHNSON of Texas. Mr. Speaker, well, here we go again. Our 
President, without consulting the Congress, has allowed the United 
Nations to make a decision to bomb in Bosnia. It is going on as I 
speak. The U.S. F-18's, according to the press, are over there bombing. 
U.N. control of U.S. forces is unconscionable, without resorting to 
consent from the Congress. We did not declare war.
  If one American life is lost because of these actions, I think it is 
a disgrace to American integrity.

                          ____________________
```
