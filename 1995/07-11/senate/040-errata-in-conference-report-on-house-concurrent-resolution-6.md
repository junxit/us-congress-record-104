# ERRATA IN CONFERENCE REPORT ON HOUSE CONCURRENT RESOLUTION 67

**Speaking:** Domenici, Pete V. (D000407) [R-NM]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 67

```
     ERRATA IN CONFERENCE REPORT ON HOUSE CONCURRENT RESOLUTION 67

● Mr. DOMENICI. Mr. President, due to a printing error, the 
table in the conference report on House Concurrent Resolution 67 
setting forth the budget authority and outlay allocations for Senate 
committees incorrectly shows a budget authority allocation of $1,400 
million to the Senate Veterans' Affairs Committee for 1996.
  The 1996 budget authority allocation to the Senate Veterans' Affairs 
Committee is actually $1,440 million. Therefore, the Veterans' Affairs 
allocation for fiscal year 1996 is as follows:
```
