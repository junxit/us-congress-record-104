# MORNING BUSINESS

```

  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Frist). Under the previous order, there 
now will be a period for the transaction of morning business, not to 
extend beyond the hour of 9:45 a.m., with Senators permitted to speak 
therein for not to exceed 10 minutes each.
```
