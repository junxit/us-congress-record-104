# MEASURE READ FOR FIRST TIME--S. 1500

**Speaking:** Santorum, Rick (S000059) [R-PA]

**Measures:** S. 1500

```
                  MEASURE READ FOR FIRST TIME--S. 1500

  Mr. SANTORUM. Mr. President, I understand S. 1500, introduced today 
by Senator Brown, is at the desk and I ask for its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator is correct. The clerk will read 
the bill for the first time.
  The bill clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 1500) to establish the Cache La Poudre River 
     National Water Heritage Area in the State of Colorado, and 
     for other purposes.

  Mr. SANTORUM. Mr. President, I now ask for its second reading, and I 
object to my own request on behalf of Senators on the Democratic side 
of the aisle.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. An objection is heard.

                          ____________________
```
