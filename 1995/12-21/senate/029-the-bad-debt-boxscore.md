# THE BAD DEBT BOXSCORE

**Speaking:** Helms, Jesse (H000463) [R-NC]

```
                         THE BAD DEBT BOXSCORE

  Mr. HELMS. Mr. President, as of the close of business yesterday, 
December 20, the Federal debt stood at $4,988,966,775,602.69, a little 
more than $11 billion shy of the $5 trillion mark, which the Federal 
debt will exceed in a few weeks.
  On a per capita basis, every man, woman, and child in America owes 
$18,938.20 as his or her share of that debt.
```
