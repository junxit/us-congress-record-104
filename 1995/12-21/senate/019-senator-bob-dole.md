# SENATOR BOB DOLE

**Speaking:** Moynihan, Daniel Patrick (M001054) [D-NY]

```
                            SENATOR BOB DOLE

  Mr. MOYNIHAN. With great precision and with equal interest, Mr. 
President, it has been a quarter of a century since I first knew Bob 
Dole and worked with him. He would find it interesting that we began 
working in an effort with a Republican President to establish a 
guaranteed income as a way of getting us out of our welfare problems. 
We are still in them. We will be in them much of the evening.
  But in 25 years I have not known a man I have respected more. I have 
not worked with anyone with greater consequence. He is an ornament to 
this institution and to this Nation. We are proud of him.
  I thank the Senator from Nevada.
  Mr. REID addressed the Chair.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator from Nevada.

                          ____________________
```
