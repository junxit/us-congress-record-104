# APPOINTMENT OF ADDITIONAL CONFEREES ON H.R. 2491, SEVEN-YEAR BALANCED BUDGET RECONCILIATION ACT OF 1996

**Measures:** H.R. 2491

```
 APPOINTMENT OF ADDITIONAL CONFEREES ON H.R. 2491, SEVEN-YEAR BALANCED 
                   BUDGET RECONCILIATION ACT OF 1996

granted in clause 6 of rule X, the Speaker appoints as additional 
conferees from the Committee on Commerce for consideration of title XVI
```
