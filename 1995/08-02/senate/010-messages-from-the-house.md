# MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** S. 21

```
                        MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE


                          ENROLLED BILL SIGNED

  At 5:59 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mr. Hays, one of its reading clerks, announced that the Speaker has 
signed the following enrolled bill:

       S. 21. An act to terminate the United States arms embargo 
     applicable to the Government of Bosnia and Herzegovina.


[[Page S11200]]

  The enrolled bill was subsequently signed by the President pro 
tempore (Mr. Thurmond).
```
