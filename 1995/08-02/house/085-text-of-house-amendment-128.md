# Text of House Amendment 128

**Speaking:** Emerson, Bill (E000174) [R-MO]

**Measures:** H.R. 2127

```
H.R. 2127

                        Offered By: Mr. Emerson

       Amendment No. 128: Page 88, after line 7, insert the 
     following new title:

                TITLE VII--ADDITIONAL GENERAL PROVISIONS

       Sec. 701. (a) Limitation on Use of Funds.--None of the 
     funds made available in this Act may be used for the expenses 
     of an electronic benefit transfer (EBT) task force.
       (b) Corresponding Reduction in Funds.--The amount otherwise 
     provided in this Act for ``DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN 
     SERVICES--Administration for Children and Families--Children 
     and families services programs'' is hereby reduced by 
     $2,000,000.
```
