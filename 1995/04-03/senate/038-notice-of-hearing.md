# NOTICE OF HEARING

**Speaking:** McCain, John (M000303) [R-AZ]

```
                           NOTICE OF HEARING


                      committee on indian affairs

  Mr. McCAIN. Mr. President, I would like to announce that the Senate 
Committee on Indian Affairs will be holding an oversight hearing on 
Wednesday, April 5, 1995, beginning at 9:30 a.m., in room 485 of the 
Russell Senate Office Building on providing direct funding through 
block grants to tribes to administer welfare and other social service 
programs.

Indian Affairs at 224-2251.
```
