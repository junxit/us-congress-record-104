# BARNUM & BAILEY CIRCUS COMMEMORATION

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 34

```
                  BARNUM & BAILEY CIRCUS COMMEMORATION

                                 ______


                        SMITH AMENDMENT NO. 449
  Mr. SMITH proposed an amendment to the concurrent resolution (H. Con. 
Res. 34) concurrent resolution authorizing the use of the Capitol 
Grounds for the Ringling Bros., and Barnum & Bailey Circus anniversary 
commemoration; as follows:

       On page 2, strike lines 9 through 13, and insert the 
     following: ``performers, on the Capitol Grounds, on April 3, 
     1995, or on such other date as the Speaker of the House of 
     Representatives and President pro tempore of the Senate may 
     jointly designate.

     SEC. 2. CONDITIONS.

       No elephants shall be allowed on the Capitol Grounds for 
     the purpose of this event.''.
```
