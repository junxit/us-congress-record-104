  Mr. THURMOND. Mr. President, on behalf of the leader, in executive 
session, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate immediately proceed to 
the consideration of the following nominations on the Executive 
Calendar, en bloc: Calendar Nos. 52-62 and 64-66, and all nominations 
placed on the Secretary's desk.

to reconsider be laid upon the table, en bloc; and that any statements 
relating to the nominations appear at the appropriate place in the
