# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 660, HOUSING FOR OLDER PERSONS ACT OF 1995

**Measures:** H.Res. 126, H.R. 660

```
 REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 660, HOUSING 
                     FOR OLDER PERSONS ACT OF 1995

report (Rept. No. 104-98) on the resolution (H. Res. 126) providing for 
the consideration of the bill (H.R. 660) to amend the Fair Housing Act
```
