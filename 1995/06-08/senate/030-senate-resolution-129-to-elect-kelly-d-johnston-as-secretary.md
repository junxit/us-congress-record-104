# SENATE RESOLUTION 129--TO ELECT KELLY D. JOHNSTON AS SECRETARY OF THE SENATE

**Measures:** S.Res. 129

```
 SENATE RESOLUTION 129--TO ELECT KELLY D. JOHNSTON AS SECRETARY OF THE 
                                 SENATE

  Mr. NICKLES (for Mr. Dole) submitted the following resolution; which 
was considered and agreed to:

                              S. Res. 129
```
