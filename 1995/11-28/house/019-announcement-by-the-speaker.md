# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER

**Measures:** S. 440, S. 1328

```
                      ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER

  The SPEAKER. The Chair desires to announce that pursuant to clause 4 
of rule I, the Speaker signed the following enrolled bills on Monday, 
November 20, 1995:

       S. 440, to amend title 23, United States Code, to provide 
     for the designation of the National Highway System, and for 
     other purposes; and
       S. 1328, to amend the commencement dates of certain 
     temporary Federal judgeships.
```
