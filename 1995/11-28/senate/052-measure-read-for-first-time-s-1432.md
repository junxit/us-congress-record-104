# MEASURE READ FOR FIRST TIME--S. 1432

**Speaking:** Pressler, Larry (P000513) [R-SD]

**Measures:** S. 1432

```
                  MEASURE READ FOR FIRST TIME--S. 1432

  Mr. PRESSLER. Mr. President, I send the enclosed bill to the desk and 
ask for its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will read the bill for the first 
time.
  The assistant legislative clerk read as follows:


[[Page S 17675]]

       A bill (S. 1432) to amend title II of the Social Security 
     Act to provide for increases in the amounts of allowable 
     earnings under the Social Security earnings limit for 
     individuals who have attained retirement age, and for other 
     purposes.

  Mr. PRESSLER. I now ask for its second reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Mr. PRESSLER. I object to my own request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The bill will remain at the desk until the 
next legislative day.

                          ____________________
```
