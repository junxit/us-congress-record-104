# REPORT OF THE RAILROAD RETIREMENT BOARD FOR FISCAL YEAR 1994--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT--PM 97

```
 REPORT OF THE RAILROAD RETIREMENT BOARD FOR FISCAL YEAR 1994--MESSAGE 
                       FROM THE PRESIDENT--PM 97

  The PRESIDING OFFICER laid before the Senate the following message 
from the President of the United States, together with an accompanying 
report; which was referred to the Committee on Labor and Human 
Resources:

To the Congress of the United States:
  I transmit herewith the Annual Report of the Railroad Retirement 
Board for Fiscal Year 1994, pursuant to the provisions of section 
7(b)(6) of the Railroad Retirement Act and section 12(1) of the 
Railroad Unemployment Insurance Act.
                                                  William J. Clinton.  
  The White House, November 28, 1995.

                          ____________________
```
