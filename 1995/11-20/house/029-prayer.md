# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Rev. James David Ford, D.D., offered the following 
prayer:
  With every morning Sun there is the reminder of a new day and with 
each new day there are occasions to do the works of justice. Teach us, 
gracious 

[[Page H 13357]]
God, to make good use of the time allotted to us so that our efforts 
will allow us to be the people You would have us be. Remind us 
specially at this Thanksgiving season of the privilege it is to be good 
stewards of all the riches that have come to our Nation. Above all else 
may we be found faithful in our commitment to the good traditions of 
the land so that justice will flow down as waters and righteousness 
like an everflowing stream. In Your name, we pray. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
