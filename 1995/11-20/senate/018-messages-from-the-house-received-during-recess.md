# MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING RECESS

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 123

```
             MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING RECESS

  Under the authority of the order of the Senate of January 4, 1995, 
the Secretary of the Senate, on November 20, 1995, during the recess of 
the Senate, received a message from the House of Representatives 
announcing that the Speaker has signed the following enrolled joint 
resolution (H.J. Res. 123) making further continuing appropriations for 
the fiscal year 1996, and for other purposes.
  Under the authority of the order of the Senate of January 4, 1995, 
the enrolled joint resolution was signed on November 20, 1995, during 
the recess of the Senate by the President pro tempore (Mr. Thurmond).

                          ____________________
```
