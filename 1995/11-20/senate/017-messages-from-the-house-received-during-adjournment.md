# MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING ADJOURNMENT

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 123

```
          MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING ADJOURNMENT

  Under the authority of the order of the Senate of January 4, 1995, 
the Secretary of the Senate, on November 19, 1995, during the 
adjournment of the Senate, received a message from the House of 
Representatives announcing that the House agrees to the amendment of 
the Senate to the joint resolution (H.J. Res. 123) making further 
continuing appropriations for the fiscal year 1996, and for other 
purposes.
```
