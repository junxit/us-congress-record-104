# THE BAD DEBT BOXSCORE

**Speaking:** Helms, Jesse (H000463) [R-NC]

```
[[Page S15379]]


                         THE BAD DEBT BOXSCORE

  Mr. HELMS. Mr. President, as of the close of business yesterday, 
October 19, the Federal debt stood at $4,974,014,009,081.49. We are 
still about $27 billion away from the $5 trillion mark, unfortunately, 
we anticipate hitting the $5 trillion mark sometime later this year or 
early next year.
  On a per capita basis, every man, woman, and child in America owes 
$18,881.44 as his or her share of that debt.
```
