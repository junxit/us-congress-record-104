# ORDER TO PROCEED TO H.R. 1976

**Speaking:** Dole, Robert J. (D000401) [R-KS]

**Measures:** H.R. 1976

```
                     ORDER TO PROCEED TO H.R. 1976

  Mr. DOLE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that at the hour of 
10 o'clock the Senate proceed to calendar No. 186, H.R. 1976, the 
Agriculture appropriations bill, and that no votes occur on Monday 
prior to the hour of 5:15 p.m.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. DOLE. For the information of all Senators, we are going to begin 
the Agriculture appropriations bill at 10. So we hope Members will 
offer amendments on Monday, and we can complete action by the lunch 
recess on Tuesday. Also, by previous consent, three rollcall votes will 
occur on Tuesday, at approximately 2:45, with respect to the welfare 
reform bill.


                          ____________________
```
