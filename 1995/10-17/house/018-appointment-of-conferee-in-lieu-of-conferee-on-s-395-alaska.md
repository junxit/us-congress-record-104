# APPOINTMENT OF CONFEREE IN LIEU OF CONFEREE ON S. 395, ALASKA POWER ADMINISTRATION ASSET SALE AND TERMINATION ACT

**Measures:** S. 395

```
  APPOINTMENT OF CONFEREE IN LIEU OF CONFEREE ON S. 395, ALASKA POWER 
             ADMINISTRATION ASSET SALE AND TERMINATION ACT

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The Chair appoints, without objection, Mr. 
Oberstar as a conferee for consideration of House amendment No. 4 for 
the conference on the bill S. 395 to fill the vacancy resulting from 
the resignation from the House of the gentleman from California, Mr. 
Mineta.
  There was no objection.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The Clerk will notify the Senate of the 
change in conferees.
```
