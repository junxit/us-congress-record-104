# Text of House Amendment 50

**Speaking:** Edwards, Chet (E000063) [D-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 2126

```
H.R. 2126

                        Offered By: Mr. Edwards

       Amendment No. 50: Page 94, after line 3, insert the 
     following new section:
       ``Sec. 8107. The amounts otherwise provided by this Act are 
     revised by reducing the amount made available for ``Research, 
     Development, Test and Evaluation, Defense-Wide'', and 
     increasing the amount made available for ``Operation and 
     Maintenance, Defense-Wide'', by $40,300,000 and $23,000,000, 
     respectively.''
```
