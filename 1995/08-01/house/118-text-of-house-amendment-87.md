# Text of House Amendment 87

**Speaking:** Goodling, William F. (G000291) [R-PA]

**Measures:** H.R. 2127

```
H.R. 2127

                        Offered By: Mr. Goodling

       Amendment No. 87: Page 75, after line 24, insert the 
     following new section.


                    national institute for literacy

                     (including transfer of funds)

       ``Sec. 514. For expenses to carry out the literacy program 
     of the National Institute for Literacy under section 384 of 
     the Adult Education Act (20 U.S.C. 1213c), to be derived from 
     amounts provided in this Act for ``Education, Research, 
     Statistics, and Improvement'', $4,869,000.''
```
