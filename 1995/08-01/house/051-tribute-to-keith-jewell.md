# TRIBUTE TO KEITH JEWELL

**Speaking:** Gonzalez, Henry B. (G000272) [D-TX]

```
                              {time}  1700
                        TRIBUTE TO KEITH JEWELL

  (Mr. GONZALEZ asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. GONZALEZ. I rise also to add my voice to those expressing regret 
at our photographer, Keith Jewell's departure.
  Mr. Speaker, I was here when he started as a novice, and we had the 
first really full professional photographer the House had; who employed 
him, Dev O'Neill' and ever since then I have learned to respect him, 
and it is with a great sense of sadness that I notice his departure and 
wish him well.

                          ____________________
```
