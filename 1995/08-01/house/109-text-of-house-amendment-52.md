# Text of House Amendment 52

**Speaking:** Sanders, Bernard (S000033) [I-VT]

**Measures:** H.R. 2126

```
H.R. 2126

                        Offered By: Mr. Sanders

       Amendment No. 52: Page 94, after line 3, insert the 
     following new section:
       ``Sec. 8107. None of the funds made available in title III 
     may be used for the procurement of any article produced or 
     manufactured outside of the United States, except pursuant to 
     a contract in effect before the date of the enactment of this 
     Act.''
```
