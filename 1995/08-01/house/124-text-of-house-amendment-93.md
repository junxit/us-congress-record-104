# Text of House Amendment 93

**Speaking:** McIntosh, David M. (M000481) [R-IN]

**Measures:** H.R. 2127

```
H.R. 2127

                        Offered By: Mr. McIntosh

       Amendment No. 93: Page 41, after line 8, insert the 
     following new section:
       Sec. 210. Of the amount otherwise provided by this title 
     for ``Health Care Financing Administration--Program 
     Management'', $200,000 shall be available only for 
     compensation to Henry County Memorial Hospital, in New 
     Castle, Indiana.
```
