# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR IMMEDIATE CONSIDERATION OF CONCURRENT RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR ADJOURNMENT

**Measures:** H.Res. 179

```
     REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR IMMEDIATE CONSIDERATION OF 
            CONCURRENT RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR ADJOURNMENT

  Mrs. WALDHOLTZ, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 104-168) on the resolution (H. Res. 179) providing 
for immediate consideration of a concurrent resolution providing for 
adjournment of the House and Senate for the Independence Day district 
work period, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be 
printed.
```
