# REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING POINTS OF ORDER AGAINST CONFERENCE REPORT ON H.R. 483, MEDICARE SELECT POLICIES

**Measures:** H.Res. 180, H.R. 483

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING POINTS OF ORDER AGAINST CONFERENCE REPORT 
                 ON H.R. 483, MEDICARE SELECT POLICIES

  Ms. PRYCE, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged report 
(Rept. No. 104-169) on the resolution (H. Res. 180) waiving points of 
order against the conference report accompanying the bill (H.R. 483) to 
amend title XVIII of the Social Security Act to permit Medicare select 
policies to be offered in all States, and for other purposes, which was 
referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
