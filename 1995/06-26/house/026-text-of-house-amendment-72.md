# Text of House Amendment 72

**Speaking:** Wilson, Charles (W000570) [D-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 1868

```
H.R. 1868

                         Offered By: Mr. Wilson

       Amendment No. 72: On Page 78 following line 6 insert a new 
     general provision:
       ``None of the funds in this Act may be used to provide 
     assistance to the Government of Armenia if it is made known 
     to the President that the Government of Armenia is 
     participating in the blockade of Nakhichevan.
```
