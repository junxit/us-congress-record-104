# Text of House Amendment 73

**Speaking:** Wolf, Frank R. (W000672) [R-VA]

**Measures:** H.R. 1868

```
H.R. 1868

                          Offered By: Mr. Wolf

       Amendment No. 73: Page 19, after line 8, insert the 
     following:
       (k) Of the funds appropriated under this heading and under 
     the heading ``Assistance for Eastern Europe and the Baltic 
     States'', not to exceed $30,000,000 shall be made available 
     for police training and exchanges, and investigative and 
     technical assistance activities related to international 
     criminal activities.
```
