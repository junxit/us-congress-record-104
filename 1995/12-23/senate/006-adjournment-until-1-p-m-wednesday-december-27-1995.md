# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 1 P.M. WEDNESDAY, DECEMBER 27, 1995

```
         ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 1 P.M. WEDNESDAY, DECEMBER 27, 1995

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. I now announce that the Senate 
stands in adjournment, under the previous order, until Wednesday, 
December 27, 1995, at 1 p.m.
  Thereupon, at 11:05 a.m., the Senate adjourned, under the order of 
Friday, December 22, 1995, until Wednesday, December 27, 1995, at 1 
p.m.
```
