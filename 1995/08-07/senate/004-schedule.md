# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Frist, William H. (F000439) [R-TN]

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. FRIST. Mr. President, this morning, the leader time has been 
reserved and there will be a period for morning business until the hour 
of 10:30. At 10:30, the Senate will resume consideration of the welfare 
bill.
  The majority leader has stated that rollcall votes can be expected 
during today's session but will not occur prior to the hour of 4:30 
this afternoon. All Senators should be aware that the cloture vote on 
the Department of Defense authorization bill has been postponed to a 
later time to be determined by the majority leader, if that vote is 
necessary.
  It is the hope of Senator Dole to complete action on the Department 
of Defense authorization during today's session, as well as to make 
progress on the welfare bill.

                          ____________________
```
