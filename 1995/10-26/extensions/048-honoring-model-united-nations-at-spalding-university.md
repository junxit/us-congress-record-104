# HONORING MODEL UNITED NATIONS AT SPALDING UNIVERSITY

**Speaking:** Ward, Mike (W000139) [D-KY]

```
          HONORING MODEL UNITED NATIONS AT SPALDING UNIVERSITY

                                 ______


                             HON. MIKE WARD

                              of kentucky

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 26, 1995

  Mr. WARD. Mr. Speaker, I wish to recognize the Model United Nations 
that will take place at Spalding University in Louisville, KY on 
November 10, 1995. This will be the first time that the collegiate 
level Model United Nations [U.N.] conference will be held in the 
Commonwealth of Kentucky.
  The Model U.N. Conference will give college students a wonderful 
opportunity to gain insight into the substance of international affairs 
by having them serve as delegates of assigned countries. The students 
will also learn about the role of the U.N. in the international 
community and the internal workings of the U.N. The communication 
skills practiced during the conference will no doubt prove to be a 
valuable experience to all of the participants.
  I am proud to host the Spalding University Model United Nations 
Conference in my district. Due to the vital role the United States must 
play in world affairs, a conference of this nature can only prove to be 
of worthwhile nature.

                          ____________________
```
