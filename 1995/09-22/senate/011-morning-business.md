# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Jeffords, James M. (J000072) [R-VT]

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. JEFFORDS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that there now 
be a period for the transaction of morning business with Senators 
permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each, not to extend 
beyond the hour of 2:20 p.m. today.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
