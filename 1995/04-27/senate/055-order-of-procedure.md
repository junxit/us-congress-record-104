# ORDER OF PROCEDURE

**Speaking:** Kyl, Jon (K000352) [R-AZ]

```
                           ORDER OF PROCEDURE

  Mr. KYL. Mr. President, for the information of all of our colleagues, 
the leader has asked me to announce that the Senate will return to 
session on Monday. However, there will be no rollcall votes during 
Monday's session. Under the order, any Member who wishes to offer a 
medical malpractice amendment must offer and debate that amendment on 
Monday. Any votes ordered on any of those amendments will be stacked to 
occur at 11 a.m. on Tuesday.

                          ____________________
```
