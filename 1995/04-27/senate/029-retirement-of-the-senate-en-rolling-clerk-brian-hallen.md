# RETIREMENT OF THE SENATE EN- ROLLING CLERK, BRIAN HALLEN

**Speaking:** Kyl, Jon (K000352) [R-AZ]

**Measures:** S.Res. 112

```
        RETIREMENT OF THE SENATE EN- ROLLING CLERK, BRIAN HALLEN

  Mr. KYL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the immediate consideration of Senate Resolution 112 
submitted earlier today by Senators Dole, and Daschle concerning the 
retirement of Brian Hallen, the Senate enrolling clerk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The clerk will report the resolution by title.
  The assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A resolution (S. Res. 112) commending the Senate Enrolling 
     Clerk upon his retirement.

  The Senate proceeded to consider the resolution.
```
