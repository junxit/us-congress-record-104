# ADDITIONAL SUPPORT FOR H.R. 1020

**Speaking:** Upton, Fred (U000031) [R-MI]

**Measures:** H.R. 1020

```
                    ADDITIONAL SUPPORT FOR H.R. 1020

                                 ______


                            HON. FRED UPTON

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, December 19, 1995

  Mr. UPTON. Mr. Speaker, I am proud to be the sponsor of H.R. 1020, 
the Integrated Spent Nuclear Fuel Management Act of 1995, a bill that 
will make the Federal Government live up to its promise of building and 
operating a high-level nuclear waste repository by January 31, 1998. 
While nearly 200 of my colleagues have cosponsored H.R. 1020, there are 
several that were unable to do so after the bill was put on the House 
calendar. I would like to acknowledge the following Members as 
supporters of doing what is right, making the Government live up to its 
promises: Charles Bass, Helen Chenoweth, Barbara Cubin, Jay Dickey, Van 
Hilleary, Marge Roukema, Mac Thornberry.

                          ____________________
```
