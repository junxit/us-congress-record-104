# CLOTURE VOTE ON MOTION TO PROCEED TO THE LABOR-HHS APPROPRIATIONS BILL POSTPONED UNTIL WEDNESDAY

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

```
CLOTURE VOTE ON MOTION TO PROCEED TO THE LABOR-HHS APPROPRIATIONS BILL 
                       POSTPONED UNTIL WEDNESDAY

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the cloture 
vote on the motion to proceed to the Labor-HHS appropriations bill be 
postponed to occur on Wednesday at a time to be determined by the 
majority leader after consultation with the minority leader.
```
