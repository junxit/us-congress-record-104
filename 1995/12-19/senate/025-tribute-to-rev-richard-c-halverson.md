# TRIBUTE TO REV. RICHARD C. HALVERSON

**Speaking:** Mack, Connie, III (M000019) [R-FL]

```
                  TRIBUTE TO REV. RICHARD C. HALVERSON

  Mr. MACK. I rise today to extend my heartfelt condolences to the 
family of Rev. Richard Halverson. In his position as the U.S. Senate 
Chaplain for the past 14 years, Reverend Halverson acted as spiritual 
leader to me personally, as well as to the entire Senate. His 
unwavering devotion, knowledge, and guidance have been a powerful 
example of living by one's convictions. It is an example from which we 
should derive inspiration as we search for the true meaning in our 
lives. I will keep the family of Reverend Halverson in my thoughts and 
prayers during their time of grief.

                          ____________________
```
