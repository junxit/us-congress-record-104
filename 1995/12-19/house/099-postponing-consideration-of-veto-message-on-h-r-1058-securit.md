# POSTPONING CONSIDERATION OF VETO MESSAGE ON H.R. 1058, SECURITIES LITIGATION REFORM ACT UNTIL WEDNESDAY, DECEMBER 20, 1995

**Speaking:** Thomas, William M. (T000188) [R-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 1058

```
                              {time}  1730
   POSTPONING CONSIDERATION OF VETO MESSAGE ON H.R. 1058, SECURITIES 
        LITIGATION REFORM ACT UNTIL WEDNESDAY, DECEMBER 20, 1995

  Mr. THOMAS. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent, if the Chair lays 
before the House a veto message from the President on the bill, H.R. 
1058 today, that the objections of the President be spread at large 
upon the Journal and that the message and bill be ordered printed as a 
House document; and that consideration of the veto message be postponed 
until tomorrow, December 20, 1995.

[[Page H15157]]

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Hastings of Washington). Is there 
objection to the request of the gentleman from California?
  There was no objection.
```
