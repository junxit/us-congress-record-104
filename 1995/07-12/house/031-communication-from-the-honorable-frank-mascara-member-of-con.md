# COMMUNICATION FROM THE HONORABLE FRANK MASCARA, MEMBER OF CONGRESS

```
   COMMUNICATION FROM THE HONORABLE FRANK MASCARA, MEMBER OF CONGRESS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Honorable Frank Mascara, Member of Congress:

                                    Congress of the United States,


                                     House of Representatives,

                                    Washington, DC, July 11, 1995.
     Hon. Newt Gingrich,
     Speaker, House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Mr. Speaker: I hereby submit my resignation from the 
     Committee on Government Reform and Oversight, effective July 
     11, 1995.
           Very truly yours,
                                                    Frank Mascara,
     Member of Congress.

                          ____________________
```
