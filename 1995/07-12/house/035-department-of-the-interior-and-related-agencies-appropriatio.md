# DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR AND RELATED AGENCIES APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 1996

**Speaking:** Regula, Ralph (R000141) [R-OH]

**Measures:** H.R. 1977

```
  DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR AND RELATED AGENCIES APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 
                                  1996

  Mr. REGULA. Mr. Speaker, I move that the House resolve itself into 
the Committee of the Whole House on the State of the Union for the 
consideration of the bill (H.R. 1977) making appropriations for the 
Department of the Interior and related agencies for the fiscal year 
ending September 30, 1996, and for other purposes; and pending that 
motion, Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that general debate be 
limited to not to exceed 1 hour, the time to be equally divided and 
controlled by the gentleman from Illinois [Mr. Yates] and myself.

                          ____________________
```
