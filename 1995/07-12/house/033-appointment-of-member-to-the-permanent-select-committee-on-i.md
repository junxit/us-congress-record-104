# APPOINTMENT OF MEMBER TO THE PERMANENT SELECT COMMITTEE ON INTELLIGENCE

```
APPOINTMENT OF MEMBER TO THE PERMANENT SELECT COMMITTEE ON INTELLIGENCE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection and pursuant to the 
provisions of clause 1 of rule XLVIII and clause 6(f) of rule X, the 
Chair announces the Speaker's appointment of the gentleman from 
Colorado [Mr. Skaggs] to the Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence 
to fill the existing vacancy thereon and to rank after the gentleman 
from Texas [Mr. Coleman].
  There was no objection.
```
