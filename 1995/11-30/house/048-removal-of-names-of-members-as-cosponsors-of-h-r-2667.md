# REMOVAL OF NAMES OF MEMBERS AS COSPONSORS OF H.R. 2667

**Speaking:** Barton, Joe (B000213) [R-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 2667

```
         REMOVAL OF NAMES OF MEMBERS AS COSPONSORS OF H.R. 2667

  Mr. BARTON of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the 
gentleman from Virginia [Mr. Davis], the gentleman from Virginia [Mr. 
Wolf], and the gentlewoman from Maryland [Mrs. Morella], be removed as 
cosponsors of H.R. 2667, a bill which I had introduced.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Texas?
  There was no objection.
```
