# UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT--H.R. 1833

**Speaking:** Hutchison, Kay Bailey (H001016) [R-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 1833

```
                 UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT--H.R. 1833

  Mrs. HUTCHISON. I ask unanimous consent that at 4 p.m. on Monday, 
December 4, the Senate turn to the consideration of Calendar No. 249, 
H.R. 1833, the partial-birth abortions ban, for debate only.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mrs. HUTCHISON. And for the information of all Senators, debate will 
begin on the partial-birth abortion ban at 4 p.m. on Monday. However, 
no votes will occur during Monday's session of the Senate.

                          ____________________
```
