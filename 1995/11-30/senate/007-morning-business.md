# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Thomas, Craig (T000162) [R-WY]

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, there will now be a 
period for the transaction of morning business not to extend beyond the 
hour of 2 p.m., with Senators permitted to speak for up to 5 minutes 
each.
  The Chair, in his capacity as a Senator from the State of Colorado, 
suggests the absence of a quorum.
  The clerk will call the roll.
  The assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. THOMAS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The Senator has 1 hour.

                          ____________________
```
