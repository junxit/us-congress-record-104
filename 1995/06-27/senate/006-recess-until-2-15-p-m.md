# RECESS UNTIL 2:15 P.M.

**Speaking:** Brown, Hank (B000919) [R-CO], Bradley, Bill (B001225) [D-NJ]

**Measures:** S. 969

```
                         RECESS UNTIL 2:15 P.M.

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the Senate will 
stand in recess until the hour of 2:15 p.m.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 12:33 p.m., recessed until 
2:15 p.m.; whereupon, the Senate reassembled when called to order by 
the Presiding Officer (Mr. Kyl).
  Mr. BROWN. Mr. President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. BRADLEY. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. BRADLEY. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that I may 
proceed as if in morning business for up to 3 minutes.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The Senator from New Jersey is recognized.
  Mr. BRADLEY. I thank the Chair.
  (The remarks of Mr. Bradley and Mrs. Kassebaum pertaining to the 
introduction of S. 969 are located in today's Record under ``Statements 
on Introduced Bills and Joint Resolutions.'')

                          ____________________
```
