# NOTICE OF HEARING

**Speaking:** McCain, John (M000303) [R-AZ]

**Measures:** S. 814

```
                           NOTICE OF HEARING


                      committee on indian affairs

Committee on Indian Affairs will be holding a hearing on Wednesday, 
June 28, 1995, beginning at 9:45 a.m., in room 485 of the Russell 
Senate Office Building on S. 814, a bill to provide for the
```
