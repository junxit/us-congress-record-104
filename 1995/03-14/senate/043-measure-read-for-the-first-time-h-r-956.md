# MEASURE READ FOR THE FIRST TIME--H.R. 956

**Speaking:** Dole, Robert J. (D000401) [R-KS], Glenn, John H., Jr. (G000236) [D-OH]

**Measures:** H.R. 956

```
               MEASURE READ FOR THE FIRST TIME--H.R. 956

  Mr. DOLE. Mr. President, I inquire of the Chair if H.R. 956 has 
arrived from the House of Representatives.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator is correct.
  Mr. DOLE. Therefore, I ask for its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will read the bill for the first 
time.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 956) to establish legal standards and 
     procedures for product liability litigation, and for other 
     purposes.

  Mr. DOLE. Mr. President, I now ask for its second reading.
  Mr. GLENN. Mr. President, I object.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard. The bill will be read for 
the second time on the next legislative day.

                          ____________________
```
