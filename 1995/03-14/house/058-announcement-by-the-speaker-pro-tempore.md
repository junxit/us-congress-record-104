# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** H.R. 517, H.R. 531, H.R. 536, H.R. 562, H.R. 694

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Debate has been concluded on all motions to 
suspend the rules.
  Pursuant to clause 5, rule I, the Chair will now put the question on 
each motion to suspend the rules on which further proceedings were 
postponed earlier today in the order in which those motions were 
entertained. Votes will be taken in the following order: H.R. 531, as 
amended; H.R. 694, as amended; H.R. 562, as amended; H.R. 536, as 
amended; and H.R. 517, all by the yeas and nays.
  The Chair alerts Members of the House that the Chair will reduce to 5 
minutes the time for any electronic vote after the first such vote in 
this series.

                          ____________________
```
