# REPORT ON THE OPERATION OF THE ANDEAN TRADE PREFERENCE ACT--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT--PM 13

```
  REPORT ON THE OPERATION OF THE ANDEAN TRADE PREFERENCE ACT--MESSAGE 
                       FROM THE PRESIDENT--PM 13

  The PRESIDING OFFICER laid before the Senate the following message 
from the President of the United States, together with an accompanying 
report, which was referred to the Committee on Finance.

To the Congress of the United States:
  I hereby submit the first report on the Operation of the Andean Trade 
Preference Act. This report is prepared pursuant to the requirements of 
section 203 of the Andean Trade Preference Act of 1991.
                                                  William J. Clinton.  
  The White House, February 8, 1995.

                          ____________________
```
