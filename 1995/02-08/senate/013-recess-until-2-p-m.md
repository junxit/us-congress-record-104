# RECESS UNTIL 2 P.M.

**Speaking:** Dole, Robert J. (D000401) [R-KS]

```
                          RECESS UNTIL 2 P.M.

  Mr. DOLE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
stand in recess until 2:00 p.m.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 12:35 p.m., recessed until 2 
p.m.; whereupon, the Senate reassembled when called to order by the 
Presiding Officer (Mr. Frist).
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The majority leader is recognized.

                          ____________________
```
