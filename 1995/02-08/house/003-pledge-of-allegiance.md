# PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE

```
                          PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Lazio of New York). Will the gentleman 
from Washington [Mr. White] come forward and lead the House in the 
Pledge of Allegiance.
  Mr. WHITE led the Pledge of Allegiance as follows:

       I pledge allegiance to the Flag of the United 
     States of America, and to the Republic for which it stands, 
     one nation under God, indivisible, with liberty and justice 
     for all.
```
