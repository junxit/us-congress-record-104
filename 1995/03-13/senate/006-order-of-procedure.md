# ORDER OF PROCEDURE

**Speaking:** Hatfield, Mark O. (H000343) [R-OR], Domenici, Pete V. (D000407) [R-NM]

```
                           ORDER OF PROCEDURE

  Mr. HATFIELD. Mr. President, I am authorized to indicate there will 
be no rollcall votes during today's session of the Senate.
  I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. DOMENICI. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. DOMENICI. Mr. President, I ask that I may speak for 2 minutes as 
in morning business.

                          ____________________
```
