# SENATE RESOLUTION 70--ELECTING CHAPLAIN OF THE U.S. SENATE

**Measures:** S.Res. 70

```
       SENATE RESOLUTION 70--ELECTING CHAPLAIN OF THE U.S. SENATE

  Mr. KEMPTHRONE (for Mr. Dole) submitted the following resolution; 
which was considered and agreed to:

                               S. Res. 70

       Resolved, That Doctor Lloyd John Ogilvie, of California, 
     be, and he is hereby, elected Chaplain of the Senate as of 
     March 11, 1995.
```
