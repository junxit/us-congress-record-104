# ROSE FITZGERALD KENNEDY

**Speaking:** Boxer, Barbara (B000711) [D-CA]

```
                        ROSE FITZGERALD KENNEDY

  Mrs. BOXER. Mr. President, I send my condolences to the Kennedy 
family. The Kennedy family has given this country great men and women. 
They have been profiles in courage in so many ways, and Rose Kennedy 
certainly was one of those profiles in courage.
  I just want to send my deepest sympathy to my friends in the Kennedy 
family. In behalf of the people of California, we send our condolences 
to the family.

                          ____________________
```
