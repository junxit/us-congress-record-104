                    COMMITTEE MEETINGS FOR THURSDAY,

        (Committee meetings are open unless otherwise indicated)


                                 Senate

  Committee on Banking, Housing, and Urban Affairs, business 
meeting, to mark up S. 1260, to reform and consolidate the public

nominations, 10 a.m., SH-216.

to Federal forest land management and to compare land management 
cost and benefits on Federal and State lands, 9 a.m., SD-366.

calendar business, 10 a.m., SD-226.
  Committee on Indian Affairs, to hold hearings on S. 1341, to 
provide for the transfer of certain lands to the Salt River Pima-
Maricopa Indian Community and the city of Scottsdale, Arizona, 9:30 
a.m., SR-485.

quality of care in nursing homes, 9:30 a.m., SD-628.
  Special Committee To Investigate Whitewater Development 
Corporation and Related Matters, to meet to discuss the status of 
hearings on issues relating to the President's involvement with the 
Whitewater Development Corporation, 9:30 a.m., SH-216.
