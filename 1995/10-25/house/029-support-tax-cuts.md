# SUPPORT TAX CUTS

**Speaking:** Johnson, Sam (J000174) [R-TX]

```
                            SUPPORT TAX CUTS

  (Mr. SAM JOHNSON of Texas asked and was given permission to address 
the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. SAM JOHNSON of Texas. Mr. Speaker, last week in my home State of 
Texas the President admitted what Republicans and the American people 
already knew--he raised taxes too much.
  Two short years ago Mr. Clinton and the Democrats raised America's 
taxes by $258 billion, the largest tax increase in American history.
  Republicans now want to return that money back to hardworking 
Americans by providing tax cuts for families, for seniors, and for 
America's jobcreating businesses. It is time to put the people back in 
charge of their wallets. Let's get government out of our pockets.
  It's nice to see the President admit his own mistake. Fortunately 
it's not too late for him to right his wrong, he can let all Americans 
keep more of what they earn. Support tax cuts and sign the budget. It's 
good for America.

                          ____________________
```
