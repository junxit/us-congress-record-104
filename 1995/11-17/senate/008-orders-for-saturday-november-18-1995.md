# ORDERS FOR SATURDAY, NOVEMBER 18, 1995

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

```
                 ORDERS FOR SATURDAY, NOVEMBER 18, 1995

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it stand in recess until the hour of 9:15 
a.m. on Saturday, November 18, 1995; that following the prayer, the 
Journal of proceedings be deemed approved to date, the time for the two 
leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and there then be a 
period for morning business, with Senators permitted to speak for up to 
10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
