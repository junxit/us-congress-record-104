# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEE TO MEET

**Speaking:** Chafee, John H. (C000269) [R-RI]

**Measures:** H.R. 1833

```
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEE TO MEET


                       committee on the judiciary

  Mr. CHAFEE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Committee 
on the Judiciary be authorized to meet during the session of the Senate 
on Friday, November 17, 1995, at 9 a.m. to hold a hearing on H.R. 1833, 
the Partial-Birth Abortion Ban Act of 1995.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
