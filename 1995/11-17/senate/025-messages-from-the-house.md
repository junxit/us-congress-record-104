# MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.R. 2491

```
                        MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

  At 3:47 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Ms. Goetz, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House 
agrees to the report of the committee of conference on the disagreeing 
votes of the two Houses on the amendments of the Senate to the bill 
(H.R. 2491) to provide for reconciliation pursuant to section 105 of 
the concurrent resolution on the budget for fiscal year 1996.

[[Page S 17336]]
```
