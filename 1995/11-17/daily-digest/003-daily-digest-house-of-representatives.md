Bosnia Deployment: By a recorded vote of 243 ayes to 171 noes, with 2 
voting ``present'', Roll No. 814, the House passed H.R. 2606, to 
prohibit the use of funds appropriated to the Department of Defense 
from being used for the deployment on the ground of United States Armed 
Forces in the Republic of Bosnia and Herzegovina as part of any 
peacekeeping operation, or as part of any implementation force, unless 
funds for such deployment are specifically appropriated by law.
