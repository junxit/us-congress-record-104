# RECESS UNTIL TUESDAY, JANUARY 17, AT 9:15 A.M.

**Speaking:** Dole, Robert J. (D000401) [R-KS]

```
             RECESS UNTIL TUESDAY, JANUARY 17, AT 9:15 A.M.

  Mr. DOLE. Mr. President, if there is no further business to come 
before the Senate and no other Senator seeking recognition, I now ask 
consent that the Senate stand in recess under the previous order.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 4:46 p.m., recessed until 
Tuesday, January 17, 1995, at 9:15 a.m. 

```
