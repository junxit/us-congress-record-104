# ELECTION OF MEMBER TO THE COMMITTEE ON COMMERCE

**Speaking:** Boehner, John A. (B000589) [R-OH]

**Measures:** H.Res. 143

```
            ELECTION OF MEMBER TO THE COMMITTEE ON COMMERCE

  Mr. BOEHNER. Mr. Speaker, by direction of the Republican Conference, 
I offer a privileged resolution (H. Res. 143) and ask for its immediate 
consideration.
  The Clerk read the resolution, as follows:

       Resolved, That the following named Member be, and is 
     hereby, elected to the Committee on Commerce of the House of 
     Representatives: Representative Nathan Deal of Georgia.

  The resolution was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```
