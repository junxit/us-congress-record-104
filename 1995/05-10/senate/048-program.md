# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Specter, Arlen (S000709) [R-PA]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. SPECTER. Mr. President, for the information of all Senators, the 
Senate will resume consideration of the Solid Waste Disposal Act 
tomorrow. Further amendments are expected to the bill, therefore 
Senators should anticipate rollcall votes throughout Thursday's session 
of the Senate, and a late night session could occur with votes into the 
evening. A cloture motion was filed on the substitute this evening. It 
is the hope of the leader, Senator Dole, that the Friday vote could be 
vitiated if an agreement can be reached to conclude the bill by Friday. 
Otherwise, a cloture vote will occur Friday morning.
```
