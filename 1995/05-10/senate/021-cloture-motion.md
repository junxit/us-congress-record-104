# CLOTURE MOTION

**Speaking:** Specter, Arlen (S000709) [R-PA]

**Measures:** S. 534

```
                             CLOTURE MOTION

  Mr. SPECTER. Mr. President, I send a motion to invoke cloture on the 
pending matter to the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The cloture motion having been presented under 
rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We the undersigned Senators in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate 
     do hereby 
      [[Page S6453]] move to bring to a close debate on the 
     pending committee substitute amendment to S. 534, the solid 
     waste disposal bill.
         John H. Chafee, Bob Dole, Bob Smith, Jim Jeffords, Hank 
           Brown, Kit Bond, Orrin Hatch, Spencer Abraham, Jon Kyl, 
           Larry E. Craig, Kay Bailey Hutchison, Trent Lott, R.F. 
           Bennett, Pete V. Domenici, Dirk Kempthorne, Jesse 
           Helms.

                          ____________________
```
