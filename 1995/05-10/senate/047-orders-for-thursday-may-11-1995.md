# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, MAY 11, 1995

**Speaking:** Specter, Arlen (S000709) [R-PA]

**Measures:** S. 534

```
                   ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, MAY 11, 1995

  Mr. SPECTER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today it stand in recess until 9:30 a.m. 
on Thursday, May 11, 1995; that following the prayer, the Journal of 
proceedings be deemed approved, the time for the two leaders be 
reserved for their use later in the day, and the Senate then 
immediately resume consideration of S. 534, the Solid Waste Disposal 
Act.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
