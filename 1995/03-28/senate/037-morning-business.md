# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS
                                 ______


 REPORT ON THE HEALTH CARE FOR NATIVE HAWAIIANS PROGRAM--MESSAGE FROM 
                          THE PRESIDENT--PM 37

  The PRESIDING OFFICER laid before the Senate the following message 
from the President of the United States, together with an accompanying 
report; which was referred to the Committee on Indian Affairs.

To the Congress of the United States:
  I transmit herewith the Report of the Health Care for Native 
Hawaiians Program, as required by section 11 of the Native Hawaiians 
Health Care Act of 1988, as amended (Public Law 102-396; 42 U.S.C. 
11701 et seq.).
                                                  William J. Clinton.  
  The White House, March 27, 1995.

                          ____________________
```
