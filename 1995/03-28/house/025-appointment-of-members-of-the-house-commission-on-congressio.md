# APPOINTMENT OF MEMBERS OF THE HOUSE COMMISSION ON CONGRESSIONAL MAILING STANDARDS

```
APPOINTMENT OF MEMBERS OF THE HOUSE COMMISSION ON CONGRESSIONAL MAILING 
                               STANDARDS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, and pursuant to the 
provisions of section 5(b) of Public Law 93-191, the Chair announces 
the Speaker's appointment as members of the House Commission on 
Congressional Mailing Standards the following Members of the House:
  Mr. Thomas of California, Chairman; and Messrs. Roberts of Kansas; 
Ney of Ohio; Fazio of California; Clay of Missouri; and Gordon of 
Tennessee.
  There was no objection.
```
