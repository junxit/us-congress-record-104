# SENATE RESOLUTION 58--RELATIVE TO JOINT COMMITTEES

**Measures:** S.Res. 58

```
           SENATE RESOLUTION 58--RELATIVE TO JOINT COMMITTEES

  Mr. LOTT (for Mr. Stevens) submitted the following resolution; which 
was considered and agreed to:

                               S. Res. 58

       Resolved, That the following-named Members be, and they are 
     hereby, elected members of the following joint committees of 
     Congress:
       Joint Committee on Printing: Ted Stevens, Mark O. Hatfield, 
     Thad Cochran, Wendell H. Ford, and Daniel K. Inouye.
       Joint Committee on the Library of Congress: Mark O. 
     Hatfield, Ted Stevens, Thad Cochran, Claiborne Pell, and 
     Daniel P. Moynihan.
     

                          ____________________
```
