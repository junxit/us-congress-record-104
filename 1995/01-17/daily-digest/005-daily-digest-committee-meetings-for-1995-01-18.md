# Daily Digest/COMMITTEE MEETINGS FOR 1995-01-18

                         COMMITTEE MEETINGS FOR

                      WEDNESDAY, JANUARY 18, 1995

        (Committee meetings are open unless otherwise indicated)


                                 Senate

  Committee on the Budget, to hold hearings on proposed legislation 
granting the President of the United States legislative line-item 
veto authority, 10 a.m., SD-608.

  Committee on Labor and Human Resources, organizational meeting to

104th Congress, and subcommittee assignments, 9:30 a.m., SD-430.

proposed committee resolutions requesting funds for

301.
  Select Committee on Intelligence, to hold closed hearings on 
intelligence matters, 2 p.m., SH-219.

  Committee on Appropriations, Subcommittee on Energy and Water 
Development, on Downsizing Government, 2 p.m., 2362B Rayburn.
  Subcommittee on Interior and Related Agencies, on Secretary of the 
Interior, 10 a.m., B-308 Rayburn.
  Subcommittee on Labor, HHS, Education and Related Agencies, on 
Secretary of Education/Downsizing/GAO, 9 a.m., and on Secretary of

  Subcommittee on Treasury, Postal Service, and General Government,

  Committee on Economic and Educational Opportunities, hearing on

50, to designate the visitors center at the Channel Islands National

  Committee on Transportation and Infrastructure,  Subcommittee on

  Committee on Ways and Means, to continue hearings on proposals
