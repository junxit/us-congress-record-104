# SELECTION OF MEMBERS OF COMMITTEE ON WAYS AND MEANS AS OFFICIAL ADVISERS TO VARIOUS U.S. DELEGATIONS RELATING TO TRADE AGREEMENTS

```
[[Page H247]]
    SELECTION OF MEMBERS OF COMMITTEE ON WAYS AND MEANS AS OFFICIAL 
   ADVISERS TO VARIOUS U.S. DELEGATIONS RELATING TO TRADE AGREEMENTS

  The SPEAKER. Pursuant to the provisions of section 161(a) of the 
Trade Act of 1974 (19 U.S.C. 2211) and upon the recommendation of the 
chairman of the Committee on Ways and Means, the Chair has selected the 
following members of that committee to be accredited by the President 
as official advisers to the U.S. delegations to international 
conferences, meetings, and negotiation sessions relating to trade 
agreements during the 1st session of the 104th Congress:
  Mr. Archer of Texas;
  Mr. Crane of Illinois;
  Mr. Thomas of California;
  Mr. Gibbons of Florida; and
  Mr. Rangel of New York.

                          ____________________
```
