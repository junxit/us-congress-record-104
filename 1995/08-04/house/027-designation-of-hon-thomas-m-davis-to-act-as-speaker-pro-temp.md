# DESIGNATION OF HON. THOMAS M. DAVIS TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS THROUGH SEPTEMBER 6, 1995

```
 DESIGNATION OF HON. THOMAS M. DAVIS TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO 
  SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS THROUGH SEPTEMBER 6, 1995

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Speaker:

                                               Washington, DC,

                                                   August 4, 1995.
       I hereby designate the Honorable Thomas M. Davis to act as 
     Speaker pro tempore to sign enrolled bills and joint 
     resolutions through September 6, 1995.
                                                    Newt Gingrich,
                          Speaker of the House of Representatives.

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, the designation is agreed 
to.
  There was no objection.
  

                          ____________________
```
