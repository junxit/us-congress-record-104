# PERMISSION FOR COMMITTEE ON ECONOMIC AND EDUCATIONAL OPPORTUNITIES HAVE UNTIL FRIDAY, SEPTEMBER 1, 1995 TO FILE REPORT ON H.R. 1594, PLACING RESTRICTIONS ON DEPARTMENT OF LABOR INVESTMENTS WITH...

**Speaking:** Goodling, William F. (G000291) [R-PA]

**Measures:** H.R. 1594

```
PERMISSION FOR COMMITTEE ON ECONOMIC AND EDUCATIONAL OPPORTUNITIES HAVE 
 UNTIL FRIDAY, SEPTEMBER 1, 1995 TO FILE REPORT ON H.R. 1594, PLACING 
 RESTRICTIONS ON DEPARTMENT OF LABOR INVESTMENTS WITH EMPLOYEE BENEFIT 
                                 PLANS

  Mr. GOODLING. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the Committee 
on Economic and Educational Opportunities may have until noon on 
Friday, September 1, 1995, to file a report on H.R. 1594, a bill to 
place restrictions on the promotion by the Department of Labor of 
economically targeted investments in connection with employee benefit 
plans.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Pennsylvania?
  There was no objection.
  

                          ____________________
```
