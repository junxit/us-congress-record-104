# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there will now 
be a period for the transaction of morning business not to extend 
beyond the hour of 10 a.m., with 45 minutes to be under the control of 
the Senator from West Virginia [Mr. Byrd].
  The able Senator from West Virginia is recognized.
```
