# THE CHAPLAIN'S PRAYER

**Speaking:** Byrd, Robert C. (B001210) [D-WV]

```
                         THE CHAPLAIN'S PRAYER

  Mr. BYRD. Mr. President, I thank the venerable Chair. I also thank 
our Chaplain for his prayer, reminding us of our insignificance and of 
the majestic greatness and love of an omnipotent, omnipresent, and 
omniscient God and of our Lord and Savior, Jesus Christ, who gave his 
life as a ransom for many.

                          ____________________
```
