# EXTENSION OF TIME FOR MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Santorum, Rick (S000059) [R-PA], Dodd, Christopher J. (D000388) [D-CT]

```
                 EXTENSION OF TIME FOR MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. SANTORUM. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that a period 
for the transaction of morning business be extended until 5 p.m. under 
the same rules governing the previous unanimous consent agreement.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection? Without objection, it is 
so ordered.
  Mr. SANTORUM. Mr. President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. DODD. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the call for 
the quorum be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mrs. Hutchison). Without objection, it is so 
ordered.
  Mr. DODD. Madam President, parliamentary inquiry.
  Are we in morning business, as I understand it?
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. That is correct.

                          ____________________
```
