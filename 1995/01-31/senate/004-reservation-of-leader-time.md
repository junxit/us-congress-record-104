# RESERVATION OF LEADER TIME

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 1

```
                       RESERVATION OF LEADER TIME

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, time for the two leaders has been reserved.


                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, there will be a period for the transaction 
of morning business not to extend beyond 10 a.m. with Senators 
permitted to speak for not more than 5 minutes each with the following 
Senators to speak for up to the designated times of 15 minutes: Senator 
Domenici and Senator Breaux.
  The Senate will then resume consideration of House Joint Resolution 1 
at 10 a.m., the balanced budget amendment to the Constitution.
  There will be a recess between the hours of 12:30 p.m. and 2:15 p.m. 
for the weekly policy luncheons to meet.
  Mr. President, I yield the floor.
  Mr. COCHRAN addressed the Chair.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Jeffords). The Senator from Mississippi is 
recognized.

                          ____________________
```
