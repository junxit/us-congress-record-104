# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 1788, THE AMTRAK REFORM AND PRIVATIZATION ACT OF 1995

**Measures:** H.Res. 284, H.R. 1788

```
  REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 1788, THE 
              AMTRAK REFORM AND PRIVATIZATION ACT OF 1995

report (Rept. No. 104-370) on the resolution (H. Res. 284) providing 
for the consideration of the bill (H.R. 1788) to reform the statutes
```
