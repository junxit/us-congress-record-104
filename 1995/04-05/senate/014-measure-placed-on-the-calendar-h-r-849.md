# MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--H.R. 849

**Speaking:** Coats, Daniel (C000542) [R-IN], Boxer, Barbara (B000711) [D-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 849

```
                MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--H.R. 849

  Mr. COATS. Mr. President, I understand there is a bill that is ready 
to be read a second time?
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Ashcroft). The clerk will read the bill 
the second time.
  The bill clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 849) to amend the Age Discrimination in 
     Employment Act of 1967 to reinstate an exemption for certain 
     bona fide hiring and retirement plans applicable to State and 
     local firefighters and law enforcement officers; and for 
     other purposes.

  Mr. COATS. Mr. President, I object to further proceedings on the bill 
at this time.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The bill will be placed on the calendar.
  The distinguished Senator from California is recognized.
   [[Page S5176]] Mrs. BOXER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent to 
continue for a full 15 minutes as in morning business.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
