```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING POINTS OF ORDER AGAINST CONFERENCE REPORT 
 ON H.R. 889 EMERGENCY SUPPLEMENTAL APPROPRIATIONS AND RESCISSIONS FOR 
                            FISCAL YEAR 1995
```
