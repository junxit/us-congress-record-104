# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 483, MEDICARE SELECT EXPANSION

**Measures:** H.Res. 130, H.R. 483

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 483, MEDICARE 
                            SELECT EXPANSION

  Ms. PRYCE, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged report 
(Rept. No. 104-103) on the resolution (H. Res. 130) providing for 
consideration of the bill (H.R. 483) to amend title XVIII of the Social 
Security Act to permit medicare select policies to be offered in all 
States, and for other purposes, which was referred to the House 
Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
