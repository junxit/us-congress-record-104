# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** H.R. 260, H.R. 402, H.R. 558, H.R. 1091, H.R. 1296

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Foley). Pursuant to clause 5 of rule I, 
the Chair will now put the question on each motion to suspend the rules 
on which further proceedings were postponed on Monday, September 18, 
1995, in the order in which that motion was entertained.
  Votes will be taken in the following order: H.R. 402 by the yea and 
nays, H.R. 1091 by the yeas and nays, H.R. 260 by the yeas and nays, 
H.R. 1296 by the yeas and nays, and H.R. 558 by the yeas and nays.
  The Chair will reduce to 5 minutes the time for any electronic vote 
after the first such vote in this series.
```
