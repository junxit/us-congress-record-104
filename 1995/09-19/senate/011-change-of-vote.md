# CHANGE OF VOTE

**Speaking:** Rockefeller, John D., IV (R000361) [D-WV]

```
                             CHANGE OF VOTE

  Mr. ROCKEFELLER. Mr. President, on rollcall 440 I voted aye; my 
intention was to vote no. I did not know it was a tabling amendment.
  I ask unanimous consent that I be permitted to change my vote, which 
in no way will change the outcome of the vote.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
