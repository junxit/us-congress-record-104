# QUORUM CALL

**Speaking:** Santorum, Rick (S000059) [R-PA], Kerrey, J. Robert (K000146) [D-NE], Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS], Ford, Wendell H. (F000268) [D-KY]

```
                              QUORUM CALL

  Mr. SANTORUM. Mr. President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. KERREY. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  Mr. SANTORUM. Objection.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard.
  The clerk will continue calling the roll.
  The legislative clerk resumed the call of the roll and the following 
Senators entered the Chamber and answered to their names:

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. A quorum is not present.

and nays.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there a sufficient second?
  There is a sufficient second.
  The yeas and nays were ordered.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The question is on agreeing to the motion of 
the Senator from Pennsylvania. The yeas and nays were ordered, and the 
clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk called the roll.
  Mr. LOTT. I announce that the Senator from Missouri [Mr. Ashcroft], 
the Senator from Georgia [Mr. Coverdell], the Senator from Texas [Mr. 
Gramm], the Senator from North Carolina [Mr. Helms], the Senator from

Senator from Pennsylvania [Mr. Specter], and the Senator from Alaska 
[Mr. Stevens] are necessarily absent.

Wyoming [Mr. Simpson] would vote ``yea.''

Georgia [Mr. Nunn] are necessarily absent.

because of a funeral.
```
