# APPOINTMENT OF CONFEREES--H.R. 2076

**Measures:** H.R. 2076

```
                  APPOINTMENT OF CONFEREES--H.R. 2076

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Mr. President, I understand that pursuant to 
the order of September 29, 1995, the Chair is authorized to appoint 
conferees on the part of the Senate for H.R. 2076, the Commerce, 
Justice, State appropriations bill for fiscal year 1996.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER appointed Mr. Gregg, Mr. Hatfield, Mr. Stevens, 
Mr. Domenici, Mr. McConnell, Mr. Jeffords, Mr. Cochran, Mr. Hollings, 
Mr. Byrd, Mr. Inouye, Mr. Bumpers, Mr. Lautenberg, and Mr. Kerrey of 
Nebraska conferees on the part of the Senate.

                          ____________________
```
