# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the Senate will 
proceed to a period for morning business until 10 a.m., with Senators 
allowed to speak for not more than 5 minutes, with the exception of the 
Senator from Iowa [Mr. Grassley] who is entitled to speak for 10 
minutes.
  The Senator from Iowa is recognized.
```
