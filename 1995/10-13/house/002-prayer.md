# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Rev. James David Ford, D.D., offered the following 
prayer:
  As we go on with our lives and seek to be the people You would have 
us be, we pray, almighty God, that we would heed the words of the 
scriptures and do justice, love mercy, and ever walk humbly with You. 
May that primary perspective of mercy and justice be our conviction as 
we seek to live our lives in service to others. In Your name, we pray. 
Amen.

                          ____________________
```
