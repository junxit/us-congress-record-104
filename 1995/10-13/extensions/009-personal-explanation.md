                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                             HON. TOBY ROTH

                              of wisconsin

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 13, 1995

  ``No'' on the Kennedy amendment to H.R. 2405, rollcall vote No. 710.
  ``No'' on the Lofgren amendment to H.R. 2405, rollcall vote No. 709.
