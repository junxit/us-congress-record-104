# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Fawell, Harris W. (F000049) [R-IL]

**Measures:** H.R. 5

```
[[Page E183]]
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______


                         HON. HARRIS W. FAWELL

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, January 25, 1995
  Mr. FAWELL. Mr. Speaker, during consideration of amendments to H.R. 
5, the Unfunded Mandates Reform Act of 1995, I was recorded as voting 
aye on roll call vote 26. This vote was on an en block amendment 
offered by Mr. Green (D-TX), and my vote should have been recorded as a 
nay in this instance. I would ask that the record reflect my opposition 
to the Green amendment numbered 26.
```
