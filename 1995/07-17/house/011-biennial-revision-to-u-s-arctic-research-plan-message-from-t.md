# BIENNIAL REVISION TO U.S. ARCTIC RESEARCH PLAN--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES

```
   BIENNIAL REVISION TO U.S. ARCTIC RESEARCH PLAN--MESSAGE FROM THE 
                     PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following message 
from the President of the United States; which was read and, together 
with the accompanying papers, without objection, referred to the 
Committee on Science:

To the Congress of the United States:
  Pursuant to the provisions of the Arctic Research and Policy Act of 
1984, as amended (15 U.S.C. 4108(a)), I transmit herewith the fourth 
biennial revision (1996-2000) to the United States Arctic Research 
Plan.
                                                  William J. Clinton.  
  The White House, July 14, 1995.
  

                          ____________________
```
