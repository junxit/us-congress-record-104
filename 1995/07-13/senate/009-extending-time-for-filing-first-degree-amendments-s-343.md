# EXTENDING TIME FOR FILING FIRST-DEGREE AMENDMENTS--S. 343

**Speaking:** Simpson, Alan K. (S000429) [R-WY], Dole, Robert J. (D000401) [R-KS]

**Measures:** S. 343

```
       EXTENDING TIME FOR FILING FIRST-DEGREE AMENDMENTS--S. 343

  Mr. SIMPSON. Mr. President, on behalf of the leader, I ask unanimous 
consent that, notwithstanding the provisions of rule XXII, all Senators 
have until 5 p.m. today in order to file first-degree amendments to the 
pending Dole-Johnston substitute to S. 343, the regulatory reform bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. DOLE. Madam President, was leader time reserved?
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator is correct.
  

                          ____________________
```
