# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there will now 
be a period for the transaction of morning business not to extend 
beyond the hour of 10:45 a.m., with Senators permitted to speak therein 
for not to exceed 5 minutes each.
  Under the previous order, the Senator from Wyoming [Mr. Thomas] will 
be recognized to speak for up to 25 minutes.
```
