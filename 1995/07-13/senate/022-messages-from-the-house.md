# MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.R. 1905

```
                        MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

  At 11:29 a.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mr. Hays, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
passed the following bill, in which it requests the concurrence of the 
Senate:

       H.R. 1905. An act making appropriations for energy and 
     water development for the fiscal year ending September 30, 
     1996, and for other purposes.

  The message also announced that pursuant to the provisions of section 
169(b) of Public Law 102-138, the Speaker appoints the following 
Members to the U.S. Delegation to the Parliamentary Assembly of the 
Conference on Security and Cooperation in Europe on the part of the 
House: Mr. Smith of New Jersey, Vice Chairman, Mr. Hoyer, Mr. 
Torricelli, Mr. Sawyer, Mr. Coleman, Mr. Forbes, Mr. Cardin, and Ms. 
Slaughter.

                          ____________________
```
