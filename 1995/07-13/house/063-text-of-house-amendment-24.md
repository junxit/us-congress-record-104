# Text of House Amendment 24

**Speaking:** Owens, Major R. (O000159) [D-NY]

**Measures:** H.R. 1976

```
H.R. 1976

                         Offered By: Mr. Owens

       Amendment No. 24: Page 70, line 15, strike lines 15 through 
     19 and insert in lieu thereof the following:
       ``Sec. 724. None of the funds appropriated or otherwise 
     made available by this Act shall be used to pay the salaries 
     of personnel who carry out an export enhancement program 
     (estimated to be $1,000,000,000 in the President's fiscal 
     year 1996 Budget Request (H. Doc. 104-4)) if the aggregate 
     amount of funds and/or commodities under such program exceeds 
     $500,000,000.''
```
