# CHANGE OF VOTE

**Speaking:** Biden, Joseph R., Jr. (B000444) [D-DE]

```
                             CHANGE OF VOTE

  Mr. BIDEN. Mr. President, on rollcall vote No. 274, I voted ``nay.'' 
It was my intention to vote ``aye.'' Therefore, I ask unanimous consent 
that I be permitted to change my vote. This will not change the outcome 
of the vote. I have checked with both leaders.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
