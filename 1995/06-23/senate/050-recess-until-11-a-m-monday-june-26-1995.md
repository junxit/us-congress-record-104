# RECESS UNTIL 11 A.M., MONDAY, JUNE 26, 1995

```
              RECESS UNTIL 11 A.M., MONDAY, JUNE 26, 1995

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the Senate now 
stands in recess until 11 a.m. Monday, June 26, 1995.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 3:04 p.m, recessed until Monday, June 26, 
1995, at 11 a.m.
```
