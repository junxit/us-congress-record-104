# PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE

```
[[Page H 9749]]


                          PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Will the gentlewoman from Colorado [Mrs. 
Schroeder] come forward and lead the House in the Pledge of Allegiance.
  Mrs. SCHROEDER led the Pledge of Allegiance as follows:

       I pledge allegiance to the Flag of the United States of 
     America, and to the Republic for which it stands, one nation 
     under God, indivisible, with liberty and justice for all.
```
