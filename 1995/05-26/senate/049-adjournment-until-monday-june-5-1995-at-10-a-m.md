# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL MONDAY, JUNE 5, 1995, AT 10 A.M.

**Speaking:** Dole, Robert J. (D000401) [R-KS]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 72

```
           ADJOURNMENT UNTIL MONDAY, JUNE 5, 1995, AT 10 A.M.

  Mr. DOLE. Mr. President, if there is no further business to come 
before the Senate, I now ask that the Senate stand in adjournment under 
the provisions of House Concurrent Resolution 72.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 2:20 p.m., adjourned until 
Monday, June 5, 1995, at 10 a.m.
```
