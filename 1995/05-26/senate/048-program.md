# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Dole, Robert J. (D000401) [R-KS]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. DOLE. So I would say for the information of all Senators, when 
the Senate is reconvened following the Memorial Day recess, we will 
resume consideration of the antiterrorism bill. Under the previous 
consent agreement, amendments are limited to the bill. Therefore 
Senators should be aware that rollcall votes can be anticipated on 
Monday. However, we will have no rollcall votes until--they will not 
begin before 5 o'clock on Monday, June 5. Both Senators Biden and Hatch 
have indicated to both leaders, Democratic and Republican leaders, that 
they will have amendments. There will be votes. And that they will be 
prepared at 11 o'clock on Monday, June 5, to move forward as rapidly as 
possible on the antiterrorism bill.

                          ____________________
```
