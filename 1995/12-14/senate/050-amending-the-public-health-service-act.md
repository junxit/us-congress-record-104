# AMENDING THE PUBLIC HEALTH SERVICE ACT

**Speaking:** Bond, Christopher S. (B000611) [R-MO]

**Measures:** H.R. 1747

```
                 AMENDING THE PUBLIC HEALTH SERVICE ACT

  Mr. BOND. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent the Senate proceed 
to the immediate consideration of H.R. 1747, just received from the 
House.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The clerk will report.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 1747) to amend the Public Health Service Act, 
     to permanently extend and clarify malpractice coverage for 
     health centers, and for other purposes.

  Mr. BOND. I ask unanimous consent the bill be deemed read a third 
time, passed, the motion to reconsider be laid upon the table, and any 
statements relating to the bill be placed at the appropriate place in 
the Record.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The bill (H.R. 1747) was deemed read three times and passed.

                          ____________________
```
