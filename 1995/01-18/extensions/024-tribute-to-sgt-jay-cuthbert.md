# TRIBUTE TO SGT. JAY CUTHBERT

**Speaking:** Meek, Carrie P. (M000628) [D-FL]

```
                      TRIBUTE TO SGT. JAY CUTHBERT

                                 ______


                          HON. CARRIE P. MEEK

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                       Wednesday, January 18, 1995
  Mrs. MEEK of Florida. Mr. Speaker, after 40 years of dedicated 
service on the Metro-Dade Police Department, Sgt. Jay Cuthbert has 
retired. I speak for our entire Dade County community in expressing our 
deepest gratitude for his exemplary work over four decades of faithful 
and courageous service.
  By any standard, Sergeant Cuthbert is a remarkable public servant. he 
dedicated his entire career to one of the toughest and most difficult 
jobs in our society--law enforcement. He has devoted his life's work to 
the care and protection of the people of our Dade County community.
  Mr. Speaker, we are grateful to Sergeant Cuthbert for the countless 
families and individuals who have benefited from his compassion and 
untiring efforts. I want him to know of my deepest respect for what he 
has done for all of us over these past 40 years, and above and beyond 
the call of duty.
  A grateful community says thank you for a job well done.
  

                          ____________________
```
