# EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

**Speaking:** Woolsey, Lynn C. (W000738) [D-CA]

**Measures:** S. 2

```
                          EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

                                 ______


                          HON. LYNN C. WOOLSEY

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Wednesday, January 18, 1995
```
