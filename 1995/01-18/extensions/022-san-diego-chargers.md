# SAN DIEGO CHARGERS

**Speaking:** Packard, Ron (P000005) [R-CA]

```
[[Page E121]]
                           SAN DIEGO CHARGERS

                                 ______


                            HON. RON PACKARD

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Wednesday, January 18, 1995
  Mr. PACKARD. Mr. Speaker, the people of San Diego are supercharged. 
Coming back from a 7-point halftime deficit, the Chargers steamrolled 
over the Pittsburgh Steelers. Not even the persistent environmental 
disasters back home could keep this team from hopping a plane to Miami. 
The Chargers are going to their first Super Bowl in franchise history.
  This year's Super Bowl will be a classic northern California versus 
southern California battle. Whatever the consequences might be, this 
underdog team has come back with a vengeance time after time. The 
people of San Diego and their team are heading to Miami full of 
determination and pride. I pay tribute to their commitment and cohesion 
as they charge forward to the Super Bowl.


                          ____________________
```
