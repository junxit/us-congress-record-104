# RECESS UNTIL TOMORROW AT 9 A.M.

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

```
                    RECESS UNTIL TOMORROW AT 9 A.M.

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, observing no other Senator wishing to be 
recognized to speak, I move that the Senate now recess under the 
previously agreed to order.
```
