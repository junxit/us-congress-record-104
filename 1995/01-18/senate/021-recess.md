# RECESS

**Speaking:** Dole, Robert J. (D000401) [R-KS]

```
                                 RECESS

  Mr. DOLE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
stand in recess until 2:05 p.m.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 1:35 p.m., recessed until 
the hour of 2:05; whereupon, the Senate reassembled when called to 
order by the Presiding Officer [Mr. Gregg].
  Mr. DOLE. Mr. President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. DOLE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
