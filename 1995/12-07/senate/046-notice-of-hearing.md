# NOTICE OF HEARING

**Speaking:** McCain, John (M000303) [R-AZ]

**Measures:** S. 814, S. 1159

```
                           NOTICE OF HEARING


                      committee on indian affairs

  Mr. McCAIN. Mr. President, I would like to announce that the Senate 
Committee on Indian Affairs will hold an open markup on December 12, 
1995, at 9:30 a.m. in room 485 of the Russell Senate Office Building. 
The markup agenda will include S. 814, to provide for the 
reorganization of the Bureau of Indian Affairs, and S. 1159, to 
establish an American Indian Policy Information Center.
  Those wishing additional information should contact the Committee on 
Indian Affairs at 224-2251.

                          ____________________
```
