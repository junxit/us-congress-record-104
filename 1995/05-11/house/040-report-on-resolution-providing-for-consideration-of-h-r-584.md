# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 584, CONVEYANCE OF THE FAIRPORT NATIONAL FISH HATCHERY TO THE STATE OF IOWA

**Measures:** H.Res. 145, H.R. 584

```
     REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 584, 
 CONVEYANCE OF THE FAIRPORT NATIONAL FISH HATCHERY TO THE STATE OF IOWA

  Mr. McINNIS, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 104-117) on the resolution (H. Res. 145) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 584) to direct the Secretary of the 
Interior to convey a fish hatchery to the State of Iowa, which was 
referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
