# The House met at 10 a.m.

```
   [[Page H2489]] The House met at 10 a.m.


                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Rev. James David Ford, D.D., offered the following 
prayer:
  We pray, O God, that in the business of every day, we will use our 
time wisely so we will gain healthy and holy lives. Remind us that our 
value comes not only in action in the cluttered hours of work, but also 
in reflection and meditation and prayer and an awareness of Your 
abiding spirit in our lives. As we take some time for those precious 
moments of quiet deliberation and circumspection, may we grow in the 
assurance that Your power and Your peace are sufficient for our needs. 
Amen.

                          ____________________
```
