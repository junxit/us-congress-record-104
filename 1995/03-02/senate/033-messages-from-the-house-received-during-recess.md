# MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING RECESS

**Measures:** S. 257

```
             MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING RECESS

  Under the authority of the order of the Senate of January 4 1995, the 
Secretary of the Senate, on March 1, 1995, during the recess of the 
Senate, received a message from the House of Representatives announcing 
that the Speaker has signed the following enrolled bill:

       S. 257. An act to amend the charter of the Veterans of 
     Foreign Wars to make eligible for membership those veterans 
     that have served within the territorial limits of South 
     Korea.

  Under the authority of the order of the Senate of January 4, 1994, 
the enrolled bill was signed on March 1, 1995, during the recess of the 
Senate by the President pro tempore (Mr. Byrd).

                          ____________________
```
