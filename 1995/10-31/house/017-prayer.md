# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Rev. James David Ford, D.D., offered the following 
prayer:
  From the beginning of time, O God, Your benediction of grace has not 
changed; through the steadfast heritage of righteous people, Your 
blessed work has been accomplished; through Your faithful and abiding 
word, we have been enriched and the meaning of life has been 
proclaimed, and through Your love we have been forgiven and redeemed 
and made new. On this new day we offer our thanksgivings for the bounty 
of Your blessings to us and to all people. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
