# SENATE RESOLUTION 190--TO AUTHORIZE THE PRINTING OF A REVISED EDITION OF THE SENATE ELECTION LAW GUIDEBOOK

**Measures:** S.Res. 190

```
 SENATE RESOLUTION 190--TO AUTHORIZE THE PRINTING OF A REVISED EDITION 
                  OF THE SENATE ELECTION LAW GUIDEBOOK

  Mr. WAGNER (for himself and Mr. Ford) submitted the following 
resolution; which was considered and agreed to:

                              S. Res. 190

       Resolved, That the Committee on Rules and Administration is 
     directed to prepare a revised edition of the Senate Election 
     Law Guidebook, Senate Document 103-13, and that such document 
     shall be printed as a Senate document.
       Sec. 2. There shall be printed 600 additional copies of the 
     document specified in section 1 of this resolution for the 
     use of the Committee on Rules and Administration.

                          ____________________
```
