# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Craig, Larry E. (C000858) [R-ID]

**Measures:** H.R. 2002

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. CRAIG. Mr. President, this morning the leaders' time is reserved 
and there will be a period for morning business until 10 a.m. with 
Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each. At 10 a.m., the 
Senate will begin consideration of the conference report to accompany 
H.R. 2002, the Transportation appropriations bill.
  The majority leader has announced that there will be no rollcall 
votes prior to 2:15 today. The Senate will recess from 12:30 to 2:15 
for the weekly policy conferences to meet.
```
