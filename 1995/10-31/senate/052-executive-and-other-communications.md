                   EXECUTIVE AND OTHER COMMUNICATIONS

     Housing, and Urban Affairs.
       EC-1565. A communication from the Chairman of the

     Auditor, transmitting, pursuant to law, the report entitled

     Fiscal Year 1994''; to the Committee on Governmental Affairs.
       EC-1567. A communication from the Director of the Office of 
     Personnel Management, transmitting, pursuant to law, the 
     report on the Employee Assistance Program for fiscal year 
     1994; to the Committee on Governmental Affairs.
