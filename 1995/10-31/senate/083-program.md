# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Smith, Bob (S000606) [R-NH]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. SMITH. Mr. President, for the information of all Senators, at 
approximately 12 noon on Wednesday, it will be the intention of the 
majority leader to turn to the consideration of the House message to 
accompany the budget reconciliation bill in order to appoint conferees 
on the part of the Senate. Several rollcall votes may be necessary on 
motions to instruct. However, there is an overall 10-hour limitation on 
those motions. Members can, therefore, expect rollcall votes throughout 
Wednesday's session of the Senate.
```
