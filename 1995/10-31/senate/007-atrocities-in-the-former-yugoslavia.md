# ATROCITIES IN THE FORMER YUGOSLAVIA

**Speaking:** Craig, Larry E. (C000858) [R-ID]

```
                  ATROCITIES IN THE FORMER YUGOSLAVIA

  Mr. CRAIG. Mr. President, let me join my colleague from Texas in her 
most clarion call this morning to the humanity of the world that this 
Nation be a part of stopping the atrocities that are allegedly going on 
in the former Yugoslavia. It is, without question, a great human 
disaster under any measurement.
  I appreciate the words of my colleague from Texas this morning. She 
has been an outspoken, clear voice on this issue for the last good many 
weeks as these reports have come in to remind us and push this Senate 
and this country in the direction of causing a settlement to occur 
there that is just for both sides. I thank my colleague for that.

                          ____________________
```
