# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Dole, Robert J. (D000401) [R-KS]

```
[[Page S16372]]


                            MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. DOLE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that there now be a 
period for the transaction of morning business with Senators permitted 
to speak for 5 minutes each until the hour of 3:30 p.m.
  At 3:30 p.m., it will be my intention to call up the conference 
report to accompany the energy-water appropriations bill. A rollcall 
vote has been requested. Therefore, another vote is expected during 
today's session of the Senate. We hope to adjourn fairly early this 
evening.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
