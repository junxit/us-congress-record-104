# APPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS OF THE BOARD OF VISITORS TO THE U.S. NAVAL ACADEMY

```
   APPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS OF THE BOARD OF VISITORS TO THE U.S. NAVAL 
                                ACADEMY

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, and pursuant to the 
provisions of section 6968(a) of title 10, United States Code, the 
Chair announces the Speaker's appointment as members of the Board of 
Visitors to the U.S. Naval Academy the following Members of the House: 
Mr. Skeen, of New Mexico; Mr. Gilchrest, of Maryland; Mr. Hoyer, of 
Maryland; and Mr. Mfume, of Maryland.
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
