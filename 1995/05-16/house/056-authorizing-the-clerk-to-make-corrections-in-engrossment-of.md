**Speaking:** Shuster, Bud (S000394) [R-PA]

**Measures:** H.R. 961

```
 AUTHORIZING THE CLERK TO MAKE CORRECTIONS IN ENGROSSMENT OF H.R. 961, 
                     CLEAN WATER AMENDMENTS OF 1995

  Mr. SHUSTER. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that, in the 
engrossment of the bill, H.R. 961, the Clerk be authorized to correct

the actions of the House in amending the bill, H.R. 961.
```
