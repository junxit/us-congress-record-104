# APPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS OF THE BOARD OF VISITORS TO THE U.S. MILITARY ACADEMY

```
 APPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS OF THE BOARD OF VISITORS TO THE U.S. MILITARY 
                                ACADEMY

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, and pursuant to the 
provisions of section 4355(a) of title 10, United States Code, the 
Chair Announces the Speaker's appointment as members of the Board of 
Visitors to the U.S. Military Academy the following Members of the 
House: Mrs. Kelly of New York; Mr. Taylor, of North Carolina; Mr. 
Hefner, of North Carolina; and Mr. Laughlin, of Texas.
  There was no objection.
  

                          ____________________
```
