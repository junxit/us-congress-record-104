# APPOINTMENT OF MEMBERS TO THE BOARD OF TRUSTEES OF THE INSTITUTE OF AMERICAN INDIAN AND ALASKA NATIVE CULTURE AND ARTS DEVELOPMENT

```
  APPOINTMENT OF MEMBERS TO THE BOARD OF TRUSTEES OF THE INSTITUTE OF 
     AMERICAN INDIAN AND ALASKA NATIVE CULTURE AND ARTS DEVELOPMENT

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, and pursuant to the 
provisions of section 1505 of Public Law 99-498 (20 U.S.C. 4412), the 
Chair announces the Speaker's appointment to the Board of Trustees of 
the Institute of American Indian and Alaska Native Culture and Arts 
Development the following Members of the House: Mr. Young of Alaska; 
and Mr. Kildee of Michigan.
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
