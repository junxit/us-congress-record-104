# APPOINTMENT OF INDIVIDUALS AS MEMBERS OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF THE OFFICE OF COMPLIANCE

```
APPOINTMENT OF INDIVIDUALS AS MEMBERS OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF THE 
                          OFFICE OF COMPLIANCE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, and pursuant to the 
provisions of section 301 of Public Law 104-1, the Chair announces on 
behalf of the Speaker and minority leader of the House of 
Representatives and the majority and minority leaders of the U.S. 
Senate their joint appointment of the following individuals to the 
Board of Directors of the Office of Compliance:
  Mr. Glen D. Nager of Washington, DC, chairman, to a 5-year term;
  Ms. Virginia A. Seitz of Washington, DC, to a 5-year term;
  Mr. Jerry M. Hunter of Missouri, to a 4-year term;
  Mr. James N. Adler of California, to a 4-year term; and
  Mr. Lawrence Z. Lorber of Washington, DC, to a 3-year term.
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
