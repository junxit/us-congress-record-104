# APPOINTMENTS BY MAJORITY AND MINORITY LEADERS

```
             APPOINTMENTS BY MAJORITY AND MINORITY LEADERS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair, on behalf of the Majority and 
Minority Leaders of the Senate and the Speaker and Minority Leader of 
the House of Representatives, pursuant to Public Law 104-1, announces 
the joint appointment of the following individuals as members of the 
Board of Directors of the Office of Compliance: Glen D. Nager, of 
Washington, D.C., for a term of 5 years and to serve as Chair; Virginia 
A. Seitz, of Washington, D.C., for a term of 5 years; Jerry M. Hunter, 
of Missouri, for a term of 4 years; James N. Adler, of California, for 
a term of 4 years; and Lawrence Z. Lorber, of Washington, D.C., for a 
term of 3 years.


                          ____________________
```
