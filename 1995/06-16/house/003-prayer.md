# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Rev. James David Ford, D.D., offered the following 
prayer:
  With hearts of gratitude and praise, O gracious God, we offer our 
thanks for Your Word that points us in the right way, that accompanies 
us in the valley of the shadow, that never abandons us though we forget 
or despise, that inspires and encourages us no matter what the concern, 
that forgives us and pardons us of all guilt, that reminds us that in 
all the moments of life we are never alone, for Your Word of faith and 
hope and love is with us always. Amen.
```
