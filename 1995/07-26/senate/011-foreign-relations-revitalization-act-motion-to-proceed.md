**Measures:** S. 908

```

  Mr. LOTT. Therefore, I now move to proceed to S. 908, the State

           Snowe, Bob Dole, Thad Cochran, Paul Coverdell, Larry E.

           Trent Lott.

vote occur on Friday at 10 a.m. and the mandatory quorum under rule 
XXII be waived.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. LOTT. I now withdraw the motion to proceed.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The motion is withdrawn.
```
