# CLOTURE MOTION

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

**Measures:** S. 961

```
                             CLOTURE MOTION

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, for all the reasons cited on this side, I 
therefore now move to proceed to S. 961, the foreign aid authorization 
bill, and send a cloture petition to the desk on the motion.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The cloture motion having been presented under 
rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.
  The bill clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

  We the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the provisions of 
rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, do hereby move to bring 
to a close debate on the motion to proceed to S. 961, the Foreign 
Assistance Authorization bill:
       Dan Coats, Spencer Abraham, Nancy Landon Kassebaum, Rick 
     Santorum, Jesse Helms, Judd Gregg, Strom Thurmond, Olympia 
     Snowe, Bob Dole, Thad Cochran, Paul Coverdell, Larry E. 
     Craig, Phil Gramm, Kay Bailey Hutchison, Rod Grams, Trent 
     Lott.

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the cloture 
vote occur on Friday, immediately following the 10 a.m. cloture vote if 
not invoked, and that the mandatory quorum under rule XXII be waived.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I now withdraw the motion to proceed.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The motion is withdrawn.
  Mr. LOTT. I yield the floor.

                          ____________________
```
