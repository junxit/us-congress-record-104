# APPOINTMENT OF CONFEREES ON H.R. 1854, LEGISLATIVE BRANCH APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 1996

**Speaking:** Packard, Ron (P000005) [R-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 1854

```
       APPOINTMENT OF CONFEREES ON H.R. 1854, LEGISLATIVE BRANCH 
                        APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 1996

  Mr. PACKARD. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent to take from the 
Speaker's table the bill (H.R. 1854) making appropriations for the 
legislative branch for the fiscal year ending September 30, 1996, and 
for other purposes with Senate amendments thereto, disagree to the 
Senate amendments, and agree to the conference asked by the Senate.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from California? The Chair hears none, and without objection, 
appoints the following conferees: Messrs. Packard, Young of Florida, 
Taylor of North Carolina, Miller of Florida, Wicker, Livingston, Fazio, 
Thornton, Dixon, and Obey.
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
