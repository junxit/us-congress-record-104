# Text of House Amendment 28

**Speaking:** Bilbray, Brian P. (B000461) [R-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 2099

```
H.R. 2099

                        Offered By: Mr. Bilbray

       Amendment No. 28: At the end of the bill, insert after the 
     last section (preceding the short title) the following new 
     section:
       Sec.  . No part of the funds appropriated in this act shall 
     be used for the development or analysis of any information 
     when it is made known to the Federal official having 
     authority to obligate or expend such funds that such 
     information is intended or designed to influence in any 
     manner any member of a State or local legislature, to favor 
     or oppose, by vote or otherwise, any legislation or 
     appropriation by a State or local legislature, whether before 
     or after the introduction of any measure proposing such 
     legislation or appropriation.
```
