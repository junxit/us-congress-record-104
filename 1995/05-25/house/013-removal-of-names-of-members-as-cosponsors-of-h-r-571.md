# REMOVAL OF NAMES OF MEMBERS AS COSPONSORS OF H.R. 571

**Speaking:** Bonilla, Henry (B000617) [R-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 571

```
         REMOVAL OF NAMES OF MEMBERS AS COSPONSORS OF H.R. 571

  Mr. BONILLA. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the gentleman 
from New York [Mr. Schumer], the gentleman from New Jersey [Mr. 
Torricelli], and the gentleman from Wisconsin [Mr. Barrett] be removed 
as cosponsors of H.R. 571. The gentlemen misunderstood the substance of 
that bill, and we have agreed to remove them as cosponsors.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Texas?
  There was no objection.
  

                          ____________________
```
