# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, MAY 26, 1995

**Speaking:** Hatch, Orrin G. (H000338) [R-UT]

**Measures:** S. 735

```
                    ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, MAY 26, 1995

  Mr. HATCH. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today it stand in recess until the hour 
of 10 a.m. on Friday, May 26, 1995; that, following the prayer, the 
Journal of proceedings be deemed approved to date; the time for the two 
leaders be reserved for their use later in the day; and the Senate then 
immediately resume consideration of S. 735, the antiterrorism bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  

                          ____________________
```
