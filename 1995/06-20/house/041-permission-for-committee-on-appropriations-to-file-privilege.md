# PERMISSION FOR COMMITTEE ON APPROPRIATIONS TO FILE PRIVILEGED REPORT ON BILL MAKING APPROPRIATIONS FOR ENERGY AND WATER DEVELOPMENT, 1996

```
PERMISSION FOR COMMITTEE ON APPROPRIATIONS TO FILE PRIVILEGED REPORT ON 
   BILL MAKING APPROPRIATIONS FOR ENERGY AND WATER DEVELOPMENT, 1996

  Mr. MEYERS of Indiana. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Committee on Appropriations have until midnight tonight to file a 
privileged report on a bill making appropriations for energy and water 
development for the fiscal year ending September 30, 1996, and for 
other purposes.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Indiana?
  There was no objection.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. All points of order are reserved on the 
bill.

                          ____________________
```
