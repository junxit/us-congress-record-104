# PARLIAMENTARY INQUIRY

**Speaking:** Beilenson, Anthony C. (B000318) [D-CA]

```
                         PARLIAMENTARY INQUIRY

  Mr. BEILENSON. A parliamentary inquiry, Mr. Speaker.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Hefley). The gentleman is recognized for 
his parliamentary inquiry.
  Mr. BEILENSON. Mr. Speaker, am I correct in saying that the next vote 
will be on the previous question on the rule on legislative branch 
appropriations?
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The gentleman is correct.
  Mr. BEILENSON. Continuing my inquiry, if I may, Mr. Speaker, if the 
previous question is defeated, will I be recognized to control the hour 
of additional debate time?
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The Member had led the fight against the 
previous question. The answer would be yes.
  Mr. BEILENSON. Continuing my inquiry, if I may, Mr. Speaker, if I 
control the time, would I be in a position to offer an amendment to the 
rule?
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. A proper amendment would be in order.

                          ____________________
```
