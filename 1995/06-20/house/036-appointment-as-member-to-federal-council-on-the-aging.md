# APPOINTMENT AS MEMBER TO FEDERAL COUNCIL ON THE AGING

```
         APPOINTMENT AS MEMBER TO FEDERAL COUNCIL ON THE AGING

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Hefley). Without objection, and pursuant 
to the provisions of section 204(a) of the Older Americans Act of 1965 
(42 U.S.C. 3015(a)), as amended by section 205 of Public Law 102-375, 
the Chair announces the Speaker's appointment to the Federal Council on 
the Aging for a 3-year term on the part of the House to fill the 
existing vacancy thereon the following member from private life: Mr. 
Charles W. Kane of Stuart, FL.
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
