# Text of House Amendment 86

**Speaking:** Frank, Barney (F000339) [D-MA]

**Measures:** H.R. 1868

```
H.R. 1868

                 Offered By: Mr. Frank of Massachusetts

       Amendment No. 86: Page 78, after line 6, insert the 
     following new section:


                   limitation on funds for indonesia

       Sec. 564. None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used for assistance for Indonesia.
```
