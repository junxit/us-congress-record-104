# DESIGNATION OF MEMBER AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS THROUGH MONDAY, JULY 10, 1995

```
DESIGNATION OF MEMBER AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND 
            JOINT RESOLUTIONS THROUGH MONDAY, JULY 10, 1995

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Speaker:

                                    Washington, DC, June 30, 1995.
       I hereby designate the Honorable Frank Wolf to act as 
     Speaker pro tempore to sign enrolled bills and joint 
     resolutions through July 10, 1995.
                                                    Newt Gingrich,
     Speaker of the House of Representatives.

                          ____________________
```
