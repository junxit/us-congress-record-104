# REVISED EDITION OF STANDING RULES OF THE SENATE

**Speaking:** Gorton, Slade (G000333) [R-WA]

```
            REVISED EDITION OF STANDING RULES OF THE SENATE

  Mr. GORTON. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Committee 
on Rules and Administration be directed to prepare a revised edition of 
the Standing Rules of the Senate, and that such standing rules be 
printed as a Senate document.
  I further ask unanimous consent that 2,500 additional copies of this 
document be printed for the use of the Committee on Rules and 
Administration.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
