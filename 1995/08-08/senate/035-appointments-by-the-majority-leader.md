# APPOINTMENTS BY THE MAJORITY LEADER

```
                  APPOINTMENTS BY THE MAJORITY LEADER

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair on behalf of the majority leader, 
after consultation with the Democratic leader, pursuant to Public Law 
93-415, as amended by Public Law 102-586 announces the appointment of 
James L. Burgess of Kansas to the Coordinating Council on Juvenile 
Justice and Delinquency Prevention, effective July 5, 1995.
  The Chair on behalf of the majority leader, in consultation with the 
Democratic leader, pursuant to Public Law 102-246, appoints the 
following individual to the Library of Congress Trust Fund Board: Adele 
C. Hall of Kansas to a 5 year term.
```
