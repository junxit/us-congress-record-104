# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Gorton, Slade (G000333) [R-WA]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. GORTON. Mr. President, for the information of all Senators, the 
Senate will resume consideration of the Interior bill at 9 a.m. 
tomorrow, with a rollcall vote occurring at 9:30 a.m. Additional 
rollcall votes can be expected to occur during Wednesday's session of 
the Senate in relation to the Interior bill, the DOD authorization 
bill, the DOD appropriations bill and/or the Transportation 
appropriations bill. All Members should expect a late night session on 
Wednesday in order to make progress on any or all of these bills.
```
