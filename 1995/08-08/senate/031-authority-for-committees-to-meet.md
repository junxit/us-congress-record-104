# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

**Speaking:** Packwood, Bob (P000009) [R-OR]

```
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET


                     committee on foreign relations

  Mr. PACKWOOD. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Committee on Foreign Relations be authorized to meet during the session 
of the Senate on Tuesday, August 8, 1995, at 10 a.m.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.


                    select committee on intelligence

  Mr. PACKWOOD. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Select 
Committee on Intelligence be authorized to meet during the session of 
the Senate on Tuesday, August 8, 1995, at 2 p.m. to hold a closed 
hearing on intelligence matters.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
