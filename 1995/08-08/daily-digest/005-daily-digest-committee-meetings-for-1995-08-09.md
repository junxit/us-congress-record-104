# Daily Digest/COMMITTEE MEETINGS FOR 1995-08-09

**Measures:** S. 487, H.R. 2108

```
                         COMMITTEE MEETINGS FOR

        (Committee meetings are open unless otherwise indicated)


                                 Senate

  Committee on Governmental Affairs, Subcommittee on Oversight of 
Government Management and The District of Columbia, to hold hearings 
on H.R. 2108, to permit the Washington Convention Center Authority

Washington Convention Center and for preconstruction activities 
relating to a new convention center in the District of Columbia, to

  Committee on Foreign Relations, to hold joint hearings with the 
Select Committee on Intelligence, to examine the extent of war 
crimes in the Balkans, 9:30 a.m., SD-G50.
  Committee on Indian Affairs, business meeting, to mark up S. 487, 
to establish a Federal Indian Gaming Regulatory Commission to 
regulate Indian gaming operations and standards, 9:30 a.m., SD-106.
```
