# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, NOVEMBER 15, 1995

**Speaking:** Dole, Robert J. (D000401) [R-KS]

```
                ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, NOVEMBER 15, 1995

  Mr. DOLE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it stand in adjournment until the hour of 
12 noon, Wednesday, November 15; that following the prayer, the Journal 
of the proceedings be deemed approved to date, no resolutions come over 
under the rule, the call of the calendar be dispensed with, the morning 
hour be deemed to have expired, the time for the two leaders be 
reserved for their use later in the day.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. DOLE. Mr. President, I ask further that tomorrow, from 12 to 
12:30, there be a period for morning business, with a 5-minute time 
limitation.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
