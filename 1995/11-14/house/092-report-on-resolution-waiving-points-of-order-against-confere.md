# REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING POINTS OF ORDER AGAINST CONFERENCE REPORT ON H.R. 2020, TREASURY, POSTAL SERVICE, AND GENERAL GOVERNMENT APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 1996

**Measures:** H.Res. 267, H.R. 2020

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING POINTS OF ORDER AGAINST CONFERENCE REPORT 
    ON H.R. 2020, TREASURY, POSTAL SERVICE, AND GENERAL GOVERNMENT 
                        APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 1996

  Mr. DIAZ-BALART, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 104-338) on the resolution (H. Res. 267) waiving 
points of order against the conference report to accompany the bill 
(H.R. 2020) making appropriations for the Treasury Department, the U.S. 
Postal Service, the Executive Office of the President, and certain 
independent agencies for the fiscal year ending September 30, 1996, and 
for other purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar and 
ordered to be printed.
```
