# MIDDLE EAST PEACE FACILITATION ACT

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

**Measures:** H.R. 2589

```
                   MIDDLE EAST PEACE FACILITATION ACT

  Mr. LOTT. I ask unanimous consent that the Senate proceed to the 
immediate consideration of H.R. 2589 just received from the House.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection. The clerk will report.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 2589) to extend authorities under the Middle 
     East Peace Facilitation Act of 1994 until December 31, 1995, 
     and for other purposes.

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection to the immediate 
consideration of the bill?
  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the bill.
  Mr. LOTT. I ask unanimous consent that the bill be considered, read a 
third time, passed, and the motion to reconsider be laid upon the 
table, and that any statements relating to this measure appear at the 
appropriate place in the Record as though read.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  So the bill (H.R. 2589) was deemed read the third time and passed.

```
