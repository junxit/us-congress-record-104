# ANNOUNCEMENT OF INTENTION TO OFFER MOTION TO INSTRUCT ON H.R. 2126, DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 1996

**Speaking:** Metcalf, Jack (M000669) [R-WA]

**Measures:** H.R. 2126

```
  ANNOUNCEMENT OF INTENTION TO OFFER MOTION TO INSTRUCT ON H.R. 2126, 
             DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 1996

  (Mr. METCALF asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.

clause 1(c), I am announcing that tomorrow I will offer a motion to
```
