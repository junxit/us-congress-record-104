**Measures:** S. 1, H.J.Res. 1, S. 2, H.R. 4, H.R. 5, H.R. 8

                      CONGRESSIONAL PROGRAM AHEAD

                   Week of January 9 through 14, 1995

                             Senate Chamber

  On Monday, Senate will resume consideration of S. 2, Congressional 
Accountability Act.
