# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
COMMITTEE ORGANIZATION
Committee on the Budget: Met for organizational purposes.
IS TODAY'S SCIENCE POLICY PREPARING US FOR THE FUTURE?
Committee on Science: Held a hearing on ``Is Today's Science Policy 
Preparing Us for the Future?'' Testimony was heard from Ronald H. 
Brown, Secretary of Commerce; Daniel S. Goldin, Administrator, NASA; 
Carol M. Browner, Administrator, EPA; Neal F. Lane, Director, NFS; and 
Jack Gibbons, Director, Office of Science and Technology Policy.

Joint Meetings
EMPLOYMENT SITUATION
Joint Economic Committee: Committee held hearings to examine the 
employment-unemployment situation for December, receiving testimony 
from Katharine G. Abraham, Commissioner, Bureau of Labor Statistics, 
Department of Labor.
  Committee recessed subject to call.
```
