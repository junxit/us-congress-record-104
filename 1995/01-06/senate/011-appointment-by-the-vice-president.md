# APPOINTMENT BY THE VICE PRESIDENT

**Speaking:** Dole, Robert J. (D000401) [R-KS]

```
                   APPOINTMENT BY THE VICE PRESIDENT

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair, on behalf of the Vice President, 
pursuant to the provisions of 20 U.S.C., sections 42 and 43, appoints 
the following Senators as members of the Board of Regents of the 
Smithsonian Institution: the Senator from Mississippi [Mr. Cochran] and 
the Senator from Wyoming [Mr. Simpson].
  The Chair, on behalf of the President of the Senate, pursuant to 
Public Law 85-874, as amended, appoints the Senator from Kansas [Mr. 
Dole] to the Board of Trustees of the John F. Kennedy Center for the 
Performing Arts.
  The Chair, in his capacity as a Senator from Montana, suggests the 
absence of a quorum.
  The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. DOLE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
