# SENATE RESOLUTION 32--MAKING MINORITY PARTY APPOINTMENTS TO SENATE COMMITTEES

**Measures:** S.Res. 32

```
  SENATE RESOLUTION 32--MAKING MINORITY PARTY APPOINTMENTS TO SENATE 
                               COMMITTEES

  Mr. DASCHLE submitted the following resolution; which was considered 
and agreed to:

                               S. Res. 32

       Resolved, That the following shall constitute the minority 
     party's membership on the committees for the One Hundred and 
     Fourth Congress, or until their successors are chosen:
       Committee on the Budget: Mr. Exon, Mr. Hollings, Mr. 
     Johnston, Mr. Lautenberg, Mr. Simon, Mr. Conrad, Mr. Dodd, 
     Mr. Sarbanes, Mrs. Boxer, and Mrs. Murray.
       Committee on Rules and Administration: Mr. Ford, Mr. Pell, 
     Mr. Byrd, Mr. Inouye, Mr. Moynihan, Mr. Dodd, and Mrs. 
     Feinstein.
       Committee on Small Business: Mr. Bumpers, Mr. Nunn, Mr. 
     Levin, Mr. Harkin, Mr. Kerry (MA), Mr. Lieberman, Mr. 
     Wellstone, Mr. Heflin, and Mr. Lautenberg.
       Committe on Veterans' Affairs: Mr. Rockefeller, Mr. Graham, 
     Mr. Akaka, Mr. Campbell, and Mr. Dorgan.
       Special Committee on Aging: Mr. Pryor, Mr. Glenn, Mr. 
     Bradley, Mr. Johnston, Mr. Breaux, Mr. Reid, Mr. Kohl, Mr. 
     Feingold, and Ms. Moseley-Braun.

                          ____________________
```
