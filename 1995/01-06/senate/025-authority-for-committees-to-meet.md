# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

**Speaking:** Dole, Robert J. (D000401) [R-KS]

**Measures:** S. 1

```
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET


            Committee on Banking, Housing, and Urban affairs

  Mr. DOLE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Committee 
on Banking, Housing, and Urban Affairs be authorized to meet during the 
session of the Senate on Friday, January 6, 1995, to conduct a hearing 
to examine issures involving municipal, corporate and individual 
investors in derivative products and the use of highly leveraged 
investment strategies.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.


                   committee on governmental affairs

  Mr. DOLE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent on behalf of the 
Governmental Affairs Committee for authority to meet on Friday, January 
6 for a markup on S. 1, unfunded mandates.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  

                          ____________________
```
