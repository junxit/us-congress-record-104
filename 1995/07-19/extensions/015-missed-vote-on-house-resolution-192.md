# MISSED VOTE ON HOUSE RESOLUTION 192

**Speaking:** Tiahrt, Todd (T000260) [R-KS]

**Measures:** H.Res. 192

```
                  MISSED VOTE ON HOUSE RESOLUTION 192

                                 ______


                            HON. TODD TIAHRT

                               of kansas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, July 19, 1995
  Mr. TIAHRT. Mr. Speaker, due to the fact that I was unavoidably 
detained last evening, I missed the rollcall vote on House Resolution 
192, which called for the House inspector general to complete a more 
detailed audit of the House, Had I been present on rollcall vote No. 
525 I would have voted ``yes.''
```
