# RECESS UNTIL 5:15 P.M.

**Speaking:** Dole, Robert J. (D000401) [R-KS]

```
                         RECESS UNTIL 5:15 P.M.

  Mr. DOLE. Mr. President, I now move that the Senate stand in recess 
until 5:15 p.m.
  The motion was agreed to, and at 4:12 p.m., the Senate recessed until 
5:15 p.m.; whereupon, the Senate reassembled when called to order by 
the Presiding Officer (Mr. Abraham).
  Mr. DOLE addressed the Chair.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The majority leader is recognized.
```
