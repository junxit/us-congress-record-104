# RECESS UNTIL 8:15 P.M.

**Speaking:** Dole, Robert J. (D000401) [R-KS]

```
                         RECESS UNTIL 8:15 P.M.

  Mr. DOLE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
stand in recess until 8:15.
  There being no objection, at 7:55 p.m., the Senate recessed until 
8:14 p.m.; whereupon, the Senate reassembled when called to order by 
the Presiding Officer (Mr. Brown).
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair, in his capacity as the Senator from 
Colorado, suggests the absence of a quorum. The clerk will call the 
roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. DOLE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
