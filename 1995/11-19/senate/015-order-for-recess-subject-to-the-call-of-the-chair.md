# ORDER FOR RECESS SUBJECT TO THE CALL OF THE CHAIR

**Speaking:** Gorton, Slade (G000333) [R-WA]

```
           ORDER FOR RECESS SUBJECT TO THE CALL OF THE CHAIR

  Mr. GORTON. Mr. President, I am requested by the leadership to ask 
unanimous consent that at the conclusion of my remarks, those of the 
distinguished Senator from California [Mrs. Feinstein], and those of 
the distinguished Democratic leader, the Senate stand in recess subject 
to the call of the Chair.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The Senator from Washington is recognized.
```
