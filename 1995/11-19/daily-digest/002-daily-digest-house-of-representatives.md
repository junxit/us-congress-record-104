                        House of Representatives


Chamber Action

  Pages H13340-41, H13347

appropriations for the Departments of Veterans Affairs and Housing and 
Urban Development, and for sundry independent agencies, boards, 
commissions, corporations, and offices for the fiscal year ending 
September 30, 1996 (H. Rept. 104-355).

designates Representative Goodlatte to act as Speaker pro tempore for 
today.
  Page H13299

requirement of clause 4(b) of rule XI with respect to consideration of 
certain resolutions reported from the Committee on Rules.

  Pages H13299-H13306
Motion To Proceed: By a yea-and-nay vote of 199 yeas to 189 nays with 
26 voting ``present,'' the House agreed to the DeLay motion that 
Representative Mica be permitted to proceed in order.
  Pages H13313-14
Consideration of Suspensions: House agreed to H. Res. 275, providing 
for consideration of motions to suspend the rules. Agreed to order the 
previous question on the rule by a yea-and-nay vote 247 yeas to 169 
nays, Roll No. 817.
  Pages H13307-18
Budget Reconciliation: House agreed to H. Res. 279, providing for the 
consideration of the Senate

section 105 of the concurrent resolution on the budget for fiscal year 
1966.
  Pages H13318-19

  Page H13319
National Highway System Designation: House agreed to the conference 
report on S. 440, to amend title 23, United States Code, to provide for 
the designation of the National Highway System--clearing the measure 
for the President.
  Pages H13319-25
Targeted Continuing Resolution: By a yea-and-nay vote of 416 yeas, Roll

making further continuing appropriations for the fiscal year 1996.

  Pages H13325-32
Motion To Adjourn: By a yea-and-nay vote of 32 yeas to 361 nays with 1

Linder motion to adjourn.
  Page H13333

further continuing appropriations for the fiscal year 1996--clearing

November 20.

Senate Messages: Messages received from the Senate appear on pages

18, H13332, and H13333. There were no quorum calls.

November 19.
