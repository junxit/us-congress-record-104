# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 1601, INTERNATIONAL SPACE STATION AUTHORIZATION ACT OF 1995

**Measures:** H.Res. 228, H.R. 1601

```
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 1601, 
         INTERNATIONAL SPACE STATION AUTHORIZATION ACT OF 1995

  Mr. SOLOMON, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 104-258) on the resolution (H. Res. 228) providing 
for the consideration of the bill (H.R. 1601) to authorize 
appropriations to the National Aeronautics and Space Administration to 
develop, assemble, and operate the international space station, which 
was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
