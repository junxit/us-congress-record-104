# RECESS

**Speaking:** Warner, John (W000154) [R-VA]

```
                                 RECESS

  Mr. WARNER. Mr. President, in accordance with the usual schedule of 
the Senate on Tuesday, there will be the caucus luncheons, and 
therefore I ask unanimous consent that the Senate stand in recess until 
the hour of 2:15.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 12:42 p.m., recessed until 
2:15 p.m.; whereupon the Senate reassembled when called to order by the 
Presiding Officer (Mr. DeWine).
```
