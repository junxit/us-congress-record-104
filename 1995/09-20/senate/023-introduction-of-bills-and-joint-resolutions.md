# INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** S. 1262, S. 1263, S. 1264

```
              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second time by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Mr. FORD:
       S. 1262. A bill to provide for the establishment of certain 
     limitations on advertisements relating to, and the sale of, 
     tobacco products, and to provide for the increased 
     enforcement of laws relating to underage tobacco use, and for 
     other purposes; to the Committee on Commerce, Science, and 
     Transportation.
           By Mr. CONRAD (for himself, Mr. Pressler, Mr. Thurmond, 
             and Mr. Inouye]:
       S. 1263. A bill to direct the Secretary of Health and Human 
     Services to revise existing regulations concerning the 
     conditions of payment under part B of the medicare program 
     relating to anesthesia services furnished by certified 
     registered nurse anesthetists, and for other purposes; to the 
     Committee on Finance.
           By Mr. DASCHLE:
       S. 1264. A bill to provide for certain benefits of the 
     Missouri River basin Pick-Sloan project to the Crow Creek 
     Sioux Tribe, and for other purposes; to the Committee on 
     Indian Affairs.
```
