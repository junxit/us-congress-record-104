# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Rev. James David Ford, D.D., offered the following 
prayer:
  Your hand of mercy, O God, is strong enough to give us hope in the 
depths of our hearts; Your voice of comfort, O God, is sure enough to 
give us Your peace and assurance; Your mind, O God, is wise and 
perceptive and counsels us in the ways of life and Your mighty acts of 
compassion and grace, O God, give us confidence and promise in our 
daily lives. In Your name, we pray. Amen.
```
