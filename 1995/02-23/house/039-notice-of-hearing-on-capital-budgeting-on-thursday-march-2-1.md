# NOTICE OF HEARING ON CAPITAL BUDGETING ON THURSDAY, MARCH 2, 1995

**Speaking:** Horn, Stephen (H000789) [R-CA]

```
   NOTICE OF HEARING ON CAPITAL BUDGETING ON THURSDAY, MARCH 2, 1995

  Mr. HORN. Mr. Speaker, I rise to announce that the Subcommittee on 
Government Management, Information and Technology will be holding a 
hearing on capital budgeting on Thursday, March 2, 1995, in room 2154 
Rayburn House Office Building at 2 p.m. The purpose of this hearing 
will be to examine the policy aspects of a capital budget.
```
