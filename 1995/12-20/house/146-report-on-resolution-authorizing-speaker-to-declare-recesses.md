# REPORT ON RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING SPEAKER TO DECLARE RECESSES SUBJECT TO THE CALL OF THE CHAIR FROM DECEMBER 23, 1995 THROUGH DECEMBER 27, 1995

**Measures:** H.Res. 320

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING SPEAKER TO DECLARE RECESSES SUBJECT TO 
 THE CALL OF THE CHAIR FROM DECEMBER 23, 1995 THROUGH DECEMBER 27, 1995

  Mr. GOSS, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged report 
(Rept. No. 104-432) on the resolution (H. Res. 320) authorizing the 
Speaker to declare recesses subject to the call of the Chair from 
December 23, 1995, through December 27, 1995, which was referred to the 
House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
