**Measures:** H.R. 4, H.Res. 319

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING POINTS OF ORDER AGAINST CONFERENCE REPORT 
ON H.R. 4, THE PERSONAL RESPONSIBILITY AND WORK OPPORTUNITY ACT OF 1995

  Mr. GOSS, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged report 
(Rept. No. 104-431) on the resolution (H. Res. 319) waiving points of 
order against the conference report to accompany the bill (H.R. 4) to

spending, and reduce welfare dependence, which was referred to the
```
