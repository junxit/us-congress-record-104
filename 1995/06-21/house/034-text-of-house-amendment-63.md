# Text of House Amendment 63

**Speaking:** Roemer, Tim (R000385) [D-IN]

**Measures:** H.R. 1868

```
H.R. 1868

                         Offered By: Mr. Roemer

       Amendment No. 63: Page 78, after line 6, insert the 
     following new section:


                     limitation on funds for russia

       Sec. 564. Of the funds appropriated in this Act under the 
     heading ``Assistance for the New Independent States of the 
     Former Soviet Union'', not more than $150,000,000 may be made 
     available for Russia.
```
