# GIVE JAPAN THE RAW DEAL

**Speaking:** Traficant, James A., Jr. (T000350) [D-OH]

```
                        GIVE JAPAN THE RAW DEAL

  (Mr. TRAFICANT asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute.)
  Mr. TRAFICANT. Mr. Speaker, here we go again. Japan wants a 
compromise. Japan wants another last minute deal. Japan wants. Japan 
wants. Japan wants.
  Ladies and gentlemen, from President Nixon through President Bush, 
Japan has been able to wriggle out from every crisis. Last month's 
trade deficit hit a record $11.4 billion and Japan wants another last-
minute deal.
  Beam me up here. American jobs are going overseas. And we are giving 
Japan last-minute deals. I say give Japan the deal, the raw deal. The 
same raw deal they have been giving American workers for the last 40 
years.
  They have earned it. They deserve it. Think about it.
  

                          ____________________
```
