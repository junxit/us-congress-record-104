# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Dole, Robert J. (D000401) [R-KS]

**Measures:** S. 440

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. DOLE. Mr. President, this morning leader time has been reserved.
  The Senate will meet in executive session to begin 3 hours of debate 
on the nomination of Dr. Foster.
  At 12 noon, or right around 12 noon, there will be a cloture vote on 
the Foster nomination. If cloture is not invoked the Senate will resume 
consideration of S. 440, the highway bill.
  I just suggest that Members can expect votes. We hope to complete 
action on the highway bill today. I understand there are only one or 
two major amendments and many others are in the process of being worked 
out, or may not be offered.

                          ____________________
```
