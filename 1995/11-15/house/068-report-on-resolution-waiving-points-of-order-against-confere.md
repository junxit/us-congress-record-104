# REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING POINTS OF ORDER AGAINST CONFERENCE REPORT ON H.R. 2126, DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 1996

**Measures:** H.Res. 271, H.R. 2126

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING POINTS OF ORDER AGAINST CONFERENCE REPORT 
      ON H.R. 2126, DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 1996

  Mr. McINNIS, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 104-346) on the resolution (H. Res. 271) waiving

(H.R. 2126) making appropriations for the Department of Defense for the 
fiscal year ending September 30, 1996, and for other purposes, which
```
