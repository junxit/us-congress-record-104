# FURTHER MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** H.R. 1868, H.R. 2020

```
                    FURTHER MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Mr. Lundregan, one of its clerks, 
announced that the Senate recedes from its amendment numbered 115 to 
the bill (H.R. 1868) ``An Act making appropriations for foreign 
operations, export financing, and related programs for the fiscal year 
ending September 30, 1996, and for other purposes.''.
  The message also announced that the Senate agrees to the report of 
the committee of conference on the disagreeing votes of the two Houses 
on the amendments of the Senate to the bill (H.R. 2020) ``An Act making 
appropriations for the Treasury Department, the United States Postal 
Service, the Executive Office of the President, and certain Independent 
Agencies, for the fiscal year ending September 30, 1996, and for other 
purposes.''.
  The message also announced that the Senate recedes from its amendment 
numbered 132 to the above-entitled bill.

                          ____________________
```
