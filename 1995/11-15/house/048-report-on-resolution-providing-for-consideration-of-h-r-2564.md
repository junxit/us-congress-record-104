```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2564, LOBBYING 
                         DISCLOSURE ACT OF 1995

(Rept. No. 104-342) on the resolution (H. Res. 269) providing for the
```
