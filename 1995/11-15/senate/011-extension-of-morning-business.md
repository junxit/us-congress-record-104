# EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Grams, Rod (G000367) [R-MN]

```
                     EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. GRAMS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the period for 
the transaction of morning business be extended to the hour of 1:30 
p.m. today, with Senators permitted to speak for up to 5 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection? Without objection, it is 
so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
