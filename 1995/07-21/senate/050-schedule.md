# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Dole, Robert J. (D000401) [R-KS]

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. DOLE. I would just say for the information of all Senators, under 
the previous order the Senate will begin consideration of the gift ban/
lobbying bill on Monday morning. We hope to be able to reach an 
agreement on both of these measures that will allow us to complete 
action on the resolution on Monday. Rollcall votes, if they are to 
occur, will not occur before 5 p.m. on Monday, so there will be no 
rollcall votes before 5 p.m.
  I cannot say with certainty, but I would be fairly certain there will 
be rollcall votes after 5 p.m., either on final passage or on 
amendments.

                          ____________________
```
