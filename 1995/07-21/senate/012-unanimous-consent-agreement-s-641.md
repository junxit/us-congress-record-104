# UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT--S. 641

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

**Measures:** S. 641

```
                  UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT--S. 641

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate, at 
1:30 p.m., turn to the consideration of Calendar No. 47, S. 641, the 
Ryan White Care Act.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. LOTT. It is the hope of the leadership that all of the opening 
statements would be concluded on this bill today and an amendment would 
be laid down for consideration when the Senate returns to this item 
next week.
  With that announcement, there will be no further votes today. The 
first votes on Monday will occur beginning at 5 p.m.

                          ____________________
```
