# ANNOUNCEMENT OF COMMITTEE MEETING

**Speaking:** Packwood, Bob (P000009) [R-OR]

```
                   ANNOUNCEMENT OF COMMITTEE MEETING

  Mr. PACKWOOD. Mr. President, the Finance Committee has not yet had 
its hearing of Lawrence Summers to be Under Secretary of the Treasury. 
We will be convening the Finance Committee as soon as the last vote is 
over. I would appreciate it if Members can get there reasonably 
promptly. It is a controversial nomination. I hope it will not take a 
long time. We will be taking it up at about a quarter to 1, whenever we 
finish with the vote. I thank my friend from Montana.

                          ____________________
```
