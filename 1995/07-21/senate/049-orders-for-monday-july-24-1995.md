# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, JULY 24, 1995

**Speaking:** Dole, Robert J. (D000401) [R-KS]

**Measures:** S. 101

```
                    ORDERS FOR MONDAY, JULY 24, 1995

  Mr. DOLE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent when the Senate 
completes its business today it stand in recess until the hour of 9 
a.m. on Monday, July 24, 1995; that following the prayer, the Journal 
of proceedings be deemed approved to date, time for the two leaders be 
reserved for their use later in the day, and the Senate then 
immediately begin consideration of S. 101, the gift ban/lobbying bill, 
under the terms of the consent order of June 9.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
