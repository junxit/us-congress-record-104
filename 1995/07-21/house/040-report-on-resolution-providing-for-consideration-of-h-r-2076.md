# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2076, DEPARTMENTS OF COMMERCE, STATE, AND JUSTICE, THE JUDICIARY, AND RELATED AGENCIES APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 1996

**Measures:** H.Res. 198, H.R. 2076

```
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2076, 
DEPARTMENTS OF COMMERCE, STATE, AND JUSTICE, THE JUDICIARY, AND RELATED 
                   AGENCIES APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 1996

  Mr. GOSS, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged report 
(Rept. No. 104-200) on the resolution (H. Res. 198) providing for the 
consideration of the bill (H.R. 2076), making appropriations for the 
Departments of Commerce, Justice, and State, the Judiciary, and related 
agencies for the fiscal year ending September 30, 1996, and for other 
purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be 
printed.
```
