# REPORT OF THE U.S. ARCTIC RESEARCH PLAN--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT--PM 66

```
REPORT OF THE U.S. ARCTIC RESEARCH PLAN--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT--PM 
                                   66

  The PRESIDING OFFICER laid before the Senate the following message 
from the President of the United States, together with an accompanying 
report; which was referred to the Committee on Governmental Affairs.

To the Congress of the United States:
  Pursuant to the provisions of the Arctic Research and Policy Act of 
1984, as amended (15 U.S.C. 4108(a)), I transmit herewith the fourth 
biennial revision (1996-2000) to the United States Arctic Research 
Plan.
                                                  William J. Clinton.  
  The White House, July 14, 1995.
  

                          ____________________
```
