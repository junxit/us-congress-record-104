# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, JULY 17, 1995

**Speaking:** Dole, Robert J. (D000401) [R-KS]

**Measures:** S. 343

```
                    ORDERS FOR MONDAY, JULY 17, 1995

  Mr. DOLE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent when the Senate 
completes its business today, it stand in recess until the hour of 9:30 
a.m. on Monday, July 17, 1995; that following the prayer, the Journal 
of proceedings be deemed approved to date, and the time for the two 
leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and there then be a 
period for morning business until 10 a.m., with Senators permitted to 
speak up to 5 minutes each; further, that at the hour of 10 a.m., the 
Senate immediately resume consideration of S. 343, the regulatory 
reform bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
