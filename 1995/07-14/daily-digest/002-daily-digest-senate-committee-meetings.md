MEXICO
Committee on Banking, Housing, and Urban Affairs: Committee held 
hearings to examine the United States program for Mexico, focusing on 
Mexico's economic stabilization efforts and the Administration's recent 
decision to disburse certain funds to Mexico, receiving testimony from 
Robert E. Rubin, Secretary of the Treasury; and Lawrence Summers, Under 
Secretary of the Treasury for International Affairs.
  Hearings were recessed subject to call.
