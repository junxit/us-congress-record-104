# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Specter, Arlen (S000709) [R-PA]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. SPECTER. Mr. President, for the information of all Senators, the 
Senate will resume consideration of the Bosnia legislation and the 
pending Dole substitute amendment.
  All Members should, therefore, be aware that rollcall votes may occur 
throughout Wednesday's session of the Senate. Also, under the 
provisions of the prior consent agreement, the majority leader may 
return to the consideration of the regulatory reform bill by a call for 
the regular order. Therefore, rollcall votes may occur on that 
legislation as well, including a third cloture vote on the Dole-
Johnston substitute amendment.
```
