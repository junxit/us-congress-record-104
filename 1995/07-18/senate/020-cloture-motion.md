# CLOTURE MOTION

**Measures:** S. 343

```
[[Page S 10221]]


                             CLOTURE MOTION

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, pursuant to rule 
XXII, the Chair lays before the Senate the pending cloture motion, 
which the clerk will state.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     hereby move to bring to a close debate on the pending 
     substitute amendment to S. 343, the Regulatory Reform Bill:
         Bob Dole, Bill Roth, Fred Thompson, Spencer Abraham, Kay 
           Bailey Hutchison, Jon Kyl, Chuck Grassley, Craig 
           Thomas, Orrin Hatch, Larry E. Craig, Mitch McConnell, 
           Conrad Burns, Bob Smith, Jesse Helms, Jim Inhofe, Judd 
           Gregg.

                          ____________________
```
