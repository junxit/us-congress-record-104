# Text of House Amendment 52

**Speaking:** Miller, Dan (M000720) [R-FL]

**Measures:** H.R. 1976

```
H.R. 1976

                   Offered By: Mr. Miller of Florida

       Amendment No. 52: Page 54, line 7, strike ``the program.'' 
     and insert in lieu thereof ``the program: Provided further, 
     That none of the funds in this account shall be available to 
     any State that does not use the competitive bidding process 
     for the procurement of infant formula as required by the 
     Child Nutrition Act as of July 18, 1995.''
```
