# Text of House Amendment 58

**Speaking:** Sanders, Bernard (S000033) [I-VT]

**Measures:** H.R. 1976

```
H.R. 1976

                        Offered By: Mr. Sanders

       Amendment No. 58: Page 71, after line 2, insert the 
     following:
       Sec. 726. After April 1, 1996, none of the funds made 
     available in this Act may be used to pay the salaries and 
     expenses of the Food and Drug Administration unless it is 
     made known to the Federal disbursing official concerned that 
     a test has been developed to show whether synthetic bovine 
     growth hormone (BGH) (also called bovine somatotropin (BST)) 
     is present in milk, that such a test is being developed by 
     the FDA as quickly as practicable, or that, despite the input 
     of all interested persons, the development of such a test is 
     impossible at this time.
```
