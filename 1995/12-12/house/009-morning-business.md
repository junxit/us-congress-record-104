# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to the order of the House of May 
12, 1995, the Chair will now recognize Members from lists submitted by 
the majority leader and minority leader for morning hour debates. The 
Chair will alternate recognition between the parties, with each party 
limited to 30 minutes and each Member, other than the majority and 
minority leaders, limited to 5 minutes.
```
