# VISIT TO THE SENATE BY ISRAELI PRIME MINISTER SHIMON PERES

**Speaking:** Helms, Jesse (H000463) [R-NC]

```
       VISIT TO THE SENATE BY ISRAELI PRIME MINISTER SHIMON PERES

  Mr. HELMS. Mr. President, I have the honor, along with Senator Pell 
from the Foreign Relations Committee, of presenting the new Prime 
Minister from Israel, Shimon Peres.
  I ask unanimous consent the Senate stand in recess for 6 minutes.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.


                                 Recess

  Thereupon, at 5:45 p.m., the Senate recessed until 5:52 p.m.; 
whereupon, the Senate reassembled when called to order by the Presiding 
Officer (Mr. Grams).
  Mr. NICKLES addressed the Chair.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator from Oklahoma.

                          ____________________
```
