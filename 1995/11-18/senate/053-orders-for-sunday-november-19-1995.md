# ORDERS FOR SUNDAY, NOVEMBER 19, 1995

**Speaking:** Dole, Robert J. (D000401) [R-KS]

```
                  ORDERS FOR SUNDAY, NOVEMBER 19, 1995

  Mr. DOLE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today it stand in adjournment until the hour of 
2:30, p.m., Sunday, November 19, that following the prayer, the Journal 
of proceedings be approved to date, no resolutions come over under the 
rule, the call of the calendar be dispensed with, and the morning hour 
be deemed to have expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for 
their use later in the day, and there then be the period for morning 
business with Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
