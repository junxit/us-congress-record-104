# UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT

**Speaking:** McCain, John (M000303) [R-AZ], Conrad, Kent (C000705) [D-ND]

```
                      UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT

  Mr. McCAIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senator 
from South Carolina be granted such time as he may use as in morning 
business, and following that we proceed to discussion of the line-item 
veto.
  I yield to my colleague from North Dakota, if he has a request or a 
comment.
  Mr. CONRAD. Mr. President, I say to the floor manager, I would like, 
if possible, 5 minutes at the end of the remarks of the distinguished 
Senator from South Carolina.
  Mr. McCAIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that following the 
remarks of the Senator from South Carolina, the President pro tempore 
of the Senate, that the Senator from North Dakota be recognized for up 
to 10 minutes for any remarks that he may make.
  [[Page S4122]] The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Inhofe). Without objection, 
it is so ordered.


                          ____________________
```
