# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

**Speaking:** Dole, Robert J. (D000401) [R-KS]

```
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET


              subcommittee on environment and public works

  Mr. DOLE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the full 
Committee on Environment and Public Works be granted permission to meet 
Friday, March 17, 1995, at 9:30 a.m., to conduct a hearing on 
Department of the Interior and Department of Defense consultations 
concerning conservation of endangered species at Fort Bragg, NC, and on 
legislation regarding public uses of the Back Bay National Wildlife 
Refuge in Virginia.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  

                          ____________________
```
