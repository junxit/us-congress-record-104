# CLOTURE MOTION

**Measures:** S. 534

```
                             CLOTURE MOTION

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the hour of 10 
o'clock having arrived, the clerk will report the motion to invoke 
cloture.
  The assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     hereby move to bring to a close debate on the pending 
     committee substitute amendment to S. 534, the solid waste 
     disposal bill
         John H. Chafee, Bob Dole, Bob Smith, Jim Jeffords, Hank 
           Brown, Kit Bond, Orrin Hatch, Spencer Abraham, Jon Kyl, 
           Larry E. Craig, Kay Bailey Hutchison, Trent Lott, R.F. 
           Bennett, Pete V. Domenici, Dirk Kempthorne, Jesse 
           Helms.

                          ____________________
```
