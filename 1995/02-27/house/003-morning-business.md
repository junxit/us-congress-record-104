# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to the order of the House of 
January 4, 1995, the Chair will now recognize Members from lists 
submitted by the majority and the minority leaders for morning hour 
debates. The Chair will alternate recognition between the parties, with 
each party limited to 30 minutes and each Member other than the 
majority and minority leaders limited to 5 minutes.
  The Chair now recognizes the gentlewoman from Colorado [Mrs. 
Schroeder] for 5 minutes.
```
