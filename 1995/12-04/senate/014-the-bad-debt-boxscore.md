# THE BAD DEBT BOXSCORE

**Speaking:** Helms, Jesse (H000463) [R-NC]

```
                         THE BAD DEBT BOXSCORE

  Mr. HELMS. Mr. President, as of the close of business Friday, 
December 1, the Federal debt stood at $4,989,268,168,883.55. We are 
still about $11 billion away from the $5 trillion mark. Unfortunately, 
we anticipate hitting this mark sometime later this year or early next 
year.
  On a per capita basis, every man, woman, and child in America owes 
$18,939.35 as his or her share of that debt.

                          ____________________
```
