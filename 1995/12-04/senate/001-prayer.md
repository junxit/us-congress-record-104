# prayer

```
                                 prayer

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Lloyd John Ogilvie, offered the following 
prayer:
  Sovereign Lord, help us to see our work here in Government as our 
divine calling and mission. Whatever we are called to do today, we want 
to do our very best for Your glory. Our desire is not just to do 
different things, but to do the same old things differently: with 
freedom, joy, and excellence. Give us new delight for matters of 
drudgery, new patience for people who are difficult, new zest for 
unfinished details. Be our lifeline in the pressures of deadlines, our 
rejuvenation in routines, and our endurance whenever we feel enervated. 
May we spend more time talking to You about issues than we do talking 
about issues to others. So may our communion with You give us deep 
convictions and high courage to defend them. Spirit of the living God, 
fall afresh on us so we may serve with fresh dedication today. In the 
Lord's name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
