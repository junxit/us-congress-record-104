# INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** S. 1442, S. 1443

```
              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second time by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Mr. JOHNSTON:
       S. 1442. A bill to authorize the Secretary of Health and 
     Human Services to award a grant for the establishment of the 
     National Center for Sickle Cell Disease Research, and for 
     other purposes; to the Committee on Labor and Human 
     Resources.
           By Mr. SIMON (for himself and Ms. Moseley-Braun):
       S. 1443. A bill to designate the United States Post Office 
     building located at 102 South McLean, Lincoln, Illinois, as 
     the ``Edward Madigan Post Office Building,'' and for other 
     purposes; to the Committee on Governmental Affairs.
```
