# SENATE RESOLUTION 184--MAKING MAJORITY PARTY APPOINTMENTS TO CERTAIN SENATE COMMITTEES FOR THE 104TH CONGRESS

**Measures:** S.Res. 184

```
 SENATE RESOLUTION 184--MAKING MAJORITY PARTY APPOINTMENTS TO CERTAIN 
                SENATE COMMITTEES FOR THE 104TH CONGRESS

  Mr. KEMPTHORNE (for Mr. Dole) submitted the following resolution; 
which was considered and agreed to:

                              S. Res. 184

       Resolved, That the following shall constitute the majority 
     party's membership on the following standing committees for 
     the 104th Congress, or until their successors are chosen:
       Agriculture: Mr. Lugar, Mr. Dole, Mr. Helms, Mr. Cochran, 
     Mr. McConnell, Mr. Craig, Mr. Coverdell, Mr. Santorum, Mr. 
     Warner, and Mr. Grassley.
       Banking, Housing, and Urban Affairs: Mr. D'Amato, Mr. 
     Gramm, Mr. Shelby, Mr. Bond, Mr. Mack, Mr. Faircloth, Mr. 
     Bennett, Mr. Grams, and Mr. Domenici.
       Commerce, Science, and Transportation: Mr. Pressler, Mr. 
     Stevens, Mr. McCain, Mr. Burns, Mr. Gorton, Mr. Lott, Mrs. 
     Hutchison, Ms. Snowe, Mr. Ashcroft, and Mr. Frist.
       Governmental Affairs: Mr. Stevens, Mr. Roth, Mr. Cohen, Mr. 
     Thompson, Mr. Cochran, Mr. McCain, Mr. Smith, and Mr. Brown.

                          ____________________
```
