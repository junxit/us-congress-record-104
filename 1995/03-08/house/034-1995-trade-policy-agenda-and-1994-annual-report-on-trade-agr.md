# 1995 TRADE POLICY AGENDA AND 1994 ANNUAL REPORT ON TRADE AGREEMENTS PROGRAM--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES

```
  1995 TRADE POLICY AGENDA AND 1994 ANNUAL REPORT ON TRADE AGREEMENTS 
        PROGRAM--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following message 
from the President of the United States, which was read and, together 
with the accompanying papers, without objection, referred to the 
Committee on Ways and Means.

To the Congress of the United States:
  As required by section 163 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended (19 
U.S.C. 2213), I transmit herewith the 1995 Trade Policy Agenda and 1994 
Annual Report on the Trade Agreements Program.
                                                  William J. Clinton.  
  The White House, March 8, 1995.

                          ____________________
```
