# APPOINTMENT OF DEPUTY SENATE LEGAL COUNSEL

**Speaking:** Dole, Robert J. (D000401) [R-KS]

**Measures:** S.Res. 86

```
               APPOINTMENT OF DEPUTY SENATE LEGAL COUNSEL

  Mr. DOLE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the immediate consideration of Senate Resolution 86, 
submitted earlier today by Senators Dole and Daschle, regarding the 
Deputy Senate Legal Counsel, that the resolution be considered and 
agreed to, and the motion to reconsider be laid upon the table.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  So the resolution (S. Res. 86) was agreed to, as follows:
       Resolved, That the appointment of Thomas B. Griffith to be 
     Deputy Senate Legal Counsel, made by the President pro 
     tempore this day, shall become effective on March 13, 1995, 
     and the term of service of the appointee shall expire at the 
     end of the One Hundred Fifth Congress.
     

                          ____________________
```
