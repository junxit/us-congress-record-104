# REPORT ON THE ADMINISTRATION OF THE FEDERAL RAILROAD SAFETY ACT OF 1970--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT--PM 30

```
  REPORT ON THE ADMINISTRATION OF THE FEDERAL RAILROAD SAFETY ACT OF 
                1970--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT--PM 30

  The PRESIDING OFFICER laid before the Senate the following message 
from the President of the United States, together with an accompanying 
report; which was referred to the Committee on Commerce, Science, and 
Transportation.

To the Congress of the United States:
  I transmit herewith the 1993 annual report on the Administration of 
the Federal Railroad Safety Act of 1970, pursuant to section 211 of the 
Act (45 U.S.C. 440(a)).
                                                  William J. Clinton.  
  The White House, March 8, 1995.
  

                          ____________________
```
