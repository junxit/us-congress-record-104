# RECESS

```
                                 RECESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Hearing no objection, under the previous 
order, the hour of 12:30 nearly having arrived, the Senate will now 
stand in recess until the hour of 2:15 p.m.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 12:22 p.m., recessed until 2:16 p.m.; 
whereupon, the Senate reassembled when called to order by the Presiding 
Officer (Mr. Grams).
```
