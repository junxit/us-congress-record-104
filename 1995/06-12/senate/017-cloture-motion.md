# CLOTURE MOTION

**Speaking:** Lott, Trent (L000447) [R-MS]

**Measures:** S. 652

```
                             CLOTURE MOTION

  Mr. LOTT. Mr. President, I send a cloture motion to the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The cloture motion having been presented under 
rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.
  The assistant legislative clerk read as follows:


                             Cloture Motion

       We the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on S. 652, the 
     Telecommunications Competition and Deregulation Act:
       Trent Lott, Larry Pressler, Judd Gregg, Don Nickles, Rod 
     Grams, Rick Santorum, Craig Thomas, Spencer Abraham, J. James 
     Exon, Bob Dole, Ted Stevens, Larry E. Craig, Mike DeWine, 
     John Ashcroft, Robert F. Bennett, Hank Brown, Conrad R. 
     Burns.

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The acting majority leader.

                          ____________________
```
