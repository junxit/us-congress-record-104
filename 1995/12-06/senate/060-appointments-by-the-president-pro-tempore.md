# APPOINTMENTS BY THE PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

```
               APPOINTMENTS BY THE PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair, on behalf of the President pro 
tempore, pursuant to Public Law 99-83, appoints the following 
individuals to the Commission for the Preservation of America's 
Heritage Abroad: Rabbi Chaskel Besser of New York, E. William Crotty of 
Florida, and Ned Bandler of New York.
  The Senator from New Hampshire is recognized.
```
