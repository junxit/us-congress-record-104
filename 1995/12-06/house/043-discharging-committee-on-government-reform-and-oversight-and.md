# DISCHARGING COMMITTEE ON GOVERNMENT REFORM AND OVERSIGHT AND REREFERRAL OF H.R. 564 TO CERTAIN STANDING COMMITTEES

**Speaking:** Clinger, William F., Jr. (C000523) [R-PA]

**Measures:** H.R. 564

```
DISCHARGING COMMITTEE ON GOVERNMENT REFORM AND OVERSIGHT AND REREFERRAL 
               OF H.R. 564 TO CERTAIN STANDING COMMITTEES

  Mr. CLINGER. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the Committee 
on Government Reform and Oversight be discharged from the consideration 
of the bill, H.R. 564, which was misreferred, and that H.R. 564 be 
rereferred to the Committee on the Budget as the primary committee and, 
in addition, to the Committee on Transportation and Infrastructure.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Pennsylvania?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
